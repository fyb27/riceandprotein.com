About Rice and Protein

Welcome to Rice and Protein!

When I first started this journey, I was going in completely blind. Absolutely no clue what I was doing. Calories, macros, protein, they all just sounded like fancy words people at the gym threw around. Didn't mean anything to me.

I was 120kg. Tried keto, gave up after two weeks. Tried starving myself, lasted three days. Nothing stuck because none of it made sense and none of it was sustainable for a guy who eats rice every day and considers char kuey teow a personality trait.

Eventually I figured out the basics, applied them consistently, and went from 120kg down to 80kg. No supplements. No crazy diets. No giving up nasi lemak forever. Just understanding how food actually works and making smarter choices most of the time.

That's why I built this site. I wanted to put everything I wished I had when I started, in one place, explained simply, for free. If you're new to all this and feel lost, you're exactly who this is for.

I really do hope you find it useful. If you have questions or just want to say hi, drop me a message. Happy dieting lah!
