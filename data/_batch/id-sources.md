## id-3

- gado-gado: 390 kcal per 285g plate. TKPI gado-gado 137 kcal/100g (via wiki.ambisius.com and Kemenkes citations, https://wiki.ambisius.com/makanan-khas/gado-gado/nutrisi-dan-kandungan-gizi); cross-check FatSecret Indonesia 318 kcal per 241g bowl, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/gado-gado. Picked the TKPI scaling because a warung plate with lontong is heavier than the FatSecret bowl.
- sayur-asem: 80 kcal per 240g bowl. FatSecret Indonesia, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/sayur-asem
- sayur-lodeh: 160 kcal per 240g bowl. FatSecret Indonesia 162 kcal, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/sayur-lodeh
- ketoprak: 520 kcal per cart plate (about 285g). FatSecret Indonesia 402 kcal per 200g (201 kcal/100g) scaled to a real plate, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/ketoprak
- seblak: 450 kcal per komplit bowl. FatSecret Indonesia 262 kcal per 200g basic seblak, 315 per bowl, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/seblak; toppings (bakso, sosis, macaroni) added from components.
- coto-makassar: 290 kcal per bowl, no ketupat. FatSecret Indonesia 289 kcal per 241g bowl, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/coto-makassar; TKPI coto mangkasara 84 kcal/100g via nilaigizi.com (https://nilaigizi.com/gizi/detailproduk/814/nilai-kandungan-gizi-coto-mangkasara-sapi-masakan) as cross-check.
- sop-buntut: 380 kcal per restaurant bowl, no rice. Estimate from components (oxtail meat, broth, vegetables). TKPI sop buntut 71 kcal/100g as served via nilaigizi.com (https://nilaigizi.com/gizi/detailproduk/838/nilai-kandungan-gizi-Sop-buntut,-masakan) gives 390 for a 550g bowl, consistent. The FatSecret "sop buntut" entry (68 kcal per bowl) is clearly an instant seasoning and was ignored.
- sate-kambing: 370 kcal per 10 skewers with kecap. FatSecret Indonesia 32 kcal per skewer, 216 kcal/100g, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/sate-kambing; kecap dressing estimated.
- tongseng: 420 kcal per bowl. FatSecret Indonesia 169 kcal/100g, 422 kcal per bowl, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/tongseng
- bebek-goreng: 380 kcal per piece with sambal and lalapan. FatSecret Indonesia 337 kcal per piece, 286 kcal/100g, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/bebek-goreng
- pempek: 400 kcal per kapal selam with cuko. FatSecret Indonesia 195 kcal/100g, 39 kcal per small piece, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/pempek; cuko estimated from palm sugar content. NutriNusa lists kapal selam at 335 without sauce, in line.
- batagor: 600 kcal per 5 piece portion with sauce. FatSecret Indonesia 290 kcal/100g, 58 kcal per piece, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/batagor; sauce from components.
- siomay-bandung: 580 kcal per full plate. FatSecret Indonesia siomay 51 kcal per piece, 138 kcal/100g, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/siomay; plate built from components (tofu, potato, egg, peanut sauce).
- martabak-manis: 270 kcal per 90g slice. FatSecret Indonesia, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/martabak-manis
- cilok: 320 kcal per cup of 10 with sauce. FatSecret Indonesia 266 kcal/100g, 40 kcal per piece, 319 kcal per 120g serving, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/cilok

## id-4

- es-teh-manis: 90 kcal per 240ml glass. FatSecret Indonesia, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/es-teh

The FatSecret generic "es kopi dengan susu dan gula" entry (30 kcal) is a diluted instant coffee and was ignored.

- kolak: 390 kcal per bowl. FatSecret Indonesia 163 kcal/100g, 391 kcal per bowl, 196 kcal per 120g, https://www.fatsecret.co.id/kalori-gizi/umum/kolak
