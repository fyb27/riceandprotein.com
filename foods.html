Malaysian Food Calories

How many calories are really in your favourite hawker and kopitiam food. Honest numbers, the macros, and how to eat it lighter without giving it up.

This is every dish I could get honest numbers for, 291 of them, sorted into 11 categories. Nasi lemak, char kuey teow, roti canai, teh tarik, the kuih your colleague brings on Monday. Click any one for the macro breakdown, where the calories actually hide, and how to order it lighter.

Nothing here is banned. I ate at mamak and kopitiam the whole way from 128kg to 86kg. The only thing that changed was that I knew what each plate cost me before I ordered it, which is the entire point of this page.

Malaysian street food has no nutrition labelling, so these are careful estimates against typical hawker portions, not lab results. Treat them as a guide that is right often enough to be useful.

Common questions

What is the highest calorie Malaysian food?
Of the 291 dishes on this site, Nasi Ambeng tops the list at 1080 calories per 1 portion (single-pax share from communal platter). Nasi Kandar (950) and Banana Leaf Rice (850) are close behind. Anything deep fried, built on coconut milk, or served as a full communal plate tends to land up here.
What is the lowest calorie Malaysian food?
Clear soups and plain vegetable sides are where the genuinely cheap calories are. Latok (Sea Grapes) at 30 calories per 1 small plate, Ulam-Ulaman at 60 calories per 1 plate with sambal belacan, Sup Sayur at 60 calories per 1 bowl are the kind of thing you can pile on a plate without thinking about it. Go looking for the outright lowest number and you end up at Butod (20 calories per 1 grub (grilled)), which is a real answer but not a lunch.
How many calories is a typical Malaysian meal?
Across everything on this site the average is about 339 calories a serving, but that mixes kuih with full rice plates. A realistic hawker lunch, one rice or noodle dish plus a drink, usually lands between 600 and 900 calories. Nasi kandar with a couple of lauk and a teh ais can pass 1,200 on its own.
Where do these calorie numbers come from?
They are built from standard nutrition data for the ingredients, weighed against typical hawker and kopitiam portion sizes. Malaysian street food has no official nutrition labelling, so every figure here is a considered estimate, not a lab result. Stalls vary, and the same dish can swing 20 percent either way depending on how heavy the oil hand is.
Berapa kalori dalam makanan Malaysia?
Purata untuk 291 hidangan di laman ini ialah kira-kira 339 kalori satu hidangan. Yang paling tinggi ialah Nasi Ambeng (1080 kalori) dan antara yang paling rendah ialah Latok (Sea Grapes) (30 kalori). Semua angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa dan berbeza ikut kedai.
