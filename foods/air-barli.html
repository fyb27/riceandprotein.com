How Many Calories in Air Barli?

Air barli is a Malaysian barley drink made by boiling pearl barley with rock sugar until the water turns slightly milky and sweet, often served cold at kopitiams and hawker stalls.

100kcal / 1 glass (hawker/kopitiam)
A standard 300ml kopitiam glass of air barli comes in at about 100 calories. Most of that is from the dissolved rock sugar, around 48 calories from roughly 2.5 teaspoons of sweetener. The pearl barley itself contributes about 52 calories of starch that leaches into the water during boiling. So yeah, this is basically flavoured sugar water with a faint grain taste. Not terrible in the grand scheme of Malaysian drinks, but the calories are almost entirely carbs (24g), zero fat, barely any protein.

The real talk

Air barli is one of those drinks you grab at the kopitiam when you want something that feels healthy, and honestly it's not wrong to think that way. Compared to teh tarik or Milo ais, 100 calories for a full glass is pretty decent. The drink is just boiled pearl barley water sweetened with rock sugar, sometimes with a squeeze of lemon if you order barli limau. The barley grain barely shows up calorie-wise since most of it stays solid and you're drinking the water, not eating the grain. To be upfront: these numbers are a ballpark estimate because solid nutrition data for kopitiam air barli is pretty thin, and sweetness levels vary from stall to stall.

What actually moves the calorie count here is simple: sugar. A lightly sweetened glass sits around 100 calories, but a generous auntie who pre-sweetens a whole pot heavily can push you to 130 calories without you knowing. Barli kosong with no sugar at all drops to around 30 calories, basically just hot grain water. Barli limau adds maybe 10 calories from the lemon and sometimes a touch more sugar to balance the sourness, landing around 110 calories. If you order frequently, asking for kosong or kurang manis is an easy way to shave 50 to 70 calories per glass across a week without feeling like you're giving up anything.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order barli kosong or kurang manis. No sugar or less sugar drops it from 100 calories down to 30 to 60, and the drink still tastes like barley water.
  • Barli limau kosong is a solid option. The lemon gives it enough flavour that you don't miss the sweetness as much.
  • Skip the refill at the self-serve stalls. That second glass is another 100 calories you probably didn't plan for.
  • Drink it hot. Hot drinks tend to get sipped slower, so you drink less overall.
  • If you're making it at home, use stevia or skip the sweetener entirely. The barley starch gives a faint natural sweetness on its own.

Common questions

How many calories in Air Barli?
A standard kopitiam glass of air barli (300ml) is around 100 calories. The sugar is the main source, roughly 48 calories from about 2.5 teaspoons of dissolved rock sugar. Order it kosong and it drops to about 30 calories.
What is air barli exactly?
Air barli is pearl barley boiled in water with rock sugar until the liquid turns slightly cloudy and sweet. You drink the liquid, not the grain. It's a common kopitiam and hawker stall drink served cold or hot, sometimes with lemon (barli limau). It's been around Malaysian coffee shops forever.
Is air barli a healthy drink?
Compared to teh tarik or Milo ais, 100 calories for a full glass is actually on the lower end for kopitiam drinks. There's no fat and no real protein either, so it's essentially sweetened water. It's fine as an occasional drink. If you want it to work for a diet, ask for kosong or kurang manis.
Berapa kalori dalam Air Barli?
Air Barli ada kira-kira 100 kalori untuk 1 gelas (300g), dengan 1g protein, 24g karbohidrat dan 0g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
