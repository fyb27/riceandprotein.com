How Many Calories in Air Kelapa?

Fresh young coconut water with soft jelly flesh, one of the lightest things you can drink at a Malaysian hawker stall.

100kcal / 1 whole young coconut (air + isi)
One whole young coconut (water plus flesh) comes in at 100 calories for roughly 390g of drink and snack combined. The coconut water alone is about 66 calories for 300ml, and the soft jelly flesh adds another 34 calories on top. So yeah, air kelapa is genuinely one of the lowest-calorie things you can order at a hawker stall.

The real talk

Bro, 100 calories for a whole coconut. The water is 66 calories for 300ml and the isi adds 34 calories, so we are talking about a legit low-calorie option that actually fills you up a bit. The 21g of carbs here is a mix of natural sugars from the water and flesh, and at that total it is not going to wreck your deficit if you are eating one a day. This is the drink you want to be ordering at the pasar malam instead of that teh tarik with three teaspoons of condensed milk.

The thing that changes the maths here is whether you eat the flesh or skip it. Air sahaja, just the water, is only 65 calories. Add the isi kelapa muda and you get 34 extra calories for 90g of soft jelly, which is honestly worth it because the flesh keeps you more satisfied. What you want to avoid is the blended versions some stalls sell with added sugar or syrup, because suddenly your 100-calorie coconut turns into a 200-calorie dessert drink. Stick to the plain, chop it open in front of you version and you are fine lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order air sahaja without the flesh to drop from 100 calories down to 65 calories, a small but real saving.
  • Skip any stall that adds sugar syrup or flavouring to the coconut water, that doubles the calories for no reason.
  • Drink it plain and cold, no ice blending, blending sometimes means added ingredients you did not ask for.
  • Use it as a swap for teh tarik or Milo ais, both of which are 150 to 250 calories, and you save a decent chunk across the week.
  • If you want the flesh, eat it slowly, it is actually quite filling for 34 calories and will stop you snacking right after.

Common questions

How many calories in Air Kelapa?
A whole young coconut with water and flesh is about 100 calories (390g total). The water alone is 66 calories for 300ml, and the soft jelly flesh adds 34 calories. If you skip the flesh, you are looking at 65 calories.
Is air kelapa good for weight loss?
Honestly, yes. At 65 to 100 calories depending on whether you eat the flesh, it is one of the best drink options at a hawker stall. It hydrates you, has a bit of natural sugar to settle a craving, and keeps the calorie count low. Just make sure nobody is adding syrup to it.
How much sugar is in coconut water?
The whole coconut order here has 21g of carbs, most of which is a mix of natural sugars from the water and flesh. That is comparable to a small banana. It is not zero sugar, but it is a far better choice than teh tarik or any syrup-based drink at the stall.
Berapa kalori dalam Air Kelapa?
Air Kelapa ada kira-kira 100 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (390g), dengan 1g protein, 21g karbohidrat dan 2g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
