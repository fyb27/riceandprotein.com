How Many Calories in Air Tebu?

Also known as Sugarcane Juice.

Fresh-pressed sugarcane juice that tastes innocent but packs 57g of pure sugar into a single cold glass.

220kcal / 1 glass (with ice)
Air Tebu comes in at 220 calories per glass (around 300ml with ice). That's all from sugar, because sugarcane juice is basically liquid sucrose with zero fat and zero protein. The number is honest and it's not small. A plain glass with ice sits at 220 cal, add lime and you're at 230, and if you go with asam boi it creeps up to 250 because of the sugar and salt coating on the plum.

The real talk

So Air Tebu is 220 calories and every single one of those calories comes from sugar. No fat, no protein, just 57g of pure sucrose, glucose, and fructose pressed straight out of the sugarcane. The thing is, it doesn't feel like 220 calories because it's cold, it's refreshing, and it goes down in about 90 seconds. That's the trap lah. Your brain barely registers it as food, but your calorie count absolutely does. If you're someone who grabs one of these every time you're at the pasar malam or waiting for your food at the mamak, that habit alone could be adding 1,500 calories a week to your total.

The math shifts a bit depending on what you add. Plain with ice is your baseline at 220 cal. Throw in a squeeze of lime and you're at 230, basically no difference. But asam boi pushes it to 250 because that preserved plum has a proper sugar and salt coating on it. Also worth knowing: the portion size matters a lot here. A small cup is maybe 200ml, a big roadside plastic bag can easily be 400 to 500ml, which means you could be looking at 300 to 370 calories for one drink. If you're serious about tracking, eyeball the portion or ask for a standard size, bro. Don't let the uncle upsell you a jumbo bag without knowing what you're signing up for.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order one glass, not two. It sounds obvious but at the roadside stall the uncle will pour you a second without asking and suddenly you've had 440 calories of juice before your meal even arrives.
  • Plain with ice only. Skipping asam boi saves you 30 calories and quite a bit of sodium from the salt coating on that plum.
  • Pair it with a low-calorie meal, not nasi kandar. Air Tebu as a drink with something like grilled fish or a light soup makes more sense than drinking it alongside a 700-calorie rice plate.
  • Ask for less ice if you want more juice per glass, but don't ask for a bigger glass thinking it's 'just juice'. Bigger glass means more sugar, full stop.
  • Treat it as an occasional drink, not your daily default. If you switch your daily Air Tebu habit to plain water or cincau, you save over 1,500 calories a week without changing anything else you eat.

Common questions

How many calories in Air Tebu?
One standard glass of fresh-pressed Air Tebu with ice is 220 calories. That goes up to 230 if you add lime, and around 250 with asam boi.
Is Air Tebu healthy or just sugar water?
Honest answer: it's mostly sugar. Sugarcane does have trace minerals but the main thing you're drinking is about 57g of carbs in one glass. It's not poison, but if you're watching your calories it adds up fast, especially if you have two glasses without thinking about it.
Can I drink Air Tebu if I'm trying to lose weight?
You can, just count it. At 220 calories a glass it's not terrible compared to a large Teh Tarik or a Sirap Bandung, but it's also not a free drink. Stick to one glass, skip the asam boi, and make sure the rest of your meal is on track.
Berapa kalori dalam Air Tebu?
Air Tebu ada kira-kira 220 kalori untuk 1 gelas (300g), dengan 0g protein, 57g karbohidrat dan 0g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
