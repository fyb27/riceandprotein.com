How Many Calories in Ais Kacang (ABC)?

Malaysia's favourite shaved ice dessert, loaded with red beans, jelly, corn, and enough syrup to make your dentist cry.

350kcal / 1 bowl (hawker standard)
A standard bowl of Ais Kacang (ABC) at your local hawker comes in at around 350 calories for a 350g serving. That's not terrible for a dessert, but it's almost entirely sugar from the syrups and condensed milk. The shaved ice itself is basically nothing, only 5 calories. All the damage comes from those generous pours of rose syrup and gula melaka, plus the condensed milk drizzled on top. Add a scoop of ice cream and you're looking at 430 calories. Add durian? 480. It adds up fast for something that feels so light going down.

The real talk

So 350 calories for a bowl of ABC, is that good or bad? Honestly, for a dessert it's not the worst thing in the world, but you need to understand where those calories come from because the breakdown is wild. The shaved ice, which is like 250g of the bowl, contributes maybe 5 calories. Basically nothing. The entire calorie count sits in the 70g of syrups at 140 calories and the 35g of condensed milk at 100 calories. That's 240 calories of pure sugar before you even count the red beans and toppings. Fair warning though, these numbers are a genuine ballpark estimate because solid nutritional data for Malaysian hawker desserts is thin. The actual count depends entirely on how heavy-handed your uncle with the syrup bottle is.

Here's what really moves the needle: the syrup pour and whether they use condensed or evaporated milk. Ask for kurang manis and you can probably knock it down to around 220 calories, that's not a small saving. Swap condensed for evaporated milk and you cut another chunk of fat and sugar. The toppings like attap chee, corn, and agar jelly together are only about 45 calories for 100g, so those are not your enemy here. Red beans at 55 calories for 60g are actually decent, a bit of fibre and protein in there. Where people really go off the rails is adding ice cream (430 cal total) or durian (480 cal for 2 small pieces). If you're dieting, ABC is fine as an occasional thing, just skip the ice cream scoop and say kurang manis like you mean it.

How to eat it lighter

  • Say kurang manis when you order. That one request can drop it from 350 to around 220 calories.
  • Ask the stall to use evaporated milk instead of condensed milk. Less sugar, less fat, still creamy.
  • Skip the ice cream scoop. That single addition adds 80 to 100 calories for what is basically a garnish.
  • Go easy on the durian add-on. Two small pieces put you at 480 calories total, which is starting to look like a full meal.
  • Share a bowl lah. Half a bowl is 175 calories and you still get the full taste experience.

Common questions

How many calories in Ais Kacang (ABC)?
A standard hawker bowl of Ais Kacang is around 350 calories for a 350g serving. The syrups and condensed milk account for most of it (about 240 calories between them). The shaved ice itself is only 5 calories.
Is ABC a healthy dessert choice when dieting?
Compared to cakes or fried desserts it's on the lighter end, but 350 calories is still 350 calories. The upside is you can control it. Order kurang manis and swap condensed for evaporated milk and you're down to roughly 220 calories, which is pretty reasonable for a dessert.
Does adding durian to ABC make a big difference?
Yes, noticeably. Two small pieces of durian (about 80g) add roughly 120 to 130 calories, pushing the total to around 480 calories. Durian is about 150 calories per 100g so it adds up quickly even in small amounts.
