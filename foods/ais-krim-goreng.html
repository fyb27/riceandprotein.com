How Many Calories in Ais Krim Goreng (Fried Ice Cream)?

Ais krim goreng is around 360 calories a piece. Deep fried ice cream is double trouble, fat plus sugar.

360kcal / 1 piece
One piece of ais krim goreng is about 360 calories. You take a scoop of ice cream, wrap it in batter or breadcrumbs, and deep fry it. So you get the sugar and fat of ice cream plus the oil and carbs of frying. It is rich on rich.

The real talk

This is dessert showing off. Take ice cream, which is already sugar and cream, then coat it and dunk it in hot oil so it picks up another layer of fat and crunch. The frying alone adds serious calories on top of the ice cream inside. That is how a single ball lands at around 360.

Honestly it is delicious and a fun thing to order once in a blue moon. Just do not kid yourself, this is the heaviest dessert in the lineup at 18g of fat a piece. Bro one of these is basically half a burger in dessert form. Share it, or have one and skip the second drink.

How to eat it lighter

  • Share one piece, half is a more reasonable 180 calories
  • Skip the chocolate or caramel drizzle they love to add on top
  • Have it as the whole dessert, do not pair it with another sweet drink

Common questions

Is fried ice cream the worst dessert calorie-wise?
It is up there. At around 360 calories with 18g of fat per piece, it combines the sugar of ice cream with the oil of deep frying. One of the heaviest on the dessert menu.
Why is ais krim goreng so high in fat?
Because you are deep frying cream. The ice cream already has fat, then the batter soaks up oil during frying, stacking it to around 18g per piece.
Can I fit fried ice cream into a diet?
Occasionally, yes, if you budget for it like a small meal. Sharing one piece is the easiest way to enjoy it without the full 360 calorie hit.
Berapa kalori dalam Ais Krim Goreng?
Ais Krim Goreng ada kira-kira 360 kalori untuk 1 keping (150g), dengan 5g protein, 45g karbohidrat dan 18g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
