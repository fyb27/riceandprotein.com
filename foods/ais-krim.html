How Many Calories in Ais Krim (Local Ice Cream)?

Ais krim potong is the OG Malaysian ice cream on a stick, made with coconut milk and sugar, sold in flavours like red bean, corn, coconut, and durian.

80kcal / 1 stick (potong)
One stick of ais krim potong is about 80 calories for a standard 65g bar. That is for your typical corn or coconut flavour. Red bean comes in lower at around 70 calories, while durian can push up to 110 calories per stick because durian puree is genuinely calorie-dense. The base is mostly sugar syrup and santan, so the calories are almost entirely carbs and fat, with barely any protein.

The real talk

Ais krim potong is the frozen treat you grew up eating from a styrofoam box at the school canteen or from a guy on a bicycle. It is a block of flavoured ice cream frozen on a stick, made with coconut milk, sugar syrup, and whatever filling goes in it, red bean, corn, pandan, or durian. At 80 calories a stick, it is actually one of the more reasonable desserts you can have. The calories come mainly from sugar (40 cal from sweeteners) and santan (17 cal). Durian is the only one you need to watch, at 110 cal per stick, because durian puree adds real density.

The math changes almost entirely by flavour choice. Red bean potong sits at 70 cal and the red bean filling actually adds a bit of fibre. Corn is 80 cal. Coconut is 85 cal, slightly richer santan base. Durian is 110 cal. So if you swap your durian stick for a red bean one you save 40 calories, which is real. Portion size also matters because some artisan or pasar malam versions are bigger than the standard 65g bar. A 90g homemade-style potong could easily be 120 cal. Stick to the individually wrapped commercial ones if you want predictable numbers.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick red bean flavour over durian. That swap alone cuts about 40 calories per stick.
  • Eat one stick, not two. Sounds obvious but pasar malam sellers sometimes push you to buy three for some deal.
  • Skip the big slab versions sold at dessert shops. Those are usually 150 cal or more per serving.
  • Buy the individually wrapped commercial bars like King's Potong where the calorie count is printed on the label.
  • Eat it as a standalone treat, not after a full meal. At 80 cal it is a fine dessert on its own.

Common questions

How many calories in Ais Krim (Local Ice Cream)?
One standard stick of ais krim potong is about 80 calories (65g). Red bean flavour is around 70 cal, corn is 80 cal, coconut is 85 cal, and durian is the highest at around 110 cal per stick.
What exactly is ais krim potong?
It is a traditional Malaysian ice cream frozen in a rectangular block on a wooden stick. The base is coconut milk (santan) and sugar, with fillings like red bean, sweet corn, pandan, or durian. You will find it at school canteens, pasar malam, and convenience stores.
Is ais krim potong healthy compared to other desserts?
For a Malaysian dessert, it is actually on the lighter end. At 70 to 110 calories per stick it beats a bowl of cendol (around 330 cal) or ais kacang (around 350 cal) by a wide margin. The downside is it has almost no protein, so it is a treat, not a meal.
Berapa kalori dalam Ais Krim?
Ais Krim ada kira-kira 80 kalori untuk 1 cucuk (65g), dengan 1g protein, 14g karbohidrat dan 3g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
