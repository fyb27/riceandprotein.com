How Many Calories in Akok (Kelantan Kuih)?

2 pieces of akok are about 180 kcal. Baked egg and coconut kuih, sweet and rich.

180kcal / 2 pieces
Two pieces of akok come in around 180 calories, so roughly 90 each. It is a Kelantan kuih baked from eggs, coconut milk, and palm sugar, so it is soft, creamy, and sweet. It is baked not fried, but the coconut milk and sugar still make it richer than it looks for such a small piece.

The real talk

Akok is small and innocent looking, like a little flower shaped cake. But it is egg, coconut milk, and palm sugar baked together, so each piece is about 90 calories of rich sweet custard. Two pieces with your kopi is 180.

It is not the worst thing, it is baked so there is no frying oil, and the egg gives it a touch of protein. But the palm sugar and coconut milk mean it is denser than the size suggests. Enjoy two, do not sit there finishing the whole tray lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Stick to 2 pieces, the small size makes it easy to lose count fast
  • Pair with black coffee or plain tea, no extra sugar drink on the side
  • Treat it as your one sweet thing for the day, not an all-afternoon graze

Common questions

How many calories in akok?
About 90 calories per piece, so 180 for two. Bigger or sweeter versions can run higher.
Is akok fried?
No, akok is baked, traditionally in special moulds over a fire. That means no soaked frying oil, the calories come from coconut milk and palm sugar.
Is akok ok for a diet?
In small amounts yes. It is baked and the egg gives a little protein, but the coconut milk and palm sugar make it rich, so keep it to 2 pieces.
Berapa kalori dalam Akok?
Akok ada kira-kira 180 kalori untuk 2 keping (60g), dengan 4g protein, 24g karbohidrat dan 8g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
