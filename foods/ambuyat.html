How Many Calories in Ambuyat?

Brunei and Sabah's iconic sago starch staple, twirled on bamboo forks and dipped in sour cacah sauce, simple in calories but serious in cultural weight.

245kcal / 1 serving ambuyat with basic cacah dip (per person)
A standard serving of ambuyat with basic cacah dip is roughly 245 calories for about 430g of food. That sounds like a lot of food for not many calories, and it is, because ambuyat is almost entirely water-swollen sago starch. The catch is that almost all of those 245 calories come from 54g of carbs, with barely 3g protein and 1g fat. On its own it keeps your stomach busy but does nothing for muscle. The full restaurant set with ikan goreng and stir-fried greens jumps to around 470 calories, which is a much more complete meal.

The real talk

Ambuyat is the national dish of Brunei and a proper staple in Sabah and Sarawak too. If you have never had it, imagine sago starch cooked into a thick, sticky, translucent gel, kind of like very dense wallpaper paste but weirdly satisfying. You twirl a big blob of it around a bamboo fork called chandas, dip it into a sour, spicy cacah sauce made from dried shrimp, belacan, chili and lime, and swallow the whole thing without chewing. Seriously, you do not chew ambuyat. At 245 calories a serving it sounds diet-friendly, but be honest with yourself: the plain ambuyat itself is just 210 calories of pure sago carbs with 3g protein. It will fill you up for maybe an hour before your body figures out there is nothing substantial in there. These numbers are a ballpark estimate by the way, hard nutritional data for ambuyat is basically non-existent, so treat the figures as a reasonable guide rather than gospel.

What actually changes the maths is what you eat alongside it. The plain ambuyat set at 245 calories is fine as a base, but nobody eats ambuyat alone for a full meal. Add one piece of ikan goreng, around 80g of fried fish, and that is roughly 180 calories extra. Add a plate of sayur tumis and you are looking at another 60 calories. The full set lands around 470 calories total, which is actually a solid, filling meal at a reasonable calorie count. The tempoyak cacah version adds about 40 calories from fermented durian fat and carbs and pushes you to 280 calories for the ambuyat portion alone. The real trap is not the ambuyat itself, it is ordering two servings because the first one felt light.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order the full set with ikan goreng and sayur tumis instead of eating plain ambuyat alone. You get more protein and fibre, and the total meal at 470 calories is more satisfying than two rounds of plain ambuyat.
  • Stick to the basic cacah dip rather than tempoyak cacah. The tempoyak version adds around 40 extra calories from durian fat, and honestly the basic cacah hits different anyway.
  • Ask for extra cacah on the side and use it to slow yourself down. The tangy, spicy dip makes you more mindful of each bite and stops you from inhaling two portions back to back.
  • If you are at a restaurant, pair your ambuyat with a steamed or grilled fish rather than fried. You get the same protein with less added oil.
  • One portion of ambuyat is genuinely a lot of volume for 245 calories. Stick to one and let it settle before deciding you want more.

Common questions

How many calories in Ambuyat?
A standard serving of ambuyat with basic cacah dip is around 245 calories per person (approximately 430g total). Plain ambuyat on its own is 210 calories, and the full restaurant set with ikan goreng and stir-fried greens comes to about 470 calories.
What exactly is ambuyat and how do you eat it?
Ambuyat is a thick, sticky, gel-like paste made from sago starch, essentially the staple carb of Brunei and parts of Sabah and Sarawak. You twirl it around a bamboo fork called chandas, dip it into a sour spicy sauce called cacah (made from dried shrimp, belacan, chili and lime), and swallow without chewing. It has a neutral flavour on its own so the cacah does most of the heavy lifting.
Is ambuyat healthy or good for weight loss?
It is low in fat and low in calories for the volume you eat, which is a plus. The problem is it is almost pure carbohydrate with barely any protein (3g per serving), so it will not keep you full for long on its own. For weight loss, order the full set with fish and vegetables so you actually get some protein in the meal. Treat the ambuyat as your carb portion rather than the whole meal.
Berapa kalori dalam Ambuyat?
Ambuyat ada kira-kira 245 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (430g), dengan 3g protein, 54g karbohidrat dan 1g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
