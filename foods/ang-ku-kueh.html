How Many Calories in Ang Ku Kueh?

Also known as Ang Koo Kueh, Red Tortoise Cake, 红龟粿, Kuih Angku.

Ang ku kueh is the red, tortoise shaped glutinous rice kueh with a sweet mung bean or peanut filling, pressed in a wooden mould and steamed on a piece of banana leaf. A Hokkien celebration kueh sold daily at Singapore hawker stalls.

180kcal / 1 piece
One standard ang ku kueh is about 180 calories for a 60g piece, with around 4g of fat. A green bean filling piece is about 141 calories and a peanut filling piece about 240. The glutinous rice skin is roughly 90 calories on its own.

The real talk

It is dense because it is meant to be. The skin is glutinous rice flour with sweet potato and oil, kneaded until it is chewy, about 90 calories for a 35g skin. The filling is the swing. Sweet mung bean paste at 25g is about 80 calories, which gives the 180 figure for a standard piece. The all green bean version with a thinner skin is about 141. The peanut version, with ground peanuts and sugar, is about 240 because peanuts are 60 percent fat.

One is a snack. Three is a meal. A box of 3 mixed ang ku kueh is about 550 calories, which is more than a plate of chicken rice, and the box gets finished in the car. The stalls sell them by the piece for about S$1.20, so buy the one you want and walk away.

The mini ones, about half the size, are around 90 calories each. The jumbo ones sold for prayers and birthdays are close to 300. Same kueh, three sizes, a big range. Look at the size before you decide it is a small snack.

How to eat it lighter

  • Choose the green bean filling over peanut. That is about 100 calories less per piece.
  • Buy one, not a box. A box of 3 is about 550 calories.
  • Look for the mini size. Half the weight, about 90 calories.
  • Eat it with a kopi o, not a teh c. The kueh already has enough sugar, about 15g per piece.
  • Skip the extra oil. Some stalls brush the kueh with oil after steaming, and you can wipe it off with the banana leaf.

Common questions

How many calories in ang ku kueh?
About 180 calories for a standard piece. The green bean version is around 141 and the peanut version about 240, because ground peanuts carry more fat than mung bean paste.
Is ang ku kueh healthy?
It is a sweet snack, about 180 calories with 15g of sugar and 3g of protein. One piece is fine. It is easy to eat three, which puts you at 550 calories from a snack.
Why is ang ku kueh red?
The skin is coloured red for luck, traditionally with food colouring, and shaped like a tortoise for long life. The colour does not change the calories, which come from the glutinous rice skin and the sweet filling.
Is peanut or green bean ang ku kueh better?
For calories, green bean at about 141 against 240 for peanut. The peanut version has slightly more protein at about 5g, but the extra 100 calories are mostly fat and sugar.
Berapa kalori dalam Ang Ku Kueh?
Ang Ku Kueh ada kira-kira 180 kalori untuk 1 keping (60g), dengan 3g protein, 33g karbohidrat dan 4g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
