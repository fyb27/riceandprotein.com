How Many Calories in Appam?

Also known as Apom, Appam Manis, Hoppers, Palappam, Appam with Gula Melaka.

Appam is the bowl shaped fermented rice and coconut pancake with a soft, spongy centre and crisp lacy edges, cooked in a small curved pan and sold at Indian stalls and pasar malams in Singapore with sugar, grated coconut or gula melaka.

138kcal / 1 piece
One appam is about 138 calories for a 50g piece, with around 2g of fat. The rice flour batter is about 105 calories, the coconut milk in the batter about 15, and the sugar around 18. Two appam with grated coconut and gula melaka syrup is about 380.

The real talk

The pancake on its own is thin and mostly air at the edges. Rice flour, a little coconut milk and a spoon of sugar in a fermented batter, cooked without oil in a pan. That is 138 for one. Compare that to a thosai at 170 or a piece of roti prata at 300 with curry, and appam is the light option at the Indian stall.

The toppings are a different story. The pasar malam version comes with a spoon of grated coconut at about 55, and a pour of gula melaka syrup at about 60 per spoon. Two appam with coconut and syrup is about 380, and two with sugar and a slab of margarine, which some stalls do, is close to 400. The version with a whole egg cracked in the middle is about 210 per piece but it has 9g of protein, which is a better trade.

The size varies. A small pasar malam appam is about 40g and 110 calories. A large restaurant appam served with curry or sothi is closer to 80g and 220. Restaurant appam with a bowl of chicken curry is about 450 for two pieces, which is a full meal.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it plain or with a light sprinkle of sugar. That keeps it at about 140 a piece.
  • Skip the gula melaka syrup or ask for one spoon. Each spoon is about 60 calories.
  • Get the egg appam. It adds about 70 calories and 6g of protein, which is a fair trade.
  • Two is plenty. Three with toppings is nearly 600.
  • Have it with kopi o or teh o. The appam already has sugar in the batter.

Common questions

How many calories in appam?
About 138 calories for a standard 50g piece. Two plain appam are 276, and two with grated coconut and gula melaka syrup are about 380.
Is appam healthy?
The pancake is light, with about 2g of fat and no frying oil. It is low in protein at 3g. The egg version is the better choice if you want it to hold you until lunch.
Is appam lower in calories than thosai?
Yes. A plain thosai is about 170 calories and a plain appam about 138. Both are fermented rice batters, but appam is smaller and cooked without oil in a curved pan.
What is the difference between appam and apam balik?
Different dishes. Apam balik is the thick Malaysian peanut pancake at about 290 per wedge. Appam is the thin Indian rice flour pancake at 138 per piece.
Berapa kalori dalam Appam?
Appam ada kira-kira 138 kalori untuk 1 keping (50g), dengan 3g protein, 26g karbohidrat dan 2g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
