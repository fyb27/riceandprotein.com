How Many Calories in Arroz Caldo?

Also known as Arroz Caldo with Chicken, Chicken Rice Porridge, Lugaw na may Manok.

Rice porridge cooked with ginger, garlic, chicken pieces and safflower, topped with toasted garlic, spring onion, a boiled egg and calamansi. What you eat when it rains or when you are sick.

400kcal / 1 bowl
One carinderia bowl of arroz caldo is about 400 calories for a 350g serving with a piece of chicken and half a boiled egg. The porridge itself is around 200 calories, the chicken 120, the egg 35 and the toasted garlic in oil about 45. A plain lugaw bowl without chicken is closer to 220.

The real talk

Porridge stretches a little rice a long way. A 350g bowl of arroz caldo has only about 60g of dry rice in it, cooked in 5 times its weight of broth, so the rice contributes about 200 calories. That is less than a cup of plain rice. The chicken piece, usually a thigh or a chunk of breast with skin, adds 100 to 150. Kawaling Pinoy's recipe card lands at 572 per serving because it splits a whole chicken 6 ways, which is a much bigger piece than a carinderia gives you.

The toppings are where the oil hides. Toasted garlic is fried in oil and spooned on with some of that oil, about 45 calories. Some places also drizzle chicken fat or use the annatto oil from inasal, which adds 60 more. A whole boiled egg instead of half is another 35. A fully loaded bowl with a big thigh, whole egg and extra oil is about 550 calories, still a reasonable meal.

The calamansi and patis are free, so use them. A bowl costs 50 to 70 pesos at a carinderia and 90 to 130 at a Goto King or a mall stall, and the mall versions come with more chicken and often a side of tokwa't baboy, which is another 250. As comfort food goes, arroz caldo is one of the better deals: warm, filling, about 20g of protein and under 400 calories if you keep it simple.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for breast instead of thigh and pull the skin off. About 60 calories less per bowl.
  • Go light on the toasted garlic oil, or ask for the garlic without the oil. Saves about 30 to 40.
  • Skip the chicharon topping that some stalls add. A small handful is 100 calories of fried pork skin.
  • Add more ginger, calamansi and patis for flavour. All of them are close to zero.

Common questions

Is arroz caldo good for weight loss?
Yes, it is one of the better carinderia bowls. About 400 calories with 20g of protein and a lot of water, so it fills you up. Keep the garlic oil and chicharon topping in check.
What is the difference between arroz caldo, lugaw and goto?
Same porridge base. Lugaw is plain (about 220 calories), arroz caldo has chicken (about 400), goto has beef tripe (about 380).
How many calories in a bowl of arroz caldo without egg?
About 365 calories. The half egg is only 35, so it is not the thing to skip. The garlic oil and chicken skin are bigger.
Is arroz caldo high in carbs?
About 45g per bowl, which is less than a single cup of rice (55g) because the rice is diluted with broth.
