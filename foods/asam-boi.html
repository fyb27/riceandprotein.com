How Many Calories in Asam Boi Drink?

Sour plum drink, usually lime plus asam boi and sugar, around 110 calories.

110kcal / 1 glass
An asam boi drink is roughly 110 calories a glass. The sour plum and lime trick your tongue into thinking it is not sweet, but stalls add plenty of sugar to balance the sour, plus the preserved plums themselves carry sugar and salt. So the tangy taste hides a fair bit of sugar.

The real talk

Asam boi is the sour plum drink, usually lime and water with those preserved plums dropped in. The sour really fools you, it does not taste sweet so people assume it is light. But the stall adds sugar to balance the sour bite, and the plums are preserved in sugar and salt to begin with.

So you sip something tangy and refreshing and feel virtuous, meanwhile it is around 110 calories of mostly sugar. Not a disaster, but not the diet hero you think it is. Ask for kurang manis and the asam boi flavour still comes through strong.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order kurang manis, the sour plum still gives plenty of flavour
  • Ask for fewer plums and less syrup
  • Add more plain water or ice to dilute the sugar hit

Common questions

How many calories in an asam boi drink?
Around 110 calories a glass. The sour taste hides a decent amount of sugar.
Is asam boi drink healthy?
It is light-ish but not sugar-free. The plums and added syrup carry sugar despite the sour flavour.
Why does asam boi taste sour but still have sugar?
Stalls add sugar to balance the sour plum, so the tangy taste masks the sweetness.
