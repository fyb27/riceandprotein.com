How Many Calories in Ayam Bakar?

Grilled marinated chicken, a warung staple, and one of the better calorie-to-protein deals in Malaysian lauk if you pick the right version.

290kcal / 1 piece (thigh or drumstick quarter)
One piece of ayam bakar (a thigh or drumstick quarter, about 170g) comes in at 290 calories, with 28g protein, 6g carbs, and 16g fat. That number covers the grilled chicken with skin plus the marinade. It is honestly one of the better lauk options at the warung if you are watching your intake, because you are getting solid protein without the calorie count going completely out of hand.

The real talk

Ayam bakar is genuinely one of the more diet-friendly things you can pick off a nasi campur counter, which is not something you can say about most Malaysian lauk. At 290 calories for a whole piece of thigh or drumstick, you are getting 28g of protein for that spend. The calories mostly come from the chicken itself with skin (245 cal) and the marinade adds about 45 calories on top. Where things go sideways is the madu version. That honey glaze is not a small difference, it pushes a piece up to 330 calories and bumps your carbs noticeably. Berempah sits in the middle at 300 calories because the rempah paste adds a bit of oil. So the version matters, but none of them are disaster territory lah.

What actually changes the maths is three things: version, skin, and what you eat with it. Biasa saves you 10 to 50 calories versus berempah or madu. Removing the skin before eating knocks off roughly 40 to 60 calories. And the rice underneath is the real wildcard, 200g of white rice is already 260 calories before you even touch the chicken. If you are eating ayam bakar at a warung and wondering why you are not losing weight, the honest answer is probably not the ayam. It is the rice, the sambal, and the kuah that come with it. Keep the chicken, manage the sides.

How to eat it lighter

  • Skip the madu version. Ayam bakar madu clocks in at 330 calories versus 280 for the plain biasa, purely because of the honey glaze sugar.
  • Remove the skin before eating. Most of the protein is in the meat, and you still get plenty of the marinade flavour from the flesh itself, so you are not eating cardboard. Dropping the skin saves you 40 to 60 calories per piece.
  • Watch your nasi. The ayam itself is fine. It is the mountain of rice underneath that wrecks the meal. Keep rice to 150 to 180g cooked.
  • Pair it with ulam or kangkung belacan instead of a heavy gravy side. Gravy is where hidden calories creep in.
  • Ask for the chicken without the extra sambal drizzled on top at the warung counter. That sambal can add another 30 to 60 calories depending on how heavy-handed the aunty is.

Common questions

How many calories in Ayam Bakar?
One standard piece (thigh or drumstick quarter, about 170g) has 290 calories. That includes the grilled chicken with skin and the marinade. Ayam bakar madu (honey glaze) pushes it up to around 330 calories per piece because of the added sugar.
Is ayam bakar good for weight loss?
It can be, yes. At 290 calories with 28g of protein per piece, it is one of the leaner lauk options at a nasi campur or warung counter. The main thing to watch is the marinade sugar, especially the madu version. Stick to biasa or berempah and pair it with plain rice or ulam instead of nasi lemak and you are in decent shape.
Does removing the skin reduce calories significantly?
Yes, a fair bit. The skin on a thigh or drumstick adds roughly 40 to 60 calories depending on size. If you are being careful, removing the skin before eating drops you closer to 230 to 250 calories per piece and cuts the fat noticeably.
Berapa kalori dalam Ayam Bakar?
Ayam Bakar ada kira-kira 290 kalori untuk 1 keping (170g), dengan 28g protein, 6g karbohidrat dan 16g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
