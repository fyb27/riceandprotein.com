How Many Calories in Ayam Masak?

Everyday stir-fried and braised chicken dishes. The chicken skin, frying oil and kicap sugar set the calories.

280kcal / 1 piece in sauce
A piece of home-style ayam masak (the chicken plus its sauce, no rice) is around 280 calories. The chicken skin and absorbed oil are the base, and the soy braised kicap version is the richest because of added sugar.

The real talk

Ayam masak covers all the everyday chicken dishes your mum cooks, kunyit, halia, serai, kicap. They land around 250 to 300 calories for a piece, which is honestly reasonable, because the chicken is mostly protein. The calories that creep in are the skin, the oil it is fried in, and the sugar in the kicap manis braise.

Of the lot, ayam masak halia (ginger) tends to be the lightest and ayam masak kicap the heaviest, because kicap manis is loaded with sugar. None of them are bad choices though. Pair any of them with rice and vegetables and you have a balanced plate, which is more than most hawker food gives you.

How to eat it lighter

  • Take the skin off if you are watching it. That is where a chunk of the fat sits.
  • Ginger (halia) and lemongrass (serai) versions are lighter than the sweet kicap braise.
  • It is a solid protein lauk. The rice portion matters more than the chicken here.

Common questions

How many calories in ayam masak?
Around 280 calories for a piece of chicken in its sauce (no rice). The skin and frying oil are the base, with the kicap version a bit higher from added sugar.
Which ayam masak is lowest in calories?
Ayam masak halia (ginger) at around 250, because it uses less sugar. Ayam masak kicap is the highest at around 300 from the sweet soy braise.
Is ayam masak good for a diet?
Yes, it is a solid protein lauk at around 250 to 300 calories a piece. Take the skin off and pair it with vegetables and a controlled rice portion.
Berapa kalori dalam Ayam Masak?
Ayam Masak ada kira-kira 280 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (140g), dengan 23g protein, 6g karbohidrat dan 18g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
