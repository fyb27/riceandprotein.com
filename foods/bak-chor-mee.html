How Many Calories in Bak Chor Mee?

Also known as Minced Meat Noodles, Mee Pok Dry, 肉脞面, Bak Chor Mee Pok.

Bak chor mee is the Singapore hawker bowl of mee pok or mee kia tossed in vinegar, chilli, soy and pork lard, topped with minced pork, sliced pork, liver, mushrooms and a couple of meatballs, with a small soup on the side.

511kcal / 1 bowl (dry)
A standard dry bowl of bak chor mee is about 511 calories for a 311g serving, with roughly 23g of fat. The noodles alone are around 230 of those calories, and the lard and chilli dressing is another 90 to 100 before any meat goes in.

The real talk

The number that surprises people is not the pork, it is the dressing. A dry bowl gets a spoon of lard, a spoon of chilli oil, and the crispy lard bits on top. That is about 20g of fat right there, close to 90 calories, and the stall does it before you can say anything. The 150g of mee pok is another 230 calories. Meat, liver, mushrooms and two meatballs together come to only about 170. So a 511 calorie bowl is really 60 percent noodles and oil.

The soup version drops to around 420 calories because there is no lard dressing and the noodles sit in clear broth instead of oil. Same pork, same liver, same mushrooms. If you order dry, you are paying about 90 calories for the vinegar and lard taste. Some people think that is worth it. Just know the trade.

Price matters here too. The S$4 bowl at a normal hawker centre is the 511 calorie one. The S$6 or S$8 bowl at the famous stalls comes with extra meat, an extra ladle of lard and sometimes abalone, and it lands closer to 650 to 700 calories. Bigger bowl, more noodles, more oil. The fancier the stall, the heavier the bowl.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order soup instead of dry and you save close to 90 calories from the lard and chilli oil.
  • Ask for less lard or no lard bits. Most stalls will do it and the bowl drops by 40 to 60 calories.
  • Get mee kia instead of mee pok. It is thinner and the portion by weight is usually a bit smaller, around 30 calories less.
  • Eat the pork, liver and meatballs first. About 20g of protein for under 200 calories is the good part of the bowl.
  • Skip the extra meatball order. Each pork ball is about 35 calories.

Common questions

Is bak chor mee healthy?
It is a decent protein bowl if you go soup. Dry bak chor mee at 511 calories with 23g of fat is heavier than it looks because of the lard dressing. Soup at around 420 with 20g of protein is one of the better hawker lunches.
How many calories in bak chor mee soup?
About 420 calories for a standard bowl. The noodles are still 230 of that, but there is no lard or chilli oil dressing, which is where the dry version picks up its extra 90 calories.
Is mee pok or mee kia lower in calories?
Mee kia by a small margin. A mee kia portion is usually 20 to 30g lighter, which is about 30 calories less. The dressing and toppings are identical, so the difference is minor.
Can I eat bak chor mee while losing weight?
Yes. A 511 calorie bowl fits into most 1,800 to 2,000 calorie days. Order it soup or with less lard, do not add extra meatballs, and it is a normal lunch.
Berapa kalori dalam Bak Chor Mee?
Bak Chor Mee ada kira-kira 511 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (311g), dengan 22g protein, 55g karbohidrat dan 23g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
