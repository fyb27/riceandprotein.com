How Many Calories in Bak Kwa?

Also known as Bakkwa, Chinese BBQ Pork Jerky, 肉干, Rougan, Bee Cheng Hiang Bak Kwa, Long Yok.

Bak kwa is the sweet, smoky grilled pork slice sold by weight from Singapore chains like Bee Cheng Hiang, Lim Chee Guan and Fragrance, eaten by the square during Chinese New Year and in bak kwa buns the rest of the year.

179kcal / 1 slice (54g)
One standard square of bak kwa is about 179 calories for a 54g slice, with around 13g of protein, 17g of sugar and 7g of fat. That works out to about 330 calories per 100g. A 500g box is around 1,650 calories, and a 1kg box is about 3,300.

The real talk

Bak kwa is minced pork pressed thin, marinated in sugar, soy, fish sauce and honey, dried and then grilled over charcoal. The drying concentrates everything. A slice of 54g is about 45g of pork and 10g of glaze, and the glaze is mostly sugar. That gives 13g of protein, which is decent, and 17g of sugar, which is a can of soft drink's worth in three slices. At 179 a slice it is fine as a snack and dangerous as a box.

The Chinese New Year queue is where the real damage happens. A 500g box is about 1,650 calories and it does not survive the first evening in most households. Ten slices over an afternoon is 1,790, which is a full day of food for most people trying to lose weight. The slices are thin and sweet and stop feeling like meat after the second one.

The lean cuts change the number a little. Chilli bak kwa is about the same. The minced pork slices are the standard at 179. The sliced whole meat version is slightly leaner, about 160. The bacon bak kwa, made from pork belly, is around 230 a slice because of the fat. The gold coin mini slices are about 60 each, which makes them easier to count and easier to lose count of.

How to eat it lighter

  • Buy 100g, not 500g. The shop will sell you two slices and that is 360 calories, a clear stopping point.
  • Choose the sliced whole meat version over minced. It is a little leaner, about 160 a slice.
  • Skip the bacon bak kwa. Pork belly takes it to about 230 a slice.
  • Cut a slice into strips and eat it with rice or in a sandwich. One slice as part of a meal makes sense, five slices as a snack does not.
  • Do not keep the box on the coffee table. This is the only tip that matters during Chinese New Year.

Common questions

How many calories in one slice of bak kwa?
About 179 calories for a standard 54g square, based on the Bee Cheng Hiang label. That is about 330 calories per 100g, with 13g of protein and 17g of sugar per slice.
Is bak kwa healthy?
It is high in protein and high in sugar at the same time. One slice has 13g of protein, which is good, and 17g of sugar, which is not. One or two slices is fine. A box during Chinese New Year is 1,650 to 3,300 calories.
How many calories in 1kg of bak kwa?
About 3,300 calories. A 1kg box is roughly 18 slices at 179 each. Spread over a family it is nothing, eaten by two people over a weekend it is a lot.
Is bak kwa keto or high protein?
It is high protein but not keto. Each slice has about 17g of carbs, nearly all sugar from the glaze. Beef jerky without sugar is the closer fit for that.
Berapa kalori dalam Bak Kwa?
Bak Kwa ada kira-kira 179 kalori untuk 1 keping (54g), dengan 13g protein, 17g karbohidrat dan 7g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
