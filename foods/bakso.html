How Many Calories in Bakso?

Also known as Bakso Sapi, Mie Bakso, Bakso Kuah, Bakso Urat, Indonesian Meatball Soup.

Bakso is Indonesia's beef meatball soup from the pushcart: springy meatballs in clear beef broth with yellow noodles, bihun, a piece of tofu, fried shallots and a lot of kecap and sambal.

480kcal / 1 bowl (5 meatballs, noodles, tofu, broth)
A standard cart bowl of bakso is about 480 calories. FatSecret Indonesia lists a medium meatball at 57 calories and five of them at around 283; the noodles, bihun, tofu and broth add roughly 200. A bakso urat or bakso jumbo bowl runs 600 or more.

The real talk

One medium bakso is about 57 calories, and 60% of that is fat, not beef. Cart bakso is beef mixed with tapioca flour and fat, which is why it is bouncy and cheap. Five meatballs is about 285 calories and 17g protein. A bowl of 'bakso 10' at Rp 15,000 is easy to find and it doubles the count.

The rest of the bowl is smaller than people assume. Mie kuning 50g is 70, bihun 30g is 35, one tahu is 40 and the clear broth is 40 if it is not too oily. So a standard bowl is 480 and the meatballs are 60% of it. This is why bakso without noodles (bakso kuah only) at 340 is a decent protein snack.

The extras change everything. Bakso urat (tendon) is bigger, about 90 calories each. Bakso telur has a whole egg inside, about 130. Bakso jumbo or bakso beranak is a 150g ball with smaller ones inside, 300 calories on its own. A bakso Malang bowl with pangsit goreng, siomay and tahu goreng is around 620.

Then the sauce. People pour in kecap manis (25 calories per tablespoon) and sambal (often chili in oil, 40 per tablespoon) until the soup goes brown. Two spoons each and that is 130 calories added to what was a clear soup.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order bakso kuah without mie. Five meatballs, tofu and broth is about 340 calories with 22g protein.
  • Skip the kecap manis or use one spoon. Each tablespoon is 25 calories of sugar.
  • Use rawit or vinegar chili instead of sambal in oil. The oil-based sambal is 40 calories a spoon.
  • Bakso halus (plain) over bakso urat or bakso telur. The plain ones are the smallest and leanest.
  • Ask for extra sawi or tauge. Adds crunch, adds nothing to the calories.

Common questions

How many calories in one bakso?
About 57 calories for a medium cart-sized beef meatball, according to FatSecret Indonesia. Bakso urat is around 90 and bakso telur about 130.
How many calories in a bowl of bakso?
About 480 calories for a standard bowl with five meatballs, noodles, tofu and broth. Without noodles it is about 340.
Is bakso good for a diet?
Decent if you skip the noodles and the sweet soy. Five meatballs in broth is 340 calories with 22g protein. The problem is the sauces and the extras, not the meatballs.
How many calories in bakso Malang?
Around 620 calories for a full bowl with siomay, fried wonton, tahu goreng and meatballs. The fried items add about 140 over a normal bowl.
Berapa kalori Bakso?
Bakso mengandung sekitar 480 kalori per 1 mangkuk (480g), dengan 24g protein, 40g karbohidrat dan 24g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
