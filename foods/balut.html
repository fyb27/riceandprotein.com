How Many Calories in Balut?

Also known as Balot, Fertilised Duck Egg, Balut Penoy.

A fertilised duck egg incubated for 14 to 18 days, boiled and eaten warm from the shell with rock salt or spiced vinegar. Sold by the balut vendor shouting past your gate at night for 20 to 25 pesos.

190kcal / 1 egg
One balut is about 190 calories with about 14g of protein and 14g of fat, per the figures cited for both chicken and duck balut. That is roughly 2.5 chicken eggs' worth of nutrition in one shell. The salt and vinegar add nothing, and the broth you sip first is about 5 calories.

The real talk

Balut is an egg, so it counts like an egg, just a big one. A duck egg is about 70g of edible content versus 50g for a chicken egg, and duck yolks are richer. The figure that circulates from Philippine sources is 188 calories and 14g of crude protein per balut, and that lines up with USDA duck egg data at about 185 calories per 70g. The embryo adds some protein and calcium (about 100mg) but does not change the calories much.

As a snack it is one of the best protein deals on the street. 190 calories with 14g of protein for 20 to 25 pesos beats almost everything else the same vendor sells. Chicharon, which balut vendors often carry as well, is 150 calories for a small bag with 8g of protein and a lot more salt. The yolk carries the fat, about 14g, half of it saturated, and about 600mg of cholesterol, which is the thing to be aware of if you eat 2 or 3 a night.

Penoy is the unfertilised version and is basically a boiled duck egg at about 180 calories. Balut sa puti, the younger 14 to 16 day egg with a white membrane and less embryo, is about the same. The only way balut gets expensive is the habit of eating it with 2 sticks of pork BBQ and a beer, which turns a 190 calorie snack into 600.

How to eat it lighter

  • One balut is a good 190 calorie snack. Two is still fine at 380. Three a night is where the cholesterol gets silly.
  • Use the spiced vinegar instead of salt if you are watching sodium. Both are calorie free.
  • Eat it instead of a late dinner, not on top of one. It is filling for its size.
  • If you want the protein without the yolk fat, eat the white and the embryo and leave half the yolk. About 120 calories that way.

Common questions

Is balut high in protein?
Yes, about 14g per egg, which is more than 2 chicken eggs. It is one of the highest protein snacks per peso on the street.
Is balut high in cholesterol?
Yes, about 600mg per egg, mostly in the yolk. That is 3 chicken eggs' worth. One a day is fine for most people; several a night is not.
How many calories in penoy?
About 180, close to balut. Penoy is an unfertilised duck egg, so it is a plain hard boiled duck egg with no embryo.
Is balut good for weight loss?
As a snack, yes. 190 calories, 14g of protein, very filling, and it takes a few minutes to eat. Just do not add the pork BBQ and the beer every time.
