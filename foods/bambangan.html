How Many Calories in Bambangan?

Bambangan is a wild Sabah mango eaten pickled, in coconut gulai, or raw in salads like hinava, and the pickled version is one of the lightest condiments on any Sabah spread.

35kcal / 1 condiment serving, jeruk bambangan (pickled)
Jeruk bambangan comes in at just 35 calories for an 85g condiment serving. That's the pickled version you'd get on the side with your rice, made from sour unripe bambangan flesh with grated seed paste and a bit of salt and chili. Basically zero fat, 8g of carbs, basically nothing protein-wise. It's one of the lowest-calorie condiments you'll find on a Sabah table, so go ahead and pile it on.

The real talk

If you're not from Sabah, you've probably never heard of bambangan, and that's a tragedy. It's a wild mango species (Mangifera pajang) that grows in Borneo, and it does not taste like the sweet Harum Manis you buy at the pasar malam. This thing is aggressively sour and fibrous, and when they pickle it as jeruk, they grate the seed kernel into the mix too, which gives it this funky, slightly bitter edge. At 35 calories for an 85g serving, the pickled version is essentially a free food. The carbs are all coming from the fruit flesh itself, there's no added sugar in the traditional preparation, and the fat is literally zero. It's mostly just fruit, salt, and chili. If you're eating this as a condiment alongside plain rice and some grilled fish, you're doing great.

What changes the maths is the cooking method. The jeruk version at 35 calories is not the same as bambangan gulai. Once you cook bambangan in coconut milk curry, a 150g serving jumps to around 140 calories because coconut milk is adding 80 to 100 calories of saturated fat on its own. The bambangan flesh isn't the problem there, the santan is. The hinava version sits in the middle at around 60 calories because the raw bambangan is mixed into a lime-cured seafood salad with no oil, just the fruit contribution of about 30 to 40 calories to the dish. So if you want bambangan at its lightest, eat it jeruk style as a condiment, not as the star of a coconut gravy.

How to eat it lighter

  • Stick to the jeruk version as a condiment, not as a main dish, and you're keeping it at 35 calories per serving.
  • Skip the bambangan gulai if you're watching calories. The coconut milk alone adds 80 to 100 calories that have nothing to do with the fruit.
  • If you're having bambangan in hinava, keep the portion small since the salad is low-cal but the total dish calories depend on what fish goes in.
  • Don't add sugar to your jeruk. Traditional versions have none. If you're buying pre-made pickled bambangan, check the label because some commercial ones add sugar.
  • Use bambangan jeruk as a replacement for heavier condiments like sambal with oil or peanut sauce. Same flavour punch, fraction of the calories.

Common questions

How many calories in Bambangan?
A standard condiment serving of jeruk bambangan (85g, pickled) has 35 calories, with 8g carbs, 1g protein and essentially no fat. If it's cooked in coconut milk as gulai, a 150g serving jumps to around 140 calories because of the santan.
What exactly is bambangan?
Bambangan is a wild mango species (Mangifera pajang) native to Borneo, particularly Sabah. It's not sweet like regular mango. It's sour and fibrous, and the seed kernel is also edible and gets grated into the pickle. You'll find it at Kadazan-Dusun tables and in Sabah markets, usually as jeruk (pickled condiment), in coconut gulai, or raw in hinava salad.
Is bambangan healthy?
The pickled version is genuinely low-calorie at 35 calories per serving, with no fat and no added sugar in traditional preparation. The fibre from the fruit flesh is a bonus. The gulai version is higher in calories due to coconut milk, not the fruit itself. As a condiment to replace heavier sauces, jeruk bambangan is a solid choice if you're watching your intake.
Berapa kalori dalam Bambangan?
Bambangan ada kira-kira 35 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (85g), dengan 1g protein, 8g karbohidrat dan 0g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
