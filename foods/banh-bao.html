How Many Calories in Banh Bao?

Also known as Bánh bao, Vietnamese Steamed Pork Bun, Banh Bao Nhan Thit, Banh Bao Trung Cut.

A fist sized steamed bun of soft white dough filled with seasoned minced pork, wood ear mushroom, a slice of lap xuong sausage and one or two quail eggs. Sold warm from glass cabinets on street corners and at every com binh dan for 12,000 to 25,000 dong.

360kcal / 1 bun (pork and quail egg)
A standard banh bao nhan thit with quail egg is around 360 calories, in line with the Long Chau figure of 360 per bun and the Pharmacity range of 350 to 400 for the 2 egg version. The dough is 220 of it, the pork filling about 85, and the quail eggs and lap xuong the last 55. A small 25g bun from a bakery bag is about 75.

The real talk

The dough is where the calories are, and it is more dough than you think. A street banh bao weighs 140 to 160g and roughly 90g of that is steamed wheat dough made with sugar and a little oil, about 240 calories per 100g cooked. So 220 from the bun alone before you find the filling. The pork filling is 35g of minced shoulder with onion and wood ear, around 85. Two quail eggs are 30 and the slice of lap xuong is 25. That is your 360.

It looks like a snack and eats like a meal. At 360 calories and only 14g of protein, a banh bao is more than a plate of banh cuon (300) and close to a bowl of pho ga (400), but nowhere near as filling, because 150g of bread does not sit in the stomach the way 500g of soup does. The usual move is a banh bao and a ca phe sua da for breakfast, about 490 together, and hunger by 10am.

The variants spread wide. Long Chau lists banh bao nhan dau xanh (mung bean) at 150, banh bao trung muoi (salted egg) at 150 for a smaller bun, banh bao khong nhan chien (fried plain) at 180, and the big pork and 2 egg version at 350 to 400. The chain shop ones, Tho Phat style, run 180 to 200g and 400 plus. A banh bao chien, deep fried instead of steamed, adds 80 to 100 of oil to whichever version you pick.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick the single quail egg bun over the 2 egg or dac biet one. Saves 30 to 60.
  • Add a boiled egg or a glass of unsweetened soy milk instead of a second bun. About 75 to 90 for another 7g of protein.
  • Skip banh bao chien. Frying adds 80 to 100 calories to the same bun.
  • The mini bakery banh bao (20 to 30g) are 60 to 90 each. Two of those with an egg beats one big one.
  • Treat it as breakfast, not a snack between meals. 360 calories is a meal's worth.

Common questions

How many calories in 1 banh bao nhan thit?
About 360 for a standard bun with pork and quail egg. The 2 egg version is 350 to 400 depending on size, and mini bakery buns are 60 to 90.
Is banh bao good for weight loss?
Not especially. It is 360 calories with only 14g of protein and 48g of refined carbs, so it does not keep you full for its size. A mung bean bun at 150 or half a bun with an egg is the better version.
How much protein is in a banh bao?
Around 14g from the pork, quail eggs and the wheat dough. That is half what you get from a bowl of pho for a similar calorie count.
Banh bao or banh mi, which has more calories?
A banh bao is about 360 and a banh mi thit is 500, but the banh mi has 20g of protein and vegetables. Per calorie the banh mi is the better breakfast.
