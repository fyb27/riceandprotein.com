How Many Calories in Banh Cuon?

Also known as Bánh cuốn, Vietnamese Steamed Rice Rolls, Banh Cuon Thanh Tri.

Paper thin steamed rice sheets rolled around minced pork and wood ear mushroom, cut up, showered with fried shallots and served with slices of cha lua (pork loaf) and a bowl of warm nuoc cham. Hanoi breakfast, 25,000 to 35,000 dong.

300kcal / 1 plate with cha lua
A plate of banh cuon with cha lua is around 300 calories. The 200g of rice rolls with their pork and mushroom filling is about 220, the two slices of cha lua are 60, and the fried shallots and dipping sauce add 45. Plain banh cuon with only mushroom is about 200 a plate.

The real talk

Banh cuon is one of the lightest things you can eat at a Hanoi breakfast stall, and the reason is water. The rice batter is steamed on cloth into sheets a millimetre thick, so a 200g plate of rolls is only about 220 calories even with the pork and wood ear filling. Compare that with 180g of xoi at 600. The filling is minced pork shoulder, maybe 30g per plate, so 60 of the 220 is meat.

The sides are where the plate goes up. Cha lua, the smooth boiled pork loaf, is 130 calories per 100g, so the two slices you get are about 60. Fried shallots are 25 for a teaspoon and most sellers are generous. The nuoc cham is warm, sweet and diluted, around 20 calories a bowl. Some stalls add a drop of ca cuong essence, which is just aroma. That is 300 for the standard plate.

The heavier orders are banh cuon trung, with a soft egg steamed inside the roll, and banh cuon with nem chua ran (fried fermented pork) or a fried banh tom on the side. Egg adds about 80 per roll. A plate with cha, egg and two fried nem is around 450 to 500. The published range of 200 to 600 per plate covers plain rolls at the bottom and minced pork with all the extras at the top.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order banh cuon chay or nhan moc nhi, mushroom filling only. About 200 a plate.
  • Ask for it hanh phi, less fried shallots. Each teaspoon is about 25 calories.
  • Skip the nem chua ran or banh tom on the side. Each fried piece is 80 to 120.
  • One slice of cha lua instead of two saves 30 and you still get the taste.
  • A plate of banh cuon plus a boiled egg is a decent 375 calorie breakfast with 20g of protein.

Common questions

How many calories in banh cuon?
About 200 for a plain plate and 300 with cha lua. Versions with egg inside or fried nem on the side run 380 to 500.
Is banh cuon good for weight loss?
Yes, it is one of the lighter street breakfasts at 300 calories with 14g of protein. Add a boiled egg for protein and skip the fried sides.
Banh cuon or pho, which is lighter?
Banh cuon at 300 versus pho at 450. Pho has more protein though, 28g against 14g, so it keeps you full longer.
What is banh cuon made of?
A thin batter of rice flour and tapioca starch steamed into sheets, filled with minced pork and wood ear mushroom, and served with cha lua and diluted fish sauce.
