How Many Calories in Banh Flan?

Also known as Bánh flan, Caramen, Kem flan, Vietnamese Caramel Custard.

A small egg and milk custard baked in a ramekin over a layer of burnt sugar caramel, tipped out and served in Saigon with crushed ice and a spoon of black coffee, or plain as caramen in Hanoi. 5,000 to 10,000 dong each from a cart.

125kcal / 1 cup with coffee and ice
A single street banh flan of about 80g is around 125 calories. Long Chau gives 145 per 100g for a plain flan, and the custard is 90 of the cup, the caramel layer 30, and the spoon of black coffee 5. The small 45g ones sold in packs of 3 to 6 are 65 to 70 each.

The real talk

Flan is eggs, milk and sugar, and there is no fat added beyond the yolks. A cup uses about 1 egg, 60ml of milk and 15g of sugar in the custard, so 90 calories, plus a 12g caramel layer that is pure sugar at 30. Long Chau's 145 per 100g is exactly what that adds up to. Compared with the other things on a che cart, a 125 calorie flan is about a third of a che ba mau (330).

The size of the cup decides everything. Long Chau's 65 to 70 per piece refers to the small 40 to 50g flans from supermarket packs and carts that sell 3 for 20,000 dong. Nobody eats one of those; three is 200. The big 100g quan version with a thick caramel base is 145, and banh flan pho mai (cheese flan) is 200 per 100g because of the cream cheese folded in. Flan with condensed milk drizzled on top, which some Saigon carts do, is 160 to 170.

It is one of the few desserts with real protein, about 4g per cup from the egg and milk, and the caramel gives you the sweet hit at 30 calories rather than the 90 of coconut milk on a che. A flan and a ca phe den after dinner is 130. A flan and a ca phe sua da is 255. The Hanoi caramen version served with sua chua (yogurt) and coffee jelly is 220 to 250 for a bigger bowl.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order the plain one, not flan pho mai. Cheese flan is 200 per 100g versus 145.
  • Skip the condensed milk drizzle. It adds 35 to 45 to a 125 calorie cup.
  • One flan with black coffee and ice is the whole dessert at 130. Do not pair it with che.
  • Buy singles, not the 3 for 20,000 pack. Three small ones is 200 calories that feel like nothing.
  • Leave the caramel pooled at the bottom. It is 30 calories of pure sugar.

Common questions

How many calories in 1 banh flan?
About 125 for a standard 80g street flan, or 145 per 100g per Long Chau. The small 45g cart ones are 65 to 70 each.
Is banh flan good for a diet?
It is one of the better desserts: 125 calories with 4g of protein and no added fat beyond the egg. The risk is eating three because they are small.
How many calories in flan pho mai?
About 200 per 100g, so a standard cup is 160 to 200. The cream cheese adds fat that a plain flan does not have.
Banh flan or che, which has fewer calories?
Banh flan, easily. A cup is 125 against 330 for a glass of che ba mau and 400 for che Thai.
