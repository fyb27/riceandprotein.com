How Many Calories in Banh Trang Tron?

Also known as Bánh tráng trộn, Vietnamese Rice Paper Salad, Rice Paper Mix, Banh Trang Tron Sai Gon.

Strips of dried rice paper tossed in a plastic bag with shredded green mango, quail eggs, beef jerky, dried shrimp, peanuts, fried shallots, rau ram, kumquat juice and sate chili oil. The after school snack of the whole country, 15,000 to 25,000 dong a bag from a cart outside any school gate.

450kcal / 1 bag (150g)
A normal 150g bag of banh trang tron is around 450 calories. Vinmec puts it at 300 per 100g with 16g of fat, 33g of carbs and 5g of protein, and a 200g bag at close to 600. The rice paper itself is 200 of the 450; the sate oil, shallot oil and peanuts are another 150.

The real talk

Rice paper is dried starch, so it is dense before anything touches it. The 60g of banh trang strips in a bag is about 200 calories, the same as a plate of bun. Then the seller adds what makes it taste good: a spoon of sate (chili in oil) and a spoon of mo hanh (shallot oil), together 12g of oil and about 90 calories, plus 10g of crushed peanuts at 60. The quail eggs are 45 for 3, the bo kho (beef jerky) and dried shrimp about 45. Mango, rau ram and kumquat are 10. Total 450.

It does not register as a meal because it is a bag you eat with a toothpick standing up. But 450 calories is a bowl of pho bo, and the Vinmec note that 94.5 percent of the energy comes from starch and fat means it does almost nothing for hunger. Most people eat one at 4pm and then have a full dinner at 7. Add the second bag that a lot of students buy and you are at 900 before dinner.

The size of the bag is the whole story. Vinmec's 300 per 100g is fine, but a cart outside a school in District 1 sells 100g bags at 15,000 dong (300 calories) and 200g bags at 25,000 (600). Banh trang cuon, the rolled version with the same toppings, is 350 to 400 for a similar weight because it uses less oil. Banh trang nuong, the grilled sheet with egg and cheese that tourists call Vietnamese pizza, is 250 to 300 per sheet and often two are ordered.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for it sa te, less chili oil, and khong mo hanh. That is 60 to 90 calories out of the bag.
  • Buy the small 100g bag (about 300) and eat it slowly. The 200g one is a full meal at 600.
  • Extra quail eggs are the best add-on: 15 each and they bring protein. Extra peanuts are 60 a spoon.
  • Banh trang cuon with the same toppings is about 100 lighter because there is less oil coating the strips.
  • Have it with a glass of tra da (0 calories), not a tra sua (350 plus).

Common questions

How many calories in 1 bag of banh trang tron?
About 300 for a 100g bag, 450 for a 150g one and close to 600 for 200g, using Vinmec's figure of 300 calories per 100g.
Is banh trang tron fattening?
It is easy to overeat. A standard bag is 450 calories with 20g of fat and only 12g of protein, so it adds to the day without filling you up. One bag a week is fine; one a day on top of three meals is 3,000 extra calories a week.
How many calories in banh trang nuong?
About 250 to 300 per sheet with egg, dried shrimp, spring onion and a little cheese. Two sheets is a 550 calorie snack.
What is in banh trang tron?
Dried rice paper strips, green mango, quail eggs, beef jerky, dried shrimp, peanuts, fried shallots, rau ram herb, kumquat juice and sate chili oil. Mixed in a bag and eaten straight away.
