How Many Calories in Banh Xeo?

Also known as Bánh xèo, Vietnamese Sizzling Pancake, Vietnamese Crepe, Banh Xeo Mien Tay.

A crisp turmeric rice flour and coconut milk crepe fried in a hot pan with pork belly, prawns and bean sprouts folded inside, torn up and wrapped in mustard leaf and herbs, then dipped in nuoc cham. Big and thin in the south, small and thick in the centre. About 20,000 to 40,000 dong each.

350kcal / 1 medium pancake
A medium banh xeo is around 350 calories. The batter is rice flour with coconut milk and turmeric, about 170 for one pancake, and it is fried in enough oil to make it crisp, which adds 90. The prawn and pork inside are about 70, the bean sprouts 15. Small central Vietnam ones are 250; the huge southern ones are 500.

The real talk

Banh xeo looks like a light, vegetable heavy snack and it is really a fried pancake. The batter for one medium pancake is about 60g of rice flour and 40ml of coconut milk, 170 calories before it hits the pan. Then it hits the pan with a ladle of oil, because the crispness comes from shallow frying, and a medium pancake absorbs about 10g, 90 calories. That is 260 before the filling.

The filling is the healthy bit. Two or three prawns and a few slices of pork belly are about 70 calories for 40g, and the bean sprouts are 15. The published figure of 315 to 350 for a medium pancake with 11g fat, 40g carbs and 14g protein lines up with that. What changes it is size. A Hue or Da Nang banh xeo is palm size and 250; a Mekong style one is the size of the pan and 450 to 500.

Nobody eats one. The typical order at a banh xeo quan is two per person, with a plate of mustard greens and herbs and a bowl of sweet fish sauce, about 40 calories per bowl. Two medium pancakes plus sauce is 740. Two southern ones is 1,000. The greens are free and the pancake is meant to be wrapped in them, so at least the meal has volume.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order the central style small ones. About 250 each versus 350 for medium and 500 for the big southern kind.
  • Wrap every piece in a large mustard leaf with herbs. The greens are free and slow you down.
  • Ask for it dau, less oil, at a quan that cooks to order. Saves 30 to 50 per pancake.
  • One pancake plus a bowl of canh or a goi cuon is a lighter meal than two pancakes.
  • Ask for more tom (prawn) and less ba chi (pork belly) in the filling.

Common questions

How many calories in one banh xeo?
About 350 for a medium pancake with prawn and pork. Small central Vietnam ones are around 250 and the large Mekong style ones about 500.
Is banh xeo healthy?
It has some protein and comes with a lot of raw greens, but the pancake is fried in oil and made with coconut milk, so 15g of fat per medium piece. One is fine; two or three is a heavy meal.
Is banh xeo gluten free?
Usually yes. The batter is rice flour, turmeric and coconut milk. Some stalls add a little wheat flour or beer for crispness, so ask if it matters.
How much oil is in banh xeo?
A medium pancake absorbs about 10g of frying oil, roughly 90 calories, which is a quarter of the total.
