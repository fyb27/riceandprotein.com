How Many Calories in Bebek Goreng?

Also known as Bebek Goreng Madura, Bebek Goreng Surabaya, Nasi Bebek, Bebek Goreng Sambal Hitam, Indonesian Fried Duck.

Bebek goreng is duck simmered in spices until tender, then deep fried until the skin shatters, served with rice, lalapan and a sambal. Surabaya does it with a fiery red sambal; Madura carts in Jakarta do it with sambal hitam and a spoon of duck oil.

380kcal / 1 piece (thigh) with sambal and lalapan, no rice
One piece of bebek goreng with sambal and lalapan is about 380 calories. FatSecret Indonesia lists one piece of fried duck at 337 calories with 28g fat and 15g protein, and a full 236g portion at 674. Add rice and the standard nasi bebek is about 650.

The real talk

Duck is fattier than chicken before it goes anywhere near a fryer. A piece of fried duck thigh with skin is 337 calories on FatSecret with 76% of that from fat, against 390 for a bigger piece of ayam goreng with more meat. Per gram of meat, duck costs you more. The skin is where it lives: strip it and the same piece is about 220.

The Madura cart version is the one to watch. Nasi bebek Madura comes with rice, a small piece of duck, sambal hitam (a dark, oily shallot sambal), serundeng and a spoon of the duck frying oil poured over the rice. That spoon is 90 calories on its own. The Rp 20,000 to 30,000 plate is about 650, and the duck is the smallest part of it by weight.

Bebek goreng Surabaya style, the Pak Slamet and Purnama type, gives you a bigger piece, a bright red sambal bawang and raw lalapan. The piece is 380 with sambal, and the plate with rice is around 640. The sambal bawang is made with hot oil so a spoon is 55, but the cabbage, cucumber and basil are nearly zero.

The protein is only 22g per piece because a duck thigh is small and a lot of the weight is skin and bone. For the same 380 calories, ayam bakar gives you about 32g protein. Bebek is a flavour order, not a protein order, and that is fine once a week.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for dada (breast) instead of paha. Duck breast has less skin per gram of meat, about 60 calories less per piece.
  • Peel off the fattiest skin flaps. The crispy skin is the best part and it is also 40% of the calories.
  • Say no to the minyak bebek on the rice. That spoon of duck oil is 90 calories for zero food.
  • Half rice. Nasi bebek with 100g rice is 520 instead of 650.
  • Bebek bakar (grilled) if the place does it. About 100 calories less because nothing is fried.

Common questions

How many calories in bebek goreng?
About 380 calories for one fried thigh with sambal and lalapan. FatSecret lists the duck alone at 337 per piece. With rice the plate is about 650.
Is bebek goreng higher in calories than ayam goreng?
Per piece they are close, 337 for duck versus 391 for a bigger piece of chicken. Per gram of meat the duck is fattier, because more of the piece is skin and fat.
How many calories in nasi bebek Madura?
About 650 calories. The duck oil spooned over the rice adds around 90 and the sambal hitam is oily too, so the rice ends up carrying more fat than the duck.
Is fried duck bad for cholesterol?
Duck skin is high in saturated fat. Remove the skin and the piece drops to about 220 calories with far less fat, and the meat itself is close to chicken thigh.
Berapa kalori Bebek Goreng?
Bebek Goreng mengandung sekitar 380 kalori per satu porsi biasa (190g), dengan 22g protein, 6g karbohidrat dan 30g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
