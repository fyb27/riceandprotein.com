How Many Calories in Bee Hoon Goreng?

Fried rice vermicelli. The noodles are light, but the wok oil is what sets the calories.

450kcal / 1 plate
A plate of bee hoon goreng is around 450 calories. The rice vermicelli itself is light, so the calories really come from the stir fry oil. The soup version drops about 150 calories because it skips the frying.

The real talk

Fried bee hoon is one of those dishes where the noodle is innocent and the oil is guilty. Rice vermicelli is light on its own, but stir frying it means a generous wok of oil that the thin strands soak right up. That is why a plate lands around 450 calories despite looking modest, and why the official Malaysian food database puts fried mee hoon at 173 calories per 100g.

Add seafood or extra egg and it climbs to 500 plus. The smart move if you want it lighter is bee hoon sup, the soup version, which skips the frying entirely and drops to around 290. Same noodles, no wok oil, big calorie difference.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less oil (kurang minyak). The frying oil is the main calorie source.
  • Order bee hoon sup (soup) instead of goreng to save around 150 calories.
  • Add egg or vegetables for substance rather than extra noodles.

Common questions

How many calories in bee hoon goreng?
Around 450 calories a plate. The rice vermicelli is light, so most of the calories come from the stir fry oil it soaks up.
Is fried bee hoon healthy?
It depends on the oil. The noodles are light, but frying adds a lot. The soup version (bee hoon sup) is much lighter at around 290. Ask for less oil if you get it fried.
Why is bee hoon goreng higher than it looks?
Thin rice vermicelli soaks up stir fry oil readily. The official food database lists fried mee hoon at 173 calories per 100g, so a normal plate clears 450 mostly from oil.
Berapa kalori dalam Bee Hoon Goreng?
Bee Hoon Goreng ada kira-kira 450 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (370g), dengan 14g protein, 62g karbohidrat dan 15g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
