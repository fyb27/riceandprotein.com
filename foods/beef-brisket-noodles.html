How Many Calories in Beef Brisket Noodles (Ngau Lam Min)?

Also known as 牛腩麵, Ngau Lam Min, Beef Brisket Noodle Soup, Hong Kong Brisket Noodles, 牛腩河.

Ngau lam min is thin egg noodles in clear beef broth with chunks of brisket that have been simmered for 3 or 4 hours until they fall apart. Noodle shops from Central to Kowloon City charge HK$45 to HK$70 a bowl.

550kcal / 1 bowl (thin egg noodles in soup)
One bowl of beef brisket noodles with thin egg noodles is about 550 calories, based on Centre for Food Safety analysis of a 640g bowl. The same brisket on thick noodles is 520, on rice vermicelli 610, and on ho fun 670. Protein is high at around 50g, which is more than most Hong Kong noodle dishes.

The real talk

Brisket noodles are one of the better deals on a Hong Kong noodle menu if you care about protein. CFS measured 50g of protein in a 640g bowl, which is close to two chicken breasts, for 550 calories. The brisket is the reason: about 120g of slow cooked beef per bowl. The trade off is fat, at 17g with 6g of it saturated, because brisket is a fatty cut and the broth is skimmed less than you would like.

The noodle choice moves the number more than people expect. Thin egg noodles land at 550 and thick at 520, but a bowl of ho fun with the same brisket is 670 because flat rice noodles are heavier and soak up more soup. Rice vermicelli sits at 610. If you always order 牛腩河 out of habit, switching to 牛腩麵 saves about 120 calories a bowl with the same amount of beef on top.

The soup is the sodium problem, not the calorie problem. CFS found 3,100mg of sodium in a bowl of brisket with thin noodles, which is one and a half days of the 2,000mg WHO limit in a single lunch. Most of it is in the broth, so the trick is simple: eat the noodles and the meat, leave at least half the soup. The famous brisket shops also serve a curry version (咖喱牛腩) and that adds around 100 calories from the coconut and oil in the sauce.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order thin egg noodles rather than ho fun. Same brisket, about 120 fewer calories.
  • Ask for lean brisket (瘦腩) or tendon (牛筋). The tendon version is 480 calories with only 9g of fat.
  • Leave half the soup. That alone cuts sodium by well over 1,000mg.
  • Add a plate of blanched choy sum without oyster sauce (走油). About 30 calories and it stretches the meal.

Common questions

How many calories in a bowl of beef brisket noodles?
About 550 calories for a 640g bowl with thin egg noodles, according to Centre for Food Safety data. The ho fun version is about 670.
Is beef brisket noodle soup healthy?
It has 50g of protein per bowl, which is excellent, but also around 3,100mg of sodium, mostly in the broth. Eat the noodles and beef, skip most of the soup.
Which noodle is lowest in calories with brisket?
Thick egg noodles at about 520 calories, then thin egg noodles at 550. Rice vermicelli is 610 and ho fun is 670 for the same amount of brisket.
