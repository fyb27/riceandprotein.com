How Many Calories in Beef Chow Fun (Gon Chau Ngau Ho)?

Also known as 乾炒牛河, Gon Chau Ngau Ho, Dry Fried Beef Ho Fun, Stir Fried Beef Rice Noodles, Beef Ho Fun.

Gon chau ngau ho is wide rice noodles tossed over a screaming hot wok with sliced beef, bean sprouts, spring onion and dark soy until the edges char. Every cha chaan teng and dai pai dong does it, HK$55 to HK$80 a plate.

970kcal / 1 plate
One plate of dry fried beef ho fun is around 970 calories, based on Centre for Food Safety testing of a 650g restaurant portion. Carbs make up 140g of that, from roughly 400g of rice noodles, and fat is 33g from the oil the noodles need to fry without sticking.

The real talk

The number surprises people because there is nothing creamy or deep fried on the plate. But the wok needs a lot of oil to keep 400g of flat rice noodles from tearing, around 30g per plate, and rice noodles themselves are dense. CFS measured 140g of carbohydrate in one plate, which is the same as three and a half bowls of white rice. The beef is only about 100g, so protein is a modest 26g for nearly 1,000 calories.

Among Hong Kong fried noodle dishes this is actually mid table. Singapore fried vermicelli is 1,100. Fried noodles with black bean spare ribs is 1,500. Fried instant noodles with pork chop is 1,200. Beef ho fun at 970 is the one everyone names as the villain because it is the most ordered, and a dietitian in the HKET ranking pointed out it does not even make the top three. That is not a reason to order it twice a week, just context.

Portion size at a dai pai dong is the real issue. The 650g CFS plate is a standard single order, but the plates in Sham Shui Po and Mong Kok that come with a mound of noodles hanging off the edge are closer to 900g and 1,300 calories. Sodium is 2,300mg a plate from the soy sauce alone. If you split a plate between two people and add a plate of greens, each person gets a 500 calorie meal, which is reasonable.

How to eat it lighter

  • Share one plate between two. Half a plate is about 485 calories.
  • Ask for less oil (少油). Some places will, and it can save 100 to 150 calories.
  • Order the wet version (濕炒牛河) if you must have ho fun. The gravy style uses less oil, roughly 750 calories a plate.
  • Pair it with blanched vegetables, not another fried dish. The noodles are already the whole carb budget for the day.

Common questions

How many calories in a plate of beef chow fun?
About 970 calories for a 650g plate, according to Centre for Food Safety data. Dai pai dong portions can pass 1,300.
Why is dry fried beef ho fun so high in calories?
Around 400g of rice noodles (440 calories) plus about 30g of oil (265 calories) needed to fry them without sticking. The beef is only about 200 of the total.
Is wet or dry fried ho fun healthier?
Wet (濕炒) is lower, roughly 750 calories, because the noodles are blanched rather than fried in oil and the gravy is mostly stock and starch.
