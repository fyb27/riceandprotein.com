How Many Calories in Beef Hor Fun?

Also known as Beef Kway Teow, 牛肉河粉, Beef Hor Fun Gravy, Sliced Beef Hor Fun.

Beef hor fun is flat rice noodles fried on high heat until charred, then covered in a starchy egg gravy with tender sliced beef and a few stalks of green vegetable. A cze char and hawker standard in Singapore.

697kcal / 1 plate
A standard plate of beef hor fun is about 697 calories for a 450g serving, with around 28g of fat. The 250g of wok fried kway teow is about 320 calories, the egg gravy 120, the beef 130, and the frying oil the remaining 130.

The real talk

Hor fun is fried twice, in effect. First the noodles are tossed in a hot oiled wok to get the char, which is where 250g of kway teow picks up most of its 320 calories and a good part of the 130 calories of oil. Then the gravy is poured on, which is stock thickened with cornstarch and egg, about 120 calories. The 80g of sliced beef is the smallest number on the plate at 130. So the beef is not the problem. The noodle and the oil are.

This is the plate that beats char kway teow for stealth. Nobody thinks a gravy noodle is heavy because it looks wet and soft. But 697 calories is nearly the same as a plate of char kway teow at 742, and more than a plate of Singapore Hokkien mee at 522. The gravy hides the oil. It does not remove it.

Cze char portions are the other issue. A S$6 hawker plate is the 697 calorie one. A S$8 to S$10 cze char plate for one is usually 30 percent bigger and lands around 880. The same amount of beef, more noodles, more gravy. Two people ordering one plate to share with a plate of vegetables is the sane approach.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order the soup version, beef kway teow soup, which is about 400 calories. Same beef, no wok oil, no starchy gravy.
  • Ask for less oil when frying. Cze char stalls will usually do it and it saves 50 to 80 calories.
  • Ask for more beef and less noodles. Beef is about 1.6 calories per gram, the fried kway teow closer to 1.3 plus oil.
  • Share one plate between two and order a plate of stir fried kai lan at about 150 calories on the side.
  • Leave the last third of the gravy on the plate. It is starch and oil, not flavour you will miss.

Common questions

Is beef hor fun healthy?
Not especially. At 697 calories with 28g of fat, it is close to char kway teow territory. The upside is 28g of protein from the beef. Order it as an occasional meal, not a daily lunch.
How many calories in the hor fun gravy?
About 120 calories for a standard ladle of 150g. It is chicken or pork stock thickened with cornstarch and swirled with egg. It looks heavy but the oil in the fried noodles is the bigger number.
Is beef hor fun or wat tan hor higher in calories?
About the same. Wat tan hor with seafood and pork is around 640 calories, beef hor fun about 697. The beef slices are marinated with oil and cornstarch, which adds a little.
How much protein is in beef hor fun?
Around 28g per standard plate, most of it from 80g of sliced beef and the egg in the gravy. It is one of the higher protein noodle plates, just also one of the higher calorie ones.
Berapa kalori dalam Beef Hor Fun?
Beef Hor Fun ada kira-kira 697 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (450g), dengan 28g protein, 78g karbohidrat dan 28g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
