How Many Calories in Begedil (Bergedil)?

Also known as Bergedil, Perkedel.

2 fried potato patties run about 190 kcal. Tasty but oily, easy to eat five.

190kcal / 2 pieces
Two pieces of begedil come in around 190 calories, so roughly 95 each. It is mashed potato with a bit of minced meat, shaped into a patty and deep fried, so most of the damage is potato plus the oil it soaks up. One or two next to your nasi is fine, just do not treat them like free side dishes.

The real talk

Begedil feels harmless because it is small and it is just potato, right? But it is fried, and fried potato drinks oil like nobody business. Each little patty is around 95 calories before you even dip it in chilli.

The problem at a kenduri is you take five without thinking, that is nearly 500 calories of side dish on top of your rice and curry. If you love begedil, take two, enjoy them properly, and move on lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Air fry or oven bake instead of deep fry, you save a big chunk of the oil calories
  • Stick to 2 pieces and let your rice portion be the filler, not the begedil
  • Pat them on tissue first so the surface oil drains off before you eat

Common questions

How many calories in one begedil?
About 95 calories per piece, depending on size and how much meat is inside.
Is begedil bad for weight loss?
Not bad in small amounts. It is fried potato so it is calorie dense, but 1 to 2 pieces fits fine in a deficit. The trap is eating five without noticing.
Is air fried begedil healthier?
Yes, you cut a good amount of the absorbed oil, so an air fried piece can be closer to 70 calories instead of 95.
Berapa kalori dalam Begedil?
Begedil ada kira-kira 190 kalori untuk 2 keping (90g), dengan 6g protein, 20g karbohidrat dan 10g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
