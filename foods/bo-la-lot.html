How Many Calories in Bo La Lot?

Also known as Bò lá lốt, Bò nướng lá lốt, Beef in Betel Leaf, Grilled Beef Wrapped in Lolot Leaf.

Seasoned minced beef and pork fat rolled in lolot leaves and grilled over charcoal until the leaves char, then topped with scallion oil and crushed peanuts. Served with banh hoi, rice paper, herbs and mam nem at street grills from 4pm. Around 50,000 to 70,000 dong a plate.

580kcal / 1 plate (5 rolls)
A street plate of five bo la lot rolls is around 580 calories, about 115 per roll. The mince is beef mixed with pork fat, so a 40g roll is 90 to 110 before the mo hanh and peanuts on top add another 30 per roll. The full set with banh hoi, rice paper and dipping sauce is about 850.

The real talk

Bo la lot is a fat bomb hiding behind a leaf. The mince is beef mixed with pork fat or pork mince, usually 20 to 30 percent fat, because lean beef dries out on the grill. So a 40g roll is about 90 to 110 calories, and the published range for a 50 to 60g roll is 120 to 180. Five rolls on a plate is 440 to 550 before toppings. The lolot leaf is nothing, about 1 calorie each.

Then the seller pours mo hanh over the plate, scallions fried in oil, a tablespoon is 80 calories, and sprinkles peanuts, another 60. That is how a plate of five reaches 580. It comes with mam nem, fermented anchovy sauce with pineapple and sugar, about 40 calories for a small bowl, and most tables have two.

The way it is actually eaten is the problem: as a set with banh hoi (fine rice vermicelli sheets, 200 calories for a normal serving), rice paper (60 for two sheets), and a plate of herbs and cucumber. Wrapped and dipped, the full bo la lot banh hoi is 850 to 900 calories, and it is usually an appetiser at a bia hoi table before the main dishes. Two beers on top is another 300.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for khong mo hanh, no scallion oil. That is 80 calories off a plate of five.
  • Wrap in rice paper with lots of herbs and skip the banh hoi. Saves 200 and you still get the bite.
  • Three rolls, not five, as a side dish. About 350.
  • Dip lightly. The mam nem has sugar and pineapple, about 40 per bowl if you finish it.
  • Some quan grill bo la lot with lean beef only, which is drier but 30 fewer calories a roll. Ask.

Common questions

How many calories in one bo la lot roll?
About 90 to 115 for a 40g street roll with toppings. Larger 50 to 60g rolls are 120 to 180 each.
Is bo la lot healthy?
It is high in protein, 30g per plate of five, but also high in fat at 44g because the mince is cut with pork fat and topped with scallion oil. Fine occasionally, not a diet food.
How many calories in bo la lot banh hoi?
About 850 for a full set of five rolls with banh hoi, rice paper, herbs and mam nem. The banh hoi alone is around 200.
What is the leaf used for bo la lot?
Lolot leaf (la lot, Piper sarmentosum), a relative of betel. It adds aroma and almost no calories.
