How Many Calories in Bo Lo Yau (Pineapple Bun with Butter)?

Also known as 菠蘿油, Bo Lo Yau, Pineapple Bun with Butter, Polo Yau.

Bo lo yau is a warm pineapple bun split open with a thick slice of cold butter pushed inside so it half melts. It is the signature cha chaan teng snack, usually HK$12 to HK$20 with a milk tea set.

390kcal / 1 bun with butter
One bo lo yau is around 390 calories: about 310 from the pineapple bun and 75 from the 10g butter slab. Fat nearly doubles compared to a plain bun, going from 10g to about 19g, because butter is almost pure fat.

The real talk

The butter is the whole point and also the whole problem. A cha chaan teng slab is usually 10g, sometimes 15g at the generous places, and butter is 7.2 calories per gram. That means the slab is 75 to 110 calories with zero protein and zero carbs. It is the easiest calorie you will ever eat because it melts into the bun and you barely notice it is there.

A bo lo yau plus a hot milk tea is the classic afternoon tea set, and it lands at about 500 calories. That is a quarter of a normal adult's day in a snack that takes 5 minutes to eat. Compare it to a bowl of century egg congee at 260 calories, which will keep you full for three hours. The bun set will not.

Not all bo lo yau are equal. The famous places in Mong Kok and Sai Wan use bigger 100g buns and thicker butter, so those push 450 to 480 calories. A basic bakery bun with a thin slice is closer to 350. The tell is how much butter is visible when they bring it out. If you can see a yellow slab thicker than a HK$10 coin, assume the high end.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for half the butter. Most cha chaan tengs will do it and you save about 40 calories without losing the taste.
  • Order it with a hot lemon tea (36 calories) instead of milk tea (110) and the set drops to under 430.
  • Share one bun and each have a boiled egg or a small bowl of congee instead. You get the taste and some actual protein.
  • Treat it as a once a week snack rather than a daily 3pm habit. Five bo lo yau a week is nearly 2,000 calories, which is more than half a kilo of body fat per month if nothing else changes.

Common questions

How much butter is in a bo lo yau?
Usually a 10g slab, sometimes 15g at generous places. That is 75 to 110 calories of pure fat added to a 310 calorie bun.
Is bo lo yau worse than a croissant?
About the same. A plain croissant is around 270 calories with 14g fat; a bo lo yau is 390 with 19g fat but is also bigger. Per gram they are close.
Can I eat bo lo yau while losing weight?
Yes, as long as it fits your day. At 390 calories it is a snack, not a meal. Have it and eat lighter at dinner, or skip the butter and save 75 calories.
