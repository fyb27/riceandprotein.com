How Many Calories in Bo Ne?

Also known as Bò né, Bò né ốp la, Vietnamese Sizzling Beef Plate, Bo Ne Banh Mi.

Thin slices of marinated beef seared on a cast iron plate with butter, a fried egg, a slab of pate, a xiu mai meatball and onion, brought to the table spitting (ne means dodge) with a banh mi and a small salad. Saigon breakfast from 5am, 40,000 to 65,000 dong.

680kcal / 1 hot plate with 1 banh mi
A standard bo ne plate with a fried egg, pate, one xiu mai and one banh mi is around 680 calories. Long Chau puts the beef plate itself at 400 to 600 depending on toppings and oil, and the bread adds 190. The 90g of beef is only 170 of it; the egg, pate, xiu mai and the butter it all sizzles in are 310 together.

The real talk

The beef is the lean part of the plate. Long Chau lists lean beef at 147 calories per 100g and fattier cuts at 239 to 306, and a bo ne portion is 80 to 100g of thin sliced round or flank, about 170 once marinated in oil and oyster sauce. What takes the plate to 680 is everything around it: a fried egg at 100, 15g of pate at 50, one xiu mai at 70, and a knob of butter plus oil on the iron plate at 90. That is 310 of extras before the bread.

The banh mi is the other 190, and a lot of quan put two on the table. Dip both into the egg yolk and pate and you are at 870 for breakfast. Bo ne dac biet adds a sausage and a second egg, another 200. Long Chau's warning that a full plate with sausage, egg, pate, bread and potatoes gets close to 1,000 calories is about right for the big 65,000 dong versions.

It is a good protein breakfast if you strip it back, 38g on the standard plate. Bo ne with just beef, one egg and one bread is about 480 and keeps you full until lunch, which is the opposite of a 360 calorie banh bao. The cast iron plate is the thing to watch: whatever butter is left on it at the end got absorbed by the bread you wiped it with, and 10g of butter is 75 calories.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order bo ne khong pate, khong xiu mai. Beef and egg only is about 480 with one bread.
  • One banh mi, not two. The second loaf is 190 plus whatever it soaks up.
  • Ask for it bo (less butter). Most quan will sear in oil only if you say so, saving 40 to 50.
  • Do not wipe the plate. The pooled butter and egg yolk on the iron is 60 to 80 calories.
  • Eat the tomato, cucumber and onion first. They are 15 calories and the only fibre on the plate.

Common questions

How many calories in a plate of bo ne?
About 680 with egg, pate, xiu mai and one banh mi. Long Chau puts the plate at 400 to 600 before bread, and the full version with sausage and two loaves can reach 1,000.
Is bo ne healthy for breakfast?
It is high in protein at 38g, which is good. The downside is 38g of fat from butter, pate, egg yolk and xiu mai. Beef and egg with one bread is a solid 480 calorie breakfast.
How many calories in bo ne without bread?
Around 490 for the standard plate. The banh mi is 190, and most of the bread's job is mopping up butter and yolk, which adds more.
Bo ne or pho bo, which has more calories?
Bo ne at 680 versus pho bo at 450. Bo ne has 10g more protein, but 26g more fat.
