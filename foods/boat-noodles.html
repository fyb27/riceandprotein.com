How Many Calories in Boat Noodles (Kuay Teow Reua)?

Also known as ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเรือ, Kuaitiao Ruea, Guay Tiew Rua, Thai Boat Noodle Soup, Kuay Teow Nam Tok.

Rice noodles in a dark, intense broth thickened with pork or beef blood, with sliced meat, meatballs, morning glory and bean sprouts, topped with fried garlic and pork cracklings. Served in small bowls so you order five.

300kcal / 1 bowl
A standard 200g bowl of boat noodles is about 300 calories, per the dietitians at eatwellconcept.com, for a normal recipe without extra pork cracklings. The small 15 to 20 baht bowls that most shops serve are around 180. Most people eat three to five of those, which is 540 to 900 calories.

The real talk

Boat noodles are cheap per bowl and expensive per meal. A single small bowl is about 180 calories: a small tangle of sen lek noodles, four slices of pork, one meatball, a splash of dark broth. Nobody eats one. The stack of empty bowls is the whole culture of the dish, and a stack of five is 900 calories, which is more than a plate of pad see ew.

The broth is not as heavy as it looks. The dark colour comes from blood and dark soy, not fat, and 100ml is about 60 calories. What adds up is the topping: a spoon of kaap moo (pork cracklings) is 40 calories, and the fried garlic in oil is another 20. Ask for it without cracklings and the bowl drops to about 260.

It is a good high volume, low calorie choice if you set a number before you sit down. Three small bowls is about 540 calories with 40g of protein, plenty of broth and vegetables, and you will feel full. The mistake is the side plate of deep fried pork skin or the sweet Thai tea that always seems to come with it.

How to eat it lighter

  • Decide on three bowls before you order, not after. Three small bowls is about 540 calories; six is 1,080.
  • Say mai sai kaap moo (no pork cracklings) to save 40 calories a bowl.
  • Choose sen lek or sen mee (thin rice noodles) over sen yai or egg noodles; thinner noodles hold less broth and oil.
  • Order nam (soup) rather than haeng (dry). The dry version is tossed in oil and dark soy and runs 30 to 50 calories more per bowl.

Common questions

How many calories in 5 bowls of boat noodles?
About 900 if they are the small 15 to 20 baht bowls at 180 each. That is a full dinner's worth, and it is easy to do without noticing.
Is boat noodle broth fattening?
Not really. The dark colour is from blood and soy, and 100ml of broth is about 60 calories. The pork cracklings and fried garlic on top are the fattier part.
Are boat noodles healthy?
In moderation they are a decent choice: about 300 calories per standard bowl with 14g of protein, lots of broth, some vegetables. The sodium is high, and the count depends entirely on how many bowls you stack.
