How Many Calories in Bossam?

Also known as 보쌈, Korean Boiled Pork Wraps, Bossam Pork Belly, Suyuk, 수육.

Bossam is pork belly boiled with doenjang, garlic and coffee or onion until tender, sliced thick and wrapped in napa cabbage or lettuce with radish kimchi, salted shrimp and garlic. Kimchi-making day food, and jokbal's neighbour on the delivery app.

620kcal / 1 person's share (about 180g pork with kimchi and wraps)
One share of bossam is about 620 calories, based on the fatsecret.kr figure of 290 kcal per 100g for the boiled pork belly. A share is roughly 180g of meat (520 calories), plus 60g of bossam kimchi at about 75 and the wraps and salted shrimp. A small order for the table, around 500g of pork, is close to 1,700.

The real talk

People assume boiled is lean. It is not, when the cut is belly. Boiling renders some fat into the pot, but 100g of bossam pork is still about 290 calories, and a share of 6 or 7 thick slices is 520. The 33g of protein is fine, but 50g of fat in one sitting is the number to look at.

The bossam kimchi is sweeter than regular kimchi. It is radish and cabbage in a gochugaru dressing with sugar, pear and often salted fish, and a 60g pile is about 75 calories with 8g of sugar. Still, the kimchi, cabbage wraps, raw garlic and saeujeot together are under 100 calories a share. The pork is 85 percent of the plate.

Delivery bossam at 35,000 to 40,000 won for a small order comes with the same set as jokbal: makguksu, a bowl of rice or two, sometimes a small sundae. Two people splitting a small order with the noodles and rice are at about 1,250 each. Some shops now offer a leaner cut (앞다리살 or 목살 bossam) which drops to about 200 kcal per 100g.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for the leaner cut if the menu has it (앞다리 or 목살 수육). That is about 360 per share instead of 520 for the pork.
  • Wrap in napa cabbage, not lettuce, and use bigger leaves. More vegetable per bite slows you down.
  • Trim the thick fat cap off each slice. Each slice has about 15 calories of pure fat on the edge.
  • Skip the makguksu and rice in the set. Bossam and kimchi alone is 620; with noodles it is over 1,000.
  • Share a small order between 3 people. 500g of pork split 3 ways is 480 each instead of 720.

Common questions

Is bossam healthier than samgyeopsal?
Marginally. Boiled belly is about 290 kcal per 100g versus 349 grilled, and you eat it with more vegetables. A share is still 620 calories, so it is not diet food.
How many calories in bossam kimchi?
About 75 for a 60g side portion, with roughly 8g of sugar. It is sweeter than regular kimchi.
How many calories in a small bossam order?
About 1,700 for a 소 order with around 500g of pork, before the noodles and rice in the set.
Is boiled pork belly lower in fat than grilled?
A bit. Boiling renders roughly 15 percent of the fat into the water, but belly is still about 25g of fat per 100g after boiling.
