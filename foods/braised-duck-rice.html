How Many Calories in Braised Duck Rice?

Also known as Teochew Braised Duck Rice, Lor Ark Png, 卤鸭饭, Duck Rice.

Braised duck rice is the Teochew hawker plate of duck simmered in a dark soy, galangal and five spice braise, sliced over yam rice or white rice with braised gravy, a braised egg and tau kwa on the side.

604kcal / 1 plate
A standard plate of braised duck rice is about 604 calories for a 380g serving, with around 21g of fat. The 220g of yam rice is about 300 calories, the 100g of braised duck around 200, and the gravy, egg and tau kwa make up the rest.

The real talk

Duck has a reputation for being fatty and it is, but braising takes a lot of that out. 100g of Teochew braised duck with skin is about 200 calories, which is less than the 220g of yam rice at 300. Yam rice is rice cooked with taro, dried shrimp and shallot oil, so it is about 40 calories heavier than plain rice for the same scoop. Then a spoon of braising gravy at 50, half a braised egg at 40, and a piece of tau kwa at 20. Total 604.

The gravy is the sneaky part. It is the braising liquid thickened with starch, and it goes over the rice and the duck. One ladle is around 50 calories, and the uncle is not shy with it. Ask for less and you save that. Ask for extra, which people do because it is the flavour, and the plate is closer to 660.

Roast duck rice from the Cantonese stalls is a different number. Roast duck keeps the skin crisp and the fat under it, so a roast duck plate is around 700. Braised is the lighter duck. If you want duck for lunch and you are counting, this is the one.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for white rice instead of yam rice. It saves about 40 calories and you keep all the duck.
  • Ask for less gravy over the rice. One ladle is about 50 calories.
  • Peel the skin off the duck slices. Braised duck skin is soft, so it comes off easily, and it is about 40 calories per plate.
  • Order the duck with a bowl of kway teow soup instead of rice. The soup version is around 420 calories.
  • Add a side of braised tau kwa or the preserved vegetable. Both are under 40 calories and stretch the meal.

Common questions

Is braised duck rice healthy?
It is a fair hawker plate. About 604 calories with 30g of protein and 21g of fat. The braising removes some of the duck fat, so it is lighter than roast duck rice at around 700.
Is duck higher in calories than chicken?
Per 100g with skin, yes. Braised duck is about 200 calories per 100g against 170 for soy chicken breast with skin. Skin off, the two are close, around 130 to 140 per 100g.
How many calories in yam rice?
About 300 calories for a 220g hawker scoop. Plain white rice is about 260 for the same weight. The difference is the shallot oil and dried shrimp it is cooked with.
How many calories in braised duck without rice?
About 200 calories for a 100g portion of duck with skin, plus around 50 for the gravy. With the braised egg and tau kwa it is roughly 310, which works as a meal with a plate of vegetables.
Berapa kalori dalam Braised Duck Rice?
Braised Duck Rice ada kira-kira 604 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (380g), dengan 30g protein, 72g karbohidrat dan 21g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
