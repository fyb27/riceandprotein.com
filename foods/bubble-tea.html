How Many Calories in Bubble Tea (Pearl Milk Tea)?

Milk tea plus chewy tapioca pearls and a lot of sugar, easily 380 calories a cup.

380kcal / 1 medium (500ml)
A medium 500ml bubble tea with full sugar and pearls lands around 380 calories. The pearls alone are about 150 of that, and the sugar syrup adds another 150 or so before the milk and creamer even come in. Push it to a large with extra toppings and you are at 500 plus.

The real talk

Bro, bubble tea is not a drink, it is a dessert you sip through a fat straw. The tea part is almost nothing. It is the pearls and the sugar that do all the damage. Those chewy pearls are pure starch soaked in syrup, so a normal scoop is 150 calories just sitting at the bottom of your cup.

Then they ask you sugar level and most people just say normal, which is full sugar lah. Full sugar plus pearls plus non dairy creamer and you drank a small meal. One a day, every day, that is 2660 calories a week you did not chew. No wonder the weight does not move.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order 30 percent sugar or less, the tea still tastes sweet trust me
  • Skip the pearls or swap to grass jelly, saves you 100 plus calories
  • Go small instead of large, that alone cuts off 100 to 150 calories

Common questions

How many calories in a bubble tea?
A medium 500ml with pearls and full sugar is around 380 calories. Small with less sugar can drop to 150, large with extra toppings climbs past 500.
Are the pearls the worst part?
Pretty much. The tapioca pearls add about 150 calories on their own, all starch. Cut the pearls and you cut the biggest chunk.
Can I drink bubble tea and still lose weight?
Yes, if you fit it in your budget. Go small, 30 percent sugar, no pearls and it is just a treat, not a meal you drank by accident.
Berapa kalori dalam Bubble Tea?
Bubble Tea ada kira-kira 380 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (500g), dengan 5g protein, 70g karbohidrat dan 9g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
