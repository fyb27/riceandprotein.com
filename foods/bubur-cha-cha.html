How Many Calories in Bubur Cha Cha?

Also known as Bubor Cha Cha.

Sweet potato and yam cubes with sago bobbing around in pandan-scented coconut milk, served warm or cold. One of those desserts that tastes like your grandma made it.

360kcal / 1 bowl
One bowl of Bubur Cha Cha (around 300g) comes in at roughly 360 calories. That's not outrageous for a dessert, but it's not light either. The coconut milk broth alone contributes about 120 calories, and then gula melaka adds another 95. So nearly 60% of your calories are just from the liquid before you even get to the sweet potato and yam. Still, 360 calories for a full bowl that genuinely satisfies a sweet craving is pretty decent compared to, say, a slice of cake or a bubble tea.

The real talk

360 calories per bowl is honestly fair for a dessert, but know what you're paying for. The gula melaka and santan together are about 215 calories before the solid stuff even touches your lips. The sweet potato (52 cal), yam (55 cal), and sago (38 cal) are the innocent bystanders here. Carbs are high at 62g, fat is moderate at 12g, and protein is basically nothing at 3g. Worth flagging that these numbers are a ballpark estimate because hard nutrition data for hawker Bubur Cha Cha is genuinely thin. Different stalls vary a lot in how heavy-handed they are with the coconut milk and sugar. Take the 360 as a reasonable middle-ground figure, not gospel.

What actually swings the number is how much santan they pour. A kopitiam version that's thick and creamy can push you to 430 calories no problem, while a lighter street stall version might land closer to 300. If you're served extra gravy (the broth part), you're adding roughly 10 to 15 calories per extra 10g of that liquid. The sago and tapioca jelly are basically pure carbs but they're not the main offender. Cold versus warm makes zero difference to your calorie count, so don't kid yourself that ais version is the diet option, bro. If you want to actually reduce it, the only real lever is asking for less gravy.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less broth. The coconut milk gravy is 120 calories for 130g. Getting half a ladle less saves you 40 to 50 calories immediately.
  • Share a bowl. At 360 calories it is not tragic, but half a bowl as a dessert after a meal is a smarter call than a full one on top of nasi kandar.
  • Eat the warm version slowly. It cools down, you eat less fast, you feel full sooner. Cold iced versions go down way too quick.
  • Skip the extra gula melaka drizzle that some stalls offer on top. That's another 30 to 40 calories you don't need.
  • Count it as your dessert for the day, not a snack between meals. It's sweet enough to genuinely satisfy a craving so treat it like that and move on.

Common questions

How many calories in Bubur Cha Cha?
A standard bowl of Bubur Cha Cha (about 300g) has around 360 calories. The coconut milk and gula melaka account for most of it, roughly 215 calories combined.
Is Bubur Cha Cha fattening?
It is not the worst dessert choice at 360 calories a bowl, but it has very little protein and about 62g of carbs, so it does not keep you full for long. If you are counting calories, one bowl is fine as your dessert for the day. Two bowls lah, that's when it becomes a problem.
Is the cold or warm version of Bubur Cha Cha lower in calories?
Neither. The calories are identical at 360. Serving it over ice does not change what's in the bowl. The ice just dilutes the broth slightly as it melts, so if anything the iced version might taste a bit lighter but the calorie content is the same.
Berapa kalori dalam Bubur Cha Cha?
Bubur Cha Cha ada kira-kira 360 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (300g), dengan 3g protein, 62g karbohidrat dan 12g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
