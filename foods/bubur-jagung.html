How Many Calories in Bubur Jagung?

Bubur Jagung is a warm sweet corn porridge cooked in coconut milk with sago pearls and sugar, a common dessert at pasar malam stalls and kopitiams across Malaysia.

360kcal / 1 bowl (hawker / kopitiam standard)
A standard hawker bowl of Bubur Jagung (280g) has about 360 calories. The corn itself is not the issue, only 70 calories worth. The real story is the 80g of full-fat coconut milk at 185 calories and another 97 calories from sugar, whether white sugar or gula Melaka. Add the sago pearls and you are already at 360 before you even pick up the spoon.

The real talk

Bubur Jagung is basically sweet corn swimming in santan and sugar. It is a warm dessert porridge, very common at pasar malam and kopitiam dessert stalls, and it looks totally harmless in the bowl. But 360 calories for a dessert portion is not nothing. To be upfront, the numbers here are a ballpark estimate because solid lab data for Malaysian hawker bubur jagung is thin. What we know for certain is that the fat comes almost entirely from the coconut milk (17g fat, mostly saturated) and the carbs come from corn, sago, and sugar stacked together. If you are someone trying to lose weight and you finish your nasi lemak and then bowl this down as an afterthought, that is an extra meal worth of calories you probably did not account for.

The version you get matters a lot. A stall using diluted or low-fat coconut milk drops the whole thing to around 220 calories. A generous pasar malam bowl with extra santan and gula Melaka can push past 450 calories. If the stall adds cubed sweet potato (ubi keledek), that is another 40 to 50 calories on top. The sago pearls themselves are pretty innocent at only 30 calories for 30g. Portion size is the other lever since most stalls just ladle freely, and a generous pour can easily be 320g to 340g instead of the standard 280g.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask the stall if they do a lighter version with diluted or low-fat santan. Saves roughly 100 to 140 calories right there.
  • Skip the add-ons. No sweet potato, no extra sago, no second scoop of gula Melaka drizzle on top.
  • Treat it as your dessert only, not a side dish on top of a full meal. At 360 calories it is a meaningful chunk of your day.
  • Split a bowl with someone. Pasar malam portions are generous and half a bowl still satisfies the craving.
  • Go earlier in the evening when the santan is less concentrated. The longer it sits on the burner, the more the water evaporates and the richer it gets.

Common questions

How many calories in Bubur Jagung?
A standard hawker bowl of Bubur Jagung (280g) has about 360 calories. The coconut milk alone contributes 185 of those calories, with sugar adding another 97.
What exactly is Bubur Jagung?
It is a warm Malaysian dessert porridge made from sweet corn kernels cooked in full-fat coconut milk with white sugar or gula Melaka and sago pearls. You will find it at pasar malam stalls and kopitiam dessert corners, usually served warm in a bowl.
Is Bubur Jagung healthy?
The corn and sago themselves are fine. The issue is the full-fat santan and sugar, which push the calorie count to 360 per bowl with 17g of fat. It is not a diet food but having it occasionally as a proper dessert rather than an everyday snack is perfectly reasonable.
Berapa kalori dalam Bubur Jagung?
Bubur Jagung ada kira-kira 360 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (280g), dengan 4g protein, 50g karbohidrat dan 17g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
