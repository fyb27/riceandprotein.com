How Many Calories in Bubur Lambuk?

A Ramadan spiced rice porridge cooked with meat, coconut milk and aromatics, most famous from mosque free distributions every evening.

350kcal / 1 bowl
One bowl of Bubur Lambuk (350g) comes in at around 350 calories. That's for the standard mosque-distribution style with minced beef, dried shrimp, moderate coconut milk and the usual spices. It's not a low-calorie food but it's not a calorie bomb either, sitting somewhere in the middle compared to other Malaysian one-bowl meals.

The real talk

350 calories for a bowl of bubur lambuk is honestly pretty reasonable for a full one-dish meal, bro. The rice base is only 150 calories because it's been cooked down into porridge with a ton of water, so you're getting a lot of volume for not that many carbs. The calories actually come mostly from the coconut milk (60 calories in just 40ml) and the cooking oil or ghee with the aromatics (55 calories worth). The meat at 65 calories is the most useful part nutritionally, giving you 18g protein which is solid for a porridge. Fair warning though: hard data on bubur lambuk is thin, so treat this as a ballpark estimate. The mosque recipe varies every year depending on who's cooking, and the quantities of santan and ghee are not exactly standardized.

What really changes the maths is how heavy-handed the cook is with the santan and ghee. The mosque version at 350 calories uses moderate coconut milk. A restaurant version with full santan and generous ghee jumps to around 430 calories, same bowl size, just creamier. On the lighter end, the vegan version with minimal coconut milk and no meat drops to about 220 calories, which is genuinely low for a filling bowl of porridge. If you're collecting your free bubur at the masjid during Ramadan and you want to keep it sensible, the portion size matters too. A smaller bowl at 250g is probably closer to 250 calories. The gravy at the bottom of the container is where most of the fat hides, so if you're watching it, go easy on ladling that last bit out.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less gravy at the bottom of the pot, that's where the coconut milk and ghee concentrate
  • Go for the chicken version over beef if you have a choice, saves you about 50 calories a bowl
  • Stick to one bowl and pair with plain vegetables rather than a second helping
  • Skip the crackers or keropok on the side, those add 80 to 120 calories real fast
  • If you're making it at home, swap half the coconut milk for low-fat evaporated milk and cut ghee to 5g instead of 10g

Common questions

How many calories in Bubur Lambuk?
A standard 350g bowl of Bubur Lambuk has around 350 calories. That's the typical mosque-style version with minced beef, moderate coconut milk and spices. A lighter chicken version is closer to 300 calories, and a rich restaurant version with more santan can reach 430 calories.
Is Bubur Lambuk healthy or fattening?
It's actually one of the more balanced Ramadan foods lah. You get 18g of protein from the meat, a reasonable 40g of carbs from the porridge base, and the volume of the bowl keeps you full. The fat (12g) mostly comes from coconut milk and ghee. Compared to something like nasi lemak or murtabak, it's pretty manageable if you don't go back for a second bowl.
Why is Bubur Lambuk so popular during Ramadan?
Mosques all over Malaysia distribute it free every evening during Ramadan, especially Masjid Kampung Baru in KL which has been doing it for decades. It's a tradition more than anything. Nutritionally it works well too because it's easy to digest after a long fast, and the rice porridge base sits comfortably in your stomach without wrecking you before Terawih prayers.
Berapa kalori dalam Bubur Lambuk?
Bubur Lambuk ada kira-kira 350 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (350g), dengan 18g protein, 40g karbohidrat dan 12g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
