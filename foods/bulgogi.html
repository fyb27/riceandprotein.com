How Many Calories in Bulgogi?

Also known as 불고기, Korean Marinated Beef, Beef Bulgogi, So-bulgogi.

Bulgogi is thin-sliced beef marinated in soy sauce, sugar, pear and garlic, then grilled or pan-cooked with onions and mushrooms. It is one of the leaner dishes on a Korean barbecue menu.

330kcal / 1 serving (200g cooked, no rice)
A 200g serving of beef bulgogi is about 330 calories, using the fatsecret.kr figure of 163 kcal per 100g for cooked so-bulgogi. It gives you around 34g of protein and 15g of fat. Add a 210g bowl of rice and the meal lands near 630 calories.

The real talk

This is the dish to order when everyone else is ordering samgyeopsal. Bulgogi uses sirloin or chuck sliced paper thin, so 200g of cooked meat is 330 calories, roughly half of what the same weight of pork belly costs you. The 34g of protein per serving is real, not marketing.

The marinade is where the sugar hides. A typical bulgogi sauce is soy, sugar, grated pear, garlic and sesame oil, and 30g of it per serving adds about 55 calories and 10g of sugar. Ttukbaegi bulgogi, the brothy version served bubbling in a stone bowl with glass noodles, comes in at 320 to 380 calories per serving with the noodles included.

The catch is that bulgogi is almost always eaten with rice, and the sweet juices make rice go fast. One bowl of rice is 300 calories. Two bowls, which the sauce practically demands, turns a 330 calorie dish into a 930 calorie meal. A bulgogi lunch set at 10,000 to 13,000 won typically lands at about 700 with rice and side dishes.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it with one bowl of rice and stop there. Rice is the swing factor, 300 calories per bowl.
  • Pile on the onions, mushrooms and scallions from the pan. They are cooked in the same sauce and add about 20 calories per handful.
  • Choose beef bulgogi over pork bulgogi (돼지불고기). Pork bulgogi is about 185 kcal per 100g and usually has a gochujang sauce with more sugar.
  • Ask for less sauce, or leave the pooled sauce on the plate. That is most of the 12g of carbs.
  • Wrap it in lettuce like samgyeopsal. Same fullness, fewer spoonfuls of rice.

Common questions

How many calories in bulgogi with rice?
About 630 for a 200g serving of bulgogi plus a 210g bowl of rice. The rice is nearly half the total.
Is bulgogi healthy for weight loss?
Yes, as Korean barbecue goes. 330 calories and 34g of protein per 200g serving. The only thing to manage is the rice you eat with it.
How much sugar is in bulgogi?
Roughly 10g per serving from the marinade. That is about 2.5 teaspoons, most of it from sugar and pear.
Is pork bulgogi higher in calories than beef?
Yes. Pork bulgogi is around 185 kcal per 100g versus 163 for beef, and the gochujang-based sauce adds more sugar.
