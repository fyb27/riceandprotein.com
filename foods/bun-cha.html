How Many Calories in Bun Cha?

Also known as Bún chả, Bun Cha Ha Noi, Hanoi Grilled Pork Noodles, Obama Noodles.

Charcoal grilled pork patties and slices of belly floating in a bowl of warm sweet and sour fish sauce, with a plate of bun noodles and a basket of herbs on the side. The Hanoi lunch that Obama and Bourdain made famous, 40,000 to 50,000 dong a set.

500kcal / 1 set
One set of bun cha is around 500 calories. The 200g of bun is 220, the 100g of grilled pork patties and belly is about 230, and the bowl of dipping sauce adds 40 once you count the sugar. Most people add a nem cua be, which is another 140.

The real talk

Bun cha sits at 500 calories for a standard set, and the split is roughly half noodles, half pork. The 200g plate of bun is 220 calories on its own. The pork is where the fat is: a normal quan gives you two or three patties of minced pork shoulder plus three or four slices of grilled belly, 100g in total and about 230 calories because belly is 30 percent fat and the patties are mixed with fat too.

The dipping bowl looks innocent because it is mostly liquid, but it is fish sauce, water, vinegar and around 10g of sugar, so 40 calories before you count the pork fat that drips into it off the grill. Most people drink or spoon the whole thing. The green papaya and carrot slices in it are the only free part.

The real number for most people is not 500. It is 640 to 780, because nem cua be (the square fried crab spring roll) is 140 calories each and quan sell them in pairs. A set with two nem and a second plate of bun, which the owner will bring without asking at a busy place, is over 850 calories for one lunch.

How to eat it lighter

  • Skip the nem cua be. Each one is about 140 calories, mostly from frying oil.
  • Ask for cha bam (patties) only and leave the belly slices. Saves around 80 calories.
  • Do not accept the free noodle refill. The first plate of 200g is already 220 calories.
  • Eat the herb basket: perilla, lettuce, mint, all free and they fill you up.
  • Dip rather than drown. Spoon a little sauce over the noodles instead of drinking the bowl.

Common questions

How many calories are in bun cha with nem?
About 640 with one nem cua be and 780 with two. The nem are deep fried and run 140 calories each, so they change the meal more than the pork does.
Is bun cha healthy?
It is a fair meal at 500 calories with 28g protein and a lot of fresh herbs. The downsides are the belly fat and the sugar in the sauce. Order patties only and it is a decent lunch.
What is the difference between bun cha and bun thit nuong?
Bun cha is the Hanoi version with pork served in a bowl of warm dipping sauce; bun thit nuong is the Saigon version with grilled pork on top of noodles and sauce poured over. Both land around 480 to 500 calories.
How much sugar is in the bun cha sauce?
A typical bowl uses about 10g of sugar per serving, roughly 40 calories, plus whatever fat drips in from the grilled pork.
