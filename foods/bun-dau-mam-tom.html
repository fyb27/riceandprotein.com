How Many Calories in Bun Dau Mam Tom?

Also known as Bún đậu mắm tôm, Tofu Vermicelli with Shrimp Paste, Bun Dau, Hanoi Tofu Platter.

A bamboo tray of pressed bun noodle cakes, golden deep fried tofu, boiled pork belly, cha com (green rice pork sausage), herbs and a bowl of fermented shrimp paste whipped with lime, sugar and hot oil. Hanoi's lunch on a plastic stool, 35,000 to 60,000 dong.

700kcal / 1 platter (met) for one
A standard single met of bun dau mam tom is around 700 calories. The 200g of bun is 220, the 90g of deep fried tofu is 210, the cha com is 110 and the boiled pork belly is 120, with the mam tom dip and its spoon of hot oil adding 30. A full met with lòng (offal), dồi (blood sausage) and extra cha com goes to 1,000.

The real talk

People think of bun dau as a light tofu lunch. It is 700 calories for one person, and the tofu is the problem, not the pork. Deep fried tofu absorbs oil: 90g of fried dau comes out at around 210 calories, more than the 50g of boiled pork belly at 120. The cha com, a pork sausage mixed with young green rice and then fried, is 110 for two pieces. The pressed bun is 220 for 200g.

The mam tom bowl is 30 to 40 calories, which is nothing, but it is mixed with a spoon of the hot oil from the tofu pan to make it foam. That is what you are tasting. Some quan add a full tablespoon, 90 calories, and if you dip every piece of tofu and bun you get all of it. The herbs, cucumber and perilla on the tray are the only free part.

The real order at most Hanoi quan is the met day du, the full tray, for two people to share. It adds lòng (boiled intestine), dồi (blood sausage), cha quay (fried pork) and sometimes a fried nem. Split in two, each person eats 900 to 1,000 calories. The quan will also refill the tofu without asking if the tray looks empty. Each refill of three cubes is about 100.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for dau it chien, tofu fried briefly, or half the tofu. Each fried cube is about 70 calories.
  • Order bun dau thit luoc only, tofu and boiled pork, and skip cha com and dồi. About 550.
  • Mix your mam tom without the oil. Ask for khong mo and squeeze extra lime instead.
  • Eat all the cucumber and herbs first. They fill you up for close to zero.
  • Do not accept the tofu refill. Three more cubes is another 100 calories.

Common questions

How many calories in bun dau mam tom?
About 700 for a standard single tray with tofu, pork belly and cha com. Published estimates range from 465 for a basic tray to 800 or more for the full version.
Is bun dau mam tom healthy?
It has good protein at 32g but is high in fat at 40g, almost all from the deep fried tofu and cha com. Ordering tofu and boiled pork only brings it to a reasonable 550.
How many calories in fried tofu?
About 230 per 100g once fried, versus 80 for the same weight of plain tofu. The oil absorbed during frying nearly triples it.
Does mam tom have calories?
The shrimp paste itself is about 10 calories a tablespoon. The sugar and the spoon of hot oil whipped into it add another 20 to 90 depending on the quan.
