How Many Calories in Bun Thit Nuong?

Also known as Bún thịt nướng, Grilled Pork Vermicelli, Vietnamese Grilled Pork Noodle Bowl.

A Saigon lunch bowl: cold bun vermicelli, lettuce and herbs underneath, charcoal grilled pork slices on top, finished with crushed peanuts, scallion oil, pickled carrot and a pour of sweet fish sauce. Usually 35,000 to 45,000 dong.

480kcal / 1 bowl
A standard bowl of bun thit nuong is around 480 calories. The 200g of bun is 215, the 80g of grilled pork is about 170, and the sauce, peanuts and mo hanh (scallion oil) add another 95 between them. Order it with a cha gio on top and the bowl is 620.

The real talk

The 480 calories in bun thit nuong is fairly evenly spread, which is why people underestimate it. Nothing on the bowl looks heavy. The bun is 215 for 200g. The pork is thin sliced shoulder marinated in sugar, fish sauce and lemongrass, then grilled, so 80g is about 170 with the sugar glaze. The mo hanh spoon on top is 30 to 40 on its own, the peanuts are 15, and the sweet nuoc cham you pour over is around 40.

The marinade is the hidden bit. Vietnamese grilled pork gets a lot of sugar or honey in the marinade, which is what makes it caramelise on the charcoal. A quan may use 10g of sugar per portion of meat, another 40 calories that does not show up as fat. Combined with the sauce, one bowl can carry 20g of sugar.

The standard order in most Saigon quan is bun thit nuong cha gio, with one or two fried spring rolls chopped on top. Each cha gio is 130 to 140 calories, so the popular version of this bowl is really 620 to 750, not 480. Add the second ladle of sauce that comes when the bowl gets dry and you are at a full dinner's worth.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it without cha gio. Each roll is about 135 calories of oil and wrapper.
  • Ask them to hold the mo hanh. The scallion oil is 30 to 40 calories a spoon.
  • Pour half the nuoc cham and taste before adding more. Most of the sugar is in the sauce.
  • Ask for extra rau (herbs and lettuce). It is free and makes the bowl bigger.
  • Extra pork is a better upgrade than extra bun: 40g more pork is 85 calories and adds 10g of protein.

Common questions

How many calories in bun thit nuong cha gio?
About 620 with one cha gio and 750 with two. The spring rolls are the biggest single add-on at roughly 135 calories each.
Is bun thit nuong healthy?
It is a decent balanced bowl at 480 calories with 24g of protein and a lot of raw vegetables. The sugar in the marinade and sauce is the main downside, around 20g per bowl.
Bun thit nuong vs bun cha, which is lighter?
They are close: about 480 for bun thit nuong and 500 for bun cha. Bun cha has more fat from the belly slices; bun thit nuong has more sugar from the marinade and sauce.
