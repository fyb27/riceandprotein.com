How Many Calories in Bungeoppang?

Also known as 붕어빵, Korean Fish-Shaped Pastry, Bungeo-ppang, Red Bean Fish Bread, 잉어빵.

Bungeoppang is a fish-shaped waffle filled with sweet red bean paste, baked in a cast iron mould at street carts through winter. Sold 3 for 2,000 won, or 2 for 1,000 if you find an old cart.

110kcal / 1 piece
One bungeoppang is about 110 calories for a 60g piece, per fatsecret.kr, with around 20g of carbs. The batter is roughly 75 calories and the red bean filling 35. A standard bag of 3 is 330, which is more than most people expect from something the size of a hand.

The real talk

This is one of the lighter street snacks in Korea because it is baked in a mould, not fried. The batter is flour, sugar, egg and milk, about 75 calories per fish, and the 20g of red bean paste is 35. At 110 per piece it is less than half a hotteok. The problem is only that nobody buys one.

The bag of 3 is 330 calories, the same as a bowl of tteokbokki, and the fresh ones go down in about 2 minutes. The cream custard version (슈크림 붕어빵) is a little higher at around 130 because the filling has butter and egg yolk, and the ingeoppang (잉어빵), the shinier cousin with oil in the batter, runs about 150 per piece.

Convenience store and frozen bungeoppang are heavier. Ottogi's frozen red bean version is 165 for a 60g piece because the batter is richer, and Orion's snack pack fish is 121 for just 29g. If you want the 110 calorie number, it has to come off a cart.

How to eat it lighter

  • Buy 3, eat 1 now and give 2 away. 110 calories is a snack; 330 is a meal's worth of sugar.
  • Red bean over cream. Red bean is about 110 per piece, custard cream 130.
  • Skip the ingeoppang carts. The oil in the batter pushes it to about 150 each.
  • Eat it with unsweetened tea or coffee instead of a sweet drink. The fish already has 10g of sugar in it.
  • Pair one with a skewer of eomuk (100 calories) for a 210 calorie street snack with some protein.

Common questions

How many calories in 3 bungeoppang?
About 330 for the standard bag of 3 red bean fish, roughly 110 each.
Is bungeoppang healthier than hotteok?
Yes, by a wide margin. It is baked, not fried: about 110 per piece versus 280 for a hotteok.
What is the difference between bungeoppang and ingeoppang?
Ingeoppang has oil in the batter for a crisp, shiny skin and a thinner shell. That takes it from about 110 to 150 calories per piece.
How much sugar is in a bungeoppang?
Around 10g per piece, from the sweetened red bean paste and the batter. Three pieces is about 30g, close to the daily added sugar limit.
