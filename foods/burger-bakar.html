How Many Calories in Burger Bakar?

One loaded burger bakar is about 720 calories. Grilled patty, but the cheese, egg and sauce stack up.

720kcal / 1 burger
A loaded burger bakar (the tall grilled-burger-stall style) is around 720 calories. You get a fat flame-grilled beef patty, a slice or two of cheese, often an egg, plenty of special sauce and a soft toasted bun. That works out to roughly 35g protein, 48g carbs and 42g fat. The grilling helps a bit, but the loaded toppings pile it back on.

The real talk

Burger bakar sounds healthier than Ramly because bakar means grilled, and the patty is genuinely flame-grilled instead of swimming in margarine. But these stalls stack them tall on purpose. You get a thick patty, double cheese, an egg, sometimes beef bacon, all glued together with a heavy special sauce.

So you start with a decent grilled patty and then bury it. A basic single is around 500, but the loaded special with cheese and egg easily hits 720, and the monster double-patty ones the popular stalls sell can clear 900. The grill saves you some oil, the toppings give it all back lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order the basic single patty, no egg, single cheese. The grilled patty on its own is the reasonable part.
  • Skip or halve the special sauce. It is mayo-based and they are not shy with it.
  • Drop the egg add-on. It is an easy 90 calories you will not miss on an already loaded burger.

Common questions

How many calories in a burger bakar?
A loaded burger bakar is around 720 calories. The grilled patty is reasonable, but the cheese, egg and special sauce stack it up. A basic single is closer to 500.
Is burger bakar healthier than a Ramly burger?
The patty is, since it is flame-grilled instead of fried in margarine. But burger bakar stalls load on more cheese, egg and sauce, so the loaded ones end up just as heavy or heavier.
How do I order a lighter burger bakar?
Get the basic single patty, single cheese, no egg, and go easy on the special sauce. That keeps it around 500 instead of 720 or more.
Berapa kalori dalam Burger Bakar?
Burger Bakar ada kira-kira 720 kalori untuk 1 biji burger (320g), dengan 35g protein, 48g karbohidrat dan 42g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
