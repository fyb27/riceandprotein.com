How Many Calories in Caldereta?

Also known as Kaldereta, Beef Caldereta, Kalderetang Baka, Kalderetang Kambing.

Beef (or goat) braised in tomato sauce with liver spread, bell peppers, potatoes, carrots, olives and often cheese. The fiesta stew that shows up at every handaan and most carinderias.

390kcal / 1 carinderia serving (about 200g)
One carinderia serving of beef caldereta is about 390 calories for a 200g ladle with meat, vegetables and sauce. The beef is about 230 calories, the sauce with liver spread and cheese about 120, and the potato and carrot pieces about 40. Kawaling Pinoy's home version is 448 per serving.

The real talk

Caldereta looks like a tomato stew but the sauce is richer than tomato. The base is tomato sauce plus a can of liver spread (about 60 calories per tablespoon) plus, in most Tagalog versions, a handful of grated cheese and sometimes peanut butter. About 80g of sauce per serving is 120 calories. The beef is usually brisket or shank with some fat, about 90g cooked per scoop at 230 calories. Kawaling Pinoy's card with bottom round lands at 448 per serving; Panlasang Pinoy's with short ribs hits 610.

The cut of beef swings it by 150 calories. Shank or round is about 200 calories per 100g cooked, short ribs or brisket with fat closer to 350. Carinderias tend to use whatever is cheap, which is usually fattier. Goat caldereta (kalderetang kambing) is a bit leaner at about 340 per serving, and the pork version made with belly is the heaviest at about 480.

A serving costs 70 to 100 pesos, more than most ulam, so people do not double up on it. With 1 cup of rice it is about 650 calories, which is a normal lunch. The potato and carrot in the sauce count for something: a serving gets about 40g of them at 40 calories. The olives and bell pepper are close to zero, so they are the parts you can eat freely.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for more sauce vegetables and less meat if the cut is fatty. Potato, carrot and bell pepper are about 1 calorie per gram.
  • Skim the orange oil that pools on top. That layer is rendered beef fat and about 50 to 80 calories per serving.
  • Goat caldereta is leaner than beef if the carinderia has it, about 340 per serving.
  • One cup of rice. The sauce is thick enough that half a cup goes a long way.

Common questions

How many calories in caldereta with rice?
About 650 calories for a standard 200g serving with 1 cup of rice. With 2 cups it is around 910.
Is caldereta healthy?
It has about 28g of protein per serving and some vegetables, but the liver spread and cheese make the sauce about 120 calories per ladle. Moderate, not light.
What makes caldereta different from mechado and afritada?
The liver spread and cheese. Afritada is plain tomato sauce, about 300 per serving. Mechado adds soy and a fat strip through the beef, about 350. Caldereta is the richest at about 390.
Beef or goat caldereta, which is lower in calories?
Goat, at about 340 per serving versus 390 for beef. Goat meat is about 140 calories per 100g cooked, beef with fat closer to 250.
