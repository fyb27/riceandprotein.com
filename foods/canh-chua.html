How Many Calories in Canh Chua?

Also known as Canh chua cá, Vietnamese Sour Soup, Tamarind Fish Soup, Canh Chua Ca Loc.

A sweet and sour tamarind broth with chunks of fish, pineapple, tomato, okra, elephant ear stem and bean sprouts, finished with fried garlic and rice paddy herb. Southern Vietnam's everyday soup, served next to a bowl of rice and a clay pot of ca kho.

150kcal / 1 bowl
A bowl of canh chua ca is around 150 calories for 350ml with a 60g piece of fish. The fish is about 65, the pineapple, tomato and vegetables about 30, the tamarind broth with its sugar about 30, and the spoon of fried garlic oil on top 25. Without the fish, a ladle of the soup is about 60.

The real talk

Canh chua is the lowest calorie thing on a Vietnamese table that still tastes like a meal. A full bowl is 150 calories. The broth is water, tamarind, fish sauce and about a teaspoon of sugar per bowl, so 30 calories. The vegetables, 100g of pineapple, tomato, okra and bac ha stem, are another 30. The piece of fish, usually ca loc (snakehead) or ca dieu hong (tilapia), is 65 for 60g and it is lean.

The published range is 90 to 180 per bowl and the fish is the variable. Canh chua with ca hu or ca basa, the fatty catfish cuts with the belly on, is 250 to 280 because catfish belly is 15 percent fat. With prawns it is about 120. Vegetarian canh chua with tofu is 100. The fried garlic on top is the one thing to watch: a teaspoon of toi phi in oil is 25 calories and some quan use a tablespoon.

At a com binh dan quan the soup comes as part of a set: a plate of rice at 300, a clay pot of ca kho at 300, and canh chua at 150. The soup is the part you should eat more of. Ask for a second bowl and the meal is 900 calories with an extra 14g of protein and a lot more volume, versus adding a second helping of rice at 150 for nothing.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick ca loc or prawn over ca basa. The catfish belly version is nearly double.
  • Ask for it toi phi, less fried garlic. Each spoon of the oil is about 25.
  • Have a second bowl of canh instead of a second plate of rice. 150 calories versus 150 for rice, but with protein and fibre.
  • Eat the pineapple and okra. Most people leave them and they are the fibre.
  • Drink the broth. It is 30 calories and mostly water; the sourness kills appetite for a while.

Common questions

How many calories in canh chua ca?
About 150 for a bowl with a piece of lean fish. The published range is 90 to 180; catfish belly versions go higher, around 270.
Is canh chua healthy?
Yes. It is low calorie, low fat, has 14g of protein per bowl and a lot of vegetables. The only additions to watch are the sugar in the broth and the fried garlic oil.
Does canh chua have sugar?
About a teaspoon per bowl, 15 to 20 calories, to balance the tamarind. Some quan use more; if it tastes noticeably sweet it is probably two.
Can I eat canh chua for weight loss?
It is one of the best dishes for it. A bowl of canh chua and a small plate of rice is about 450 calories with 20g of protein and a lot of volume.
