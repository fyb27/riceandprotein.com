How Many Calories in Cart Noodles (Che Zai Min)?

Also known as 車仔麵, Che Zai Min, Cart Noodle, Hong Kong Cart Noodles.

Cart noodles are the build-your-own bowl of Hong Kong: pick a noodle, a soup base and three or four toppings from a wall of choices at the counter. About HK$35 to HK$50 depending on toppings.

570kcal / 1 bowl (oil noodles, brisket, pork blood, choy sum, curry soup)
A typical bowl of cart noodles with oil noodles, beef brisket, pork blood, choy sum and curry soup is around 570 calories. The same bowl built with rice vermicelli, fish balls, tofu and clear soup is about 360. A heavy build with pork skin, sausage, fried bean curd and satay soup reaches 850.

The real talk

Cart noodles are the only Hong Kong meal where you set the calorie count yourself at the counter. The noodle choice alone swings 100 calories: rice vermicelli is about 175 for a serving, oil noodles 220, instant noodles (出前一丁) 280 and up. Then the soup base adds anywhere from 40 for clear broth to 130 for satay. Before a single topping goes in, the bowl is somewhere between 215 and 410.

Toppings are where people lose track. Pork skin looks harmless but it is about 230 calories a portion because it is fried before it is braised. Beef brisket is 200, sausage 210, fried fish skin 140, pork intestine 180. On the other side, pork blood is 65, braised tofu 75, fish balls 95, white radish under 30. Three heavy toppings versus three light ones is a 400 calorie difference in the same bowl.

The 570 calorie bowl above is what most people actually order: oil noodles, brisket, something cheap like pork blood, a green, curry soup. It is a decent meal with 28g of protein. The danger bowl is the one built by hunger at 11pm in Sham Shui Po: instant noodles, satay soup, pork skin, sausage, fried bean curd. That is 850 calories and around 45g of fat, with less protein than the lighter bowl.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick rice vermicelli or flat rice noodles over oil noodles or instant noodles. Saves 50 to 100 calories.
  • Clear soup or brisket gravy (腩汁) over curry or satay. Saves up to 90 calories.
  • Choose two lean toppings (brisket, tofu, fish balls, pork blood, radish) instead of pork skin, sausage or fried bean curd.
  • Always add a green. Choy sum or lettuce is under 20 calories and makes the bowl bigger.
  • Leave half the soup. Satay and curry bases can carry over 1,200mg of sodium a bowl.

Common questions

How many calories in a bowl of cart noodles?
Anywhere from about 280 to 850 depending on your choices. A typical bowl with oil noodles, brisket, curry soup and two other toppings is around 570.
Which cart noodle topping has the most calories?
Pork skin, at about 230 calories a portion, because it is deep fried before braising. Sausage (210) and brisket (200) are close behind.
Which soup base is lowest in calories?
Clear broth at about 40 calories a bowl. Brisket gravy is around 70, curry 80 to 150 and satay 100 to 180.
