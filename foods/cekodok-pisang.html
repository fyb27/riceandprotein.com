How Many Calories in Cekodok Pisang (Jemput-Jemput)?

3 banana fritter balls land near 225 kcal. Sweet, fried, and you never stop at three.

225kcal / 3 pieces
Three pieces of cekodok pisang sit around 225 calories, so roughly 75 each. It is overripe banana mashed with flour and sugar, then deep fried into balls. The banana is fine, it is the flour, sugar, and frying oil stacking up that makes it add fast. Great with teh, just count how many you actually eat.

The real talk

Cekodok pisang is dangerous because it is tea time food, you eat it standing up not even sitting down. Each ball is small, maybe 75 calories, so your brain says no harm. Then you look down and the plate is empty and you ate six.

Six pieces is 450 calories of basically fried sweet dough, and you did not even feel full. If you want the banana fix, eat three with your teh and stop, or just eat an actual banana for 100 calories.

How to eat it lighter

  • Make them smaller so 3 pieces is genuinely 3 small balls, not 3 fistfuls
  • Use less sugar in the batter since ripe banana is already sweet
  • Swap to plain steamed or grilled banana when you just want the banana taste

Common questions

How many calories in one cekodok pisang?
Around 75 calories per ball for a typical small size. Bigger ones from the stall can hit 90 plus.
Is cekodok pisang healthy?
It has real banana so some potassium and fibre, but it is fried and sugared, so treat it as a snack not a health food. Three is a reasonable portion.
What is the difference between cekodok and cucur pisang?
Cekodok is rolled into balls, cucur pisang is usually flatter with sliced banana. Calories are similar per gram since both are fried.
Berapa kalori dalam Cekodok Pisang?
Cekodok Pisang ada kira-kira 225 kalori untuk 3 keping (105g), dengan 3g protein, 35g karbohidrat dan 9g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
