How Many Calories in Cha Yen?

Also known as ชาเย็น, Thai Iced Tea, Cha Thai, Thai Milk Tea, ชาไทย.

Strong black tea brewed with star anise and food colouring, poured over ice with condensed milk, evaporated milk and sugar. The orange drink in the plastic bag at every cart in Thailand, 25 to 40 baht.

300kcal / 1 glass (16 oz)
A standard 16 oz (450ml) glass of cha yen is about 300 calories, with 30 to 40g of sugar. The tea itself is zero. Almost everything comes from the 40g of condensed milk, the 40g of evaporated milk and the 2 tablespoons of sugar the vendor adds before the ice goes in.

The real talk

Cha yen is a dessert that people drink like water. The black tea is free, the ice is free, and then the vendor pours 3 things into it that are anything but. Condensed milk is 130 calories per 40g pour, evaporated milk is another 55, and the sugar syrup is 100. That is 285 calories for a drink that takes 4 minutes to finish and does nothing for hunger. Two a day, which is normal in the hot season, is 600 calories, the same as a plate of khao kha moo.

The Department of Health measured street versions with 13 teaspoons of sugar in one cup, which is why their per cup figure runs higher than most tables. Dietitian figures for a 16 oz glass land between 250 and 350 depending on the shop. The 22 oz size that chains like ChaTraMue sell is closer to 420, and adding pearls pushes it past 450. The colour tells you nothing. A pale glass just means more evaporated milk, not less sugar.

The fix is not to give it up. Cha dam yen, the same tea with sugar but no milk, is about 120 calories. Ordering wan noi (less sweet) drops a standard glass to about 200. The difference between a daily 300 and a daily 200 is 36,500 calories a year, which is roughly 4 to 5kg of body fat for changing 2 words at the counter.

How to eat it lighter

  • Say wan noi (less sweet). Most carts halve the sugar syrup and it saves around 100 calories a glass.
  • Cha dam yen, black tea with ice and no milk, is about 120 calories and still tastes like the real thing.
  • Skip the pearls and jelly. Toppings add 100 to 150 calories to a drink that already has 300.
  • Get the 16 oz, not the 22 oz. The big cup is 420 calories and you will not feel the difference in thirst.
  • Ask for less condensed milk (nom khon noi). That single pour is 130 calories on its own.

Common questions

How much sugar is in Thai iced tea?
About 30 to 40g in a 16 oz glass, which is 8 to 10 teaspoons. Street versions measured by the Department of Health went as high as 13 teaspoons.
Is cha yen worse than a soft drink?
A 325ml can of cola is 140 calories. A 16 oz cha yen is about 300, so yes, roughly double, because it has fat from the milk on top of the sugar.
Can I drink cha yen on a diet?
One less sweet glass at 200 calories fits inside most days. Two full sugar glasses at 600 is a meal you did not chew.
How many calories in ChaTraMue Thai milk tea?
The standard 22 oz cup is roughly 400 to 420 calories. Their smaller 16 oz is about 300, in line with any cart.
