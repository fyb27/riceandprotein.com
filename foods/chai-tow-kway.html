How Many Calories in Chai Tow Kway (Carrot Cake)?

Also known as Fried Carrot Cake, Singapore Carrot Cake, 菜头粿, Chye Tow Kway, Black Carrot Cake, White Carrot Cake.

Chai tow kway is the Singapore hawker dish of steamed radish cake cut into cubes and fried on a flat griddle with egg, garlic, chye poh and chilli. White is fried plain, black is fried with sweet dark soy.

493kcal / 1 plate
A standard hawker plate of chai tow kway is about 493 calories for a 295g plate, with around 28 to 35g of fat. The white version is about 403 calories and the black version with sweet dark soy is about 585. The radish cake itself is only 260 of that. The oil and egg are the rest.

The real talk

There is no carrot in it. Chai tow means white radish, and the cake is radish and rice flour steamed into a block, which is about 130 calories per 100g before frying. 200g of that is 260 calories. Then it goes onto a griddle with a big ladle of oil, one and a half eggs, chye poh and garlic. The eggs add 110, the oil about 90, and the seasonings 35. That is the 493 figure from KKH, with 35g of fat, which is more fat than a plate of char kway teow.

Black versus white is a 180 calorie decision. White is fried in oil and egg, about 403. Black is the same plate with sweet dark soy sauce poured on and caramelised, about 585. The sweet soy is sugar syrup, and the stall pours a lot of it to get the colour. If you like black, ask for less sauce and you land somewhere near 500.

The crispy style, where the stall presses the cake flat and fries it until the edges are brown, takes on more oil than the soft style. Crispy white can reach the black number. The soft, wetter style with more egg and less time on the griddle is usually the lighter plate, even though it looks messier.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order white, not black. That is about 180 calories of sweet soy avoided.
  • Ask for less oil. The stall controls the ladle, and a light fry saves 50 to 80 calories.
  • Ask for extra egg. Egg is the protein on this plate, and one more egg is 75 calories that actually fills you.
  • Order the S$3 plate, not the S$5. The bigger plate is about 50 percent more cake and oil.
  • Skip the chilli if it is oily. Some stalls fry the chilli into the cake and it adds about 30 calories.

Common questions

Is chai tow kway healthy?
Not really. A standard plate is about 493 calories with 35g of fat and only 10g of protein. The radish cake itself is fine, the problem is the oil it is fried in and the sweet soy on the black version.
Is white or black carrot cake healthier?
White. It is about 403 calories against 585 for black. The difference is the sweet dark soy sauce, which is mostly sugar and adds around 180 calories to the plate.
Does Singapore carrot cake have carrot in it?
No. It is white radish, called chai tow in Hokkien, mixed with rice flour and steamed. The name is a translation quirk. A 100g piece of the steamed cake is about 130 calories.
How many calories in a small plate of carrot cake?
About 400 for a S$3 white plate and around 480 for a S$3 black plate. The S$4 to S$5 plates run 500 to 700 depending on colour and how much oil the stall uses.
Berapa kalori dalam Chai Tow Kway?
Chai Tow Kway ada kira-kira 493 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (295g), dengan 10g protein, 48g karbohidrat dan 28g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
