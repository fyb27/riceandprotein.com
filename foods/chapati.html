How Many Calories in Chapati?

Chapati is a wholemeal unleavened flatbread cooked on a griddle at the mamak, eaten with dhal or curry, and genuinely one of the lower-calorie bread options you can order.

200kcal / 1 piece (mamak stall)
One chapati at a mamak stall comes in at roughly 200 calories per piece (about 70g). That's for the standard version with a light brush of oil or ghee worked into the dough. By itself it's not heavy, but once you start pairing it with dhal or curry, the numbers climb fast, so keep that in mind.

The real talk

Chapati is basically a simple wholemeal flatbread, flour and water, cooked dry on a tawa (flat griddle) with minimal oil. Unlike roti canai which gets folded with a good amount of ghee and flipped till flaky, chapati is more straightforward and lighter. At 200 calories a piece with 7g protein and only 4g fat, it's genuinely one of the better bread choices at the mamak. Most of those calories come from the wholemeal atta dough itself (155 cal), and the rest from the light oil brushed on. To be honest, these numbers are a ballpark estimate because solid lab data for mamak-style chapati is thin. Different stalls use different amounts of oil, so treat 200 as a reasonable middle ground.

The thing that changes the maths fast is what you eat it with. Plain chapati with no oil is closer to 165 calories, which is actually quite decent. Add a serving of dhal (about 60g alongside) and you're at 260 calories total. Order it with meat curry and you're looking at around 300 calories. If you're cutting, skip the roti canai and order chapati with dhal instead. You save maybe 200-300 calories compared to a full roti canai order, and you still walk out full. Two pieces of chapati plus dhal is a solid mamak meal for under 600 calories lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it plain or ask for less oil. The dry version without oil brushed on drops to about 165 calories per piece.
  • Pair with dhal instead of meat curry. Dhal adds around 60 calories for a serving; gravy-heavy curries add 100 or more.
  • Stop at two pieces. It sounds obvious but at the mamak it's easy to keep tearing and dipping without counting.
  • Skip the teh tarik alongside if you're watching it. A sweetened teh tarik adds another 100-plus calories that sneak up on you.
  • Add a side of plain veggie sabzi if the stall has it. More volume, barely any extra calories.

Common questions

How many calories in Chapati?
One piece of chapati at a mamak stall is around 200 calories (70g serving). The plain version without oil is closer to 165 calories, and adding dhal brings the total to about 260 calories.
What exactly is chapati?
Chapati is a wholemeal unleavened flatbread, made from atta flour (whole wheat), water, and a little oil or ghee. It's cooked on a flat griddle called a tawa. No yeast, no layering, no deep frying. That's why it ends up lighter than roti canai or naan.
Is chapati a healthier choice than roti canai at the mamak?
Calorie-wise, yes. A plain roti canai is typically 300-plus calories with significantly more fat from the ghee used in folding. Chapati at 200 calories with 7g protein and wholemeal flour is the smarter pick if you're counting, especially when you factor in the higher fibre from the wholemeal atta.
Berapa kalori dalam Chapati?
Chapati ada kira-kira 200 kalori untuk 1 keping (70g), dengan 7g protein, 36g karbohidrat dan 4g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
