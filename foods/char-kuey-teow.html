How Many Calories in Char Kuey Teow?

Also known as CKT, Char Koay Teow, Kuey Teow Goreng, Char Kway Teow.

Flat rice noodles fried in a lot of oil and lard. Shiok, but one of the heaviest hawker plates going.

742kcal / 1 plate
A standard plate of char kuey teow is around 742 calories with 38g of fat. The noodles and prawns are not the issue. The oil and lard it is fried in are where almost half the calories come from.

The real talk

CKT is one of those dishes where the calorie count genuinely surprises people. It looks like just noodles and a few prawns. But that wok is loaded with oil, and traditionally lard, to get the smoky char that makes it taste so good. A single plate can carry 38g of fat. That is more fat than a McDonald's quarter pounder.

I am not telling you to never eat it bro. CKT is one of the best things on this earth. I am telling you to know what it is. This is a treat plate, not an everyday plate. If you eat it, make it the indulgent meal of your day and keep the rest light.

Some context on what 742 calories means. For most men cutting on around 1,800 to 2,000 calories a day, one plate of CKT is 40 percent of the entire day. To burn it off you are looking at roughly 90 minutes of brisk walking. That is the honest exchange rate on that smoky wok flavour.

The noodle itself is part of the problem. Flat rice noodles are basically sponges. They drink up whatever oil or lard is in the wok, which is exactly why they taste so good and exactly why 76g of carbs arrive coated in 38g of fat. The prawns and cockles you can see are honestly the healthiest thing on the plate.

Penang style with duck egg, extra lard bits and bigger portions runs even higher, push it to 800 plus. And if the uncle asks whether you want it "special", understand that special means more of everything, including the number on this page.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less oil (kurang minyak). Most uncles will do it if you ask before they cook.
  • Add extra egg or extra prawns for protein instead of asking for extra noodles.
  • Treat it as your one indulgent meal of the day, not a default lunch.
  • Share the plate. Half a CKT plus a bowl of clear soup is a genuinely decent lunch.
  • If it is CKT for lunch, make dinner protein and vegetables only. Plan the day around the plate.

Common questions

Why is char kuey teow so high in calories?
Almost half the calories come from the oil and lard it is fried in, not the noodles. A plate carries about 38g of fat, which is a lot for one dish.
Can I eat char kuey teow on a diet?
Occasionally, yes. Treat it as your one indulgent meal of the day and keep everything else light. Asking for less oil also cuts a real chunk of the calories.
How much protein is in char kuey teow?
About 22g, mostly from the egg, prawns and cockles. The protein to calorie ratio is poor though because of all the oil.
How many calories in char kuey teow without prawns or cockles?
Barely any different, maybe 30 to 50 calories less. The seafood was never the problem. The oil, lard and noodles carry almost all of the 742 calories.
Is char kuey teow worse than nasi lemak?
Per serving, yes. A standard CKT at 742 calories beats a plain nasi lemak bungkus at 556 by almost 200 calories, and with more than double the fat.
How often can I eat CKT while losing weight?
Once a week works fine if you plan for it. Keep the other two meals that day light and high protein, think soup and grilled lauk, and the week still balances out.
Berapa kalori dalam Char Kuey Teow?
Char Kuey Teow ada kira-kira 742 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (350g), dengan 22g protein, 76g karbohidrat dan 38g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
