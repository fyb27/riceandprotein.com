How Many Calories in Char Siew Bao?

The fluffy white steamed bun stuffed with sweet BBQ pork. Soft, sweet, and more carbs than you think.

240kcal / 1 bun
One steamed char siew bao (the soft white BBQ pork bun) is about 240 calories. Most of that is the fluffy bun itself, which is enriched flour and sugar. The char siew filling adds the protein and a hit of sweetness from the sauce. The baked version with the glazed top runs higher, closer to 280 to 320.

The real talk

Everyone underestimates the bao bro. It looks light and fluffy so your brain files it as a small snack. But that soft white dough is enriched flour plus sugar, and the char siew inside is glazed in a sweet, slightly oily sauce. One steamed bun is around 240 calories, and almost no one stops at one.

The baked char siew bao, the golden glazed kind, is even heavier because the top is brushed with sugar and egg wash and sometimes butter. If you are choosing, the plain steamed white bao is the lighter call. Either way, one bao is a snack with the calories of a small meal, so count it properly.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick the steamed white bao over the baked glazed one. The bake adds sugar, egg wash and sometimes butter on top.
  • One bao plus a couple of baskets of steamed dumplings is plenty. You do not need two buns.
  • Drink unsweetened Chinese tea with it. The bun is already sweet, so a sweet drink on top is double the sugar.

Common questions

How many calories in a char siew bao?
A steamed char siew bao is about 240 calories. The baked, glazed version is higher, around 280 to 320, because of the sugar and egg wash brushed on top.
Is char siew bao fattening?
It is more carb-heavy than fatty. The fluffy bun is enriched flour and sugar, so the calories come mainly from refined carbs. It is fine occasionally, but two or three buns adds up fast.
Steamed or baked char siew bao, which is lighter?
The steamed white bun is lighter. The baked version gets a sugar and egg-wash glaze and sometimes butter, which pushes it 60 to 80 calories higher per bun.
Berapa kalori dalam Char Siew Bao?
Char Siew Bao ada kira-kira 240 kalori untuk 1 biji (110g), dengan 9g protein, 38g karbohidrat dan 6g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
