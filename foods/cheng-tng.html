How Many Calories in Cheng Tng?

Cheng tng is around 160 calories a bowl. Clear, light, almost no fat, just watch the sugar syrup.

160kcal / 1 bowl
A bowl of cheng tng is about 160 calories. It is a clear sweet soup loaded with longan, barley, white fungus and dried fruits, so there is basically no fat. Almost all the calories come from the sugar syrup it sits in.

The real talk

Cheng tng is one of the genuinely smart dessert orders. No coconut milk, no fried anything, no glutinous rice. Just a clear broth with barley, longan, gingko and a few dried bits floating around. At 160 calories with almost zero fat, this is about as light as a sweet bowl gets.

The only thing to watch is the sugar level in the syrup. Some stalls go heavy and you can drink it without realising. Ask for less sweet, get the same shiok experience for maybe 110 calories, and walk away feeling like you cheated the system.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it less sweet, the syrup is the only real calorie source
  • Have it cold without extra sugar syrup added at the end
  • Eat the solid bits and leave half the syrup in the bowl

Common questions

Is cheng tng a good dessert for dieting?
One of the best. At 160 calories with basically no fat, it beats almost every coconut milk or fried dessert. Just ask for less sugar and it gets even better.
Does cheng tng have fat?
Almost none. It is a clear soup with dried fruits and barley, so the fat is close to zero. The calories are mostly sugar.
How do I make cheng tng lighter?
Order it less sweet or leave some of the syrup behind. The solids barely add up, so cutting syrup is where you save.
Berapa kalori dalam Cheng Tng?
Cheng Tng ada kira-kira 160 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (300g), dengan 2g protein, 38g karbohidrat dan 1g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
