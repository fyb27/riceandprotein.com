How Many Calories in Chicken Adobo?

Also known as Adobong Manok, Filipino Chicken Adobo, Adobo with Rice.

Chicken braised in soy sauce, vinegar, garlic, bay leaf and peppercorns, then spooned over a cup of white rice. The default lunch at every carinderia in the country.

520kcal / 1 plate with rice
A carinderia plate of chicken adobo with 1 cup of rice is about 520 calories. That is roughly 255 calories from 180g of adobo (thigh with sauce) and 260 calories from 200g of rice. Skip the rice and the adobo alone is closer to 250.

The real talk

The adobo itself is not the problem. FatSecret puts chicken adobo at 141 calories per 100g, which is modest for a meat dish because the sauce is soy, vinegar and garlic with almost no sugar and only a spoon of oil. A 180g carinderia scoop with one thigh and some sauce lands around 255 calories. The rice is what doubles the plate. One cup of steamed rice is 200g and 260 calories, and most people order a second cup for 55 pesos more.

The variable nobody tracks is the cut. Thigh with skin is what most carinderias use, because it survives 40 minutes of simmering without drying out. A skinless breast piece of the same weight drops the adobo portion from 255 to about 170 calories. Pork adobo made with liempo goes the other way, closer to 400 calories for the same scoop, because pork belly is 50 percent fat by weight.

Watch the sauce. Adobo sauce reduces down to a thin layer of chicken fat and soy, and the habit of spooning extra sauce over the rice adds 40 to 60 calories per ladle. If the carinderia adds a spoon of oil or fries the chicken first (adobong pritong manok), add another 80 calories. The 520 figure assumes standard simmered adobo, one thigh, one cup of rice.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for breast or ask the cook to leave the skin off. Same sauce, about 85 calories less per plate.
  • Order half rice. Half a cup is 130 calories and adobo sauce covers it just fine.
  • Skip the extra sauce ladle. Each one is mostly chicken fat and soy, about 50 calories.
  • Pair it with a plain vegetable side like ensaladang talong or steamed okra instead of a second ulam.

Common questions

How many calories in chicken adobo without rice?
About 250 calories for a 180g carinderia scoop of thigh adobo with sauce. FatSecret lists 141 calories per 100g. A single thigh with sauce (152g) is listed at 107 calories, but that figure counts bone weight.
Is chicken adobo good for weight loss?
Yes, as far as ulam goes. It is high in protein (about 25g per scoop), has no sugar and no coconut milk, and the sauce is mostly vinegar and soy. Just control the rice, since 1 cup is 260 calories on its own.
Is chicken adobo or pork adobo higher in calories?
Pork adobo, by a wide margin. The same 180g scoop of pork belly adobo is about 400 calories versus 255 for chicken thigh. Chicken breast adobo is lowest at around 170.
How many calories in a cup of adobo sauce?
About 100 to 150 calories per cup, mostly from rendered chicken fat and the tablespoon of oil used to sauté the garlic. One ladle over rice is about 50 calories.
