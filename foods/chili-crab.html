How Many Calories in Chili Crab?

Also known as Singapore Chilli Crab.

Mud crab wok-tossed in a sweet, spicy tomato and chili sauce thickened with egg, served with mantou or rice and absolutely covered in sauce you will not stop mopping up.

450kcal / 1 serving, about half a medium mud crab with sauce
Half a medium mud crab with sauce comes in at around 450 kcal, with 25g protein, 20g carbs, and 22g fat. The crab meat itself is actually lean at about 100 kcal for 120g edible meat. The calories come mostly from the wok oil (roughly 180 kcal per portion) and the sweet chili and ketchup sauce base (about 105 kcal for 60g). So yes, you are basically paying 280 kcal just for the sauce and oil that makes the whole thing taste incredible.

The real talk

Chili crab is a Singapore and Malaysian seafood classic, a whole mud crab cracked open and cooked in a glossy, slightly sweet, spicy tomato and chili gravy thickened with beaten egg. It looks dramatic, tastes amazing, and is genuinely a mess to eat. At 450 kcal for half a crab with sauce, it is not the worst thing in the world, but do not let the word "crab" fool you into thinking this is some low-calorie health food. The crab meat is lean, sure. But the sauce is built on ketchup, sweet chili sauce, and a generous pour of wok oil, and that is where most of the damage happens. Worth flagging: these numbers are a ballpark estimate because solid nutritional data for chili crab is basically non-existent. Different hawkers use different amounts of oil and sauce, so treat 450 kcal as a reasonable middle-ground, not gospel.

What actually moves the number is the oil and the sauce. Some stalls are heavy-handed with the wok oil, which alone can push your portion past 200 kcal before you even count the crab. The sweet chili and ketchup base adds another 100-plus kcal for 60g of sauce, and people mop that up. The bread is where it gets serious though: two steamed mantou add about 160 kcal, taking you to 610 kcal total. Two fried mantou, which soak up oil, push it to 650 kcal. Swap to a bowl of white rice and you are at 680 kcal. If you want to keep it lower, eat the crab plain with no bread and go easy on scooping up the gravy. The crab meat itself is genuinely high protein and not the problem here.

How to eat it lighter

  • Skip the mantou entirely. Two fried mantou alone add 200 kcal on top of your 450 kcal crab, which is a big chunk for bread you are mostly using to mop sauce.
  • Go easy on the sauce. I know it is the best part, but 60g of that sweet chili gravy is already 105 kcal. Leave some in the pot instead of chasing every last drop.
  • Order steamed mantou over fried if you need the bread. Steamed is roughly 80 kcal per bun versus fried, which absorbs oil and costs more.
  • Share the crab. A whole mud crab with two people plus shared rice keeps your individual total way more manageable than going solo.
  • Pair it with a plain steamed vegetable dish, something like kailan or kangkung, instead of rice or noodles to keep the overall meal calories reasonable while still feeling full.

Common questions

How many calories in Chili Crab?
About 450 kcal for half a medium mud crab with sauce, roughly 250g. That breaks down to 25g protein, 20g carbs, and 22g fat. Add steamed mantou and you are at 610 kcal, fried mantou brings it to 650 kcal, and a bowl of rice takes it to 680 kcal.
What exactly is chili crab?
It is a whole mud crab cooked in a wok with a thick, sweet and spicy sauce made from chili, ketchup, tomato, and egg. The sauce is glossy, slightly tangy, a bit sweet, and very orange. You eat it with your hands, crack the shell yourself, and use bread or rice to mop up the sauce. It is messy and very worth it.
Is chili crab actually healthy?
The crab meat itself is lean and high in protein, 120g of edible meat is only about 100 kcal. The problem is the sauce and wok oil, which together add close to 340 kcal per serving. So it is decent protein for the calories, but not a low-calorie meal, especially once you add bread or rice on the side.
Berapa kalori dalam Chili Crab?
Chili Crab ada kira-kira 450 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (250g), dengan 25g protein, 20g karbohidrat dan 22g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
