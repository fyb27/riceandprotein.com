How Many Calories in Cilok?

Also known as Cilok Bandung, Cilok Bumbu Kacang, Cilok Goreng, Pentol, Tapioca Balls.

Cilok, from aci dicolok (tapioca on a stick), is chewy balls of tapioca flour boiled and sold from carts outside every school in West Java with peanut sauce, kecap manis and sambal. Pentol is the East Java cousin.

320kcal / 1 cup (10 pieces with peanut sauce and kecap)
A Rp 5,000 cup of about 10 cilok with peanut sauce and kecap is about 320 calories. FatSecret Indonesia lists cilok at 266 calories per 100g and 40 calories per piece, with 88% of the energy from carbs. The peanut sauce and kecap add about 40 on top of the balls.

The real talk

Cilok is tapioca starch and water, rolled into balls and boiled. That is the whole thing. FatSecret puts it at 266 calories per 100g with 58g of carbs and 2.5g protein, which makes it more carb dense than rice at 130 per 100g, because the balls are packed dough with very little water. Ten small pieces, about 105g, is 280 calories, and you can eat ten without thinking.

There is no protein worth mentioning. 3g in the whole cup, and that is mostly from the peanut sauce. Cilok isi (stuffed with a bit of minced meat or egg) helps a little, about 5g per cup. Compare it with bakso, where five meatballs give 17g protein for 285 calories. Cilok looks like bakso. It is not bakso. It is the dough without the meat.

The sauce is thinner than siomay sauce, 15g of it is about 40 calories, and the kecap and sambal squeeze is another 15. So a cup is 320, and the trouble is the cup is Rp 5,000 and it is on the way home from school. Two cups is 640 calories of starch before dinner.

Cilok goreng (fried) is about 30% more, roughly 420 a cup, because the balls pick up oil on the crispy outside. Cilok kuah (in broth) is the same balls at 300 with a bit of soup, and cilok bakar (grilled with kecap) is around 350 because of the sweet soy glaze.

How to eat it lighter

  • Five, not ten. Cilok is 40 calories a piece and half a cup is 160 with sauce.
  • Cilok isi over cilok polos. The filling is the only protein you will get.
  • Boiled or kuah, not goreng. Frying adds about 100 calories per cup.
  • Ask for sambal instead of kecap. Kecap manis is 25 calories per squeeze; rawit sambal is nearly zero.
  • Treat it as the snack, not the pre-snack. A cup of cilok and then bakso is 800 calories in an hour.

Common questions

How many calories in cilok?
About 40 calories per piece and 266 per 100g according to FatSecret Indonesia. A cup of 10 with peanut sauce is around 320.
Is cilok fattening?
It is nearly pure tapioca starch, 88% carbs, and a cup is 320 calories with no protein to fill you. It is easy to eat two cups, which is more than a plate of rice.
Cilok or bakso, which is healthier?
Bakso, easily. Five bakso give 17g protein for about 285 calories. Ten cilok give 3g protein for 320. Cilok is the dough without the meat.
How many calories in cilok goreng?
About 420 calories for 10 pieces with sauce. Frying adds roughly 10 calories per ball.
Berapa kalori Cilok?
Cilok mengandung sekitar 320 kalori per 1 cangkir (120g), dengan 3g protein, 63g karbohidrat dan 5g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
