How Many Calories in Claypot Lou Shu Fun?

Rat-tail noodles cooked in a hot claypot with minced pork, egg, and dark soy gravy. Comforting, slightly smoky, and more filling than it looks.

510kcal / 1 claypot (individual portion)
Claypot Lou Shu Fun comes in at around 510 calories for one individual claypot portion (about 390g). That's the standard hawker version with minced pork, egg, dark soy gravy, and beansprouts. Not the lightest noodle dish out there, but not a disaster either. The noodles themselves account for 195 calories, the minced pork adds 155, and the rest comes from the egg and all that dark soy and oyster sauce action. So yeah, just over 500 calories for a solid, filling meal.

The real talk

510 calories for a claypot lou shu fun is honestly pretty reasonable for a full meal, but you want to know what you're actually eating, kan. The biggest calorie contributor here isn't the noodles, it's the minced pork at 155 calories for roughly 70g. Add in the dark soy, oyster sauce, and cooking oil, and that sauce alone is about 65 calories before you even touch the food. The egg adds another 75. So you're sitting at roughly 295 calories just from the protein and sauce components before you factor in the carbs from the noodles. These are honest estimates because detailed nutrition data for claypot lou shu fun specifically is pretty thin. The 510 figure is a reasonable ballpark, not a lab-tested number.

What actually moves the needle here is portion size and how much oil the hawker uncle uses. A small kopitiam claypot comes in closer to 480 calories, while a bigger restaurant portion with salted fish on top can hit 650 calories easily. That salted fish topping sounds innocent but it's usually deep-fried, so you're adding maybe 120 to 140 calories of mostly fat. The gravy is also worth watching. Some places cook it wetter and saucier, which means more oil and more dark soy soaked into those little rat-tail noodles. If you swap minced pork for prawn and squid, you're looking at around 530 calories because seafood is leaner but still adds fat. The 22g of protein from the standard version is decent though, so at least you're not just eating empty carbs lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Skip the salted fish topping. That one addition alone can push the dish from 510 to 650 calories, mostly from deep-fried fat.
  • Ask for less oil or light oil (少油) when ordering. The cooking oil and sauces contribute about 65 calories to the dish and this is one of the easier things to reduce.
  • Go for the seafood version with prawn and squid instead of minced pork. You get roughly the same protein hit for around 530 calories, and the fat content is lower.
  • Don't add extra eggs. One whole egg is already in the standard claypot. Each additional egg is another 75 calories and you probably won't notice the difference in fullness.
  • Order the smaller kopitiam size, not the restaurant large. The size difference alone accounts for at least 100 to 140 calories, and honestly the smaller one is enough.

Common questions

How many calories in Claypot Lou Shu Fun?
A standard individual claypot of Lou Shu Fun has about 510 calories. That's for a 390g portion with minced pork, one egg, dark soy gravy, and beansprouts. A smaller kopitiam size is closer to 480 calories, while a large restaurant portion with salted fish can reach 650 calories.
Is Claypot Lou Shu Fun good for weight loss?
It's manageable. At 510 calories with 22g of protein, it's a filling meal that won't completely blow your daily budget. The minced pork and sauce are the main calorie drivers. Ordering without salted fish and asking for less oil keeps it reasonable. Just don't eat it twice a day.
What is the difference between Lou Shu Fun and regular noodles?
Lou shu fun (also called rat-tail noodles or pin noodles) are short, thick, cylindrical noodles made from rice flour. They're chewier and denser in texture than flat rice noodles like kuey teow, but their calorie density per gram when cooked is similar. 130g of cooked lou shu fun runs about 195 calories either way, so the texture difference doesn't really change the calorie count.
Berapa kalori dalam Claypot Lou Shu Fun?
Claypot Lou Shu Fun ada kira-kira 510 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (390g), dengan 22g protein, 62g karbohidrat dan 17g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
