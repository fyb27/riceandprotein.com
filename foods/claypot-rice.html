How Many Calories in Claypot Rice (Bo Zai Fan)?

Also known as 煲仔飯, 臘味煲仔飯, Bo Zai Fan, Hong Kong Clay Pot Rice, Cured Meat Claypot Rice.

Bo zai fan is rice cooked in a small clay pot over charcoal until the bottom crisps, topped with lap cheong, cured pork belly or liver sausage and finished with sweet soy. The Temple Street stalls charge HK$60 to HK$95 a pot in winter.

1300kcal / 1 pot (cured meat claypot rice)
One pot of cured meat claypot rice (臘味煲仔飯) is about 1,300 calories, based on Hong Kong dietitian figures of 1,315 kcal. Chicken and mushroom is around 1,030, minced beef with egg 1,170, and the lightest common choice, frog with mushroom, is about 860. The cured meats, the oil in the pot and the sweet soy account for more than half of the total.

The real talk

The pot is oiled before the rice goes in so the crust releases, and that is 15 to 20g of oil sitting under the rice before any topping. Then the cured meats render. One lap cheong (40g) carries about 20g of fat by dietitian estimates, and a pot gets two sausages plus a slice of cured belly or liver sausage. All that fat drips straight into 400g of rice. You are eating rice that has been fried from underneath and from above, which is why a bowl-sized portion tops 1,300.

The ranking from the dietitians is useful. Cured meat 1,315. Chicken feet and spare rib 1,300. Minced beef with egg 1,167. Pork patty 1,108. Taro with preserved duck leg 1,074. Chicken and mushroom 1,026. White eel 912. Frog and mushroom 860. The gap between the top and bottom is 450 calories, which is a whole extra meal, from the same pot of rice. The cured stuff is worst because it is preserved in fat and sugar; frog and chicken are lean meat steamed on top.

The sweet soy is the part nobody counts. It is thick, sugary and salty, and the stall pours it on from a squeeze bottle. One tablespoon holds around 825mg of sodium by dietitian estimate, and most people use two or three. A pot can easily pass 2,500mg of sodium and 20g of added sugar. The crust at the bottom (飯焦) that everyone fights over is the most oil soaked rice in the pot, roughly 4 calories per gram versus 1.3 for plain steamed rice.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick chicken and mushroom (北菇滑雞) or frog (田雞) over cured meat. That is 300 to 450 calories less per pot.
  • Share one pot between two and order a plate of blanched vegetables (走油). Half a pot is 650 calories.
  • Use one squeeze of sweet soy, not three. Saves about 60 calories and over 1,500mg of sodium.
  • Leave the oiliest crust. The bottom 50g of rice is close to 200 calories on its own.

Common questions

How many calories in a claypot rice?
About 1,300 calories for a cured meat pot, according to Hong Kong dietitians. Chicken and mushroom is around 1,030 and frog with mushroom about 860.
Why is claypot rice so high in calories?
The pot is oiled, the cured meats render 30g or more of fat into the rice, and the sweet soy adds sugar. One lap cheong alone has about 20g of fat.
Which claypot rice is the healthiest?
Frog and mushroom at about 860 calories, then white eel at 912. Both are lean meat steamed on top of the rice rather than cured meat rendering into it.
