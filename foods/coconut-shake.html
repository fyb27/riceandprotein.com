How Many Calories in Coconut Shake?

A Melaka street-food classic, blended from coconut water, scraped young coconut flesh and a scoop of vanilla ice cream, served over ice.

270kcal / 1 cup (Melaka-style, ~400 ml)
One cup of Melaka-style coconut shake is about 270 calories for roughly 380g of drink. That scoop of vanilla ice cream is doing most of the damage at 165 kcal on its own, with the coconut flesh adding another 73 kcal and the coconut water contributing just 30 kcal. So it is refreshing, sure, but it is nowhere near as light as plain coconut water. These are ballpark estimates since hard lab data for this specific drink is pretty thin.

The real talk

If you have been to Jonker Street in Melaka, you have seen the stalls lining up the coconut shells and you know the drill: coconut water, scraped young coconut flesh, one scoop of vanilla ice cream, all blended together with ice. It tastes like something your body deserves after walking around in 34-degree heat, lah. The problem is that scoop of ice cream, which accounts for more than half the calories at 165 out of 270 kcal. The coconut flesh adds another 73 kcal on top. So you are essentially having a dessert that pretends to be a cold drink. For most people having it as a treat on a Melaka day trip, that is totally fine. Just do not convince yourself it is the same as sipping plain coconut water on a hot day, because it is not even close.

The maths here are pretty simple: the ice cream is the variable that matters most. Skip the ice cream and you drop to around 60 calories, just the coconut water and flesh blended with ice. Ask for an extra scoop and you are looking at roughly 435 kcal, more than some people's full meals. Some stalls are more generous than others with the flesh, so the actual calorie count could swing 20 to 30 kcal either way. To be straight with you, bro, these numbers are a solid estimate based on the components, not a lab-verified figure, because reliable data for this specific drink is genuinely hard to find. The core lesson: the drink itself is fine, the ice cream is a choice.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask them to skip the ice cream or use half a scoop. You go from 270 kcal down to around 60 to 100 kcal just like that.
  • Do not upsize. A larger cup almost always means more ice cream and more coconut flesh, not just more water.
  • Order plain air kelapa (coconut water straight) from a different stall if you just want hydration. That is 30 to 40 kcal and actually more refreshing.
  • Share one cup with someone. Two people, 135 kcal each, everybody happy.
  • Have it after a long walk around Jonker Street so at least you have burned some of those calories first before you drink it.

Common questions

How many calories in Coconut Shake?
A standard Melaka-style coconut shake with one scoop of vanilla ice cream is around 270 calories per cup (about 380g). Without the ice cream, it drops to roughly 60 calories.
What exactly is a coconut shake?
It is a Melaka specialty: young coconut water and scraped coconut flesh blended with ice and a scoop of vanilla ice cream. The result is a cold, creamy drink that sits somewhere between a smoothie and a dessert. You see it everywhere along Jonker Street.
Is coconut shake healthy?
The coconut water and flesh parts are decent, but that scoop of ice cream adds 165 kcal and a fair bit of fat. As an occasional treat on a trip to Melaka, it is fine. As a daily drink thinking it is just coconut water, not great for the calorie budget.
Berapa kalori dalam Coconut Shake?
Coconut Shake ada kira-kira 270 kalori untuk 1 cawan (380g), dengan 4g protein, 30g karbohidrat dan 15g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
