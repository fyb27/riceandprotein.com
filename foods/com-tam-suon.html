How Many Calories in Com Tam Suon?

Also known as Cơm tấm sườn, Cơm tấm sườn bì chả, Broken Rice with Grilled Pork Chop, Com Tam Saigon.

Broken rice grains topped with a charcoal grilled pork chop, shredded pork skin with toasted rice powder (bi), a steamed egg and pork loaf (cha), scallion oil and a bowl of sweet fish sauce. Saigon's all-day plate, 35,000 to 60,000 dong.

700kcal / 1 plate (suon bi cha)
A plate of com tam suon bi cha is around 700 calories. The 220g of broken rice is 290, the grilled chop is about 220 for a 90g piece with its sugary marinade, the bi is 55, the cha is 80, and the mo hanh and nuoc mam add 40. Add op la (a fried egg) and it is 820.

The real talk

Com tam is a 700 calorie plate and it does not look like one. The rice is the biggest part: com tam plates are generous, 220g of cooked broken rice is 290 calories, and the grains soak up the fish sauce. The chop is a thin cut pork shoulder or rib marinated in sugar, honey, fish sauce and oil, then grilled over charcoal. A 90g chop is 220 once you count the marinade, and a lot of quan serve 120g chops now.

The sides add up quietly. Bi, the shredded pork skin tossed with thinh (toasted rice powder), is 55 for a small handful. Cha trung, the steamed pork and egg loaf, is 80 for a 40g slice. The spoon of mo hanh (scallion oil) on top is 30. The nuoc mam is sweet, about 15g of sugar per bowl if you pour it all, so 40 calories. None of these feel like food, and together they are 200.

The full plate at a proper Saigon com tam quan is suon bi cha op la, with a fried egg, sometimes a second cha, plus the free bowl of canh (clear soup). That is 820 to 900 calories. The published figures range from 627 to 767 for suon bi cha depending on the size of the chop and rice; 700 is a fair middle for what you actually get on the plate.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order com tam suon only, chop and rice. About 550 versus 700 for suon bi cha.
  • Ask for it it com, less rice. Cutting 70g of rice saves 90 calories.
  • Skip op la. A fried egg in oil is 110 to 120.
  • Hold the mo hanh. The scallion oil spoon is about 30.
  • Pour half the nuoc mam. The sauce has about 15g of sugar per bowl.

Common questions

How many calories in com tam suon bi cha?
About 700 for a standard plate. Published figures range from 627 to 767 depending on the size of the pork chop and the rice portion.
Is com tam fattening?
It is a full meal at 700 calories with 32g of fat, mainly from the chop marinade, scallion oil and cha. Eating it every day without adjusting the rest of your intake will add up.
How many calories in the grilled pork chop alone?
About 220 for a 90g chop, or around 290 for the larger 120g chops many quan now serve. The sugar and oil in the marinade add 40 to 50 on top of the meat.
What is bi in com tam?
Shredded boiled pork skin and lean pork mixed with thinh, toasted rice powder. A small serving is about 55 calories and mostly protein and collagen.
