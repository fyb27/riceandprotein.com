How Many Calories in Curry Fish Balls?

Also known as 咖喱魚蛋, Ga Lei Yu Daan, Hong Kong Fish Balls, Street Fish Balls, 咖哩魚蛋.

Curry fish balls are the number one Hong Kong street snack: bouncy fried fish balls simmered in a spicy curry gravy, served 5 to 7 on a bamboo skewer for about HK$10 to HK$15 from carts in Mong Kok, Sham Shui Po and outside every MTR exit.

120kcal / 1 skewer (6 balls with curry sauce)
One skewer of six curry fish balls with sauce is about 120 calories. The fish balls themselves are 65 to 76 calories for 5 or 6 pieces, according to Hong Kong Department of Health figures, and the curry gravy clinging to them adds roughly 45 more. Protein is around 8g per skewer.

The real talk

Fish balls are the cheapest calories on the street in the good sense. Six balls without sauce are about 76 calories, which is less than a single egg tart (195). The Centre for Health Protection lists them at the very bottom of its street snack ranking, under stinky tofu (174 a piece), fried intestine (160 a skewer) and egg waffle (390 a sheet). Two skewers is still under 250 calories.

The sauce is where it moves. Hong Kong curry gravy at a street cart is oil, curry powder and a lot of starch to make it stick. A tablespoon is about 51 calories. Most skewers pick up two to three tablespoons of coating, so the sauce can be worth more than the fish balls. If the cart lets the skewer drain before handing it over, you are closer to 100. If it comes dripping, closer to 150.

The real issue is sodium, not calories. One skewer of six with curry gravy carries around 600mg of sodium, nearly a third of the 2,000mg daily limit. Add the sweet sauce and chili that most people squeeze on top and it goes higher. So while this is the lightest thing on the cart for calories, it is one of the saltiest, which matters if you have blood pressure to watch.

How to eat it lighter

  • Let the skewer drain over the pot before you take it. That knocks the sauce down by about a third.
  • Skip the sweet sauce and chili on top. Each squeeze is 15 to 20 calories and more sodium.
  • Pick fish balls over siu mai at the same cart. Six siu mai are about 294 calories, four times the fish balls.
  • One skewer plus a bottle of water is a fine 120 calorie snack. Two skewers and a soft drink is 400.

Common questions

How many calories in a skewer of curry fish balls?
About 120 calories for six balls with curry sauce. The fish balls alone are around 76 calories according to HK Department of Health data; the sauce adds the rest.
Are curry fish balls healthy?
They are low in calories and have about 8g of protein per skewer, but very high in sodium at around 600mg. Fine as a snack, not great as a daily habit.
Fish balls or siu mai, which is lighter?
Fish balls by a wide margin. Six fish balls are about 76 calories; six fish siu mai are about 294 because siu mai has more starch and fat.
