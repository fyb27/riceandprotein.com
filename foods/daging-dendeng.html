How Many Calories in Daging Dendeng?

Thin, crispy spiced beef slices cooked Minang style, often served with sambal, packing more calories than the small portion suggests.

330kcal / 1 serving (3-4 thin slices)
Daging Dendeng comes in at around 330 calories per serving of 3-4 thin slices (about 90g). The beef itself only accounts for roughly 185 kcal, but the frying oil absorbed during cooking adds another 100 kcal on top. So yeah, this is one of those dishes that looks small but hits harder than you expect calorie-wise.

The real talk

330 calories for 3-4 thin slices of beef sounds reasonable until you realise those slices weigh maybe 90g total and a third of the calories (100 kcal) is literally just absorbed frying oil. The beef itself is actually decent protein, 24g per serving, which is solid. But dendeng is one of those deceptive lauk items where you see a small pile of meat and think you're being good, then you grab another serving without thinking. Worth noting this is a ballpark estimate as hard calorie data for dendeng specifically is thin, so treat 330 kcal as a useful working number rather than a precise lab result.

What really moves the number is how the beef was cooked and how much sambal they pile on. Standard crispy fried dendeng (the plain version without extra sambal topping) is where you get the 330 kcal base. Dendeng balado is specifically the crispy version dressed with a balado red chili sambal on top, which pushes it closer to 350 kcal. The dendeng lambok, the softer moist style, drops down to around 280 kcal because less oil is absorbed. Portion control is where most people slip up lah, because dendeng goes so well with plain rice that you just keep reaching. If you can stick to one serving (those 3-4 slices) and pair it with a big scoop of veg instead of extra rice, the protein-to-calorie ratio is actually pretty decent for a lauk option.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for dendeng lambok instead of the crispy fried version, you save about 50 calories per serving just from less oil absorption.
  • Eat it with plain white rice rather than nasi lemak or nasi goreng, the lauk is already doing work so keep the base simple.
  • One serving is 3-4 slices, not the whole plate. Pair it with a big portion of kangkung or ulam to fill up without adding much to your calorie total.
  • Go easy on extra sambal topping. The sambal adds 20 kcal per serving and it is easy to double that at a nasi campur counter.
  • At a nasi campur place, take dendeng as your protein pick but skip the other fried lauk alongside it, do not double up on the oily stuff.

Common questions

How many calories in Daging Dendeng?
A standard serving of daging dendeng, about 3-4 thin slices totalling 90g, comes to around 330 calories. The beef contributes 185 kcal and absorbed frying oil accounts for another 100 kcal.
Is daging dendeng good for a diet?
It is a reasonable protein source at 24g protein per serving, but the 22g of fat from deep frying means the calories stack up quickly. If you can get the moist dendeng lambok style instead, you drop to around 280 kcal. One serving with vegetables and plain rice works fine in a calorie deficit.
What is the difference between dendeng balado and dendeng lambok?
Dendeng balado is the crispy fried version specifically topped with balado red chili sambal, a named Minang preparation that sits at around 350 kcal. Plain crispy fried dendeng without the sambal topping is a separate thing at around 310-330 kcal. Dendeng lambok is softer and moist with less oil absorbed, so it sits lower at around 280 kcal per serving.
Berapa kalori dalam Daging Dendeng?
Daging Dendeng ada kira-kira 330 kalori untuk 1 hidangan (90g), dengan 24g protein, 9g karbohidrat dan 22g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
