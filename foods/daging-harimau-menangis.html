How Many Calories in Daging Harimau Menangis?

Grilled beef slices with a punchy chilli-soy-lime dip, where most of the calories live in the cut of beef, not the sauce.

630kcal / 1 hawker plate (grilled beef slices with dipping sauce)
A hawker plate of Daging Harimau Menangis comes in at around 630 calories for a standard 230g serving of grilled beef slices with the chilli-soy-lime dipping sauce. The bulk of that, about 540 calories, is coming straight from the beef itself (roughly 200g of grilled brisket or batang pinang). The marinade adds another 50 and the dipping sauce is practically free at 40. So yeah, 630 calories for what is honestly a pretty satisfying plate of grilled beef. Not bad at all if you are tracking.

The real talk

630 calories for a plate of grilled beef is honestly pretty reasonable, bro. You are getting 50g of protein out of it, which is solid. The issue is the fat, 38g, and that comes entirely from the beef cut. Batang pinang and brisket have decent fat marbling, and when it gets grilled and then soaked in a kicap manis marinade with brown sugar, those calories stack up fast. The dipping sauce is basically nothing calorie-wise, 40 calories for 30g of chilli-soy-lime, so do not stress about it. Worth flagging that these numbers are a ballpark estimate because there is very little hard calorie data published specifically for this dish, so treat 630 as a reasonable working figure, not a precise lab reading.

What actually moves the number is the cut of beef and how big the portion is. If the uncle uses a fattier brisket or gives you a generous 300g plate, you are looking at closer to 850 calories. Swap to a leaner cut like tenderloin with a lighter marinade and you drop to around 480. The marinade itself adds about 50 calories from the oyster sauce, kicap manis and sugar absorbed into the meat, so asking for less marinade or a drier grill does help a bit. Paired with plain rice this becomes a complete meal, but watch the rice portion because that is where most people accidentally eat another 300-400 calories without thinking lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for a leaner cut like sirloin or tenderloin instead of brisket, drops you from 630 to around 480 calories.
  • Request less marinade or a drier grill finish to cut the kicap manis and brown sugar contribution, saves maybe 20-30 calories.
  • The dipping sauce is already basically free at 40 calories, so dip away freely and don't stress that part.
  • Control your rice portion on the side. A standard scoop of white rice is 200-250 calories on its own, that is where most of the meal's total goes.
  • Eat this as your main protein with plain rice and a vegetable side, it is a genuinely high-protein option at 50g per plate.

Common questions

How many calories in Daging Harimau Menangis?
A standard hawker plate of Daging Harimau Menangis is around 630 calories for about 230g of grilled beef slices with dipping sauce. That number can range from 480 calories for a leaner cut to 850 calories for a large fatty brisket portion.
Is Daging Harimau Menangis good for weight loss?
It can work, actually. At 630 calories and 50g of protein, the protein-to-calorie ratio is decent. The main thing to manage is the cut of beef (leaner is better) and what you eat with it. Pair it with plain rice and vegetables and keep the total meal under 900-1000 calories and you are fine.
What does Daging Harimau Menangis mean and what cut of beef is used?
It translates literally to 'crying tiger beef', named after the raw chilli dipping sauce that supposedly makes a tiger cry. The most common cut at hawker stalls is brisket or batang pinang, which has good fat marbling and holds up well to grilling. Some places use flank or skirt steak, which tends to be leaner.
Berapa kalori dalam Daging Harimau Menangis?
Daging Harimau Menangis ada kira-kira 630 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (230g), dengan 50g protein, 13g karbohidrat dan 38g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
