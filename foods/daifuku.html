How Many Calories in Daifuku?

Also known as 大福, Daifuku Mochi, Mochi with Red Bean, だいふく.

Soft pounded rice cake wrapped around sweet azuki bean paste, dusted with starch to stop it sticking. Sold at every wagashi shop and konbini in Japan for 130 to 250 yen, with strawberry (ichigo daifuku) and salted bean (mame daifuku) the common variations.

225kcal / 1 piece
A standard 96g daifuku is 225 calories on the Japanese food composition data, with 50g of carbs and about 1g of fat. The mochi shell is roughly 115 of it and the 45g of koshian (smooth red bean paste) is 110. Nothing is fried, nothing is buttered. It is sugar and rice starch in a soft package.

The real talk

Daifuku is honest about what it is. There is no hidden fat, no cream, no oil. It is glutinous rice pounded into a shell at about 230 calories per 100g and red bean paste that is roughly half sugar by weight. One piece is 225 calories and 50g of carbs, which is the same carb load as a bowl of rice, in 3 bites. It does not feel like a bowl of rice. That is the whole problem.

The variations move the number less than you would think. Ichigo daifuku swaps some of the anko for a whole strawberry, so it lands around 190 despite being bigger. Mame daifuku with salted black beans is about 250 for a 110g piece. Kusa daifuku (mugwort) is the same as plain at about 220. The konbini versions are smaller, 70 to 80g, and run 170 to 190. The only real jump is the cream daifuku from the chiller, where whipped cream inside a mochi shell takes it to 260 with 10g of fat.

The place daifuku fits is after a protein meal, as the sweet thing, one piece. As a snack on its own it is a sugar spike with 4g of protein, and you are hungry again in an hour. Two with a canned coffee at the konbini is 550 calories and feels like nothing. The wagashi shop version is worth it. The konbini three pack is where the count goes wrong.

How to eat it lighter

  • One piece. It is 225 calories and 50g of carbs. The second one is a bowl of rice you did not need.
  • Ichigo daifuku is about 190, lower than plain, because the strawberry replaces some of the bean paste.
  • Have it after a meal with protein in it, not as a standalone snack. Same calories, much less hunger an hour later.
  • Skip the cream daifuku from the chiller. Whipped cream inside takes it to 260 and adds 10g of fat.
  • Pair with plain green tea (0 calories), not a sweetened matcha latte at 240.

Common questions

How many calories in one daifuku?
About 225 for a standard 96g piece. Konbini ones are smaller at around 180 and ichigo daifuku is about 190.
Is daifuku healthy?
It has almost no fat and 4g of protein, but it is 50g of carbs from rice starch and sugar in 3 bites. Fine as one dessert, not as a snack habit.
Is daifuku gluten free?
Yes. The shell is glutinous rice (mochigome), which has no gluten despite the name, and the filling is azuki beans and sugar.
Daifuku or mochi ice cream, which is lower?
Mochi ice cream is smaller at about 100 calories per ball. One daifuku at 225 is roughly 2 of them.
