How Many Calories in Dakgalbi?

Also known as 닭갈비, Chuncheon Dakgalbi, Korean Spicy Stir-fried Chicken, Dak-galbi.

Dakgalbi is boneless chicken thigh marinated in gochujang, stir-fried on a big iron pan at the table with cabbage, sweet potato, rice cakes and perilla leaves. Chuncheon's dish, now on every university street for 13,000 to 16,000 won a serving.

580kcal / 1 serving (1인분, about 300g, no rice)
A 1인분 of dakgalbi is about 580 calories for a 300g serving, using the fatsecret.kr figure of 195 kcal per 100g. It gives you about 35g of protein from 180g of chicken thigh, but the sweet marinade, the rice cakes and the sweet potato bring it to 45g of carbs. The fried rice at the end is another 450.

The real talk

Dakgalbi is thigh meat, skin on, which is why 180g of chicken carries 320 calories rather than the 200 you would get from breast. The gochujang marinade is about 70 calories per serving and roughly 12g of sugar, and the pan is oiled with 8g or so before anything goes in. The chicken itself is honest food; the pan around it is not.

Then the extras. Tteok (rice cakes), sweet potato and cabbage share the pan, and while the cabbage is nearly free, the rice cakes and sweet potato are about 120 calories per serving. Cheese dakgalbi, where a ring of mozzarella melts on the edge of the pan, adds about 150 per person. That version is 730 before rice.

The bokkeumbap finish is the real number. When the meat is gone, the staff scrape the pan and fry rice with seaweed and sesame oil in the leftover sauce, about 450 calories a portion, and nobody says no. Two servings of dakgalbi between two people, cheese, and fried rice is about 1,180 per person.

How to eat it lighter

  • Skip the fried rice, or share one portion between two. Half is 225 calories instead of 450.
  • Eat the cabbage and perilla wraps the way you would at samgyeopsal. The vegetables are 10 to 15 calories a handful.
  • No cheese. That ring of mozzarella is about 150 calories per person and covers up the chicken flavour anyway.
  • Order 1 serving for 2 people plus extra vegetables (야채 추가) instead of 2 servings of meat.
  • Leave the sweet potato slices. They are about 60 calories each and mostly there to look pretty.

Common questions

How many calories in dakgalbi with fried rice?
About 1,030 for one serving of dakgalbi (580) plus the bokkeumbap portion at the end (450).
Is dakgalbi healthy?
It is chicken and cabbage, so better than most Korean barbecue, but the skin-on thigh, sugar in the marinade and pan oil put it at 580 a serving. Skipping the fried rice makes the biggest difference.
How many calories in cheese dakgalbi?
About 730 per serving. The mozzarella adds roughly 150 calories per person.
Is charcoal dakgalbi lower in calories?
Yes. Chuncheon's grilled version has no pan oil, rice cakes or sweet potato, about 420 for a 250g serving of marinated thigh.
