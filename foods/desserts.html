Desserts Calories

Cendol, halo halo, mango sticky rice, bingsu, a slab of mochi. Desserts are pure enjoyment calories, close to zero nutrition, and that is completely fine as long as you spend them on purpose.

My rule while losing weight: dessert is a decision, not a habit. The ranking below tells you what each bowl costs so you can budget for the ones you actually love and skip the ones you eat out of politeness.

Desserts at a glance

There are 29 dishes ranked on this page. The lightest is Ais Krim (Local Ice Cream) at 80 kcal per 1 stick (potong), the heaviest is Bingsu at 450 kcal per 1 bowl patbingsu (cafe size, meant for 2), and the average lands around 277 calories a serving. 17 of them come in under 300 calories.

The spread here is narrower than most categories, about 370 calories between the lightest and the heaviest. That means portion size and what you order alongside it matter more than which dish you pick.

Common questions

What is the lowest calorie dessert option?
Ais Krim (Local Ice Cream) is the lightest on this page at 80 calories per 1 stick (potong). There are 17 options here under 300 calories, so you have room to pick something you actually like.
What is the highest calorie dessert here?
Bingsu tops the list at 450 calories per 1 bowl patbingsu (cafe size, meant for 2). Nothing else here comes close.
How many calories are in a typical desserts serving?
Across the 29 dishes on this page the average is about 277 calories per serving, with the middle of the pack around 280 (Pengat Pisang). Figures are estimates for a normal portion.
