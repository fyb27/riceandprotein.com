How Many Calories in Dinuguan?

Also known as Pork Blood Stew, Dinardaraan, Chocolate Meat, Tinumis.

Pork (meat, offal or both) stewed in pig's blood with vinegar, garlic, onion and a long green chili. Dark, sour, savoury, and usually eaten with puto or rice.

330kcal / 1 carinderia serving (about 180g)
One carinderia serving of dinuguan is about 330 calories for a 180g ladle. The pork pieces are about 250 of that and the blood gravy about 80. Red Ribbon's version is listed on FatSecret at 255 per cup, and versions made with pork belly go over 450.

The real talk

Pig's blood is not the problem. Cooked blood is about 80 calories per 100g and mostly protein and iron, which surprises people. What decides dinuguan is the pork that goes in. Carinderias use a mix of kasim (shoulder), a bit of belly and offal like intestine and liver, which averages about 300 calories per 100g cooked. An 80g share of that is 250 calories. Kawaling Pinoy's belly-only recipe card lands at 857 per serving, and that is the home version with 2 pounds of belly for 6 people.

Red Ribbon and Goldilocks sell it as a takeout tub, and Red Ribbon's is listed at 255 calories per cup with 34g of protein. That is leaner than most carinderias because they use more lean meat and less fat. The Ilocano dinardaraan is the opposite: drier, fattier, often with bagnet and chicharon crumbled in, and a serving is 450 to 500.

The puto is the part everyone forgets. Dinuguan at merienda comes with 2 or 3 puto, and each small puto is about 40 calories. Three puto is 120, so dinuguan with puto is about 450. With 1 cup of rice instead it is about 590. At 50 to 70 pesos a serving it is a high protein ulam at about 22g, so the calorie return is decent if the cook did not use belly.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask if it is kasim or liempo. Kasim dinuguan is about 280 per serving, liempo dinuguan about 450.
  • Take the meat and half the gravy. The gravy is about 80 calories per serving and you do not need all of it.
  • Two puto instead of three, or half a cup of rice. That is 80 to 130 calories saved.
  • Skip the chicharon topping that some stalls add. A small handful is 100 calories.

Common questions

Is dinuguan healthy?
It is high in protein (about 22g per serving) and iron from the blood. Calories depend on the pork cut: 280 with shoulder, 450 with belly. Sodium is also high from the vinegar and patis.
How many calories in dinuguan with puto?
About 450 calories for a standard serving with 3 small puto. Each puto adds about 40.
How many calories in pig's blood?
About 80 calories per 100g cooked, almost all protein. It is the pork fat, not the blood, that makes dinuguan heavy.
Is dinuguan high in cholesterol?
Yes. Blood and offal push a serving to around 150 to 200mg of cholesterol, more if there is liver. If that matters for you, keep it to once a week.
