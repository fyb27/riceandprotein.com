How Many Calories in Doenjang Jjigae?

Also known as 된장찌개, Soybean Paste Stew, Doenjang Chigae, Korean Miso Stew.

Doenjang jjigae is the fermented soybean paste stew with tofu, zucchini, potato and onion that turns up next to almost every Korean rice meal.

145kcal / 1 pot
A 400g pot of doenjang jjigae is about 145 calories, according to Korean MFDS restaurant nutrition data. That is the lowest number of any common jjigae. With a bowl of rice the meal comes to around 455, and with a grilled fish on the side you are still under 700.

The real talk

There is barely anything in it. The doenjang paste is about 40 calories for the 30g a pot uses, tofu is 60, and the vegetables (zucchini, potato, onion, chili) come to 35. The anchovy stock is close to zero. That is how a full pot that fills you up lands at 145, about the same as one slice of bread.

Because the stew is so light, it is the rice and the sides that decide your lunch. A 210g bowl of rice is 310, more than double the stew. The typical 8,000 won doenjang jjigae set comes with rice, a fried egg or gyeran-mari, and 3 to 4 banchan, so a realistic total is 500 to 600. Still one of the cheapest, lowest-calorie set meals you can order.

The versions that go higher are the ones with meat. Chadol doenjang jjigae with beef brisket adds 80 to 120 calories, and some gogi-jip serve it as a rich, oily finisher after samgyeopsal where the pork fat from the grill ends up in the pot. That version is closer to 250. On its own, at a lunch place, it is 145.

How to eat it lighter

  • It is already light. The single biggest saving is stopping at one bowl of rice instead of taking the refill.
  • Ask for extra tofu (두부 추가). Most places do it free and it adds protein for about 60 calories.
  • Pair it with grilled mackerel (고등어구이) rather than fried pork cutlet. The fish set lands near 650, the cutlet set near 1,000.
  • The chadol (brisket) version is nice but adds about 100. Order it when you want the meat, not by default.

Common questions

Is doenjang jjigae good for dieting?
Yes. At about 145 calories per pot with 10g protein and a lot of volume, it is the best stew on the menu for a calorie deficit. Just watch the rice.
How many calories in doenjang jjigae with rice?
About 455 with a standard 210g bowl of rice. With the usual banchan and an egg, plan on 550 to 600.
Is doenjang jjigae high in sodium?
Yes, roughly 1,500 to 2,000mg per pot from the fermented paste. Drinking less of the broth is the easy fix.
Doenjang jjigae or kimchi jjigae for fewer calories?
Doenjang, at 145 versus 244 for kimchi jjigae. Both are low; the difference is the pork belly in kimchi jjigae.
