How Many Calories in Durian?

Durian is the king of fruits in Malaysia, famously creamy, famously divisive, and famously more calorie-dense than almost any fruit you can name.

175kcal / 4 seeds (~120g edible flesh)
Four seeds of durian, roughly 120g of edible flesh, comes in at 175 calories. That sounds fine until you realise nobody stops at 4 seeds. A whole small kampung durian gives you around 400 to 500g of flesh, which lands at about 600 calories in one sitting. Musang King and Black Thorn push even higher per 100g, around 170 to 180 kcal per 100g, because the flesh is richer and fattier. The calories come from two places: sugars (carbs are 32g per serving) and fat (6g per serving), which is unusually high for a fruit.

The real talk

Durian is not a light snack. It is a calorie-dense fruit that sits somewhere between a dessert and a meal. The richness you taste in a good Musang King or Black Thorn seed? That is fat, specifically oleic and linoleic acids, the same types you find in nuts and avocado. Not bad fat, but still fat, and fat has 9 kcal per gram. Add the natural sugars and you get a fruit that hits 147 kcal per 100g for standard D24 and up to 175 to 180 kcal per 100g for the premium grades. Most fruit sits at 50 to 80 kcal per 100g. Durian is basically in a different weight class.

The maths changes fast based on variety and portion. Four seeds of kampung durian is about 145 calories, manageable. Four seeds of Musang King is 210 calories. A full kampung durian at the pasar malam is 600 calories. That is nearly a full meal worth of calories from fruit alone, eaten while standing, usually followed by more food. If you are tracking, weigh the flesh or count seeds honestly. Going from 4 seeds to 8 seeds does not just feel like double the enjoyment, it is double the calories.

How to eat it lighter

  • Stop at 4 seeds and actually mean it. Set the limit before you open the fruit, not halfway through.
  • Go kampung durian instead of Musang King or Black Thorn. You save 35 to 70 calories per sitting and the flavour difference is smaller than the price difference.
  • Eat it as your dessert after a lighter meal, not as a snack on top of a full dinner.
  • Avoid durian with sticky rice or any creamy preparations. Plain flesh is already rich enough without adding coconut milk on top.
  • Share a fruit with someone instead of buying one each. Bro, you do not need a whole durian to yourself.

Common questions

How many calories in Durian?
4 seeds of durian, about 120g of edible flesh, is 175 calories. Kampung durian runs a bit lower at 145 calories for 4 seeds, while premium varieties like Musang King and Black Thorn hit 210 to 215 calories for the same portion because the flesh is creamier and higher in fat.
Is durian actually bad for you if you are trying to lose weight?
Not bad exactly, just very easy to overeat. The fat and sugar content means calories add up fast. One whole kampung durian can be 600 calories. If you fit it into your daily calorie target, it is fine. The problem is most people eat durian without thinking about it as part of their calorie budget at all.
How does durian compare to other Malaysian desserts in calories?
Durian is heavier than most. A serving of cendol is around 250 to 300 calories but it is mostly sugar and coconut milk in liquid form. A bowl of ais kacang is similar. Durian at 175 calories for 4 seeds looks okay on paper but the portion creep is real, and the fat content is higher than almost any other local dessert you can name.
Berapa kalori dalam Durian?
Durian ada kira-kira 175 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (120g), dengan 2g protein, 32g karbohidrat dan 6g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
