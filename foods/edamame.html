How Many Calories in Edamame?

Also known as 枝豆, えだまめ, Salted Soybeans, Boiled Edamame.

Young soybeans boiled in the pod and salted, served in a bowl at every izakaya in Japan as the thing you eat while the first beer arrives. About 140g of beans once you throw the pods away.

175kcal / 1 bowl (250g in pods)
A standard izakaya bowl of edamame, about 250g in the pod, is roughly 175 calories once you account for the pods you do not eat. That is about 140g of beans at 125 calories per 100g, with 16g of protein and 8g of fat. It is one of the few bar snacks in Japan that is worth eating for the number.

The real talk

Edamame is the rare izakaya order that is better than it looks. A whole bowl is 175 calories and 16g of protein, which is the protein of 2 eggs for the calories of one rice ball. The fat is the good kind, 8g from the soybean itself, and the fibre is 7g. It takes 10 minutes to eat because you are popping pods, so it slows down the drinking. Nothing else on the otsumami menu comes close. Karaage is 470 for a plate. Potato salad is 250. A single kushikatsu is 100.

The salt is the only thing to think about. A bowl carries 1 to 2g of it, which makes you drink more, which is the whole point from the izakaya's side. That is where edamame goes wrong: not the beans, but the third beer they pull along at 150 each. The garlic butter and chilli oil versions that trendy places sell add 60 to 90 calories of fat per bowl and taste like it. The plain boiled ones with sea salt are the deal.

The frozen bags from the supermarket are the same thing. Three hundred grams of frozen pods is about 210 calories and it is a 3 minute microwave. As a snack at home it beats every chip and every rice cracker on protein by a mile, and a bowl of it before dinner means you eat a smaller dinner. That is the trick with high protein, high fibre food: it does not make you thinner, it makes the next meal smaller.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it plain with salt. Garlic butter and chilli oil versions add 60 to 90 calories of fat.
  • Make it the first order and the last. It slows the beer down, and the beer is where izakaya calories go.
  • Ask for less salt (shio hikaeme). Less thirst, one fewer beer.
  • At home, a 300g frozen bag is about 210 calories and 20g of protein for a 3 minute snack.
  • Pair with a highball at 70 calories instead of a beer at 150 if you are having 2 or 3.

Common questions

How many calories in a bowl of edamame?
About 175 for a 250g izakaya bowl in the pod, which is 140g of beans. Per 100g of shelled beans it is about 125.
How much protein is in edamame?
About 16g in an izakaya bowl and 11 to 12g per 100g of shelled beans. Higher than most vegetables and close to eggs for the calories.
Is edamame good for weight loss?
Yes. High protein, high fibre, low calorie, and slow to eat. A bowl before dinner reliably makes dinner smaller.
Is edamame keto?
Borderline. A bowl has about 12g of carbs, 7g of it fibre, so 5g net. Fine in a small dish, less so by the bag.
