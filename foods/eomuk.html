How Many Calories in Eomuk?

Also known as 어묵, 오뎅, Odeng, Korean Fish Cake Skewer, Eomuk-tang.

Eomuk is a sheet of fish cake folded onto a wooden skewer and kept hot in a tray of anchovy and radish broth. Street carts sell it for 1,000 won a stick with free broth in a paper cup.

100kcal / 1 skewer (with a cup of broth)
One eomuk skewer is about 100 calories, using fatsecret.kr figures of 96 kcal per skewer and 201 kcal per 100g of fish cake. The broth in the cup adds around 10 calories. Three skewers, which is a normal stop at a cart, come to 300.

The real talk

Eomuk is fish paste mixed with wheat starch and a little sugar, then fried in a sheet. A 45g skewer is about 90 calories with 6g of protein, which makes it the best deal on the cart next to a 335 calorie cup of tteokbokki. The broth is anchovy, kelp and radish, close to zero calories unless the vendor has been topping it up with the tteokbokki sauce.

Where it goes wrong is quantity and dipping. The skewers are small and people eat 4 or 5 while waiting for the tteokbokki, which is 400 to 500 calories that nobody counts. A squeeze of the sweet soy or mustard sauce adds 15 to 20 calories per stick. And the eomuk in the tray has been sitting in salty broth, so 3 skewers with a cup of broth is roughly 1,200mg of sodium.

The bunsik shop version, eomuk-tang served in a bowl with 5 or 6 pieces and radish for 4,000 to 6,000 won, is about 300 to 350 calories. It is a fair meal for the price and one of the few street foods with a decent protein-to-calorie ratio.

How to eat it lighter

  • Two skewers and a cup of broth is a 200 calorie snack with 12g of protein. Stop at two.
  • Skip the sweet soy sauce. It is 15 to 20 calories per stick and adds sodium to something already salty.
  • Use eomuk to replace twigim next to your tteokbokki. Same fullness, a third of the calories.
  • Drink the broth but do not go for a second cup. The broth is fine on calories, heavy on salt.

Common questions

How many calories in one odeng skewer?
About 100 including the broth. A thick Busan-style skewer with double the fish cake is closer to 140.
Is eomuk healthy?
It is a decent snack: 6g of protein for 100 calories. The downside is sodium, around 400mg per skewer with broth.
How many calories in a bowl of eomuk-tang?
About 330 for a bowl with 5 to 6 pieces, radish and broth. It is one of the lighter hot meals at a bunsik shop.
Is fish cake mostly fish?
Usually 60 to 70 percent fish paste, the rest wheat starch, sugar and oil from frying. That is why it has 8g of carbs per skewer.
