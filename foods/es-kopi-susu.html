How Many Calories in Es Kopi Susu?

Also known as Es Kopi Susu Gula Aren, Kopi Susu Kekinian, Kopi Kenangan Mantan, Es Kopi Susu Tetangga, Iced Palm Sugar Latte.

Es kopi susu is the Indonesian iced coffee that took over every office lobby after 2018: a shot of espresso, fresh milk, a pour of gula aren (palm sugar) syrup and ice, in a plastic cup with a brand name on it. Kopi Kenangan, Janji Jiwa, Fore and a thousand others.

220kcal / 1 regular cup (about 350ml)
A regular 350ml es kopi susu gula aren is about 220 calories. Building it up: the espresso is 5, 150ml of fresh milk is 95, 30ml of gula aren syrup is about 90 and the splash of creamer or condensed milk most chains add is 30. Published figures for a regular Kopi Kenangan Mantan sit between 180 and 220, which matches.

The real talk

Gula aren is sugar. It is palm sugar, it has a nice caramel taste and a slightly lower glycaemic index than white sugar, and it is 4 calories a gram like every other sugar. A regular kopi susu has 25 to 30g of it in syrup form, about 90 to 110 calories. The 'gula aren is healthier' line is marketing, and the doctor who went viral calculating 370 calories for a homemade version with 4 tablespoons was not exaggerating, that is what four tablespoons does.

The milk is the other half. 150ml of full cream milk is 95 calories with 5g protein, which is the only protein in the cup. Chains that use a mix of fresh milk and creamer or condensed milk push the cup higher, and the ones that use UHT full cream and a heavy hand on the syrup land at 250. The espresso shot itself is 5 calories. You are drinking a milk and sugar drink with coffee flavour.

At Rp 18,000 to 25,000 for a regular, a daily kopi susu is 220 calories a day, 6,600 a month, roughly 0.8kg of fat a month if nothing else changes. That is not a scare number, that is what a daily habit of a 220 calorie drink is. The large size is 350 to 400.

The 'less sugar' option at most chains is real and cuts the syrup by half, to about 170. 'No sugar' takes it to 110, which is basically an iced latte, and at that point it is a reasonable drink with 5g protein.

How to eat it lighter

  • 'Less sugar' or 'half sugar'. Most chains offer it, and it takes the cup from 220 to about 170.
  • 'No sugar' is a 110 calorie iced latte. Give it a week; the milk is sweeter than you think once the syrup is gone.
  • Regular, not large. The large is the same drink with another 100 to 150 calories of milk and syrup.
  • Skip the topping. Cheese foam, caramel drizzle and boba each add 60 to 120.
  • Kopi susu at a warkop (Rp 5,000, black coffee with condensed milk) is about 120. Old fashioned and cheaper in every sense.

Common questions

How many calories in es kopi susu gula aren?
About 220 calories for a regular 350ml cup from the big chains. Published figures for Kopi Kenangan Mantan regular range from 180 to 220.
Is gula aren healthier than sugar?
Slightly lower glycaemic index, same calories. 30g of gula aren syrup is about 90 calories, the same as 30g of white sugar. It is not a free pass.
How many calories in es kopi susu without sugar?
About 110 calories, all from the milk. It is an iced latte with 5g protein and it is the version to switch to if you drink one every day.
Does es kopi susu make you gain weight?
A daily regular is 220 calories, about 6,600 a month, which is roughly 0.8kg of fat if nothing else changes. Switching to less sugar or no sugar removes most of that.
Berapa kalori Es Kopi Susu?
Es Kopi Susu mengandung sekitar 220 kalori per satu porsi biasa (350g), dengan 5g protein, 33g karbohidrat dan 7g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
