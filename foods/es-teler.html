How Many Calories in Es Teler?

Also known as Es Teler 77, Es Teller, Es Campur, Indonesian Avocado Coconut Jackfruit Ice.

Es teler is the Jakarta dessert of avocado, young coconut flesh, jackfruit and kolang-kaling over shaved ice, drenched in coconut milk, sugar syrup and sweetened condensed milk. Es campur is the same idea with more jelly and less avocado.

425kcal / 1 bowl
A bowl of es teler is about 425 calories. FatSecret Indonesia lists a 240g bowl at 425 calories with 62g carbs, 20g fat and 5g protein, which is 177 per 100g. The fruit is about half of that. The other half is the condensed milk, coconut milk and syrup.

The real talk

Es teler is fruit and ice, which sounds light, but look at which fruit. Avocado, 80g of it, is 130 calories on its own. Young coconut flesh is about 35 for 60g, jackfruit is 40 for a few pieces, kolang-kaling is 20. That is 225 calories of fruit before the wet ingredients, and the avocado is more than half of it. Es buah, which uses melon, papaya and jelly, is about 250 a bowl for the same size.

Then the pour. A bowl gets about 60ml of santan at 70 calories and 40g of condensed milk at 130, sometimes with sugar syrup too. That 200 calories of liquid is what makes es teler es teler, and it is why the bowl is 425 with 20g of fat. The 'teler' means drunk, and the name is about the richness, not the alcohol.

It is Rp 15,000 to 25,000 at a warung and Rp 30,000 plus at the mall chains, and it comes after a full meal in the same sitting. Nasi padang at 690 plus es teler at 425 is 1,100 for lunch. Es teler is not a drink to have with food. It is dessert with a straw.

Es campur and es doger are cousins with the same problem: the fruit and jelly are cheap in calories, the condensed milk and syrup are not. Es doger, with tape and its pink syrup, is about 350. Es campur is 300 to 350. Es teler is the heaviest of the family because of the avocado and the santan.

How to eat it lighter

  • 'Tanpa susu kental manis'. Skipping the SKM takes about 130 off, and the santan still makes it creamy.
  • Ask for less santan or 'santannya sedikit'. That is another 35 to 70.
  • Share it. Half a bowl after a meal is 210 and honestly enough.
  • Order es kelapa muda instead. Young coconut and its water with a little syrup is about 120.
  • Es teler as the meal, not after one. At 425 calories with real fruit it is a fair afternoon snack if lunch was light.

Common questions

How many calories in es teler?
About 425 calories per bowl according to FatSecret Indonesia, with 62g carbs and 20g fat. Without the condensed milk it is about 300.
Es teler or es campur, which has more calories?
Es teler, at about 425 versus 330 for es campur. Es teler uses avocado and coconut milk; es campur uses more jelly and syrup with less fat.
Is es teler healthy?
The fruit is, especially the avocado and young coconut. The 200 calories of condensed milk and santan on top are what make it a dessert. Order it without SKM and it is a fair snack.
How many calories in es teler 77?
The mall chain bowl is in the same range, about 400 to 450 depending on size. The jumbo bowls with extra topping go past 550.
Berapa kalori Es Teler?
Es Teler mengandung sekitar 425 kalori per 1 mangkuk (240g), dengan 5g protein, 62g karbohidrat dan 20g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
