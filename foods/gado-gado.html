How Many Calories in Gado-Gado?

Also known as Gado Gado, Gado-Gado Betawi, Gado-Gado Siram, Indonesian Peanut Salad.

Gado-gado is blanched vegetables, lontong, fried tofu and tempe, boiled egg and emping, all covered in a thick peanut sauce ground to order. Jakarta calls it a salad. The peanut sauce disagrees.

390kcal / 1 plate (vegetables, lontong, tofu, tempe, half egg, peanut sauce, emping)
A standard warung plate of gado-gado is about 390 calories. TKPI (the Indonesian food composition table) lists gado-gado at 137 calories per 100g, and a full plate with lontong weighs around 285g. FatSecret Indonesia lists a smaller 241g bowl at 318 calories.

The real talk

The vegetables are not the problem. A pile of blanched kangkung, cabbage, long beans, bean sprouts and cucumber, about 150g of it, is 55 calories. You could eat three plates of that and nothing would happen. The problem is what goes on top. A 45g ladle of bumbu kacang is about 150 calories, because peanut sauce is fried peanuts, palm sugar, oil and a splash of coconut milk, and the gado-gado lady grinds it by hand so it is generous.

Then the starch. Most gado-gado comes with lontong or rice, and 60g of lontong is 65 calories. Tahu and tempe goreng add about 60 for a couple of small pieces. Half a boiled egg is 35, a few emping crackers are 25. None of these is big on its own. Together with the sauce they turn a 55 calorie vegetable pile into a 390 calorie plate, and that is the version FatSecret and TKPI agree on within about 20%.

The Rp 15,000 to 20,000 warung plate is honestly one of the better lunches in Jakarta. You get 16g protein from the egg, tofu and tempe, a lot of fibre and volume, and you are full for hours. The one thing to watch is the gado-gado siram at restaurants, where the sauce is poured until the plate is a lake. That version is closer to 550 calories, with 250 of it in the sauce.

For comparison: ketoprak, which is the same idea with bihun and no vegetables to speak of, is about 520 for a plate. Gado-gado wins because half the plate is actual vegetables. This was one of the plates I leaned on while dropping 42kg, because it is the rare street meal where asking for more costs nothing.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for 'bumbunya sedikit' or the sauce on the side. Half the peanut sauce saves about 75 calories and the vegetables still taste like gado-gado.
  • Skip the lontong. Without it the plate is about 325 calories and you barely notice, because the tofu and egg carry it.
  • Ask for a whole egg instead of half. It is 35 extra calories for 3g more protein.
  • Extra kangkung, extra tauge. The seller will usually pile it on for free.
  • Leave the emping. Three crackers are only 25 calories, but they are the part people go back for.

Common questions

How many calories in gado-gado?
About 390 calories for a standard plate with lontong, tofu, tempe, half an egg and peanut sauce. TKPI lists 137 calories per 100g.
Is gado-gado good for weight loss?
Yes, one of the best street options. Half the plate is vegetables, it has 16g protein, and the only thing to control is the peanut sauce at about 150 calories per ladle.
How many calories in gado-gado peanut sauce?
About 150 calories for a 45g ladle, roughly 3.3 calories per gram. It is peanuts, palm sugar and oil, so it adds up faster than anything else on the plate.
Gado-gado or ketoprak, which has fewer calories?
Gado-gado at about 390 versus ketoprak at about 520. Ketoprak swaps most of the vegetables for bihun and lontong, so it is more starch under the same sauce.
Berapa kalori Gado-Gado?
Gado-Gado mengandung sekitar 390 kalori per 1 piring (285g), dengan 16g protein, 32g karbohidrat dan 22g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
