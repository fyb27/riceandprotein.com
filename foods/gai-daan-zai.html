How Many Calories in Egg Waffle (Gai Daan Zai)?

Also known as 雞蛋仔, Gai Daan Zai, Egg Puff, Eggette, Hong Kong Bubble Waffle.

Gai daan zai is the honeycomb-shaped egg waffle sold from street carts and hole-in-the-wall shops across Hong Kong. One sheet has about 30 little egg-shaped bubbles, crisp outside and cakey inside, for HK$15 to HK$25.

390kcal / 1 full sheet
A standard sheet of egg waffle is about 390 calories, based on the HK Department of Health figure of 389.5 kcal per piece. Larger sheets from the popular shops weigh more and land around 525 calories. Almost all of it is carbohydrate, about 70g, from flour and sugar.

The real talk

This is the snack that fools people. It is airy, it is not greasy to the touch, and it feels like nothing. But the batter is flour, sugar, egg, evaporated milk and custard powder, and one sheet carries 70g of carbs. That is the same carb load as a bowl and a half of rice, eaten standing on the pavement in Mong Kok in about 6 minutes.

Sugar is the main driver. A typical batter uses around 30g of sugar per sheet, which is 115 calories before the flour even counts. That is why the outside caramelises and goes crisp. Fat is actually modest at about 8g, which is why it scores better than a Belgian style waffle (格仔餅) with butter, peanut butter and condensed milk, which the same data puts at 440 calories.

The trendy versions are worse. A matcha or chocolate chip sheet adds 40 to 80 calories. Roll it around a scoop of ice cream and you are at 550 or more. The plain original from a cart is the lowest calorie way to eat this, and at 390 it is still a full meal's worth of energy with only 9g of protein.

How to eat it lighter

  • Share one sheet between two people. Half a sheet is 195 calories, which is a fair snack.
  • Stick to the original flavour. Chocolate chip, cheese and matcha versions add 40 to 80 calories each.
  • Skip the ice cream roll. That version is over 550 calories, closer to a meal than a snack.
  • Eat it instead of dinner carbs, not on top of them. A sheet of egg waffle after a plate of siu mei rice is a 1,300 calorie evening.

Common questions

How many calories in one gai daan zai?
About 390 calories for a standard sheet, according to HK Department of Health data. The larger shop versions weigh more and reach 525 calories.
Is egg waffle healthier than a Belgian waffle?
Slightly. Egg waffle is about 390 calories with 8g fat. A Hong Kong style grid waffle with butter, peanut butter and condensed milk is around 440 calories with 21g fat.
Why is egg waffle so high in calories if it is not fried?
Because the batter has roughly 30g of sugar and 50g of flour per sheet. That is 70g of carbs, which is the bulk of the 390 calories. No oil needed.
