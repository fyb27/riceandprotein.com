How Many Calories in Gai Tod?

Also known as ไก่ทอด, ไก่ทอดหาดใหญ่, Kai Tod, Gai Tod Hat Yai, Thai Fried Chicken.

Thai street fried chicken, marinated in garlic, coriander root, pepper and fish sauce, coated lightly in rice flour and fried until the skin is dark and crisp. The Hat Yai version comes buried in crispy fried shallots and is sold with a bag of sticky rice.

320kcal / 1 piece (thigh, about 130g)
A single piece of gai tod, a thigh with skin of around 130g, is about 320 calories. Thai dietitian figures put a skinless breast at about 250, a thigh at 300, a hip at 350 and a thigh with hip attached at 420. The fried shallots on a Hat Yai piece add another 55 to 70 calories per 2 tablespoons.

The real talk

The cut decides the calories more than the frying does. A 120g thigh is about 300 calories, a 150g hip is 350, and the big thigh with hip piece that stalls sell for 40 baht is 420. A skinless breast piece is around 250 for 170g. So two pieces from the same tray can be 170 calories apart. If you only remember one thing, remember that the hip is the fattiest cut on the tray.

Thai fried chicken uses a thin rice flour dusting, not the thick batter you get at American style fast food chains. That matters: the coating on a piece of gai tod is maybe 15g and 45 calories, while a fast food battered thigh carries 60 to 80 calories of batter plus the oil it holds. Per piece, gai tod is usually the lighter of the two, even though it looks darker.

The full meal is where it adds up. One thigh piece plus a 150g bag of khao niao plus fried shallots and nam jim is about 600 calories. Two pieces is 900. Thai dietitians put a Hat Yai chicken meal at 500 to 800 calories depending on how many pieces and how big the rice bag is, which lines up with that.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick the breast piece (about 250 calories) or a drumstick (about 260) over the thigh with hip (420).
  • Peel the skin off one piece and you save roughly 100 calories; the marinade flavour is in the meat anyway.
  • Ask for less fried shallot on top, or brush most of it off; a big pile is 70 calories of oil soaked onion.
  • Take the small 5 baht bag of sticky rice with one piece, and add som tam for volume instead of a second piece.

Common questions

How many calories in Hat Yai fried chicken with sticky rice?
About 570 to 600 calories for one thigh piece with a 150g bag of sticky rice and fried shallots. Two pieces takes it to around 900.
Is Thai fried chicken healthier than KFC?
Per piece it is usually lower, because the rice flour dusting is thin. A gai tod thigh is about 320 calories where a heavily battered fast food thigh runs 350 to 400. The oil and the skin are still there though.
Which piece of gai tod has the fewest calories?
A wing at about 130, then skinless breast at about 250. The thigh with hip piece at 420 is the heaviest.
