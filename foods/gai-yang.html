How Many Calories in Gai Yang?

Also known as ไก่ย่าง, Thai Grilled Chicken, Isan Grilled Chicken, Kai Yang.

Whole chicken marinated in garlic, coriander root, fish sauce and palm sugar, flattened in a bamboo clamp and grilled slowly over charcoal. Sold by the half or whole at Isan stalls, always with sticky rice and som tam.

560kcal / half chicken
Half a grilled gai yang, about 300g on the bone, is roughly 560 calories with the skin on. One drumstick is about 165. The meat itself is lean at around 165 calories per 100g; the skin is where the other 200 calories sit, and it is also the best part.

The real talk

Charcoal grilled chicken is about as honest as meat gets. No batter, no deep fryer, and the fat drips into the coals instead of staying on the plate. Half a bird is 560 calories and 58g of protein, which is more protein than most people eat in a whole meal. The marinade adds 30 calories of palm sugar and fish sauce. If you were going to eat 300g of anything at a Thai stall, this is the thing to eat.

The skin is the argument. Grilled chicken skin is about 400 calories per 100g, and half a bird carries around 50g of it, so 200 of the 560 is skin. Take it off and half a chicken is about 360 calories. Nobody does this, and I am not telling you to. Just know that the skin is a third of the number, so on a day you are short, it is the easy cut.

What the chicken comes with is the real meal. A 150g bag of sticky rice is about 240 calories, som tam is 100 to 130 depending on how much palm sugar and peanut went in, and the sweet chilli dip (nam jim gai) is 60 for 2 tablespoons. A normal gai yang set is 900 to 1,000 calories and it is still one of the better lunches in the country for that number, because 60g of it is protein.

How to eat it lighter

  • Eat the half chicken with one bag of sticky rice, not two. The second bag is 240 calories you eat out of habit.
  • Peel the skin off the breast half and keep it on the leg. Saves about 100 calories and you still get the good bit.
  • Som tam Thai with less palm sugar (wan noi) drops the salad to about 90 and keeps the crunch.
  • Go easy on the nam jim gai. It is sugar syrup with chilli, about 30 calories per tablespoon.
  • Order gai yang instead of gai tod (fried chicken). The same half bird deep fried is about 800.

Common questions

Is gai yang healthy?
It is grilled, not fried, and 58g of protein in half a bird is excellent. At 560 calories with skin it is one of the better Thai mains for the size.
How many calories in gai yang with sticky rice and som tam?
About 920 for half a chicken, a 150g bag of sticky rice and a plate of som tam. Drop to one small rice bag and it is about 800.
Gai yang or gai tod, which is lower?
Gai yang by a long way. Half a grilled chicken is 560, half a deep fried one is about 800 because of the batter and oil.
How much protein is in gai yang?
Around 58g in half a chicken and about 20g in one drumstick.
