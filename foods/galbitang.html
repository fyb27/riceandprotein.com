How Many Calories in Galbitang?

Also known as 갈비탕, Korean Short Rib Soup, Beef Rib Soup, Galbi-tang.

Galbitang is beef short ribs simmered for hours in a clear broth with radish and glass noodles, served with rice, kimchi and salt on the side. The wedding banquet soup, and a 12,000 to 15,000 won lunch.

470kcal / 1 bowl (no rice)
A bowl of galbitang is about 470 calories without rice, per the fatsecret.kr 1인분 figure of 466. The 3 pieces of short rib give you around 34g of protein and most of the 30g of fat, since rib meat is marbled and the broth carries rendered fat. With the bowl of rice that comes with it, the meal is about 770.

The real talk

Short rib is a fatty cut, and the soup does not hide it. 200g of bone-in ribs, about 3 pieces, is 340 calories on its own with roughly 24g of fat. Well-run restaurants chill the broth overnight and skim the fat cap, which is why some bowls are 350 and others are 600. If there is a visible oil layer on top, assume the higher number.

The broth is otherwise honest. It is beef bone stock with radish, and 400ml is about 60 calories, less if skimmed. The 30g of glass noodles at the bottom add 45 calories, and the egg garnish and scallion another 25. Compared with seolleongtang at 425 with noodles, galbitang is close, just with more meat and more fat per bowl.

The rice is the choice. Galbitang comes with a bowl of rice, and the habit is to tip it into the soup, which is 300 calories and easy to finish because the broth makes it slide down. 770 calories with 34g of protein is a solid lunch. The 1,050 calorie version is when you take the second bowl of rice that the ajumma offers.

How to eat it lighter

  • Skim the fat off the top with your spoon before you start. That is 50 to 100 calories in the first minute.
  • Eat half the rice and put the rest aside. Half a bowl into the soup is 150 calories and still filling.
  • Ask for extra radish (무). It is nearly free and takes up space.
  • Trim the fat strip on the ribs. Each piece has about 10g of visible fat on the edge.
  • Choose galbitang over gamjatang when the choice is there. Gamjatang runs about 600 and has more fat in the broth.

Common questions

How many calories in galbitang with rice?
About 770: 470 for the soup and 300 for a standard bowl of rice tipped in.
Is galbitang healthy?
It has 34g of protein and real vegetables, but also about 30g of fat from the ribs. Skimming the broth and eating one bowl of rice keeps it around 700 for the meal.
Which is lower in calories, galbitang or seolleongtang?
Seolleongtang, at about 425 per bowl with noodles versus 470 for galbitang. Galbitang has more meat and more protein per bowl.
Why is galbitang so expensive?
Beef short rib is one of the priciest cuts in Korea, and a bowl uses 200g of it. Most restaurants charge 12,000 to 16,000 won for a bowl with 3 ribs.
