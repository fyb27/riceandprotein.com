How Many Calories in Goi Cuon?

Also known as Gỏi cuốn, Vietnamese Fresh Spring Rolls, Summer Rolls, Salad Rolls, Rice Paper Rolls.

Soft rice paper wrapped around prawns, a slice of boiled pork, bun vermicelli, lettuce and herbs, served cold with a peanut hoisin dip or nuoc cham. Sold by the pair from street carts for 10,000 to 15,000 dong each.

200kcal / 2 rolls with dipping sauce
Two goi cuon with a small bowl of peanut sauce are around 200 calories. Each roll is about 65 to 70: the rice paper is 30, the bun inside 20, the prawn and pork together 30, and the greens almost nothing. The peanut hoisin sauce is 35 for a 20ml pour; the nuoc cham option is about 15.

The real talk

Goi cuon is one of the few street snacks where the number is actually low: 65 to 70 calories per roll, 200 for two with sauce. A sheet of rice paper is about 30 calories, the 20g of bun inside is 20, and a prawn and a slice of boiled pork come to 30. There is no oil anywhere in the roll. The published figures of 49 for a prawn only roll and 67 for a pork and prawn roll match that.

The sauce is where it moves. The peanut hoisin sauce is hoisin, peanut butter, sugar and sometimes coconut milk, about 35 calories for the 20ml a cart gives you, and 90 if a restaurant gives you a full bowl and you finish it. The alternative, nuoc cham with lime, is 15. The difference over four rolls is 150 calories, which is more than two of the rolls.

The comparison people want is with cha gio, the fried version. A cha gio is 130 to 150 calories, double a goi cuon, because it is fried in oil. Four goi cuon is 260 before sauce and makes a real light lunch with 22g of protein. Four cha gio is 540. Most menus put them side by side at the same price.

How to eat it lighter

  • Choose nuoc cham (15) over peanut sauce (35 to 90). Or dip, do not pour.
  • Ask for goi cuon tom, prawn only. About 50 per roll versus 67 with pork.
  • Four rolls with nuoc cham is a 280 calorie meal with 22g of protein.
  • Skip the bun inside if the cart makes them to order. Saves 20 per roll.
  • Goi cuon over cha gio, every time. Half the calories for the same filling.

Common questions

How many calories in one goi cuon?
About 65 to 70 for a prawn and pork roll, or 50 for prawn only. Sauce adds 15 to 45 per roll depending on type and amount.
Are goi cuon good for weight loss?
Yes. They are about the lightest thing on a Vietnamese menu, with protein from the prawns and no oil. Four rolls with nuoc cham is under 300 calories.
Goi cuon or cha gio, which is healthier?
Goi cuon. A fresh roll is about 65 calories and a fried cha gio is 130 to 150, with most of the difference being frying oil.
How many calories in peanut sauce for spring rolls?
About 35 for a 20ml cart portion and up to 90 for a full restaurant bowl. Nuoc cham is about 15.
