How Many Calories in Gudeg?

Also known as Nasi Gudeg, Gudeg Jogja, Gudeg Yogyakarta, Gudeg Nangka.

Gudeg is young jackfruit stewed for hours in coconut milk and palm sugar until it turns dark and sweet. In Yogyakarta it is served over rice with spicy krecek (cow skin), a pindang egg and thick areh.

650kcal / 1 plate nasi gudeg (rice, gudeg, krecek, pindang egg, areh)
A standard plate of nasi gudeg is about 650 calories: 200g rice at 260, 100g gudeg at 130, krecek at 110, a pindang egg at 70 and a spoon of areh at 80. FatSecret Indonesia lists the gudeg stew itself at 127 calories per 100g.

The real talk

Gudeg is sweet for a reason. The jackfruit is stewed in coconut milk and gula jawa for six to eight hours, and by the end a 100g scoop is 127 calories with 22g of carbs, mostly sugar, and 5g of fat. It has almost no protein, under 2g per 100g. So the brown pile that looks like a vegetable dish is closer to a sweet stew.

Krecek is the red stuff on the side, and it is cow skin crackers rehydrated in coconut chili sauce. 50g is about 110 calories, mostly fat from the santan and the chili oil floating on top. Then areh, the thick white coconut cream, is 80 calories for a 40g spoon. Between gudeg, krecek and areh you have 320 calories before rice.

The dry Yogya style (gudeg kering) is more concentrated than the wet Solo style (gudeg basah). Kering has been cooked down longer so the same 100g scoop has more sugar and coconut, about 150 calories, while the wet version is closer to 110. If you are choosing, wet is lighter.

A Rp 15,000 to 25,000 plate at Wijilan or Mbarek gets you 650 calories with 95g of carbs and only 20g protein. Add ayam kampung opor and you are near 900, and that is the usual tourist order.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less rice and no areh. Between them that is 200 calories saved without touching the gudeg.
  • Take the telur pindang and skip the ayam opor. The egg is 70 calories with 6g protein; a piece of opor chicken in gravy is 250 or more.
  • Ask for gudeg basah (wet) if the stall does both. It has less sugar per scoop.
  • Krecek is the fattiest part of the plate. A small spoon, not a ladle.
  • Pair it with tahu or tempe bacem only if you skip the egg. Do not stack all three.

Common questions

How many calories in nasi gudeg?
About 650 calories for a plate with rice, gudeg, krecek, a pindang egg and areh. The gudeg stew itself is about 127 calories per 100g.
Is gudeg healthy?
Not really. It is sweet stewed jackfruit with coconut milk, under 2g protein per 100g and mostly sugar. It is fine as an occasional meal but it is closer to dessert than vegetables.
Does gudeg have a lot of sugar?
Yes. Roughly 22g of carbs per 100g, and most of that is palm sugar absorbed during the long stewing. The dry Yogya style has more than the wet Solo style.
How many calories in gudeg with krecek?
About 240 calories for 100g gudeg plus 50g krecek, no rice. The krecek carries most of the fat.
Berapa kalori Gudeg?
Gudeg mengandung sekitar 650 kalori per satu porsi biasa (440g), dengan 20g protein, 95g karbohidrat dan 22g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
