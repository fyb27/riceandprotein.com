How Many Calories in Gulai?

A coconut-milk spice curry that comes in many forms across Malaysia, from light gulai ikan to rich gulai kawah, with calories that rise and fall almost entirely based on how heavy the santan is.

320kcal / 1 serving (ladle of curry + 1-2 pieces protein, no rice)
Gulai comes in at around 320 calories per serving (roughly 200g, which is a ladle of curry plus 1 to 2 pieces of protein, no rice counted). That number sits mostly in the fat column because of the santan, not the chicken or fish. If you pick a leaner variant like gulai ikan you can bring it down to about 250 calories for the same serving size.

The real talk

Gulai is one of those dishes that sounds scarier than it is, calorie-wise. At 320 calories for a 200g serving with chicken and a ladle of gravy, it is not the disaster people assume. The rempah (lemongrass, turmeric, galangal and all that) barely registers at about 30 calories. The villain is the santan, which contributes roughly 110 calories just from the 80g of gravy clinging to your protein. The protein itself, at 165 calories per 100g cooked, is actually doing solid work for you. 22g of protein for 320 calories is a decent trade. Worth flagging that these are honest ballpark numbers though. There is almost no reliable lab data for home-style gulai or warung versions, so take this as a sensible estimate, not a lab result.

What actually swings the maths is the cook's hand with the santan and the protein they use. A generous pour of rich santan can quietly push a single serving from 320 to 400 calories without you noticing. Gulai ikan with tongkol or ikan patin is the leaner call, sitting around 250 calories per serving because fish is naturally lower fat than chicken thigh or beef. Gulai daging kawah, on the other hand, uses a fattier cut slow-cooked in a heavier gravy, and that lands at around 390 calories. The cooking oil adds another 15 calories from the initial frying of the rempah, which is standard for any santan-based dish. If you are eating gulai as one lauk out of a nasi campur plate, you are fine. Just go easy on the kuah lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick gulai ikan over gulai ayam or gulai daging. Tongkol or ikan patin brings the serving down to about 250 calories, saving you 70 calories before you even sit down.
  • Ask for less gravy. The santan is where the calories live. A ladle of gravy on its own is around 110 calories. Ask the makcik to go light on the kuah and you cut a meaningful chunk.
  • Eat it with plain rice, not nasi lemak or nasi minyak. The gulai itself is manageable; it is the rice base that decides whether the whole plate is 500 or 900 calories.
  • Skip the skin if you are eating gulai ayam. Chicken skin in a rich santan gravy can quietly add 40 to 60 extra calories to your plate.
  • Go for gulai tempoyak with fish rather than the kawah version with beef. The tempoyak variant sits around 320 calories versus 390 for the beef, and the flavour payoff is honestly better.

Common questions

How many calories in Gulai?
A standard serving of gulai (about 200g, one ladle of curry with 1 to 2 pieces of protein, no rice) is roughly 320 calories. That number can drop to around 250 calories if you order gulai ikan, or climb to 390 calories if you go with gulai daging kawah with a fattier cut and a richer gravy.
Is gulai good for weight loss?
It can be, actually. The protein from chicken or fish is solid, and the spice paste itself adds very little calories. The thing that inflates the number is the santan gravy. If you ask for less gravy or pick a leaner protein like tongkol or ikan patin, gulai is a reasonable lauk to have at your nasi campur plate.
What is the difference between gulai and kari (curry)?
Both use coconut milk and spice paste, but gulai is generally thinner and lighter in colour, and the flavour profile leans more towards lemongrass and turmeric rather than the heavier dry spice blend you get in Indian-style kari. Calorie-wise they are in a similar range, though kari kepala ikan or masak lemak styles can run higher depending on how much santan the cook uses.
Berapa kalori dalam Gulai?
Gulai ada kira-kira 320 kalori untuk 1 hidangan (200g), dengan 22g protein, 9g karbohidrat dan 22g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
