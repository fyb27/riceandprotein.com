How Many Calories in Gyudon?

Also known as 牛丼, ぎゅうどん, Beef Bowl, Yoshinoya Beef Bowl.

Thin sliced beef and onion simmered in a sweet soy and dashi sauce, ladled over a bowl of rice. Japan's fast food, served in under a minute at Yoshinoya, Sukiya and Matsuya.

633kcal / 1 bowl (regular)
A regular (namimori) gyudon at Yoshinoya is 633 calories, according to Yoshinoya's own nutrition data, with 19.6g of protein, 23.6g of fat and 88.2g of carbs in a 342g bowl. About 390 of those calories are the rice. The beef and sauce are the other 240.

The real talk

A regular gyudon is 633 calories at Yoshinoya and about 730 at Sukiya, because Sukiya serves a bit more rice. For around 500 yen that is one of the cheapest full meals in Japan, and the numbers are all published, which makes it easy to plan around. The rice is 250g and about 390 calories. The beef is fatty short plate cooked in sugar and soy, roughly 200 calories for the meat and 45 for the onion and sauce.

The size ladder is steep. Yoshinoya's small (komori) is about 490 calories, regular 633, atamono-omori (extra beef, same rice) about 740, omori about 850, and tokumori around 1,000. The 'mega' bowl at Sukiya is over 1,200. The chains price these only 100 to 200 yen apart, so people go up a size without thinking, and each step is roughly a bowl of miso soup's worth of rice or a burger's worth of beef.

Toppings change the picture. A raw egg is about 80 calories and 6g of protein, which is a reasonable add. Cheese is about 120. Kimchi is 20. The 'tsuyudaku' extra sauce order is mostly free in calories but adds salt. If you want the bowl to do more for you, the egg or the extra beef head is the better spend than the bigger rice.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order 'atama no omori' (extra beef, normal rice) instead of omori. More protein, about 100 fewer calories than the big rice version.
  • Add the miso soup and salad set for about 60 calories total. It slows you down and fills the bowl-sized gap.
  • Skip the cheese. That is 120 calories on top of 633.
  • Ask for 'gohan sukuname' (less rice). Yoshinoya does it free and it drops about 80 calories.
  • Yoshinoya's 'gyudon on yasai' swaps some rice for vegetables and lands around 480.

Common questions

How many calories in a Yoshinoya gyudon?
Regular is 633 calories per Yoshinoya's own data. Small is about 490, large about 850 and tokumori around 1,000.
Is Sukiya or Yoshinoya gyudon lower in calories?
Yoshinoya at 633 versus Sukiya at about 733 for regular. Sukiya's bowl carries more rice.
Is gyudon good for building muscle?
It has about 20g of protein per regular bowl, which is average. Adding an egg brings it to 26g. The 'atama no omori' extra beef version reaches about 30g.
How much rice is in a gyudon?
About 250g cooked rice in a regular bowl, which is roughly 390 calories on its own. Large bowls hold 350g or more.
