How Many Calories in Hainanese Curry Rice?

Also known as Curry Rice Singapore, 海南咖喱饭, Scissors Cut Curry Rice, Hainanese Curry Rice with Pork Chop.

Hainanese curry rice is the Singapore plate of white rice drowned in a mix of curry gravy and dark braised sauce, with a breaded fried pork chop, braised cabbage chap chye, a braised egg and often a piece of pork belly or fried fish.

760kcal / 1 plate (rice, pork chop, chap chye, egg, curry)
A standard plate of Hainanese curry rice with a pork chop is about 760 calories for a 450g serving, with around 34g of fat. The 250g of rice is about 325 calories, the breaded pork chop 260, the curry and braised gravies around 100, and the chap chye and egg the rest.

The real talk

The gravy gets the blame because the plate looks like a flood, but the fried pork chop is the biggest single item. A hawker pork chop is thin, breaded with cracker crumbs and deep fried, about 100g and 260 calories. The rice underneath is a big scoop, 250g and 325 calories. The curry gravy is thin and not very coconut heavy, so a ladle plus a ladle of braised lor sauce is only around 100. Chap chye is about 45, the braised egg 35. That is 760 for the classic order.

The trap is the second and third item. The famous curry rice stalls sell by the piece and the plate fills fast. Pork chop plus braised pork belly plus fried fish plus egg plus chap chye plus rice is over 1,100 calories. A S$6 plate can easily be that. The curry itself is not the issue. The pile of fried and braised protein is.

The way to eat this well is to pick one protein and load up on chap chye. A plate of rice, curry, chap chye, braised egg and one piece of steamed or braised pork is about 560. You still get the flood of gravy, which is what the dish is actually about.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick one protein, not three. Each extra piece of pork belly or fried fish is 150 to 250 calories.
  • Choose braised pork or fried fish over the pork chop. The breaded chop is about 260, a piece of fried fish around 150.
  • Ask for less rice. The curry rice scoop is large and 100g less rice is about 130 calories saved.
  • Double the chap chye. It is braised cabbage at about 45 calories a portion and it soaks up the gravy the same way rice does.
  • Ask for curry gravy only, skip the lor. The braised sauce has more oil and sugar, and curry only saves around 40.

Common questions

Is Hainanese curry rice unhealthy?
With a fried pork chop and three items it is around 760 to 1,100 calories, so yes, it is a heavy plate. Ordered with one braised protein and chap chye it is about 560 with 30g of protein, which is a normal lunch.
How many calories in the curry rice gravy?
About 60 calories per ladle of the curry and 40 for the braised lor sauce. It is thinner and less coconut heavy than Malay or Indian curry, which is why the gravy is not the biggest number on the plate.
How many calories in a Hainanese pork chop?
About 260 calories for a hawker piece of around 100g. It is thin pork loin, breaded with cream cracker crumbs and deep fried, so the crust and oil are most of it.
Is curry rice or nasi campur higher in calories?
Similar. Economy rice with three dishes is about 720 and Hainanese curry rice with a pork chop about 760. Both depend on how many fried items land on the plate.
Berapa kalori dalam Hainanese Curry Rice?
Hainanese Curry Rice ada kira-kira 760 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (450g), dengan 30g protein, 85g karbohidrat dan 34g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
