How Many Calories in Hakka Lei Cha (Thunder Tea Rice)?

A Hakka rice bowl loaded with stir-fried vegetables and poured over with a fragrant herb tea broth, one of the genuinely lighter hawker meals you can order without feeling guilty.

430kcal / 1 bowl
Hakka Lei Cha (Thunder Tea Rice) comes in at 430 calories per bowl (roughly 400g). That is genuinely not bad for a full rice meal. The rice itself accounts for 195 of those calories, the mixed vegetables add only 80, and the herb broth is basically free at 30. What nudges the number up is the peanut and sesame topping, which is 75 calories in just 15 grams. So yes, the "healthy" dish has its traps, but all things considered, 430 cal for a proper filling bowl is a solid deal.

The real talk

Lei Cha is probably one of the most underrated hawker options if you are watching your calories. At 430 calories for a full bowl, you are getting rice, a mountain of vegetables, tofu, anchovies, and that earthy herb broth all at once. The vegetables and broth do almost nothing to the calorie count, which is the whole point. The real calorie driver is the peanut and sesame topping, 75 calories in just 15 grams. That tiny pile of nuts costs you almost as much as 100g of cooked rice. Most people do not even think about it because it looks like a garnish, but it is basically nut butter territory in terms of calorie density.

What actually moves the number up or down is simple. The oil used when stir-frying those vegetables varies a lot between stalls. A heavy-handed wok with extra oil can easily add 50 to 80 calories across the vegetable portion. The peanut portion is the other lever: ask for less topping and you drop about 35 to 50 calories without losing any satisfaction from the meal. If you swap white rice for brown rice the calorie count stays roughly the same at 430, but you get more fibre which helps you feel full longer. And if you ask the stall to go easy on the chai por (preserved radish), you are also cutting a bit of sodium, which matters if you are retaining water.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for half the peanut and sesame topping. You save roughly 35 calories and you honestly still taste it.
  • Request less oil on the stir-fried vegetables. Lei Cha stalls vary wildly here and it can add up to 80 extra calories silently.
  • Take a smaller rice portion, say 120g instead of 150g. That shaves about 32 calories and the bowl still looks huge because of the vegetables.
  • Skip or reduce the chai por (preserved radish). Lower sodium means less water retention, which shows up on the scale faster than you think.
  • Add more broth. The herb tea broth is only 30 calories per 100g, so asking for extra keeps the bowl satisfying without touching the calorie count much.

Common questions

How many calories in Hakka Lei Cha (Thunder Tea Rice)?
A standard bowl of Hakka Lei Cha is 430 calories. That covers the rice, stir-fried vegetables, tofu, anchovies, peanut topping, and herb broth together.
Is Lei Cha good for weight loss?
Bah, it is one of the better hawker options honestly. 430 calories for a full bowl is solid. The vegetables and broth are low calorie and filling. Just go easy on the peanut topping, that is where most of the fat comes from.
What makes Lei Cha high or low calorie?
The peanuts and sesame seeds are the main variable, 75 calories in just 15 grams. After that, how much oil the stall uses on the stir-fried vegetables can swing the total by 50 to 80 calories. The herb broth itself is barely 30 calories per 100g.
Berapa kalori dalam Hakka Lei Cha?
Hakka Lei Cha ada kira-kira 430 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (400g), dengan 13g protein, 55g karbohidrat dan 14g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
