How Many Calories in Hong Kong Borscht (Lo Sung Tong)?

Also known as 羅宋湯, Lo Sung Tong, Cha Chaan Teng Borscht, Hong Kong Style Russian Soup, Tomato Beef Soup.

Hong Kong borscht is a tomato based vegetable soup with cabbage, potato, carrot, onion and a few bits of beef, nothing like the beetroot original. It comes free with most cha chaan teng set meals or HK$12 to HK$18 on its own.

90kcal / 1 bowl (250ml)
A 250ml bowl of cha chaan teng borscht is about 90 calories, based on figures from a Hong Kong nutrition service (87.5 kcal per 250g bowl, 2.5g fat). It is by far the lightest of the two set soup options; the cream soup alternative is around 170 for mushroom and 325 for the version with croutons and cream.

The real talk

When the waiter asks 紅湯定白湯 (red soup or white soup), red is the right answer every time. Borscht is about 90 calories a bowl and cream of mushroom is 170. The cream soups are made with roux and evaporated milk, so a bowl carries 11g or more of fat. Borscht is tomato, cabbage and stock with almost no fat, about 2.5g. Choosing red over white every workday saves around 400 calories a week without changing anything else you eat.

The soup is mostly vegetables and water, which is why the number is low. A bowl has maybe 15g of beef, 60g of cabbage, potato and carrot, and 170ml of tomato stock. The tomato paste and a bit of sugar give it around 12g of carbs. This is the closest thing to a volume food on a cha chaan teng menu: 250ml of hot food for 90 calories that takes the edge off before the main plate arrives.

The catch is salt. The same nutrition service put the sodium at around 1,000mg per bowl, and a dietitian estimate elsewhere puts some cha chaan teng versions near 1,950mg, which is a full day's worth. Restaurants boost the flavour with tomato paste, stock cubes and salt because the vegetables are cheap and bland. So the calories are fine; the sodium is not, and a person watching blood pressure should treat it as a few spoonfuls rather than a bowl to finish.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick borscht over cream soup. That single choice is about 80 to 235 calories saved per set.
  • Do not add the bread roll and butter that comes with the soup. That is about 180 calories on top of a 90 calorie soup.
  • If sodium matters to you, eat the vegetables and beef with a spoon and leave the liquid.
  • Make it at home with no stock cube. A homemade bowl with fresh tomatoes and lean beef is about 70 calories and under 400mg of sodium.

Common questions

How many calories in Hong Kong borscht?
About 90 calories per 250ml bowl, with around 2.5g of fat, based on Hong Kong nutrition service figures. The cream soup alternative is 170 to 325.
Is Hong Kong borscht healthy?
Low in calories and fat, and mostly vegetables. The problem is sodium, around 1,000mg or more per bowl, so it is not a soup to finish every day.
Why does Hong Kong borscht have no beetroot?
It came via Shanghai in the 1930s, where beetroot was hard to get, so cooks used tomato and cabbage instead. The tomato version is about 90 calories a bowl and stuck.
