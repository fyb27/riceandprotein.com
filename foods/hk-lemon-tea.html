How Many Calories in Hong Kong Iced Lemon Tea?

Also known as 凍檸茶, Dung Ling Cha, Iced Lemon Tea, 檸茶, Cha Chaan Teng Lemon Tea.

Iced lemon tea is black tea over ice with three or four lemon slices you crush with a long spoon, sweetened with syrup. It is the most ordered cold drink in Hong Kong cha chaan tengs, about HK$18 to HK$22.

140kcal / 1 glass (iced, 300ml)
One 300ml glass of iced lemon tea at regular sweetness is about 140 calories, all from roughly 25g of sugar syrup. Order it less sweet (少甜) and it drops to around 90 calories. The hot version with half a teaspoon of sugar is only 36 calories.

The real talk

This drink has a health halo it does not deserve. No milk, has lemon, looks light. But the tea and lemon contribute almost nothing; CFS data shows the 140 calories come entirely from about 25g of sugar syrup, which is 6 teaspoons. That is more sugar than a glass of iced milk tea (22g). It just does not taste as sweet because the lemon masks it.

The gap between hot and iced is huge. Hot lemon tea at a cha chaan teng is 36 calories because they give you a small cup and half a teaspoon of sugar. Iced is a 300ml glass and the syrup goes in by the ladle. Same ingredients, nearly four times the calories. If you are drinking it for the lemon, the hot one gives you that for basically nothing.

Less sweet actually works here more than with milk drinks. Because there is no milk fat, the sugar is the only variable. CFS puts less-sweet iced lemon tea at 90 calories and 18g sugar. Ask for no sugar (走甜) and it is under 15 calories, and you can still crush the lemon in and have a drink that tastes like something.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it 走甜 (no sugar). You get the same tea and lemon for under 15 calories.
  • If no sugar is too harsh, 少甜 (less sweet) brings it to 90 calories, saving 50.
  • Go hot instead of iced when you can. Hot lemon tea is 36 calories.
  • Crush the lemon slices hard and let the drink sit a minute. The stronger lemon flavour makes the reduced sugar version taste fine.

Common questions

How much sugar is in a Hong Kong iced lemon tea?
About 25g at regular sweetness, roughly 6 teaspoons. That is more than half the WHO daily suggestion of 40g, in one glass.
Is iced lemon tea healthier than milk tea?
Barely. Iced lemon tea is 140 calories and iced milk tea is 130. Lemon tea has no fat but more sugar. Order either one less sweet and the difference shrinks to nothing.
Does iced lemon tea help with weight loss?
Only the no-sugar version. At 140 calories a glass, the regular version is a small snack's worth of pure sugar. Order 走甜 and it becomes a near-zero calorie drink.
