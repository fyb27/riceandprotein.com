How Many Calories in KL Hokkien Mee?

KL's dark fried thick noodles, glistening with pork lard. One of the heaviest plates in the city.

720kcal / 1 plate
A plate of KL Hokkien mee is around 720 calories with 37g of fat. The thick noodles are fried in dark soy and pork lard, and that rendered lard is exactly why it tastes so good and why it is so heavy.

The real talk

KL Hokkien mee is not the Penang soup, do not mix them up. This is the dark, glossy, thick yellow noodles wok fried in dark soy sauce and a serious amount of pork lard, finished with those crispy lard cubes everyone fights over. That lard is the whole flavour and the whole calorie problem. A plate is around 720 calories with 37g of fat, most of it pork fat.

It is one of the heaviest single plates of noodles you can order in KL, right up there with char kuey teow. Shiok? Absolutely. Everyday food? No way bro. Make this the treat meal of the day and keep everything else light, and you can still enjoy it.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less lard and less oil if the stall will do it.
  • Skip the extra crispy lard cubes, tempting as they are.
  • Treat it as one indulgent meal, not a regular lunch.

Common questions

How many calories in KL Hokkien mee?
Around 720 calories a plate with 37g of fat. The thick noodles are fried in dark soy and pork lard, which is the main calorie source.
Is KL Hokkien mee the same as Penang Hokkien mee?
No, completely different. KL Hokkien mee is dark, dry, fried thick noodles with lard. Penang Hokkien mee is a prawn broth soup and much lighter at around 420 calories.
Why is KL Hokkien mee so heavy?
It is wok fried in dark soy and rendered pork lard, plus crispy lard cubes on top. That pork fat is most of the 37g of fat and is what makes it one of the heaviest noodle plates around.
Berapa kalori dalam KL Hokkien Mee?
KL Hokkien Mee ada kira-kira 720 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (420g), dengan 28g protein, 70g karbohidrat dan 37g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
