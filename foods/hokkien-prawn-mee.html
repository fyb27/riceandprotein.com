How Many Calories in Hokkien Prawn Mee (Fried)?

Also known as Singapore Hokkien Mee, Fried Hokkien Mee, 福建炒虾面, Hokkien Mee SG.

Singapore Hokkien mee is yellow noodles and bee hoon fried in a rich prawn and pork stock with egg, prawns, squid and pork belly, finished with lard, lime and a spoon of sambal.

522kcal / 1 plate
A standard plate of fried Hokkien prawn mee is about 522 calories for a 442g serving, with around 19g of fat. The 200g mix of yellow noodles and bee hoon makes up about 260 calories, and the lard, oil and sambal add another 80.

The real talk

This is the wet, braised style, not the dark KL version, so the calorie count is lower than people expect. A 442g plate has about 200g of noodles, which is 260 calories, then one egg at 75, four prawns at 50, and some squid and pork belly at 70. The stall fries it in lard and finishes it with a spoon of sambal, and that is the last 80. Total around 522, which is under a plate of char kway teow at 742.

The size jump is the real issue. The S$5 plate is the 522 calorie one. The S$8 plate at the popular stalls is not a little bigger, it is about 50 percent bigger, and it comes with more prawns, more lard and an extra egg. That plate is closer to 780 calories. Two people sharing a S$10 plate get about 500 each, which is a sensible way to do it.

The sambal is small but not free. A full spoon of hawker sambal is around 40 calories because it is fried in oil. The lime is free and does more for the taste than the sambal does. Squeeze the lime, use half the sambal, and you have lost nothing you will notice.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order the S$5 plate, not the S$8 one. The bigger plate is about 260 calories more.
  • Ask for no pork lard bits on top. That is around 50 calories of pure fat.
  • Use half the sambal and all of the lime. Sambal is about 40 calories a spoon, lime is zero.
  • Share a large plate with one other person and each add a side of vegetables or a boiled egg for protein.
  • Eat the prawns and squid. They are the lowest calorie part of the plate and give most of the 24g of protein.

Common questions

Is Singapore Hokkien mee healthier than char kway teow?
Yes, by a good margin. A standard plate is about 522 calories against 742 for char kway teow. Hokkien mee is braised in stock, so it takes on less oil than a plate fried dry in lard.
How many calories in Hokkien mee without the noodles?
The prawns, squid, pork and egg together are about 195 calories. The noodles are the bulk of the plate at around 260, plus 80 from lard and sambal.
How much protein is in Hokkien prawn mee?
Around 24g per standard plate, from the egg, prawns, squid and pork. That is a reasonable amount for a 522 calorie lunch.
Is the KL Hokkien mee higher in calories?
Yes. KL Hokkien mee is thick noodles braised in dark soy and a lot of lard, at about 720 calories a plate. The Singapore version at 522 is the lighter of the two.
Berapa kalori dalam Hokkien Prawn Mee?
Hokkien Prawn Mee ada kira-kira 522 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (442g), dengan 24g protein, 64g karbohidrat dan 19g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
