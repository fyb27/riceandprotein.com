How Many Calories in Hu Tieu Nam Vang?

Also known as Hủ tiếu Nam Vang, Phnom Penh Noodle Soup, Hu Tieu, Kuy Teav.

Chewy tapioca and rice noodles in a clear pork bone broth with minced pork, sliced pork, a prawn, a slice of liver and a quail egg on top. The Saigon breakfast that came via Phnom Penh, 35,000 to 50,000 dong.

400kcal / 1 bowl
A bowl of hu tieu Nam Vang is about 400 calories according to Vinmec, with 180g of noodles making up 200 of that and the mixed pork, prawn, liver and quail egg toppings about 140. The dry version (hu tieu kho) with soy, oil and fried garlic tossed through is closer to 480.

The real talk

Hu tieu Nam Vang is one of the lighter noodle soups in Saigon at about 400 calories, and it is a decent protein bowl because of the mixed toppings. The 180g of hu tieu noodles is 200 calories. The toppings are a spoon of minced pork, three or four slices of lean pork, one or two prawns, a slice of liver and a quail egg, about 90g together and 140 calories. The broth is clear pork bone stock, around 50 for 300ml.

The version matters. Vinmec lists plain hu tieu thit heo at 351 calories, Nam Vang at 400, hu tieu mi (with egg noodles mixed in) at 410, hu tieu bo kho at 538 and hu tieu xao (stir fried) at 646. The dry hu tieu kho is not on their list but the dressing of dark soy, sugar, pork fat and fried garlic adds about 80 calories over the soup version.

The thing that makes hu tieu bigger than the number suggests is the free bowl of broth that comes with the dry version and the pile of fried garlic in oil on top. At a busy quan the topping bowl of toi phi (fried garlic) sits on the table and people spoon it on freely. One tablespoon is around 50 calories. Two spoons of that plus a squeeze of chili sauce and the 400 calorie bowl is 520.

How to eat it lighter

  • Stick to the soup version. Hu tieu kho adds about 80 calories of dressing.
  • Go easy on the fried garlic bowl on the table. One tablespoon is about 50 calories.
  • Ask for extra lean pork or prawns instead of extra noodles. Better protein for the calories.
  • Skip the hu tieu mi option. Mixing egg noodles in adds noodles, not toppings.
  • The bean sprouts, chives and lettuce on the side plate are free. Eat all of them.

Common questions

How many calories in hu tieu kho?
About 480 for the dry version. The soup version is 400; the difference is the dressing of dark soy, sugar, pork fat and fried garlic tossed through the noodles.
Is hu tieu healthier than pho?
Slightly. Hu tieu Nam Vang is about 400 calories versus 450 for pho bo, with similar protein around 26 to 28g. Both are reasonable meals.
What is in hu tieu Nam Vang?
Tapioca rice noodles, minced pork, sliced pork, prawn, pork liver, a quail egg and clear pork bone broth, topped with chives, fried garlic and bean sprouts.
How many calories in hu tieu go?
The cheaper street cart version with just noodles, a little pork and broth is around 350 calories for a smaller bowl.
