How Many Calories in Idli?

Also known as Iddli, Idly.

Steamed fermented rice cakes served with lentil sambar and coconut chutney, and one of the few mamak orders that won't wreck your calorie budget.

275kcal / 2 pieces with sambar and coconut chutney
A standard mamak idli plate (2 pieces with sambar and coconut chutney) comes in at 275 calories for about 220g of food. The steamed rice cakes themselves are only 130 calories; the sambar adds another 80 and the coconut chutney brings 65. So the sides are doing nearly half the calorie work here. For a mamak order, 275 calories is genuinely one of the lightest things you can pick off the menu.

The real talk

275 calories for a full mamak plate is genuinely impressive. The idli cakes themselves are only 130 calories for two pieces, steamed with zero oil, which is basically unheard of at a mamak. The sambar chips in 80 calories and brings actual protein from the lentils, so it's not just empty carbs. The coconut chutney is where most people don't think twice, but those 30g add 65 calories mostly from coconut fat. It's still not a lot. If you're the kind of person who was 110kg and eating roti canai with three teh tariks for breakfast, switching to idli is honestly a cheat code.

What actually changes the maths is the oil and the portion size. Plain idli with just sambar drops you to 210 calories, so skipping the coconut chutney saves 65 without losing much enjoyment. The dangerous version is masala idli, where the cakes get tossed in spiced oil with vegetables and suddenly you're at 320 calories. Still not terrible by mamak standards, but you've added almost 50 calories from oil you probably didn't think about. Sambar is the better side to double up on if you're still hungry, it's mostly lentils and vegetables and won't move the needle much. Two orders of plain idli with sambar comes to 420 calories, which for most people is a perfectly filling breakfast.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order idli with sambar only and skip the coconut chutney to cut 65 calories and drop to 210 total.
  • Avoid masala idli. The spiced oil toss adds roughly 45 calories compared to the standard plate.
  • If you want more volume, ask for extra sambar instead of extra chutney. Sambar is mostly lentils and vegetables, so the calorie cost per spoon is much lower.
  • Pair with plain water or a small kopi-o kosong instead of teh tarik, or you've doubled the calorie count of the whole meal.
  • Two orders of plain idli with sambar is still only around 420 calories and much more filling than one roti canai.

Common questions

How many calories in Idli?
A standard mamak idli plate (2 pieces with sambar and coconut chutney) is 275 calories for about 220g. The idli cakes alone are 130 calories, sambar adds 80, and coconut chutney contributes 65.
Is idli good for weight loss?
Yes, it's one of the best mamak options if you're watching calories. The cakes are steamed with no oil, and the sambar gives you some protein from lentils. Just watch the coconut chutney and avoid masala idli if you want to keep things light.
How does idli compare to roti canai for calories?
Idli wins easily. A standard roti canai with dhal is around 360 to 400 calories, and that's before you add curry. Idli with sambar and chutney is 275 calories, and you get more volume for the price.
Berapa kalori dalam Idli?
Idli ada kira-kira 275 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (220g), dengan 10g protein, 44g karbohidrat dan 6g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
