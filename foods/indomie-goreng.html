How Many Calories in Indomie Goreng?

Also known as Indomie Mi Goreng, Indomie Goreng Telur, Indomie Goreng Original.

Indomie Mi Goreng is Indonesia's instant fried noodle, 85g of noodles with a seasoning oil, kecap manis, chili sauce and fried shallot sachet. Most people cook it with an egg.

455kcal / 1 pack cooked with 1 fried egg
One pack of Indomie goreng with a fried egg is about 455 calories. The packaging lists 380 calories per 85g pack (14g fat, 54g carbs, 8g protein), and a fried egg adds about 75. At a warkop with double noodles it is 835.

The real talk

The label is honest, and the number is 380 per pack. Most of that is the noodle block itself, about 300 calories, because instant noodles are flash fried in palm oil before they are dried. The seasoning sachets add about 80 more: the oil sachet is about 45 calories, the kecap manis 25, the chili and shallot the rest.

The problem is nobody eats one dry pack. The standard warkop order is 'Indomie goreng telur' at Rp 10,000 to 15,000, and the egg is fried in more oil, so that is 75 to 100 calories on top. Add a slice of cheese (about 60) or a kornet spoon (about 70) and you are at 600. Indomie goreng double with telur and kornet is 900 calories, and it is 11pm.

Protein is the weak point. One pack is 8g, the egg brings it to 14g. For 455 calories that is low, which is why you are hungry again in two hours. Two packs is not the fix, that is 760 calories for 16g protein. Two eggs on one pack is 530 calories for 20g protein and holds much better.

If you add the full oil sachet plus your own oil for the egg, the pack is about 19g fat, which is around 40% of the calories. Draining the noodles properly and skipping half the oil sachet takes about 25 calories off. Small, but free.

How to eat it lighter

  • One pack, two eggs, not two packs, one egg. Same 500ish calories with 20g protein instead of 16g.
  • Use half the oil sachet. The kecap and chili carry the flavour; the oil sachet is 45 calories of palm oil.
  • Throw in a handful of sawi or cabbage while the noodles boil. Adds volume for about 10 calories.
  • Skip the cheese and kornet at the warkop. Each one is 60 to 70 calories with little to show for it.
  • Do not drink the boiling water. Obvious, but the rebus version's soup is where the sodium and half the seasoning oil ends up.

Common questions

How many calories in one pack of Indomie goreng?
380 calories per 85g pack according to the packaging, with 14g fat, 54g carbs and 8g protein. With a fried egg it is about 455.
How many calories in Indomie goreng telur?
About 455 calories. The pack is 380 and a fried egg is roughly 75, a bit more if the warkop fries it in a lot of oil.
Is Indomie goreng bad for weight loss?
One pack with two eggs is about 530 calories and 20g protein, which fits a normal day. Two packs at once is 760 calories with barely any protein, and that is the version that causes problems.
How many calories in Indomie goreng double?
Two packs is 760 calories before the egg. With egg and kornet, about 900.
Berapa kalori Indomie Goreng?
Indomie Goreng mengandung sekitar 455 kalori per satu porsi biasa (250g), dengan 14g protein, 55g karbohidrat dan 19g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
