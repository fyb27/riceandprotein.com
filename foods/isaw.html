How Many Calories in Isaw?

Also known as Isaw Manok, Grilled Chicken Intestine, Isaw Baboy.

Chicken (or pork) intestines cleaned, boiled, threaded onto a skewer in a zigzag, then grilled and brushed with a sweet soy and ketchup sauce. Dipped in spiced vinegar, sold at 10 to 15 pesos a stick outside every campus.

45kcal / 1 stick (chicken)
One stick of chicken isaw is about 45 calories for a 30g skewer with its basting sauce. The intestine itself is about 30 calories and the sweet glaze about 15. Pork isaw is fattier and bigger, around 90 per stick. Five chicken sticks with vinegar is about 225 calories.

The real talk

Isaw is the cheapest high protein snack on the street and the numbers back it up. Chicken intestine is about 110 calories per 100g cooked with 15g of protein, and after cleaning and boiling a stick carries about 25g of it, so around 30 calories. Then the vendor brushes on the sauce: soy, banana ketchup, sugar and a bit of oil. About 5g clings per stick, 15 calories. Five sticks is 225 calories with 20g of protein, which beats most merienda.

Pork isaw (isaw baboy) is the heavy one. Pork intestine is about 200 calories per 100g cooked because of the fat lining, and the sticks are thicker at about 40g, so 90 calories each. Five pork sticks is 450. Same cart, same sauce, double the number. The vinegar dip (sukang maanghang) is about 5 calories per dunk, so it is free for practical purposes.

The real concern with isaw is not calories but cleaning. A well cleaned intestine is boiled for an hour before grilling. A badly cleaned one gives you a bad night. Pick the cart with a queue. The other thing to watch is the sweet sauce: some vendors dredge the stick through it twice, which doubles the glaze to about 30 calories per stick. Not a big deal on 3 sticks, more noticeable on 10.

How to eat it lighter

  • Chicken isaw over pork isaw. 45 versus 90 per stick, and the chicken has more protein per calorie.
  • Dip in vinegar, not the sweet sauce cup. The vinegar is about 5 calories, the sweet sauce about 30 per spoon.
  • Five sticks is a snack at 225. Ten sticks is a small meal at 450. Both are fine, just know which one you are having.
  • Skip the rice. Isaw is one of the few street foods that does not need it.

Common questions

Is isaw healthy?
For a street snack, yes. Chicken isaw is about 45 calories a stick with 4g of protein and very little fat. The risks are cleaning and hygiene, not calories.
How many calories in 10 sticks of isaw?
About 450 calories for chicken isaw, about 900 for pork isaw. The sweet sauce adds about 15 per stick either way.
Is isaw high in cholesterol?
Moderately. Intestines carry about 200mg of cholesterol per 100g, so 5 chicken sticks is about 250mg. If you are watching cholesterol, keep it to a few sticks.
Chicken or pork isaw, which is lower in calories?
Chicken, at about 45 per stick versus 90 for pork. Pork intestine has more fat in the lining and the sticks are bigger.
