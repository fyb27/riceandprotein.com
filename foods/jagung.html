How Many Calories in Jagung (Sweet Corn Cup)?

Sweet corn kernels served in a cup with butter, condensed milk, sugar and sometimes cheese powder, a classic pasar malam snack that tastes innocent but carries a real calorie punch from the toppings.

330kcal / 1 cup (hawker portion)
One standard hawker cup of jagung, the kind with butter, condensed milk and a sugar sprinkle, comes in at around 330 calories for a 200g portion. The corn itself is only about 130 of those calories. The other 200 come from 15g of butter, a drizzle of condensed milk and whatever else the uncle decides to throw on top. So yeah, it is a snack, not a meal, but it is not exactly free either.

The real talk

Jagung cups are everywhere at pasar malam and bazaar Ramadan stalls. You get a styrofoam cup of boiled sweet corn kernels and the uncle ladles on melted butter, drizzles condensed milk, sprinkles sugar, and sometimes hits it with cheese powder. The corn on its own is genuinely fine, 130 calories for 150g is decent volume for a snack. The problem is that butter (108 calories from just 15g) and the condensed milk drizzle (another 64 calories from 20g) together basically double the calorie count before you even finish queuing. The full loaded version with cheese powder and extra sugar pushes up to around 380 calories. Fair warning, these are honest ballpark estimates because detailed lab data for Malaysian hawker jagung cups is basically nonexistent, so treat the numbers as a sensible guide rather than gospel.

What actually moves the number is simple: the toppings, not the corn. Plain boiled kernels with nothing on them sit at 130 calories. Add butter only and you are at 240. Add the condensed milk drizzle on top of that and you land at the standard 330. Ask the stall uncle to go light on the butter and skip the condensed milk and you can reasonably drop 100 to 170 calories off the top. Jagung bakar (grilled corn on the cob brushed with spicy butter) is actually a slightly lighter choice at around 290 calories because the butter gets brushed on rather than poured, and there is usually less condensed milk involved. If you are calorie-watching at a bazaar, jagung bakar with one ear of corn is probably your better bet compared to the full loaded cup.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask the uncle to go light on the butter, even half the usual amount cuts roughly 50 to 60 calories right there.
  • Skip the condensed milk drizzle entirely, the corn is sweet enough on its own and you save 64 calories.
  • Choose jagung bakar (grilled cob) over the cup version, the brush application uses less butter and less condensed milk, coming in around 290 calories.
  • Skip the cheese powder, it only adds texture but contributes another 15 calories you probably do not notice anyway.
  • Treat it as a one-off snack, not an add-on to a full meal, because at 330 calories it is closer to a light meal than a side bite.

Common questions

How many calories in Jagung (Sweet Corn Cup)?
A standard hawker jagung cup with butter, condensed milk and sugar is around 330 calories for a 200g portion. Plain boiled corn with no toppings is just 130 calories, while the full loaded version with cheese powder and extra sugar can reach 380 calories.
What exactly is in a jagung cup?
It is boiled sweet corn kernels served in a cup, typically topped with melted butter, a drizzle of sweetened condensed milk, a sprinkle of sugar, and sometimes cheese powder. At bazaar Ramadan stalls you often also see jagung bakar, which is a whole ear of corn grilled and brushed with a sweet-spicy butter glaze.
Is jagung a healthy snack?
The corn itself, yes. Sweet corn is decent fibre, reasonable carbs, and the plain kernel version is only 130 calories. The issue is what gets added on top. Butter and condensed milk together push a normal cup to 330 calories, which is fine as a planned snack but adds up quickly if you are having it on top of a full meal at the pasar malam.
Berapa kalori dalam Jagung?
Jagung ada kira-kira 330 kalori untuk 1 cawan (200g), dengan 6g protein, 35g karbohidrat dan 16g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
