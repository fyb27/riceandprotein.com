How Many Calories in Japanese Curry Rice?

Also known as カレーライス, カレー, Kare Raisu, CoCo Ichibanya Curry, Pork Curry.

Thick, mildly sweet brown curry sauce made from a roux block, poured over a plate of white rice with fukujinzuke pickles on the side. CoCo Ichibanya lets you pick rice weight, spice level and toppings, which is where the calories swing.

701kcal / 1 plate (pork curry, rice 300g)
A standard plate of Japanese curry rice is about 701 calories, which is CoCo Ichibanya's published figure for its pork curry with 300g of rice. The rice is 468 of that and the sauce about 233. Beef curry is 823, chicken nikomi 769, and a rosu katsu curry is 1,116.

The real talk

The rice is two thirds of the plate. CoCo Ichi's standard 300g of rice is 468 calories before a drop of sauce lands on it, and the pork curry sauce itself is only about 230. That is the opposite of what most people assume. The curry looks rich, but it is a thin layer of roux, flour and a little pork over a very large pile of carbohydrate. At 127g of carbs, one plate is more carbs than 3 bowls of ramen noodles.

Toppings are how a 701 calorie plate becomes a 1,100 one. Rosu katsu curry is 1,116 by CoCo Ichi's own table, fried chicken curry is 1,026, and the cheese topping adds about 150. The base curry costs about 600 yen, and each topping is 200 to 300 yen, so the bill and the calories climb together. A 'katsu, cheese, extra sauce' order is 1,300 calories for about 1,200 yen.

The good news is that CoCo Ichi is the easiest chain in Japan to order light at. Rice comes in 100g steps. Drop from 300g to 200g and the plate is about 545 calories. Choose the 150g option and it is 467. Add the spinach or vegetable topping instead of katsu and you have a 550 calorie plate with something green on it, which is rare for a curry shop.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order rice at 200g instead of 300g. Saves 156 calories, and the plate still looks full.
  • Pick pork or chicken nikomi over beef. Beef sauce is about 120 calories more per plate.
  • Skip the katsu. Rosu katsu curry is 1,116, pork curry is 701.
  • Vegetable toppings (spinach, asparagus, mushroom) add 20 to 60. Cheese, egg and cream croquette add 100 to 200.
  • Eat the fukujinzuke pickles freely. A serving is 3 calories.

Common questions

How many calories in CoCo Ichibanya curry?
The base pork curry with 300g of rice is 701 calories. Beef is 823, chicken nikomi 769, rosu katsu curry 1,116. Every 100g of rice changes the total by 156.
Is Japanese curry high in calories?
The sauce is moderate at about 230 calories per serving. The rice is the issue at 468 for a standard 300g plate. Cut the rice to 200g and a plate is 545.
How many calories in a curry roux block?
One block of supermarket roux like Vermont or Java Curry is about 90 to 100 calories and makes one serving of sauce. Most of that is fat and flour.
What is the lowest calorie order at CoCo Ichi?
Pork curry with 150g rice and a vegetable topping, around 500 calories. The soup curry and cauliflower rice options go lower still, under 400.
