How Many Calories in Jjamppong?

Also known as 짬뽕, Korean Spicy Seafood Noodle Soup, Jjambbong, Champong.

Jjamppong is the spicy red seafood noodle soup from Korean Chinese restaurants: thick wheat noodles in a chili-oil broth with squid, mussels, pork and stir-fried cabbage. It is the other half of the jajangmyeon decision.

690kcal / 1 bowl
A bowl of jjamppong is about 690 calories, based on the Korean MFDS restaurant survey figure of 688 for a full 1인분 bowl. Around 360 of that is the 250g of wheat noodles and another 130 is the chili oil the vegetables are stir-fried in. The seafood and pork add about 150 with 30g of protein.

The real talk

Jjamppong looks like the light choice next to jajangmyeon because it is a soup. It is about 690 calories to jajangmyeon's 797, so yes, lighter, but only by 100. The noodles are identical: 250g of thick wheat noodles at about 360 calories. The difference is that the black bean sauce is fried in more oil than the chili broth.

The broth is not free. Restaurants stir-fry the cabbage, onion, squid and pork in a wok with 15g or more of chili-infused oil before adding stock, and that oil ends up as the red slick on top of the soup. Drink all the broth and you take in most of that 130 calories plus around 2,500mg of sodium, which is more than a full day's worth.

At 8,000 to 10,000 won a bowl it is a big lunch, and the bowls are large: 900g to 1,000g with broth. The lunch special that pairs it with a half plate of tangsuyuk pushes the meal past 1,300 calories. If you finish the noodles and leave half the broth, the bowl is closer to 620.

How to eat it lighter

  • Leave the broth. Eat the noodles, seafood and vegetables and you save about 70 calories and roughly half the sodium.
  • Order jjamppong-bap (짬뽕밥) if you can control the rice. A bowl of rice is 300 calories versus 360 for the noodles, and rice does not soak up the chili oil the same way.
  • Skip the tangsuyuk set. That half plate is 600 calories on its own.
  • Ask for extra vegetables (야채 많이). Most shops will, and the cabbage and bean sprouts are nearly free.
  • Pick the seafood-only version (해물짬뽕) over chadol (brisket) jjamppong, which is about 880.

Common questions

Is jjamppong or jajangmyeon lower in calories?
Jjamppong, at about 690 versus 797 for jajangmyeon. The noodles are the same; the black bean sauce uses more oil than the chili broth.
How much sodium is in jjamppong?
Roughly 2,500mg per bowl if you drink the broth, well over the 2,000mg daily guideline. Leaving the broth roughly halves it.
Is jjamppong healthy?
It has 30g of protein from seafood and pork and a decent amount of vegetables, but 690 calories and the sodium make it a full meal, not a light one.
How many calories in jjamppong-bap?
About 550 with a standard bowl of rice. Rice is slightly lower than the noodles and absorbs less chili oil.
