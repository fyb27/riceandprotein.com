How Many Calories in Jollibee Spaghetti?

Also known as Jolly Spaghetti, Filipino Sweet Spaghetti, Jollibee Spaghetti Solo.

Spaghetti in a sweet tomato and banana ketchup sauce with ground meat, sliced hotdog and grated cheese. The birthday party dish of every Filipino kid, still on the menu for adults.

560kcal / 1 regular serving (solo)
A regular solo Jolly Spaghetti is about 560 calories for a 280g serving, per the FatSecret Jollibee listing (72g carbs, 25g fat, 10g protein). The noodles are about 280 of that, the sweet sauce with meat and hotdog about 230, and the cheese about 50.

The real talk

Sweet spaghetti is heavier than it tastes because the sauce is half sugar. Banana ketchup, tomato sauce and added sugar make the sauce about 130 calories per 100g before the meat goes in. A solo serving gets around 120g of sauce with ground pork and hotdog slices, which is 230 calories. The 200g of cooked noodles is 280. FatSecret lists it at 560 per container, and a second listing for the Jolly Spaghetti at 610, so 560 to 610 is the range.

The protein is low for the calorie cost. About 15g in a solo serving, most of it from the hotdog and the sprinkle of cheese. Compare that to a 1-pc Chickenjoy with rice at 600 calories and 30g of protein. If you are choosing between the two for the same calories, the chicken meal keeps you full longer.

The combos are where it goes over 1,000. Chickenjoy with Jolly Spaghetti, which is the classic birthday plate, is about 860 calories. The family pan (about 1.2kg) is around 2,400, meant for 4 to 5 people but easily eaten by 3. At about 65 pesos for a solo it is cheap, and the sweetness means adults eat it fast and go back for the second serving at parties.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it solo, not as the Chickenjoy combo. That is 560 versus 860.
  • Skip the extra cheese sachet. About 50 calories for a sprinkle that is mostly salt.
  • At a party, take half a plate of spaghetti and a piece of chicken. About 580 total and you get both.
  • Do not pair it with the Coke float. A regular float is about 250 calories on top.

Common questions

How many calories in Jollibee spaghetti with chicken?
About 860 calories for the Chickenjoy with Jolly Spaghetti combo: 560 for the spaghetti and about 300 for the chicken piece.
Why is Filipino spaghetti sweet?
Banana ketchup and added sugar in the sauce. That sugar is also why a serving carries about 75g of carbs, more than 1.3 cups of rice.
Is Jollibee spaghetti or Chickenjoy with rice lower in calories?
They are close: about 560 for the spaghetti and 600 for a 1-pc Chickenjoy with rice. The Chickenjoy meal has double the protein, so it is the better pick for the same calories.
How many calories in a Jollibee spaghetti family pan?
About 2,400 for the whole pan. Split 4 ways that is 600 each, split 3 ways about 800.
