How Many Calories in Jus Alpukat?

Also known as Jus Alpukat Susu, Alpukat Kocok, Jus Alpukat Coklat, Avocado Juice.

Jus alpukat is avocado blended with ice, sugar syrup and sweetened condensed milk, often with chocolate condensed milk streaked down the inside of the glass. It is on every jus stall menu in Indonesia and it is the one people think is a health drink.

320kcal / 1 warung glass (about 350ml, with condensed milk)
A warung glass of jus alpukat is about 320 calories. FatSecret Indonesia lists avocado juice at 130 calories per 100g, so a 350ml glass that weighs about 300g after ice comes to 320. The avocado is about 190 of that; the condensed milk and syrup are 130.

The real talk

Avocado is the densest fruit on the stall. Half a medium avocado, about 120g, is 190 calories on its own, nearly all from fat. That is fine fat, the kind you want, but it is still 190 calories before anything else goes in the blender. A jus jeruk (orange) from the same stall is about 110. Jus alpukat is three times that and people order it because it is 'the healthy one'.

Then the stall makes it taste like dessert. Two tablespoons of sweetened condensed milk, 30g, is 95 calories. A squeeze of sugar syrup or gula cair, another 35. The chocolate SKM painted around the glass, which is the whole point visually, is 30 more. FatSecret's 130 per 100g figure already includes some of that sweetener, and a Rp 12,000 to 18,000 warung glass is bigger than the 150g lab serving, so 320 is the honest number.

Alpukat kocok, the trending version with thicker avocado, more condensed milk and sometimes a scoop of ice cream or a cheese topping, is 400 to 500 a cup. That is a meal's worth of calories in a drink, and it comes after the meal.

The fix is easy because the avocado itself is the good part. Jus alpukat 'tanpa gula, tanpa susu' is about 200 calories, all avocado, with 4g protein and 7g fibre, and it is filling in a way that no other drink on the menu is. The problem was never the fruit.

How to eat it lighter

  • 'Tanpa susu, tanpa gula'. Avocado, ice and water only is about 200 calories and it is still creamy because the avocado is.
  • 'Susunya sedikit'. One tablespoon of SKM instead of two saves about 50.
  • Skip the chocolate SKM streaks. They are about 30 calories of decoration.
  • Order it as a snack, not a drink with a meal. 320 calories next to a 600 calorie nasi padang is a 900 calorie lunch.
  • Small glass. The Rp 10,000 small at most stalls is about 250ml and closer to 230 calories.

Common questions

How many calories in jus alpukat?
About 320 calories for a standard warung glass with condensed milk and syrup. FatSecret Indonesia lists 130 calories per 100g. Without the sweeteners it is about 200.
Is jus alpukat good for diet?
The avocado is: healthy fat, fibre, very filling. The condensed milk and syrup are not. Order it tanpa gula tanpa susu and it is a decent 200 calorie snack. With everything it is a 320 calorie dessert.
How many calories in alpukat kocok?
About 450 calories, and up to 500 with ice cream or cheese on top. It uses more avocado and a lot more condensed milk than a regular jus.
Does jus alpukat make you fat?
Only the way anything does. 320 calories in a glass next to a full meal is the issue, not the avocado. One a day as a snack is fine if it replaces something.
Berapa kalori Jus Alpukat?
Jus Alpukat mengandung sekitar 320 kalori per satu porsi biasa (300g), dengan 4g protein, 38g karbohidrat dan 17g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
