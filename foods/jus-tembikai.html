How Many Calories in Jus Tembikai (Watermelon Juice)?

Blended watermelon juice, around 130 calories, higher when the stall adds sugar syrup.

130kcal / 1 glass
A glass of jus tembikai is roughly 130 calories. Watermelon is mostly water so plain it would be light, but most stalls add sugar syrup even though the fruit is already sweet. That added sugar is what pushes it up. Pure watermelon blended with nothing added would be closer to 90.

The real talk

Watermelon is like 90 percent water, so you would think jus tembikai is basically free. Problem is the stall almost always adds sugar syrup, even when the fruit is already sweet enough. That habit turns a light fruit juice into a 130 calorie glass.

It is still better than a Milo Dinosaur lah, you do get actual fruit. But juicing strips the fibre, so you lose the part that keeps you full. Honestly eating a wedge of real watermelon beats drinking it, fewer calories and you actually chew something.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for no added sugar, the watermelon is sweet enough already
  • Request it blended with just ice, no syrup
  • Eat a wedge of real watermelon instead, more filling and fewer calories

Common questions

How many calories in watermelon juice?
About 130 calories a glass at most stalls. No added sugar version is closer to 90.
Is watermelon juice healthy?
It has real fruit, but juicing removes the fibre and stalls add sugar. Eating the fruit is better.
Why does jus tembikai have so many calories?
The watermelon itself is light. The added sugar syrup is what pushes it up.
Berapa kalori dalam Jus Tembikai?
Jus Tembikai ada kira-kira 130 kalori untuk 1 gelas (350g), dengan 2g protein, 31g karbohidrat dan 0g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
