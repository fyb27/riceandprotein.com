How Many Calories in Kacang Putih?

One 40g packet of kacang putih is about 215 kcal. Feels like air, actually fried and oily.

215kcal / 1 small packet (40g)
A small 40 gram packet of kacang putih is around 215 calories. It is chickpeas and lentils fried with salt and spices, so you do get a decent 8g of protein, but the frying oil pushes it up. The texture is so light you forget you are eating fried food, that is exactly the problem.

The real talk

Kacang putih feels like nothing bro, you just keep dipping your hand in. But that little packet is fried and oily, easily 215 calories before the movie even starts. The crunch tricks your brain into thinking it is healthy chickpeas.

It does have real protein, 8g a packet, which is more than most snacks here. But one packet at the cinema turns into two with your drink and now you are at 430 calories sitting in the dark. Stick to one packet and you are fine.

How to eat it lighter

  • Choose roasted kacang putih over fried, you cut a chunk of the oil
  • Eat one packet and actually use the 8g protein as the win, do not double up
  • Skip the sugary cinema drink that usually comes with it, that is where it really stacks

Common questions

How many calories in a packet of kacang putih?
A small 40g packet is roughly 215 calories. Bigger paper-cone servings can easily double that.
Is kacang putih healthy?
It has good protein and fibre from chickpeas, but it is fried so it is calorie dense. Roasted versions are the better pick if you can find them.
Does kacang putih have protein?
Yes, around 8g per 40g packet, which is solid for a snack. That is the one genuine plus point here.
Berapa kalori dalam Kacang Putih?
Kacang Putih ada kira-kira 215 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (40g), dengan 8g protein, 22g karbohidrat dan 10g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
