How Many Calories in Kailan Ikan Masin?

Chinese kailan stir-fried with salted fish and garlic. A simple hawker side dish where the wok oil does most of the calorie heavy lifting.

200kcal / 1 plate / hawker scoop
Kailan ikan masin comes in at around 200 calories per hawker plate (roughly 150g). That sounds low for a stir-fry, but the kailan itself is basically free calories. The problem is the oil absorbed during wok frying adds about 110 calories on its own, and the salted fish throws in another 40. So yeah, 200 is a reasonable ballpark for a normal hawker scoop.

The real talk

Honestly, 200 calories is pretty decent for a stir-fried veg dish. The kailan itself costs you almost nothing, around 40 calories for 120g of cooked greens. Where it sneaks up on you is the oil. A wok fry absorbs roughly 14-15g of oil, and that alone is 110 calories. The salted fish adds another 40. So you're basically paying 150 calories just for the cooking method and flavour. Fair trade if you ask me. The ikan masin is genuinely tasty and you get 9g of protein from a veg dish, which is not bad at all. These numbers are ballpark estimates by the way, because there's not a lot of hard lab data on kailan ikan masin specifically. Different hawkers use different amounts of oil, and the ikan masin portion varies a lot. Treat this as a reasonable guide, not a precise count.

What actually changes the maths here is the oil, full stop. A hawker who woks hard and generous can easily push this past 250 calories. A lighter hand stays closer to 160. The size of your scoop matters too. A heaped plate versus a small side scoop can be a 50 to 70 calorie difference. Some places also add a bit of sugar or extra oyster sauce, which adds maybe 15 calories but a tonne of sodium on top of the already-salty ikan masin. If you're watching your calories, this is still one of the safer dishes to order at a mixed rice stall compared to masak lemak or rendang. Grab a big scoop of this instead of an oily gravy veg and you're already doing better lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it as a side, not a main. A hawker scoop is 150g. Two scoops and the oil doubles too.
  • Ask the uncle to go lighter on the oil. Not every hawker will do it, but the ones who care will.
  • Skip the extra oyster sauce drizzle some places add on top. That adds calories and a lot of sodium on top of the ikan masin.
  • Eat it with steamed chicken or plain tofu instead of a heavy gravy dish. Kailan ikan masin pairs well with simple proteins that don't add more oil.
  • If you're cooking at home, use 1 teaspoon of oil in a non-stick pan instead of the full wok fry. You can cut the oil calories from 110 down to around 40.

Common questions

How many calories in Kailan Ikan Masin?
About 200 calories per hawker plate (150g). The kailan itself is very low calorie, but the wok oil and salted fish push the total up.
Is kailan ikan masin good for weight loss?
It can be, yes. Kailan is one of the better veg dishes you can order because the base ingredient is so low in calories. The issue is oil. If you eat a smaller portion or ask the uncle to go easy on the oil, you can get a decent plate of greens under 180 calories with a solid 8-9g of protein from the ikan masin.
Why is a vegetable dish 200 calories?
Because it's a wok stir-fry. The kailan itself is only about 40 calories. It's the absorbed cooking oil that adds 110 calories all by itself. This is true of almost every Chinese stir-fried veg at a hawker. The veg is not the problem. The wok and the oil are.
Berapa kalori dalam Kailan Ikan Masin?
Kailan Ikan Masin ada kira-kira 200 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (150g), dengan 9g protein, 8g karbohidrat dan 15g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
