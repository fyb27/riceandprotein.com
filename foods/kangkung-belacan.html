How Many Calories in Kangkung Belacan?

Water spinach stir fried with shrimp paste and chilli. Healthy vegetable, cooked in a surprising amount of oil.

190kcal / 1 plate
A plate of kangkung belacan is around 190 calories. The kangkung itself is almost nothing. Nearly all the calories come from the oil it is stir fried in, which is more than most people expect for a vegetable dish.

The real talk

People order kangkung belacan thinking they are being healthy because it is a vegetable. And the kangkung itself is basically free, maybe 30 calories for the whole plate. But watch how they cook it. That kangkung goes into a wok with a generous glug of oil to fry the belacan and garlic, and it soaks a lot of it up. That is how a plate of greens ends up at 190 calories with 14g of fat.

Do not get me wrong, this is still a good order. You are getting actual vegetables and fibre, which most hawker meals are missing. Just know it is not the zero calorie health halo people assume. It is a vegetable cooked in oil, and the oil counts.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less oil (kurang minyak). The oil is the whole calorie story here.
  • Pair it with a lean protein and rice for a balanced plate, it works great as the veg side.
  • Still a smart order. Just do not assume vegetable means zero calories.

Common questions

How many calories in kangkung belacan?
About 190 calories a plate. The vegetable is only around 35 of that. The rest is the oil it is stir fried in.
Is kangkung belacan healthy?
It is one of the better hawker orders because you get real vegetables and fibre. Just know it is cooked in a fair bit of oil, so it is not calorie free. Ask for less oil to keep it light.
Why is a vegetable dish this high in calories?
The kangkung soaks up the oil used to fry the belacan and garlic. The greens are almost nothing, the oil is where the 190 calories come from.
Berapa kalori dalam Kangkung Belacan?
Kangkung Belacan ada kira-kira 190 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (200g), dengan 5g protein, 12g karbohidrat dan 14g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
