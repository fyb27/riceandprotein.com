How Many Calories in Kanom Jeen Nam Ya?

Also known as ขนมจีนน้ำยา, Khanom Chin Nam Ya, Thai Rice Noodles with Fish Curry, Kanom Jeen Nam Ya Pa.

Fresh fermented rice noodles (kanom jeen) topped with nam ya, a thin fish curry made from pounded fish, krachai (fingerroot) and a little coconut milk, served with a table of raw and pickled vegetables you pile on yourself. A 30 to 40 baht market breakfast.

330kcal / 1 plate
A 350g plate of kanom jeen nam ya is about 330 calories, the figure in the Thai Department of Health food table. About 150 calories are the 150g of rice noodles, around 150 are the fish curry, and the vegetables on the side are close to zero. A hard boiled egg on top adds 75.

The real talk

Kanom jeen looks like a lot of food for 330 calories, and it is. Fresh rice noodles are about 100 calories per 100g because they are mostly water, so the 150g nest on a plate is only 150 calories. The nam ya is a thin curry, not a thick coconut one: a stall ladle of about 150g is around 150 calories, and most of that is the fish and the small amount of coconut milk. That is the whole plate.

The type of nam ya changes the number. Nam ya pa (Isan style, no coconut) is about 100 calories a ladle, so the plate drops to 280. Nam ya kati (southern style, thick with coconut milk) is closer to 220 a ladle, so the plate climbs to 400. Nam ngiao from the North and gaeng keow wan (green curry) over kanom jeen both run 380 to 450 a plate because of the coconut and pork.

The best part is the vegetable table. Bean sprouts, long beans, cabbage, Thai basil, pickled mustard greens and cucumber are all sitting there for free, and a big pile adds about 20 calories. Load half the plate with vegetables and it eats like 600 calories of food. The one thing to be careful with is the fried dried chili and the fish sauce; the chili is fried in oil, and 5 pieces are around 30 calories.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order nam ya pa (no coconut) where it is available; it is about 50 calories lighter per plate than standard nam ya.
  • Take one nest of noodles, not two; each extra nest is about 100 calories.
  • Pile on the raw vegetables and pickled mustard greens from the side table; they are almost free.
  • Skip the green curry and nam ngiao toppings if you want the light option; they add 80 to 120 calories over nam ya.

Common questions

Is kanom jeen fattening?
The noodles are not; 150g of fresh kanom jeen is about 150 calories, less than the same weight of cooked rice. The topping decides the plate: 280 for nam ya pa, 330 for nam ya, up to 450 for green curry.
How many calories in kanom jeen nam ya with a boiled egg?
About 405 calories: the 330 calorie plate plus a 75 calorie hard boiled egg. The egg adds 6g of protein, which the plate is a bit short on.
Kanom jeen vs pad thai, which is lower in calories?
Kanom jeen nam ya at 330 for 350g is far lighter than pad thai at 577 for 244g in the Department of Health table.
