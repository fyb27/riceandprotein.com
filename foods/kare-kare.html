How Many Calories in Kare-Kare?

Also known as Kare Kare, Oxtail Peanut Stew, Kare-Kareng Buntot.

Oxtail and tripe stewed in a thick peanut and annatto sauce with banana heart, string beans, eggplant and pechay. Always eaten with bagoong alamang on the side.

500kcal / 1 serving
A single restaurant serving of kare-kare, about 250g with oxtail, vegetables and peanut sauce, is around 500 calories. The peanut sauce alone is about 200 of that. Add 1 cup of rice and a spoon of bagoong and the full plate reaches 800.

The real talk

Kare-kare is a peanut butter dish. A family-size pot uses 150 to 200g of ground peanuts or peanut butter, which is 900 to 1,200 calories of sauce spread across 6 servings. Your 80g ladle of sauce is about 200 calories before the meat. Then oxtail, which is fatty even after skimming, adds 260 calories per 100g. That is how a dish full of eggplant, string beans and banana heart still lands at 500.

The bagoong is small but not free. A tablespoon of sautéed shrimp paste is about 30 calories, and most people take three because kare-kare without it tastes flat. Sautéed bagoong has oil and sugar in it, so it is not the same as raw alamang. Count 90 to 100 calories for a normal amount at a restaurant.

Restaurant kare-kare in Manila runs 350 to 600 pesos for a serving meant for two, and it usually gets finished by one. That is 1,000 calories of ulam before rice. The crispy kare-kare trend, where lechon kawali or bagnet replaces the oxtail, adds another 300 calories per serving because you are putting deep fried belly into peanut sauce.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for more vegetables and less sauce. The banana heart, eggplant and string beans are about 25 calories a handful.
  • Choose the seafood or vegetable-only version, which lands at 250 to 380 instead of 500.
  • One tablespoon of bagoong, not three. That is 60 calories saved.
  • Share the restaurant order. It is portioned for two and priced for two.

Common questions

How many calories in kare-kare with rice?
About 760 calories for one serving with 1 cup of rice, or around 800 once you add bagoong. Two cups of rice takes it past 1,000.
Why is kare-kare so high in calories?
The sauce is ground peanuts or peanut butter, at about 600 calories per 100g, and the meat is oxtail, one of the fattier cuts. The vegetables are only about 25 calories per serving.
How many calories in bagoong alamang?
About 30 calories per tablespoon when sautéed with oil and sugar. Raw alamang is closer to 10 per tablespoon.
Is kare-kare healthy?
It has protein and vegetables, but the peanut sauce and oxtail make it one of the heaviest Filipino ulam at 500 calories per serving. The seafood or vegetable version at 250 to 380 is the better pick if you are cutting.
