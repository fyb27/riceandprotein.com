How Many Calories in Kari Kambing (Mutton Curry)?

Kari Kambing is a rich, spiced goat or mutton curry simmered in coconut milk gravy, a staple at mamak stalls and kopitiams usually eaten with roti canai or rice.

400kcal / 1 mamak/kopitiam serving (meat + gravy, no rice)
One standard mamak serving of Kari Kambing (about 300g, meat and gravy, no rice) comes in at roughly 400 calories, with 30g protein, 28g fat, and only 7g carbs. The mutton itself is solid on protein but the coconut milk gravy and cooking oil are the real calorie drivers here, not the meat.

The real talk

Kari Kambing is one of those dishes where the meat is actually decent for protein, but the fat content is no joke. You get 30g of protein from the bone-in mutton pieces, which is great, but 28g of fat comes along for the ride too. The cooking oil and the coconut milk in the gravy are the main culprits, and they stack up fast. To be straight with you, these numbers are a ballpark estimate because solid nutritional data for mamak-style kari kambing is genuinely thin. Every stall cooks it differently, some use way more coconut milk, some drown it in oil, so treat 400 calories as a reasonable midpoint rather than a precise lab figure.

What actually swings the number is the gravy. If you scoop heavy on the gravy you are easily adding another 50 to 80 calories per extra ladle just from coconut milk and oil. The bone-in pieces (about 160g as served, roughly 130g edible meat) account for around 200 calories on their own. Ask for less gravy and you can probably shave 50 to 70 calories off the total. Pairing it with naan adds about 190 calories, white rice adds roughly 220 calories, so if you are watching numbers, roti canai in moderation or even plain bread is worth thinking about versus loading up on both rice and a full ladle of gravy.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask the uncle for less gravy, specifically the coconut milk heavy part. That alone can cut 50 to 70 calories.
  • Eat it with chapati or one small roti instead of naan, saves you around 80 to 100 calories compared to naan.
  • Skip the potatoes in the curry if you are being strict, though honestly the potato portion here is small at about 20g, so prioritise reducing the gravy over the potato.
  • Order Kari Kambing Kosong if the stall offers it. That drier, lighter gravy version saves you about 70 calories per serving.
  • Pair with steamed rice rather than fried rice so your carb side stays lower calorie.

Common questions

How many calories in Kari Kambing (Mutton Curry)?
A standard mamak or kopitiam serving of Kari Kambing, about 300g of meat and gravy without rice, is around 400 calories. This goes up to about 590 calories if you add one naan, or around 620 calories with a cup of white rice.
What is Kari Kambing and how is it different from chicken curry?
Kari Kambing is a Malaysian mutton or goat curry cooked in a spiced coconut milk gravy with onion, tomato, and chilli. It is richer and fattier than chicken curry because mamak-style kari kambing is cooked in a heavier coconut milk gravy with more oil than a typical kari ayam, and mutton does carry more fat than chicken breast even if it is leaner than beef.
Is Kari Kambing a good choice if I am trying to lose weight?
It is not terrible but it is not light either. You get 30g of protein which is solid, but 28g of fat per serving means the calories stack up fast. If you control your gravy portion and skip the naan, it fits reasonably into a calorie deficit. The problem is most people pair it with rice and roti, and suddenly you are at 600 to 700 calories for that one dish alone.
Berapa kalori dalam Kari Kambing?
Kari Kambing ada kira-kira 400 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (300g), dengan 30g protein, 7g karbohidrat dan 28g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
