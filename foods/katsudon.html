How Many Calories in Katsudon?

Also known as カツ丼, かつ丼, Pork Cutlet Rice Bowl, Tonkatsu Don.

A breaded fried pork cutlet simmered with onion and beaten egg in sweet dashi soy, then slid over a bowl of rice. The soba shop and teishoku-ya heavyweight.

866kcal / 1 bowl
A standard bowl of katsudon is about 866 calories, per calorie.slism.jp for a 504g bowl. The fried pork cutlet is roughly 390 of that, the rice about 360, and the egg, onion and sweet sauce make up the last 130. It is the highest calorie of the classic donburi.

The real talk

Katsudon is 866 calories, which puts it above gyudon (633) and oyakodon (631) by a wide margin, and the reason is simple: you are eating a whole tonkatsu, breading and oil included, and then it gets simmered in a sauce with sugar. A 120g cutlet is about 390 calories, the 230g of rice under it is 360, and the egg and sweet dashi bring it home. A bowl at a soba shop or Katsuya is 900 to 1,000 yen.

The egg is the part people underestimate. One beaten egg is 80 calories, and shops often use one and a half. The sauce it cooks in has sugar and mirin, so the onion and sauce together are 50 to 60 calories rather than the near zero you get with a clear dashi. That is why katsudon runs 130 calories above a plain tonkatsu plus rice even though it looks like the same ingredients.

The size problem is that katsudon is often the 'treat' order after a hard day, and it usually comes with a small bowl of soba or udon on the side at a soba shop. That set is around 1,100 calories. If you want the cutlet, katsudon at 866 is still a better deal than the katsu curry at CoCo Ichibanya, which is 1,116 for a regular rice serving.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order hire (fillet) katsudon when it exists. The lean cutlet saves 80 to 100 calories against rosu (loin).
  • Ask for less rice. Dropping from 230g to 150g saves about 125 calories and the cutlet is still the same size.
  • Skip the side noodle set. The mini soba or udon adds 200 to 250 calories.
  • Eat it as the only heavy meal of the day. At 866 calories it is half of an 1,800 calorie budget.

Common questions

Which donburi has the most calories?
Katsudon at 866 calories tops the common ones. Unaju is about 879 but it is usually a special occasion. Gyudon is 633 and oyakodon 631.
How many calories are in the tonkatsu inside a katsudon?
About 390 for a 120g loin cutlet. The breading and absorbed oil are roughly 40 percent of that.
Is katsudon high in protein?
Fairly high at about 33g per bowl from the pork and egg. The issue is the 36g of fat and 100g of carbs that come with it.
