How Many Calories in Keropok Lekor?

Terengganu's famous fish sausage, boiled then deep-fried until chewy, served in a bag with chilli sauce on the side.

270kcal / 1 bag / ~8-10 pieces (fried)
A standard bag of keropok lekor (about 8 to 10 pieces, 120g) comes in at 270 calories. That might not sound scary, but nearly a third of those calories are pure oil soaked up during deep frying. The fish paste itself is quite lean at around 55 calories for 55g, but the sago flour base adds 135 calories of carbs, and the frying oil piles on another 80. So yes, it's a snack, not a meal replacement, and the numbers add up faster than you think if you grab two bags.

The real talk

270 calories per bag sounds manageable until you realise you're probably eating two bags while watching people fish at the jetty. The fish paste from ikan parang or tenggiri is honestly decent protein, 13g per bag, but the sago flour base is pure carbs and the frying adds 80 calories of oil on top. To be upfront, the numbers here are a solid estimate, not a lab reading. Hard data on keropok lekor is thin and different aunties fry theirs differently, so treat 270 as a reasonable ballpark, not gospel. If you're watching calories, one bag is fine. Two bags liao you're at 540 calories for a snack.

What actually swings the maths hard is which version you pick. Losong, the boiled or steamed version without frying, comes in at around 195 calories per serving because there's zero oil absorption. That's 75 calories less for the same amount of food, same fish, same sago base. The thin crispy sliced version (keropok lekor nipis) goes the other direction, hitting about 380 calories because the surface area is huge and it soaks up oil like a sponge. And if you're dipping in that sweet chilli sauce, add another 30 to 50 calories easy depending on how generous you are. Practical tip: ask for losong if the stall has it, eat one bag with a proper meal instead of as a standalone snack, and skip the second bag.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order losong (boiled version) instead of fried and you cut about 75 calories per bag automatically.
  • One bag as a side, not a standalone snack. Pair it with a proper meal so you're not reaching for a second bag because you're still hungry.
  • Go easy on the chilli dipping sauce, which can add 30 to 50 calories depending on how much you pour.
  • Avoid the thin crispy sliced version (keropok lekor nipis), it hits 380 calories per serving because of much higher oil absorption.
  • If you're buying from a stall, freshly fried pieces that aren't sitting in oil are lower in absorbed fat than ones left sitting in the fryer basket.

Common questions

How many calories in Keropok Lekor?
A standard bag of fried keropok lekor (about 8 to 10 pieces, 120g) has around 270 calories. The losong (boiled) version is lower at about 195 calories, while the thin crispy sliced version is higher at around 380 calories.
Is keropok lekor high in calories for a snack?
It's moderate but not low. At 270 calories per bag it's in the same range as a small meal, and it's easy to eat two bags without thinking. The fish itself is lean and gives you 13g of protein, but the sago flour and frying oil push the calorie count up.
Is losong healthier than fried keropok lekor?
Yes, noticeably so. Losong skips the deep frying entirely so there's no oil absorption. It comes in at around 195 calories per serving versus 270 for the fried version. Same fish, same sago base, just 75 fewer calories and much less fat.
Berapa kalori dalam Keropok Lekor?
Keropok Lekor ada kira-kira 270 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (120g), dengan 13g protein, 32g karbohidrat dan 9g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
