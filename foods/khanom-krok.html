How Many Calories in Khanom Krok?

Also known as ขนมครก, Thai Coconut Pancakes, Kanom Krok, Coconut Rice Cakes.

Little coconut and rice flour pancakes cooked in a dimpled cast iron pan, crisp on the outside and custardy in the middle, sold in pairs with corn, spring onion or taro on top. A morning market staple at 20 to 30 baht a box.

360kcal / 1 box (8 pieces)
A standard box of 8 khanom krok (4 pairs), about 130g, is roughly 360 calories. Each half shell is about 45 calories. Two things drive the number: the coconut cream in the top layer, about 120 calories per box, and the sugar in the batter, about 80. The pan is oiled between every batch too.

The real talk

Khanom krok looks light because each piece is the size of a thumb. The maths does not care about the size. Rice flour batter is 130 calories per box, the coconut cream topping is 120, the sugar is 80 and the oil brushed into the pan is another 35. That is 360 for something you finish walking from the stall to the motorbike. A box every morning with your coffee is 2,500 calories a week, about a third of a kilo of fat a month.

The MGR calorie table lists 2 pairs at 210, which puts a pair at 105. That is a big pair. Most market boxes have 8 smaller shells and the batter is thinner, so 90 per pair is closer to what you are eating. Either way, one box is a dessert, not a snack, and 48g of carbs with almost no protein means it does nothing for hunger. You will want lunch at the normal time.

There is no low calorie khanom krok. The whole thing is coconut fat and rice starch, and the version with taro or corn on top is the same number with 5 extra calories of vegetable. What works is buying half a box. Four shells at 180 calories with a black coffee is a reasonable morning. The full box plus a cha yen is 660 before 9am.

How to eat it lighter

  • Buy half a box. Four shells is about 180 calories and the craving is gone after 2.
  • Pair it with black coffee or plain water, not cha yen. That combination is 660 calories before breakfast.
  • Pick the stall that cooks thin, crisp shells. Thicker custardy ones carry more coconut cream, about 10 calories more per piece.
  • Skip the extra coconut cream some vendors drizzle on top. That spoon is 30 calories.
  • Eat it as dessert after a protein meal rather than as breakfast on its own. The 48g of sugar and starch hits softer.

Common questions

How many calories in one khanom krok?
About 45 per half shell, or 90 per pair, for thin market style pieces. Thick custardy ones are closer to 105 a pair.
Is khanom krok healthy?
It is rice flour, coconut cream and sugar, about 360 calories a box with 4g of protein. Treat it as dessert.
Is khanom krok gluten free?
Yes, the batter is rice flour and coconut. Check the stall does not add wheat flour, some do for crispness.
What is the difference between khanom krok and apam balik?
Khanom krok is a small coconut pancake at about 45 calories a piece. Malaysian apam balik is a large folded pancake with sugar and peanuts at 300 to 400 for one.
