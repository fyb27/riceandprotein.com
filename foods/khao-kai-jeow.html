How Many Calories in Khao Kai Jeow?

Also known as ข้าวไข่เจียว, Khao Khai Jiao, Thai Omelette Rice, Kai Jeow Moo Sap.

The cheapest meal in Thailand: a fluffy, crisp edged omelette fried in a lot of hot oil, usually with a spoon of minced pork, over a plate of rice with prik nam pla (fish sauce with chili and lime) and sriracha. 35 to 45 baht at any tam sang stall.

500kcal / 1 plate
A 380g plate of khao kai jeow moo sap (omelette with minced pork over rice) is about 500 calories, the Thai Department of Health figure. The rice is about 290, the two eggs 150, the minced pork 35 and the oil the omelette soaks up around 40. A plain single egg version is closer to 400.

The real talk

A Thai omelette is not a French omelette. The eggs are beaten with fish sauce and a splash of lime, then poured into a wok of oil so hot the edges puff and go brown. That is what makes it good, and it is also why two eggs at 150 calories become an omelette of about 220: the puffed edges hold oil. The Department of Health figure of 500 for the plate with pork reflects that, and hdmall lists a plain khao kai jeow at 445.

For 40 baht this is a decent protein plate. Two eggs and 20g of minced pork give you about 18g of protein, and with the rice it reaches 22g. The problem is the ratio: 220g of rice is 290 calories, more than the omelette, and it disappears under sriracha in about 4 minutes. Ask for kai song (two eggs) and khao noi (less rice) and you flip the ratio to more protein than starch.

Toppings are cheap here, calorie wise. Prik nam pla is fish sauce, chili and lime at about 5 calories a spoon. Sriracha is about 15 calories a tablespoon. The one that adds up is when a stall drops a second omelette on for 10 baht: that is another 220 calories, and a two omelette plate is over 700. Kai jeow with crab meat (kai jeow poo) at a seafood restaurant is around 550 as a plate.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order kai jeow with khao noi (less rice); the rice is 290 of the 500 calories.
  • Ask for nam man noi (less oil) for the omelette; a thin omelette absorbs about half the oil of a puffy one.
  • Get moo sap (minced pork) or extra egg rather than a second full omelette for more protein.
  • Use prik nam pla instead of sriracha; both are low but prik nam pla has no sugar.

Common questions

How many calories in a Thai omelette?
About 220 for a two egg kai jeow fried in a wok, or around 140 for a single egg one. The oil the puffed edges absorb adds 40 to 70 over the eggs themselves.
Is khao kai jeow good for weight loss?
It can be. A two egg plate with less rice is about 400 calories with 18g of protein for 40 baht. The default plate with a full scoop of rice is 500.
How much oil is in kai jeow?
The wok holds a lot, but the omelette only keeps about 4 to 8g of it, which is 36 to 72 calories. Thin omelettes absorb less than puffy ones.
