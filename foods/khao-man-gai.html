How Many Calories in Khao Man Gai?

Also known as ข้าวมันไก่, Khao Mun Gai, Thai Chicken Rice, Kao Man Gai.

Poached chicken sliced over rice cooked in chicken fat and stock, served with a fermented soybean, ginger and chili sauce, cucumber and a cup of clear soup. Thailand's version of Hainanese chicken rice, 40 to 60 baht at any shophouse.

596kcal / 1 plate
A standard plate of khao man gai is 596 calories for a 300g serving, based on the Thai Department of Health food table. About 340 of that is the chicken fat rice, 165 is the chicken, and the sauce is another 45. The clear soup on the side is close to zero.

The real talk

The rice is the problem, not the chicken. Khao man means fat rice: jasmine rice cooked in rendered chicken fat and stock, which takes plain rice from 130 calories per 100g to around 170. On a 200g scoop that is a 340 calorie base before any chicken lands on it. The poached chicken itself is honest, about 165 calories for 90g and most of that is protein.

The version you order matters more than the shop. Steamed chicken (khao man gai tom) is 596. Ask for gai tod (fried chicken) and the batter and frying oil push it to about 700. Pasom (half steamed, half fried) lands around 650. The tao jiao sauce is 45 calories for a small dish and the pickled ginger inside it is free, so do not skip the sauce, just do not drink it.

Compared with Malaysian or Singaporean chicken rice at around 607, the Thai plate is nearly identical. It is a full meal with 28g of protein. The trap is ordering piset (special) with extra chicken and extra rice, which is usually a 750 to 800 calorie plate, or adding a side of blood tofu and liver, which is more protein but another 80 calories.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order gai tom (steamed) not gai tod; that alone saves about 100 calories.
  • Ask for khao noi (less rice) or khao plao (plain rice). Plain rice instead of fat rice cuts about 80 calories.
  • Ask for ok (breast) instead of the default thigh; leaner and higher in protein.
  • Drink the clear soup and eat the cucumber first; both are nearly free and they slow you down.

Common questions

Is khao man gai healthy?
It is a complete meal with 28g of protein, but the rice is cooked in chicken fat, so 596 calories with 22g of fat for a normal plate. Steamed chicken with plain rice brings it under 500.
How many calories in khao man gai tod?
About 700 for a standard plate. The fried chicken adds batter and oil worth roughly 100 calories over the steamed version.
Khao man gai vs pad kra pao, which is lighter?
Khao man gai at 596 is slightly lighter than pad kra pao with a fried egg at 630, and it has a bit more protein. Without the egg, pad kra pao at 478 wins.
