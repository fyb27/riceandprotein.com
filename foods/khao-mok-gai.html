How Many Calories in Khao Mok Gai?

Also known as ข้าวหมกไก่, Khao Mok Kai, Thai Chicken Biryani, Thai Muslim Chicken Rice.

Thai Muslim biryani: rice cooked in chicken stock with turmeric, ghee and whole spices, with a bone in chicken thigh buried in it, served with crispy fried shallots, cucumber, a sweet and sour green chili sauce and a small bowl of clear chicken soup. 50 to 70 baht at halal stalls.

534kcal / 1 plate
A 350g plate of khao mok gai is about 534 calories, the figure listed by hdmall. The spiced rice is about 330 of that, the chicken thigh with skin around 190, and the sauce and shallots the rest. It is around 200 calories lighter than a Malaysian nasi briyani with chicken because the Thai version uses less ghee and a single chicken piece.

The real talk

Thai khao mok is built lighter than the briyani it descends from. The rice is cooked with turmeric, a spoon of ghee or butter and stock, then steamed, so 220g of it is about 330 calories against 400 or more for oily Indian style rice. The chicken is one bone in thigh, about 130g raw, cooked in the rice, so it does not get fried. That thigh with skin is about 190 calories and 20g of protein.

The green sauce (nam jim khao mok) is sugar, vinegar, green chili, mint and coriander blended. A small dish is about 25g and 20 calories, almost all sugar. The fried shallots on top are 5g and 20 calories. And the bowl of clear soup on the side is about 15 calories and worth drinking. None of these move the plate much; the rice is two thirds of the total.

Portions vary more than most Thai plates. A stall that serves a small thigh and a modest scoop of rice is around 420. A restaurant plate with a big leg quarter and a heaped rice mound is 680 or more. Khao mok nuea (beef) is a bit higher because the beef is braised in fat, and khao mok pae (goat) higher still at around 620 for the standard plate.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for khao noi (less rice); the rice is 330 of the 534 calories, so a two thirds scoop saves about 110.
  • Peel the skin off the thigh and you save around 60 calories with all the protein left.
  • Use the green sauce sparingly; it is sugar and vinegar, about 20 calories a dish.
  • Drink the clear soup and eat the cucumber first; they fill space for under 20 calories.

Common questions

Is khao mok gai healthy?
It is a reasonable one plate meal at 534 calories with 28g of protein. The rice is two thirds of the calories, so asking for less rice is the single most useful change.
Khao mok gai vs khao man gai, which has fewer calories?
Khao mok gai at 534 is a little lower than khao man gai at 596 for a 300g plate, mainly because khao man gai rice is cooked in chicken fat.
How many calories in khao mok gai without the rice?
The chicken thigh with skin, sauce and shallots is about 230 calories. The rice on its own is about 330.
