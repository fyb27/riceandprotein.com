How Many Calories in Khao Niao?

Also known as ข้าวเหนียว, Khao Niew, Thai Sticky Rice, Glutinous Rice.

Steamed glutinous rice, sold in a small plastic bag for 5 to 10 baht next to grilled chicken, moo ping, larb and som tam. The staple starch of Isan and the North, eaten by hand in small pinched balls.

250kcal / 1 bag (about 150g)
A standard 150g bag of khao niao is about 250 calories, almost all of it carbohydrate. Thai tables list steamed sticky rice at 160 calories per ladle or handful of about 100g. That is roughly 25 percent more than the same weight of plain steamed jasmine rice, because sticky rice holds less water.

The real talk

Sticky rice is not more fattening than white rice per dry grain. The difference is water. Steamed khao niao ends up at about 160 calories per 100g, while a scoop of steamed jasmine rice is about 130 per 100g. Same starch, less water, so every mouthful is denser. A small bag is 150g and 250 calories; the bigger bags some stalls sell are 200g and 320 calories.

The problem is what khao niao comes with. Nobody eats a bag of sticky rice alone. A bag with one piece of Hat Yai fried chicken is 570 calories. A bag with 3 moo ping skewers is 640. A bag with som tam and larb is a very reasonable 600 calorie meal with good protein. The rice is a constant 250; everything else decides whether lunch is 500 or 900.

One real advantage: sticky rice is slower to eat. You pinch off small balls by hand, and a 150g bag takes a while to get through, so most people stop at one bag. Compare that with a plate of rice under a curry, which disappears in 5 minutes. If you are watching intake, one bag of khao niao with a grilled protein and som tam is one of the easiest Thai meals to keep under 600.

How to eat it lighter

  • Buy the small 5 baht bag (about 100 to 120g, 160 to 190 calories) instead of the 10 baht bag.
  • Pair it with som tam and grilled meat rather than fried chicken; that swap alone saves 150 to 200 calories.
  • Eat the protein and salad first and the rice last, so you stop before finishing the bag.
  • Do not go for khao niao moon (coconut sticky rice) for a savoury meal; the coconut milk and sugar push 150g to about 350 calories.

Common questions

Is sticky rice higher in calories than white rice?
Per 100g cooked, yes: about 160 calories against 130 for steamed jasmine rice, because sticky rice holds less water. Per dry gram they are nearly identical.
How many calories in khao niao gai tod?
About 570 calories for a 150g bag of sticky rice with one fried chicken thigh. Add a second piece and you are near 900.
How many calories in a small bag of sticky rice?
A 5 baht bag is about 100 to 120g, so 160 to 190 calories. A 10 baht bag is closer to 150g and 250 calories.
Does sticky rice make you gain weight?
Not by itself. 250 calories a bag is modest. The pairings, fried chicken, moo ping fat, coconut desserts, are what push the meal up.
