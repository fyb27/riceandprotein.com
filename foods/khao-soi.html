How Many Calories in Khao Soi?

Also known as ข้าวซอย, ข้าวซอยไก่, Khao Soy, Khao Soi Gai, Chiang Mai Curry Noodles.

Northern Thai egg noodles in a coconut milk curry broth with a chicken drumstick, topped with a nest of deep fried crispy noodles, served with pickled mustard greens, shallots and lime. The Chiang Mai dish everyone travels for.

700kcal / 1 bowl
A standard bowl of khao soi gai with a chicken drumstick is about 700 calories for a 400g serving, using the foodmany.com figure of 200 calories per 100g. The coconut curry broth is about 250, the boiled and fried noodles together about 310, and the drumstick around 160. It is the most calorie dense noodle soup on a Thai menu.

The real talk

Khao soi is a curry and a noodle soup and a plate of fried snacks all in one bowl, which is why it is 700 calories when a normal Thai noodle soup is 300. The broth is coconut milk with red curry paste, about 250 calories for 200ml. The soft egg noodles underneath are 210 for 150g. Then the handful of deep fried noodles on top is another 100, and they are the part everyone loves.

The published numbers disagree more than usual on this one. Some Thai calorie tables list khao soi at around 400 per cup, which describes a small bowl with thin broth. Chiang Mai shops serve big bowls with thick coconut broth and a whole drumstick, and 700 is closer to what lands on the table. Beef khao soi with a chunk of braised shin is heavier still, around 800.

The sides are the redeeming feature. Pickled mustard greens, raw shallot, lime and chili paste are all close to zero, and they cut through the fat. The bowl has 30g of protein, so it is a complete meal. It is just not a light one, and a second bowl, which is a normal thing to do in Chiang Mai, is a 1,400 calorie lunch.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for mai sai mee krob (no crispy noodles) or eat half of the nest; that is 50 to 100 calories of deep fried flour.
  • Eat the noodles and chicken and leave the last third of the broth. Most of the coconut fat is in the liquid.
  • Order khao soi gai over khao soi nuea; the drumstick is leaner than braised beef and the bowl is about 100 calories lighter.
  • Load up on the pickled greens and shallots. They are free and they make the bowl feel bigger.

Common questions

Why is khao soi so high in calories?
Three reasons in one bowl: coconut milk broth (about 250 calories), egg noodles (210) and deep fried crispy noodles on top (100). Most Thai noodle soups have a clear broth and no fried topping.
How many calories in khao soi without the crispy noodles?
About 600 for a standard bowl. The crispy nest is roughly 100 calories, and it is the easiest thing to cut.
Is khao soi healthy?
It has 30g of protein and a proper meal's worth of food, but 38g of fat, mostly saturated from coconut. One bowl is a full meal. It is not a soup to have alongside anything else.
