How Many Calories in Khao Tom?

Also known as ข้าวต้ม, ข้าวต้มหมู, Khao Tom Moo, Thai Rice Soup, Kao Tom.

Thai rice soup: whole grains of jasmine rice simmered in stock until soft but not broken down, with minced pork or fish, garlic oil, celery, spring onion and white pepper. The late night and hangover meal of Thailand, 40 to 60 baht a bowl.

300kcal / 1 bowl
A 400g bowl of khao tom moo (with minced pork) is about 300 calories. Thai tables list plain khao tom at 120 calories per cup and khao tom pla (fish) at 325 per cup. The rice and stock are about 200 of the bowl, the minced pork 70, and the fried garlic oil on top around 30.

The real talk

Khao tom is rice stretched with water, which is why it is light. A bowl holds about 60g of dry rice equivalent, roughly the same as a small plate, but it comes as 300g of soup that takes 15 minutes to eat. That 300g of rice soup is about 200 calories. Plain khao tom in a Thai table is 120 per cup, and a stall bowl is about two and a half cups.

The toppings are the variable. Minced pork (40g, about 70 calories) is the standard. Khao tom pla uses about 60g of fish and lands at 325 per cup in the hdmall table because it comes with a bigger dose of garlic oil and celery. The garlic oil is the sneaky part: a stall spoons 8 to 10g of it on top, and that is 70 to 90 calories if they are generous, 30 if they are not.

The other version of khao tom is the khao tom kui restaurant, where you get a bowl of plain rice soup (about 200 calories) and order side dishes: salted egg, stir fried morning glory, omelette, pork with garlic. That is where a 200 calorie bowl becomes a 900 calorie dinner, but each side is a choice. Two lean sides (salted egg at 90 and stir fried greens at 120) keep it under 450.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for mai sai nam man krathiem (no garlic oil); that alone can save 50 to 90 calories.
  • Choose khao tom goong (prawn) or khao tom gai (chicken) over pork; both are around 260 a bowl.
  • At a khao tom kui restaurant, order one plain khao tom with a salted egg and a plate of pak boong; that is about 410 total.
  • Skip the pa tong go (fried dough) on the side; a pair is 124 calories.

Common questions

Is khao tom good for weight loss?
Yes. A 400g bowl is about 300 calories with 14g of protein, and the volume of water makes it filling. Ask for less garlic oil and it is closer to 250.
Khao tom vs jok, which has fewer calories?
Similar. Khao tom moo is about 300 a bowl; jok with egg is about 250 and jok moo without egg about 160. Jok is thinner, so it has slightly less rice per bowl.
How many calories in plain khao tom?
About 120 calories per cup in Thai tables, or roughly 200 for a full restaurant bowl with no toppings.
