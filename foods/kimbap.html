How Many Calories in Kimbap?

Also known as 김밥, Gimbap, Korean Seaweed Rice Roll, Kim Bap.

Kimbap is the seaweed and rice roll filled with egg, ham, pickled radish, carrot and spinach, sold sliced into 8 to 10 pieces at every bunsik shop and convenience store.

400kcal / 1 roll
A standard roll of kimbap is about 400 calories. FatSecret Korea lists Barunkim Seonsaeng at 393 for a 230g roll and CU vegetable kimbap at 356 for 272g, while a Kimbap Cheonguk roll is 485 for 300g. Each slice is 40 to 50 calories. Tuna kimbap with mayo runs about 520, and cheese or bulgogi rolls 550 to 570.

The real talk

About 150g of the roll is rice, and that rice is seasoned with sesame oil and salt before it goes anywhere near the seaweed. That is 220 calories from rice plus 45 from the sesame oil, so 265 of the 400 is there before a single filling. The egg strip, ham and crab stick together are about 95. The pickled radish, carrot and spinach are 20.

Fillings change the number a lot. Tuna kimbap is tuna mixed with mayonnaise, and the mayo alone is 90 to 100 calories, which is why Kimbap Cheonguk lists it at 570 for a 300g roll. Cheese kimbap adds a 60 calorie slice. Bulgogi kimbap adds sweet marinated beef for about 150. Vegetable kimbap with no ham or egg is the low end at 355.

The trap is the second roll. At 3,500 to 4,500 won a roll it is cheap enough that two rolls with a cup of tteokbokki is a normal order, and that is 1,170 calories for a snack that felt light because it was cold and cut into pieces. One roll and a bowl of eomuk broth is 500 and does the same job.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order vegetable (야채) kimbap. It is about 355 versus 400 for standard and 520 for tuna.
  • One roll, not two. The second roll is the whole problem.
  • Skip the tuna mayo and cheese versions. Each is 100 to 150 more than the basic roll for the same size.
  • Pair it with eomuk broth or a plain soup rather than tteokbokki. Tteokbokki on the side is another 370.

Common questions

Is kimbap good for weight loss?
One roll is a reasonable 400 calorie meal with 13g protein. The problem is that it is easy to eat two, and tuna or cheese versions add 100 to 170 each.
How many calories in one piece of kimbap?
About 40 to 50 per slice, with 8 to 10 slices in a standard roll.
Which kimbap has the most calories?
Tuna mayo, cheese and bulgogi kimbap are the highest at 520 to 570 per roll. Vegetable kimbap is the lowest at around 355.
Is convenience store kimbap lower in calories?
Usually, because the rolls are smaller: CU and GS25 rolls are 200 to 270g and 290 to 360 calories, versus 400 to 485 for a bunsik shop roll.
