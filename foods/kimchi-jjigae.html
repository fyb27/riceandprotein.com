How Many Calories in Kimchi Jjigae?

Also known as 김치찌개, Kimchi Stew, Kimchi Chigae, Kimchi Jigae.

Kimchi jjigae is the sour, spicy pork and kimchi stew that every Korean lunch spot serves in a black stone pot with a bowl of rice and a few banchan.

555kcal / 1 bowl with rice
A standard kimchi jjigae set is about 555 calories: 244 for a 400g pot of stew (MFDS restaurant nutrition data) plus around 310 for a 210g bowl of white rice. The stew on its own is light. The rice and the pork belly are what add up.

The real talk

The stew itself is 244 calories for a full 400g pot. That is less than a bowl of rice. Old kimchi has almost nothing in it, the tofu is 60 to 70 calories, and the broth is mostly water and gochugaru. The pork belly is the only heavy thing in the pot, around 165 calories for the 50g most lunch places put in. If the cook is generous with belly, you can add another 100.

The 555 figure assumes one bowl of rice, about 210g. Most 8,000 to 9,000 won lunch sets give you free rice refills, and a second bowl takes you to 865 before you touch the banchan. The gyeran-mari or fish cake on the side is another 80 to 150. It is easy to walk out of a kimchi jjigae lunch at 900 calories while thinking you ate soup.

Tuna kimchi jjigae runs lower, around 104 calories per 200g portion according to FatSecret Korea, because canned tuna in water has a fraction of the fat of belly. Budae-style versions with spam and ramyeon added are a different dish entirely and can double the number. Ask what is in the pot before you assume it is the light option.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for tuna (참치) instead of pork belly. The stew drops from around 244 to about 200 for the same pot.
  • Stop at one bowl of rice, or ask for half. That single decision is worth 150 to 300 calories.
  • Eat the kimchi, tofu and pork, and leave a third of the broth. The broth carries most of the sodium (around 1,960mg per pot) and some of the fat that renders off the pork.
  • Skip the fried banchan. A single slice of gyeran-mari is fine, the fried fish cake and japchae are where the extra 150 hides.

Common questions

Is kimchi jjigae good for weight loss?
The stew is, at 244 calories for a full pot with 15g protein. The rice is what decides the total. One bowl and it is a 555 calorie meal, which fits most deficits.
How many calories in kimchi jjigae without rice?
About 244 calories for a 400g restaurant pot with pork belly, based on Korean MFDS restaurant data. Tuna versions are closer to 200.
Why is kimchi jjigae so salty?
One pot has about 1,960mg of sodium, close to the full daily limit, because both the kimchi and the broth are salted. Leaving some broth helps.
Kimchi jjigae vs doenjang jjigae, which is lower?
Doenjang jjigae is lower at about 145 calories per pot because it uses less meat and no chili oil. Kimchi jjigae is 244.
