How Many Calories in Kitsune Udon?

Also known as きつねうどん, 狐うどん, Fried Tofu Udon, Aburaage Udon.

Thick wheat udon in a clear dashi and soy broth, topped with a large square of aburaage, fried tofu simmered in sweet soy until it soaks up the juice like a sponge. Named after the fox, who supposedly likes fried tofu.

390kcal / 1 bowl
A standard bowl of kitsune udon is about 390 calories. The 250g of cooked udon is 240 of that, the aburaage pouch about 115, and the broth around 30. It is one of the lightest noodle bowls in Japan, roughly 200 calories less than a bowl of miso ramen.

The real talk

Udon gets treated as heavy because the noodles are thick, but a bowl of kake udon, just noodles and broth, is only 270 calories at Marugame's regular size. Kitsune udon is that bowl plus one piece of sweet fried tofu, and the tofu is where the 115 extra calories sit. Aburaage is deep-fried tofu, about 380 calories per 100g before it is simmered in sugar and soy, so a 30g pouch that has soaked up sweet broth comes to around 115.

The broth is close to free. About 300ml of dashi with soy and mirin is 30 calories, though it carries 3 to 4g of salt if you drink it all. Kansai broth is lighter in colour and slightly lower in salt than Kanto broth, but the calorie difference is nothing. Leaving half the soup saves 15 calories and half the salt, which is the better reason to do it.

The number climbs with the extras, not the bowl. Marugame and Hanamaru put tempura by the till, and one kakiage is 150 to 180 calories, a chikuwa tempura about 100, an inari sushi 100 to 120 each. A kitsune udon with a kakiage and 2 inari is 720 calories, nearly double the bowl. The large size (oomori) adds about 130 calories of noodles too.

How to eat it lighter

  • Stick with the regular size. Oomori adds about 130 calories of noodles.
  • Skip the tempura tray. One kakiage adds 150 to 180, and it soaks up broth.
  • Leave half the broth. Saves only 15 calories but nearly 2g of salt.
  • Add wakame or spring onion from the free topping bar. Both are close to zero.
  • If you want more protein, an onsen egg adds 70 calories and 6g of protein, better value than a second aburaage.

Common questions

How many calories in kitsune udon?
About 390 for a regular bowl. Kake udon with no tofu is about 275, and the large size is around 520.
Is udon lower in calories than ramen?
Usually yes. Kitsune udon is 390 and a plain kake udon 275, while shoyu ramen is 421, tonkotsu 525 and miso ramen 621. The udon broth has almost no fat.
How many calories in aburaage?
A single 30g pouch simmered in sweet soy is about 115 calories. Dry aburaage is around 380 per 100g because it is deep-fried tofu.
Is kitsune udon good for a diet?
Reasonable. It is 390 calories with 14g of protein, and the broth fills you up. The trap is the tempura counter at the till, which can double the meal.
