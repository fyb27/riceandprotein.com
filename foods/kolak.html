How Many Calories in Kolak?

Also known as Kolak Pisang, Kolak Pisang Ubi, Kolak Biji Salak, Kolak Candil, Banana in Palm Sugar Coconut Milk.

Kolak is banana and sweet potato simmered in coconut milk and palm sugar with pandan. It is the Ramadan takjil: sold from Rp 8,000 in plastic cups outside every mosque at 5pm, eaten first, then followed by dinner.

390kcal / 1 bowl (banana, sweet potato, kolang-kaling, coconut milk, palm sugar)
A bowl of kolak pisang is about 390 calories. FatSecret Indonesia lists kolak at 163 calories per 100g and 391 calories per bowl, with 47% of the energy from fat. A 120g takjil cup is about 196. The banana and sweet potato are 135; the coconut milk and palm sugar are the rest.

The real talk

Kolak is sugar water with coconut fat and some fruit in it, and the fruit is the smallest number. 100g of kepok banana is about 90 calories, 40g of sweet potato is 45, and the kolang-kaling is 20. That is 155 of fruit and root, all of it fine. Then 120ml of santan at 110 and 35g of gula merah at 125. The liquid is 235 calories and it is the part people drink from the bowl at the end.

During Ramadan this is the first thing in after 13 hours of fasting, and the timing is the problem, not the kolak. A 390 calorie bowl, then es buah, then three gorengan, then a full dinner, then more at sahur. Kolak on its own is a reasonable dessert. Kolak as the opener to a 2,000 calorie evening is why people gain weight in the fasting month, which surprises everyone every year.

The versions matter. Kolak pisang ubi with thick santan from a warung is 390. Kolak biji salak, the orange sweet potato dumplings in palm sugar syrup with santan on top, is about 320 because there is less santan in the base. Kolak candil (glutinous rice balls) is similar. Bubur sumsum with kinca is lighter still at about 250. Kolak labu (pumpkin) is the lightest because pumpkin is mostly water, around 280.

At Rp 8,000 to 12,000 a cup it is cheap, and a 120g plastic cup is only 196 calories according to FatSecret. The trouble is the bowl at home is 300g and the pot is on the stove all evening.

How to eat it lighter

  • Small cup, not a bowl. The 120g takjil cup is about 196 calories against 390 for a full bowl.
  • Eat the banana and sweet potato, leave the kuah. The liquid is 235 calories of santan and sugar.
  • At home, use santan encer (thin coconut milk) and half the palm sugar. The bowl drops to about 250.
  • Kolak labu or bubur sumsum instead of kolak pisang ubi. About 100 calories less per serving.
  • Kolak or gorengan at iftar, not both. Kolak plus three gorengan is 800 calories before dinner.

Common questions

How many calories in kolak pisang?
About 390 calories for a bowl according to FatSecret Indonesia, or about 196 for a 120g takjil cup. The coconut milk and palm sugar are more than half of it.
Is kolak healthy for breaking fast?
It gives fast sugar, which is the point, but a bowl is 390 calories and only 3g protein. A small cup and then water is fine. A bowl followed by gorengan and dinner is where the Ramadan weight comes from.
How many calories in kolak biji salak?
About 320 calories per bowl. The sweet potato dumplings are dense but the base is palm sugar syrup with santan spooned on top instead of cooked through, so it is a bit lighter than kolak pisang.
How much sugar is in kolak?
About 35g of palm sugar per bowl plus the natural sugar in the banana, so 45 to 50g total. That is nearly two cans of soft drink.
Berapa kalori Kolak?
Kolak mengandung sekitar 390 kalori per 1 mangkuk (325g), dengan 3g protein, 55g karbohidrat dan 18g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
