How Many Calories in Kopi C?

Also known as Kopi C Kosong, Kopi C Peng, Coffee with Evaporated Milk, 咖啡C, Kopi Si.

Kopi c is the Singapore kopitiam coffee with evaporated milk and sugar, the middle option between kopi with sweet condensed milk and kopi o with no milk at all. Kosong means no sugar, peng means iced.

87kcal / 1 cup (hot)
A cup of hot kopi c is about 87 calories for a 200ml cup, with around 15g of carbs, nearly all sugar. Kopi c kosong, with evaporated milk and no sugar, is about 47 calories. The evaporated milk is around 35 calories and the sugar about 50.

The real talk

The C in kopi c stands for Carnation, the evaporated milk brand, which tells you what is in it. About 25ml of evaporated milk at 35 calories, plus a heaped teaspoon or two of sugar at 50, plus the coffee itself at about 2. That is 87. A normal kopi with condensed milk is about 130, because condensed milk is evaporated milk with the sugar already in it and the uncle adds more sugar anyway. Kopi c is the lighter milk coffee by about 45 calories.

The word that matters is kosong. Kopi c kosong is 47 calories, and the 40 you save is pure sugar. It takes about a week to get used to the taste, and after that the sweet version tastes like syrup. Two kopi c a day, every day, is 174 calories. Two kopi c kosong is 94. Over a year the difference is about 29,000 calories, which is roughly 4kg of fat, from changing one word.

Iced versions are bigger. Kopi c peng comes in a taller glass and the stall adds more milk and sugar to fight the ice, so it is closer to 130. A large kopi c peng from a stall that uses the big plastic cup is about 160. If you want it cold, kopi c peng kosong is about 60 and does the same job.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order kopi c kosong. It saves 40 calories per cup and you still get the milk.
  • Say siew dai, less sugar, if kosong is too far. That is about 65 calories.
  • Skip the peng. The iced version has more milk and sugar to compensate for the ice, about 130.
  • Choose kopi c over kopi. Evaporated milk is 87 against 130 for condensed.
  • Kopi o kosong is 5 calories if you want to go all the way. Kopi c kosong at 47 is the easier place to stop.

Common questions

How many calories in kopi c?
About 87 calories for a standard hot cup. Kopi c kosong without sugar is about 47, and kopi c peng, the iced version, is around 130 because of the extra milk and sugar.
Is kopi c healthier than kopi?
Yes, by about 45 calories. Kopi uses sweetened condensed milk at about 130 per cup. Kopi c uses evaporated milk with sugar added separately, so you can control the sugar by ordering kosong or siew dai.
What does the C in kopi c mean?
Carnation, the evaporated milk brand the kopitiams used. It has come to mean any coffee with evaporated milk rather than condensed milk.
How much sugar is in kopi c?
About 12g, roughly 3 teaspoons, in a standard cup. Kosong removes all of it. Siew dai roughly halves it.
Berapa kalori dalam Kopi C?
Kopi C ada kira-kira 87 kalori untuk 1 cawan (200g), dengan 2g protein, 15g karbohidrat dan 2g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
