How Many Calories in Kopi O?

Also known as Kopi O Kosong, Kopi O Peng.

Black coffee with sugar, no milk, so it is only about 60 calories from the sugar.

60kcal / 1 glass
Kopi O is black coffee with sugar and no milk, so it clocks in at roughly 60 calories a glass, all from the sugar they stir in. The coffee itself is basically zero. Order it kosong with no sugar and you are looking at 5 calories, basically free.

The real talk

Kopi O is one of the few kopitiam drinks that will not wreck you. No milk, no condensed milk, just black coffee and some sugar. That sugar is the only thing adding calories, around 55 to 60 of them, and you can kill even that by ordering kosong.

This is the move if you love your kopitiam habit but want to keep the calories tiny. Compare kopi O at 60 calories to a normal kopi with condensed milk at 140 plus. Same caffeine hit, less than half the damage. Easy swap lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order kopi O kosong (no sugar) and it drops to about 5 calories
  • Ask for kurang manis (less sweet) to halve the sugar
  • Drink it hot instead of iced, you tend to sip slower and order less sugar

Common questions

How many calories in kopi O?
About 60 calories a glass, almost all of it from the sugar. Kosong with no sugar is just 5.
Is kopi O good for weight loss?
Yes, it is one of the lightest drinks at the kopitiam. No milk means almost no calories beyond the sugar you add.
What is the difference between kopi O and kopi C?
Kopi O is black with sugar, no milk, around 60 calories. Kopi C adds evaporated milk and lands closer to 110.
Berapa kalori dalam Kopi O?
Kopi O ada kira-kira 60 kalori untuk 1 gelas (250g), dengan 0g protein, 15g karbohidrat dan 0g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
