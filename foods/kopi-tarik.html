How Many Calories in Kopi Tarik?

Malaysia's classic pulled coffee, brewed from robusta beans and mixed with sweetened condensed milk and evaporated milk for that thick, caramelly kick.

150kcal / 1 glass (kopitiam standard)
A standard glass of kopi tarik at the kopitiam runs about 150 calories. That might not sound like much, but almost all of it is coming from roughly 40g of sweetened condensed milk, which alone clocks in at 120 calories. The coffee itself is practically nothing. So you are basically drinking dessert in a cup, just dressed up as your morning routine.

The real talk

Kopi tarik is 150 calories a glass, and before you say 'aiya that's nothing lah', just think about how many you drink in a day. One kopi tarik with breakfast, one after lunch, maybe one in the afternoon because your colleague belanja. That's 450 calories from drinks alone before you've even looked at your nasi. The calories here are almost entirely from the condensed milk. We're talking about 40g of the stuff per glass, which is 120 calories of pure sugar and fat. The evaporated milk adds another 20. The actual coffee? Ten calories. So the drink you think is 'just coffee' is mostly sweetened milk with a coffee flavour. To be fair, these numbers are ballpark estimates because there's no standardised recipe at the kopitiam. Uncle behind the counter pours by feel, and some hands are heavier than others.

The variant you order makes a massive difference. Swap to kopi O (black with sugar, no milk) and you drop to 45 calories. Kopi C, which uses evaporated milk instead of condensed, sits around 90 calories. That's a 60 to 105 calorie saving per cup just from changing how you order. If you're the type who drinks three coffees a day, switching from kopi tarik to kopi C could save you 180 calories daily without giving up your kopitiam habit. Kurang manis (less sweet) shaves off a bit more too, since the uncle uses slightly less condensed milk. None of these changes feel like dieting. You're still at the same table, same kopitiam, same uncle. Just a slightly different order.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order kopi C instead of kopi tarik. Evaporated milk instead of condensed drops you from 150 to about 90 calories, and honestly the taste is still damn good.
  • Add 'kurang manis' to every order. Less condensed milk means fewer calories, and most kopitiams will do it without blinking.
  • Switch to kopi O on at least one of your daily coffees. Forty-five calories and you still get the caffeine hit you need.
  • Stop counting kopi tarik as a free item. It is not water. Three cups a day is 450 calories, which is basically a full meal.
  • If you like iced coffee, kopi peng has the same calories as hot kopi tarik, so there's no magic in the ice. The only win is if the extra water from ice melt makes you feel slightly fuller.

Common questions

How many calories in Kopi Tarik?
A standard glass of kopi tarik at the kopitiam is about 150 calories. Almost all of that comes from the sweetened condensed milk, which makes up roughly 120 of those calories on its own.
Is kopi tarik bad for weight loss?
One cup is not going to ruin anything, but it adds up fast if you drink two or three a day. Three kopi tarik is 450 calories, which is more than some people's full meals. If you want to keep the habit without the calorie hit, switch to kopi C (about 90 cal) or kopi O (about 45 cal) for most of your daily coffees.
What is the difference between kopi tarik and teh tarik calories?
They are very close. Both use sweetened condensed milk as the main ingredient, so both land around 150 calories for a standard glass. The coffee versus tea base barely changes the number. If you want lower calories, the answer for both is the same: order C or O style instead.
