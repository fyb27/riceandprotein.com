How Many Calories in Kuey Teow Kung Fu?

Crispy deep-fried flat rice noodles topped with silky egg starch gravy, prawns, and squid, a Chinese hawker classic that tastes lighter than it actually is.

700kcal / 1 plate
One plate of Kuey Teow Kung Fu is around 700 calories for a standard 450g serving with mixed seafood. The deep-fried noodles alone account for 520 of those calories because the flat rice kuey teow soaks up a serious amount of oil in the wok before the gravy even comes into the picture. Add egg starch gravy, prawns, squid, and some vegetables and you are sitting at a solid 700 cal meal that is not terrible but not light either. Bear in mind this is a ballpark estimate since detailed lab data for this specific dish is thin.

The real talk

So 700 calories for one plate, that is not shocking but it is also not cheap. The villain here is not the gravy or even the seafood, it is those fried kuey teow noodles. When you deep-fry flat rice noodles they soak oil like a sponge, and at 520 calories just from the noodle portion alone, the maths are already stacked before the gravy hits. The egg starch gravy adds another 60 cal, and the mixed seafood at 95 cal is honestly the best value thing on the plate protein-wise. So you get 28g protein for 700 cal, which is decent but not remarkable. This dish suits someone who can fit a 700 cal lunch into their day without going crazy at dinner lah.

Now the honest bit: these numbers are a ballpark estimate because solid nutritional data for Kuey Teow Kung Fu specifically is basically nonexistent, so treat the 700 cal figure as a reasonable educated guess rather than a certified lab reading. What actually swings the number is how aggressively the hawker fries the noodles and how thick they pour the gravy. A stingier uncle with a lighter hand on the oil could realistically drop this to 620 to 650 cal. A generous uncle who double-fries for crispiness and pours a mountain of gravy could push it past 750 easily. If you skip the seafood and go vegetable-only gravy, you save about 100 cal and land at 600. Add pork or chicken to the gravy and you are up at 780. The biggest single lever you have is just asking for less noodles, bro, because 210g of fried kuey teow is honestly a lot.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask the hawker for less noodles, not less gravy. The fried kuey teow is where the fat lives. Cutting the portion from 210g down to around 150g could save you 150 to 180 calories on the spot.
  • Eat more of the gravy and seafood, less of the crispy noodle base. The prawns and squid give you protein at around 95 calories per 100g, which is genuinely good value.
  • Go for the seafood version over adding pork or chicken. The vegetable-only version at 600 cal is the lightest option if you want to trim without changing the dish fundamentally.
  • Ask for extra vegetables on the side. Bok choy and baby corn add almost nothing calorie-wise and fill up more of your stomach.
  • Pair with plain water or teh o kosong instead of teh tarik or Milo. That swap alone can save you 100 to 150 extra calories from drinks.

Common questions

How many calories in Kuey Teow Kung Fu?
A standard plate of Kuey Teow Kung Fu with mixed seafood is around 700 calories for approximately 450g. The deep-fried noodle base accounts for the bulk of that at roughly 520 calories, with the egg starch gravy and seafood making up the rest.
Is Kuey Teow Kung Fu healthier than Char Kuey Teow?
They are in a similar calorie range but Kuey Teow Kung Fu tends to have more protein from the seafood-heavy gravy. Char Kuey Teow is wok-fried with lard and cockles so the fat profile is different. Neither is a diet food, but Kung Fu style gives you more volume for the calories because of the generous gravy.
Why is Kuey Teow Kung Fu so high in calories if it has seafood and vegetables?
The calories come almost entirely from the noodles, not the toppings. The flat rice kuey teow is deep-fried first, and frying absorbs a significant amount of oil. That noodle base alone is about 520 calories. The seafood at 95 calories and vegetables at 25 calories are actually quite low. If you could eat just the gravy and toppings without the fried noodles it would be a totally different meal.
Berapa kalori dalam Kuey Teow Kung Fu?
Kuey Teow Kung Fu ada kira-kira 700 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (450g), dengan 28g protein, 80g karbohidrat dan 28g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
