How Many Calories in Kuey Teow Soup?

Flat rice noodles in a clear broth with fishballs and meat slices, one of the lighter noodle options at the hawker centre.

330kcal / 1 bowl
A standard bowl of Kuey Teow Soup comes in at around 330 calories for a 400g serving. That includes the flat rice noodles, fishballs, a few meat slices, and the clear broth. For a hawker noodle dish, that is actually pretty decent. Most stall portions will land somewhere in the 300 to 380 range depending on how generous the uncle is with the fishballs and meat.

The real talk

Honestly, Kuey Teow Soup is one of the more chill choices at a hawker centre if you are trying to lose weight. 330 calories for a full bowl that has noodles, fishballs, meat slices, and broth, that is not bad at all. Compare that to Char Kuey Teow which is double the calories because everything gets fried in lard. The reason this one stays light is the cooking method: it is basically just blanched noodles sitting in a clear bone broth. The fat is only 5g for the whole bowl. Most of those calories come from the 150g of rice noodles at 175 calories. Fair warning though, these are ballpark estimates. Hard data for Malaysian hawker dishes is thin on the ground, and every stall uncle has his own idea of what a normal portion looks like.

What actually moves the number up or down is pretty simple. More noodles is the biggest variable. Some stalls give you a 200g noodle portion instead of 150g and that alone adds 60 to 70 calories. The protein swaps matter too: fishballs at 80 calories per 80g are actually quite lean, but if you upgrade to prawn plus meat, you are probably looking at 90 to 110 calories from protein instead. Toppings are the sneaky one. A drizzle of chilli oil here, a tablespoon of fried shallots there, and suddenly your 330 calorie bowl is closer to 400. The broth itself is only about 20 calories so that is not the issue. The noodles and what sits on top of them is what you should be watching.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less noodles, maybe 100g instead of 150g, and you shave off about 40 to 50 calories without feeling that much different.
  • Skip the fried shallots and extra chilli oil some stalls add as toppings. They are not counted in the base estimate but they do add up.
  • Ask for extra bean sprouts and greens at no extra calories. More volume, still light.
  • If you are choosing between pork belly slices and lean pork or chicken slices, go for the leaner cuts. The meat portion is only 40g so the difference is small but it is there.
  • Eat it without the soup if you are watching sodium. The broth is low calorie but high in salt, which causes water retention if you are tracking weight daily.

Common questions

How many calories in Kuey Teow Soup?
A standard hawker bowl of Kuey Teow Soup is around 330 calories for roughly 400g. That covers the flat rice noodles, 4-5 fishballs, a few slices of meat, and the clear broth.
Is Kuey Teow Soup good for weight loss?
It is one of the better hawker options if you are watching calories. At 330 calories a bowl with 22g of protein, it fills you up without wrecking your day. Just go easy on the fried shallots and chilli oil some stalls put on top.
What is the difference in calories between Kuey Teow Soup and Char Kuey Teow?
Big difference. Kuey Teow Soup is about 330 calories while Char Kuey Teow is closer to 650 to 750 calories for a similar weight. The wok frying with lard and oil is what kills it. Same noodles, very different outcome.
Berapa kalori dalam Kuey Teow Soup?
Kuey Teow Soup ada kira-kira 330 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (400g), dengan 22g protein, 46g karbohidrat dan 5g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
