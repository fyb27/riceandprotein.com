How Many Calories in Kuih Badak Berendam?

A soft glutinous rice ball stuffed with gula melaka and coconut, sitting in a pool of warm coconut milk santan.

100kcal / 1 piece (ball in santan)
Each piece of Kuih Badak Berendam comes in at 100 calories per ball (about 45g including the santan). That is not a lot on paper, but the calories stack fast because nobody eats just one. The glutinous dough accounts for 47 of those calories, the gula melaka and coconut filling adds 30, and the santan gravy sitting underneath contributes the last 23. So roughly 40% of the damage is from the sweet coconut sauce, which you are definitely spooning up.

The real talk

Kuih Badak Berendam is 100 calories a ball, which sounds harmless until you realise you just had four of them without thinking. The glutinous rice dough is dense with carbs (47 calories in just 20g of cooked dough), the gula melaka and coconut filling adds another 30 calories of pure sugar and fat, and then there is the santan pooled at the bottom that you are going to spoon up anyway. It is the kind of kuih that feels light because it is small, but the calorie density is actually pretty high for its size. If you are a regular kuih snacker, this is worth knowing about.

What changes the numbers most is the santan. A thin, diluted coconut milk sauce puts you at 80 calories per piece. A thick, festival-grade coconut cream version pushes you to 130. That is a 60% swing from the same basic kuih, just from how the gravy was made. The filling matters too: more gula melaka means more calories, obviously, but the coconut in the filling also carries fat. Nobody is saying avoid this lah, it is a genuinely good kuih. But if you are tracking, count the santan separately because that is where the invisible calories live.

How to eat it lighter

  • Take just one ball and skip the extra santan poured on top. The sauce alone can add 20 to 30 calories per extra ladle.
  • Ask for thin or diluted santan if you are buying from a stall. Some aunties use coconut cream; the diluted version drops you closer to 80 calories per piece.
  • Stop at two pieces. Seriously, set the limit before you start eating, not after. Three pieces is already 300 calories from something that felt like a small bite.
  • Pair it with plain water or teh o kosong, not teh tarik. You do not need extra sugar calories alongside a sweet kuih.
  • If you are making it at home, reduce the gula melaka filling by a third and use light coconut milk for the santan. You will still get the flavour but save 15 to 20 calories per piece.

Common questions

How many calories in Kuih Badak Berendam?
One piece is 100 calories for a 45g serving, which includes the glutinous ball, gula melaka filling, and the coconut milk santan. Eat three and you are at 300 calories from a snack that takes about 90 seconds to finish.
Is Kuih Badak Berendam fattening?
Not if you stick to one or two pieces. The problem is the santan gravy: thick coconut milk is calorie-dense and it is very easy to slurp up extra without realising. The balls themselves are not huge, but the sauce is where the calories quietly add up.
Can I eat kuih like this and still lose weight?
Yes, but portion control is real. Two pieces is 200 calories, which is totally manageable if you count it into your day. The issue is kuih are small and sweet, so your brain does not register them as a proper snack. Track them the same way you would track anything else.
Berapa kalori dalam Kuih Badak Berendam?
Kuih Badak Berendam ada kira-kira 100 kalori untuk 1 keping (45g), dengan 1g protein, 16g karbohidrat dan 4g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
