How Many Calories in Kuih Bom?

Kuih Bom is a deep-fried glutinous rice dough ball coated in sesame seeds and filled with sweet gula melaka grated coconut, a classic Malaysian kuih that is dangerously easy to eat more than one of.

210kcal / 1 piece
One Kuih Bom is around 210 calories for a 55g piece. That comes from the glutinous rice dough mixed with coconut milk (100 cal), the sweet gula melaka coconut filling (60 cal), sesame seed coating (20 cal), and the frying oil the dough soaks up (30 cal). It is a small ball but it is not a light snack. Worth knowing before you grab three off the kuih tray.

The real talk

Kuih Bom is that round, golden, sesame-coated ball you see at kuih stalls and pasar malam. The dough is glutinous rice flour mixed with coconut milk, deep fried until puffed up, with a soft sweet coconut filling inside. At 210 calories per piece, it is not catastrophic if you eat one. The problem is nobody eats one. The calories come from three places: carbs from the glutinous flour, fat from the coconut milk in the dough plus the frying oil absorbed, and sugar from the gula melaka filling. Fair warning: these numbers are a ballpark estimate because detailed lab data for kuih bom specifically is thin, so treat it as a reasonable guide rather than a precise count.

What changes the number most is how much oil the dough absorbs during frying. Freshly fried pieces that have been properly drained will be on the lower end. Ones sitting in a tray soaked in oil, or fried at too low a temperature, absorb more fat and could push past 230 calories easily. The filling matters too: the chocolate variant in modern kuih shops runs about 230 calories versus the classic coconut version at 210. The sweet potato dough version (keledek) drops to around 185 calories because less glutinous flour is used. Two pieces of the classic version is already 420 calories, which is a full meal's worth of calories for some people. One is fine as a snack. Two is a decision you should make consciously lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Stop at one. Seriously. One piece is a proper snack at 210 calories. Two pieces is 420 calories and at that point you could have had a real meal.
  • Look for the sweet potato dough version if you see it. At around 185 calories it saves you 25 calories per piece and the texture is actually pretty good.
  • Eat it fresh off the fryer rather than from a tray that has been sitting. Fresh ones have had less time to absorb excess oil.
  • Pair it with plain water or unsweetened tea, not teh tarik or sirap. Adding a 150-calorie drink to a 210-calorie kuih turns a snack into a 360-calorie side quest.
  • Skip the chocolate filling variant. The extra sugar and fat in the chocolate version pushes it to around 230 calories for the same size piece, which is not worth it when the coconut filling is honestly better anyway.

Common questions

How many calories in Kuih Bom?
One Kuih Bom is around 210 calories for a standard 55g piece. This covers the glutinous rice dough, sweet coconut filling, sesame coating, and absorbed frying oil.
What exactly is Kuih Bom?
It is a Malaysian deep-fried kuih made from glutinous rice flour dough mixed with coconut milk, rolled in sesame seeds, then fried until it puffs up. Inside is a sweet filling of grated coconut cooked with gula melaka. Some modern versions use chocolate filling instead. You will find it at kuih stalls, pasar malam, and traditional bakeries.
Is Kuih Bom healthy?
It is a deep-fried sweet snack, so no, it is not something you eat for nutrition. At 210 calories per piece with 9g of fat and 30g of carbs, it is a treat. One piece occasionally is fine. The issue is portion control because they are small and easy to eat several at a time without noticing.
Berapa kalori dalam Kuih Bom?
Kuih Bom ada kira-kira 210 kalori untuk 1 keping (55g), dengan 3g protein, 30g karbohidrat dan 9g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
