How Many Calories in Kuih Cincin?

A hard, dark-brown ring cookie from Sabah, deep-fried from rice flour and palm sugar until it shatters like a cracker.

120kcal / 1 piece (ring cookie)
One piece of Kuih Cincin is roughly 120 calories. Each ring cookie weighs about 18g and packs 7g of fat and 12g of carbs, which is what you get when you double-fry something in oil. The number is a ballpark estimate since solid lab data for this traditional snack is thin, but it sits comfortably in the same territory as a small curry puff or a few pieces of Kuih Bangkit.

The real talk

Kuih Cincin is a traditional ring-shaped cookie from the Bajau and Brunei Malay communities in Sabah and Brunei. If you have never seen it before, imagine a small dark-brown ring that looks a bit like a miniature doughnut, except it is bone-dry, rock-hard and crunchy all the way through. It is made from rice flour and gula apong (palm sugar), which gives it that deep caramel flavour, and it is deep-fried twice in the traditional Sabah hawker version to get that shattering crunch. At 120 calories per piece, the main culprit is the frying oil, which accounts for about 34 of those calories on its own. It is not a diet food, and nobody is pretending it is. But because it is so dense and hard, you actually chew it slowly and tend to eat fewer pieces than you think. Fair warning though: these numbers are a genuine ballpark estimate because hard nutritional data for Kuih Cincin is basically nonexistent, so treat the figures as a reasonable guide rather than gospel.

What actually moves the maths is how many you grab. One piece is 120 calories, which is manageable. Three pieces is 360 calories, which is already a medium-sized meal in cookie form. The double-fried Sabah hawker version clocks in slightly higher at around 125 calories per piece versus the softer single-fried Brunei-Malay version at about 110 calories, because more frying means more oil absorbed. If you are buying these from a stall or pasar malam, they almost always come in packs of 10 to 20 pieces, and that is where it gets dangerous. Nobody stops at two. If you portion yourself to five pieces, you are looking at 600 calories from snacks alone, which is a lot for something that does not fill you up the same way real food does. Eat them as an occasional treat, not a bag-a-day habit.

How to eat it lighter

  • Stick to a hard limit of three pieces before you close the bag. At 120 calories each, three is 360 calories total, which is already a meaningful snack.
  • Go for the soft single-fried Brunei-Malay style if you can find it. It saves you about 15 calories per piece, and over a handful of pieces that actually adds up.
  • Pair a couple of pieces with plain black coffee or unsweetened tea instead of a sweetened drink. Teh tarik alongside adds another 100 to 150 calories on top.
  • Do not eat them standing at the stall. Sit down, put a fixed amount on a plate, and walk away from the pack. Out of sight really does help here.
  • If you are craving something crunchy and Malaysian that is lower calorie, a piece of Keropok Lekor or plain Ulam-Ulaman gives you crunch for fewer calories without the double-fried oil load.

Common questions

How many calories in Kuih Cincin?
One piece of Kuih Cincin is approximately 120 calories, based on a standard 18g ring cookie. The double-fried Sabah hawker version runs slightly higher at around 125 calories, while the softer Brunei-Malay style is closer to 110 calories.
What is Kuih Cincin made of?
Kuih Cincin is made primarily from rice flour and palm sugar (gula apong), shaped into rings and deep-fried until hard and dark brown. Some versions include a small amount of wheat flour. The double-frying is what gives it that distinctive crunch and also why the fat content is relatively high for a small cookie.
Is Kuih Cincin suitable if I am trying to lose weight?
It can fit into a calorie-controlled diet if you are strict about portions. At 120 calories per ring, a couple of pieces with your coffee is fine. The problem is portion creep because they come in big packs and are very easy to keep eating. Treat it as an occasional snack, count your pieces before you start, and it is manageable lah.
Berapa kalori dalam Kuih Cincin?
Kuih Cincin ada kira-kira 120 kalori untuk 1 keping (18g), dengan 2g protein, 12g karbohidrat dan 7g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
