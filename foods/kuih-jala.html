How Many Calories in Kuih Jala (Roti Jala)?

Lacy net crepes served with chicken curry or sweet coconut kuah, a popular teatime snack and kenduri staple.

240kcal / 2 pieces with curry
Two pieces of kuih jala with chicken curry comes in at around 240 calories for a 170g serving. The lacy crepes themselves are only about 100 calories for the pair, but the coconut milk curry gravy adds roughly 110 calories on top. So it is honestly a pretty reasonable snack, as long as you are not drowning the thing in extra gravy.

The real talk

Honestly, kuih jala is one of the more diet-friendly traditional kuihs out there, bro. Two pieces with curry sits at roughly 240 calories, which is not bad at all for a proper snack. The crepes themselves are thin and airy, only about 100 calories for the pair. The real calories come from the curry gravy, around 110 calories for 80g of coconut milk-based curry, and the small bits of chicken tucked in. Worth flagging: these numbers are a ballpark estimate, because detailed lab data for kuih jala specifically is basically nonexistent. We cross-referenced the crepe batter (egg, flour, coconut milk, turmeric) and standard chicken curry macros to get here. Treat it as a reasonable guide, not gospel.

What actually swings the calories is how much gravy you pour over. Go light on the curry and you are closer to 180-190 calories. Go heavy with a generous ladle, maybe add a second dip, and you are pushing past 300 easily. Switching to sweet kuah (coconut milk dipping sauce) instead of curry drops you to about 200 calories since the sweet sauce is thinner and has less fat overall. Going from 2 pieces to 3 pieces adds roughly 50 calories from the extra crepe plus a bit more gravy. If you are at a kenduri and someone piles on three pieces with a big scoop of curry, you are probably looking at 320 calories. Still not a disaster, but it adds up if you eat four rounds because the food is just sitting there.

How to eat it lighter

  • Stick to 2 pieces and be deliberate about how much curry you ladle on. The gravy is where the calories hide.
  • Ask for sweet kuah instead of coconut curry if you are watching calories. Drops the serving by about 40 calories.
  • Use the curry as a dip rather than pouring it over. You end up using maybe half the gravy.
  • At kenduri or family meals, eat slowly. These crepes are filling faster than they look because the batter has egg and coconut milk.
  • Pair with teh o kosong or plain water instead of teh tarik. You do not want to add another 100-plus calories from a sweet drink on top.

Common questions

How many calories in Kuih Jala (Roti Jala)?
Two pieces of kuih jala with chicken curry is approximately 240 calories for a 170g serving. The crepes themselves contribute about 100 calories, with the coconut milk curry gravy adding another 110 calories. Switching to sweet kuah instead of curry brings it down to around 200 calories.
Is kuih jala fattening?
Not really, especially compared to other Malaysian kuihs. Two pieces with curry is 240 calories, which is a reasonable snack. The fat content comes mainly from the coconut milk in the curry gravy. Control the gravy portion, stick to two pieces, and you are totally fine lah, just watch the gravy.
What is the difference in calories between kuih jala with curry and with sweet kuah?
About 40 calories. Two pieces with sweet coconut dipping sauce is around 200 calories, while two pieces with chicken curry is around 240 calories. The curry is heavier because of the coconut milk base and the protein from the chicken pieces.
Berapa kalori dalam Kuih Jala?
Kuih Jala ada kira-kira 240 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (170g), dengan 11g protein, 20g karbohidrat dan 11g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
