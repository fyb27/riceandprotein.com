How Many Calories in Kuih Ketayap?

Pandan crepe rolled around sweet coconut and gula melaka filling. The filling, not the thin crepe, sets the calories.

150kcal / 1 roll (~65g)
One kuih ketayap roll is about 150 calories. The thin pandan crepe is light, so the gula melaka coconut filling inside is what drives the calories.

The real talk

Kuih ketayap, also called kuih gulung or dadar, is a thin green pandan crepe rolled around a sweet filling of grated coconut and gula melaka. The crepe itself is light, so the inti (filling) is the calorie story. One roll is around 150 calories, mostly the coconut and palm sugar inside.

It is a lovely treat and one roll is reasonable. Richer versions with butter in the crepe or extra coconut can climb toward 200 plus, so a homemade generous one is heavier than a stall one. Either way, one is a treat, not a snack.

How to eat it lighter

  • One roll is a sensible treat. Two with a sweet drink is where it adds up.
  • Stall versions are usually lighter than rich homemade ones with extra butter.
  • It is a treat with almost no protein, do not lean on it to stay full.

Common questions

How many calories in kuih ketayap?
About 150 calories per roll. The thin pandan crepe is light, so the coconut and gula melaka filling inside is the main calorie source.
Is kuih ketayap healthy?
It is a treat at around 150 calories a roll, mostly coconut and palm sugar with little protein. One is fine, just not a filling snack.
Why do some kuih ketayap have more calories?
Richer recipes add butter to the crepe or extra coconut to the filling, pushing a roll toward 200 or more. Stall versions are usually lighter.
Berapa kalori dalam Kuih Ketayap?
Kuih Ketayap ada kira-kira 150 kalori untuk 1 gulung (65g), dengan 3g protein, 22g karbohidrat dan 7g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
