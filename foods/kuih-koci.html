How Many Calories in Kuih Koci?

Banana-leaf pyramid of glutinous rice flour with sweet coconut and gula melaka filling. Double coconut makes it the dense one.

175kcal / 1 piece (~60g)
One kuih koci is about 175 calories, the heaviest of the common kuih. It packs a double dose of coconut, in the glutinous skin and the sweet filling, plus gula melaka.

The real talk

Kuih koci is that little black or green pyramid wrapped in banana leaf, with a soft glutinous skin around a sweet coconut and gula melaka filling. It is the heavyweight of the everyday kuih at around 175 calories, because coconut shows up twice, in the dough and in the inti (filling), on top of the palm sugar.

One is a satisfying treat. The pulut hitam (black glutinous) version is about the same. Just know this one is denser than the pretty layered kuih next to it on the tray, so one koci is plenty.

How to eat it lighter

  • One is enough, this is the densest of the common kuih.
  • Do not pair two koci with a sweet drink, that is a meal's worth of sugar.
  • Treat, not a snack that fills you. Almost no protein.

Common questions

How many calories in kuih koci?
About 175 calories a piece, making it one of the heaviest everyday kuih. Coconut appears twice, in the glutinous skin and the filling, plus gula melaka.
Why is kuih koci higher in calories than other kuih?
It carries a double dose of coconut, in both the dough and the sweet filling, which adds fat on top of the gula melaka sugar.
Is kuih koci pulut hitam different in calories?
Barely. The black glutinous version is around 180 versus 175 for the coconut one. The filling and gula melaka are similar.
Berapa kalori dalam Kuih Koci?
Kuih Koci ada kira-kira 175 kalori untuk 1 keping (60g), dengan 2g protein, 28g karbohidrat dan 7g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
