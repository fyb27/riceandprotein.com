How Many Calories in Kuih Lapis?

Also known as Kueh Lapis.

The peelable rainbow kuih. Almost pure starch and sugar, with coconut milk for a little fat. Carbs are the whole story.

150kcal / 1 piece (~50g)
One piece of kuih lapis is about 150 calories. It is essentially layers of rice and tapioca flour set with sugar and a little coconut milk, so it is almost pure carbs with very little protein.

The real talk

Kuih lapis is the fun peelable rainbow one, and it is basically sweetened starch. Rice flour, tapioca or sago flour, sugar and a bit of coconut milk, layered and steamed. That makes a piece around 150 calories, almost all carbs, with barely any protein since there is no egg in it.

It is a treat, plain and simple. Nothing wrong with one piece, just do not kid yourself that peeling it layer by layer makes it healthy. The carbs are the carbs whether you eat it in one bite or ten.

How to eat it lighter

  • One piece is the move, these are easy to overeat because they are fun.
  • Zero protein here, so it does not fill you up. Do not treat it as a snack that holds you over.
  • Have it with a kosong drink, not a sweet one.

Common questions

How many calories in kuih lapis?
About 150 calories for a standard piece. It is mostly rice and tapioca flour set with sugar, so it is almost entirely carbs with very little protein.
Is kuih lapis healthy?
It is a treat, not a health food. Around 150 calories of sweetened starch with no real protein, so it does not keep you full. One piece is fine.
Why is kuih lapis low in protein?
There is no egg or dairy in it, just rice and tapioca flour, sugar and coconut milk, so it is essentially pure carbohydrate.
Berapa kalori dalam Kuih Lapis?
Kuih Lapis ada kira-kira 150 kalori untuk 1 keping (50g), dengan 2g protein, 30g karbohidrat dan 4g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
