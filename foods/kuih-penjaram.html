How Many Calories in Kuih Penjaram (Pinjaram)?

A Sabah and Sarawak deep-fried rice-flour fritter sweetened with gula apong, shaped like a little UFO with a chewy centre and lacy crisp edges.

210kcal / 1 piece (medium, pasar tamu size)
One medium kuih penjaram (the standard pasar tamu size, about 50g) comes in at roughly 210 calories. Most of that comes from the rice flour batter and palm sugar together at around 167 calories, with the frying oil adding another 43 calories on top. Be aware this is a ballpark estimate since solid nutrition data for penjaram specifically is basically non-existent, but 210 calories per medium piece is a reasonable working number.

The real talk

Penjaram (also spelled pinjaram or penyaram depending on who you ask) is a Sabah and Sarawak thing that a lot of peninsular Malaysians have never actually tried. Think of it like a fried fritter shaped like a UFO or a little hat, with a thick chewy middle and a thin lacy edge that goes properly crispy from the fryer. The batter is rice flour, plain flour, and gula apong, which is Bornean palm sugar with a deep caramel, almost smoky sweetness. It is genuinely good. At 210 calories per medium piece, it is not a disaster either, but those calories are basically all carbs and frying oil. The palm sugar alone contributes about 84 calories, and the oil absorbed during frying adds another 43. There is almost no protein here, which is why penjaram is a snack, not a meal. To be straight with you: these numbers are estimates. Hard data on penjaram calories is thin, so treat 210 as a reasonable ballpark rather than a lab-tested figure.

The maths changes quite a bit depending on where you buy it and what size you grab. The small ones sold three for RM1 at Sabah tamu are only around 130 calories each because they are roughly 30g finished weight, less batter, less oil. The big ones at places like Donggongon tamu can hit 290 calories at around 70g. If you are calorie counting, the size difference matters more than anything else here. The other variable is how much oil the individual fritter absorbs during frying, which depends on oil temperature and how long it sits in the wok. A hotter, faster fry absorbs less oil. You cannot really control that at a market stall, but knowing it helps you understand why two penjaram from different stalls can taste and feel quite different. If you want to enjoy a couple pieces at the tamu without it wrecking your day, just know two medium ones is about 420 calories lah, so factor that in before adding nasi lemak on top.

How to eat it lighter

  • Go for the small tamu size (three for RM1 style) instead of the medium. You drop from 210 to 130 calories per piece, that is a real difference.
  • Eat it fresh and hot. A penjaram that has been sitting out re-absorbs some oil as it cools, so you get the lacy edge at its crispiest and the oil at its lowest right out of the wok.
  • Stop at two pieces. Two small ones is about 260 calories, which is a proper snack. Two large ones is close to 580 calories, which is basically a full meal.
  • If you are at the tamu, pair penjaram with something protein-based like a hard-boiled egg or a piece of grilled chicken rather than stacking up on more kuih. You will stay fuller and the calorie count stays manageable.
  • Skip the ones sitting in a covered container sweating. Go for the stall that is actively frying. Fresher means less oil absorption and honestly they just taste better.

Common questions

How many calories in Kuih Penjaram (Pinjaram)?
A medium kuih penjaram at pasar tamu size (about 50g) is roughly 210 calories. The small tamu ones are around 130 calories each, and the large market versions can hit 290 calories. These are estimates since there is no official nutritional data for penjaram.
What exactly is kuih penjaram?
Penjaram is a deep-fried Bornean kuih made from a rice flour and palm sugar batter. It is shaped like a UFO or small hat, with a thick chewy centre and a thin lacy edge that crisps up in the fryer. The sweetness comes from gula apong, a Sabah palm sugar with a distinctly caramel, almost smoky flavour. You find it all over Sabah and Sarawak pasar tamu, sometimes spelled pinjaram or penyaram but it is all the same kuih.
Is penjaram healthy?
Honest answer: it is a deep-fried sugar fritter, so not exactly a health food. At 210 calories per medium piece with almost zero protein and 6g of fat, it is a snack that sits purely in carbs and frying oil. That said, two small pieces at around 260 calories total is a perfectly reasonable treat if you are calorie counting. Just do not eat five of them and call it a light snack.
