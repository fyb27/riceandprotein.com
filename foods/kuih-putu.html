How Many Calories in Kuih Putu?

Kuih Putu is a traditional steamed kuih made from rice flour, filled with melted gula melaka palm sugar, and rolled in grated coconut, sold fresh off the steamer at pasar malam and morning markets across Malaysia.

230kcal / 2 pieces
Two pieces of Kuih Putu (about 60g) comes in at roughly 230 calories, with 42g carbs, 5g fat, and barely any protein to speak of. The gula melaka filling is doing the most work here, contributing around 77 calories on its own. This is a ballpark estimate, not a lab result, so treat it as a solid guide rather than gospel.

The real talk

Kuih Putu is one of those snacks that feels innocent because it is small and steamed, not fried. If you have never had it, picture a soft cylinder or disc of steamed rice flour with a molten palm sugar centre and grated coconut coating the outside. 230 calories for two pieces is not bad for a kuih, but the catch is that nobody really stops at two. Grab four and you are already at 460 calories from something you barely noticed eating. The gula melaka is the main calorie driver at around 77 kcal per serving, and the coconut adds another 46. Fair to note: calorie data for traditional kuih is thin, so this is a genuine estimate based on the known components, not a precise lab figure.

What actually changes the count is how generous the vendor is with filling and coconut. A stall that loads up the gula melaka and piles on extra coconut can easily push a serving to 270 calories or more. Putu piring, the bigger disc-shaped version, runs around 260 calories because the mold holds more dough and filling. If you are eating this as a snack, two pieces is fine. The problem is buying a whole bag of six or eight at the pasar malam and finishing them on the drive home. That is 700 plus calories gone before dinner, and your brain did not even register it as a meal.

How to eat it lighter

  • Stick to two pieces, not the whole packet. It sounds obvious but this is genuinely where most people go wrong with kuih.
  • Go for putu bambu over putu piring if you have a choice. The bamboo tube version is smaller and more consistent at around 230 calories versus 260 for the disc.
  • Skip the extra coconut topping if the vendor asks. That generous handful adds 20 to 40 calories and you honestly will not miss it.
  • Eat it as a standalone snack with plain water or teh o kosong, not paired with other kuih. It is easy to collect three or four different kuih at the market and suddenly you have had a full meal in finger food form.
  • Eat it fresh off the steamer when it is piping hot. You slow down naturally and actually taste it, instead of powering through a cold bag of them.

Common questions

How many calories in Kuih Putu?
About 230 calories for 2 pieces (60g), which is the standard hawker serving. This covers the rice flour shell, gula melaka filling, and grated coconut coating. It is an estimate since lab data for traditional kuih is limited, but it is a solid ballpark.
What exactly is Kuih Putu?
It is a Malaysian steamed kuih made from rice flour dough, shaped into cylinders (putu bambu) or discs (putu piring), with a centre of gula melaka palm sugar that melts when steamed. The outside is coated in grated coconut. It is sold fresh and hot, typically at morning markets and pasar malam.
Is Kuih Putu healthy?
It is not a health food, but it is also not deep-fried, so it is better than a lot of snacks. The problem is it is almost entirely simple carbs and sugar with almost no protein. Two pieces is a reasonable treat. A whole bag of six is basically dessert pretending to be a snack.
