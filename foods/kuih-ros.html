How Many Calories in Kuih Ros (Kuih Loyang)?

Kuih Ros, also called Kuih Loyang, is a deep-fried crispy snack made by dipping a brass mould into coconut-egg batter and frying it until it pops off in a thin, honeycomb-shaped shell.

75kcal / 2 pieces
Two pieces of Kuih Ros (about 20g) comes in at 75 calories, with 5g fat, 7g carbs, and 1g protein. On paper that sounds harmless. The problem is the serving size. Two pieces is about the weight of a 50 sen coin. At a Raya open house you're looking at 10 to 20 pieces without even noticing, which puts you at 375 to 750 calories from a snack you didn't really plan to eat.

The real talk

Kuih Ros is one of those festive snacks that looks delicate and tiny so your brain tells you it's basically calorie-free. It's not. Each piece is mostly deep-fried batter, so the fat content is doing most of the work here. The batter itself is rice flour, a bit of corn starch, sugar, coconut milk, and egg, all dipped in hot oil until a thin crispy shell forms. Pretty light per piece at 75 calories for 2 pieces, sure, but the density of oil absorbed relative to the size is what gets you. Worth flagging that these numbers are a ballpark estimate since there's almost no hard lab data on home-fried kuih, so treat the figures as a reasonable guide rather than gospel.

What actually moves the number is how many you eat and how much oil the batter soaks up, and both of those vary a lot. Thinner batter means less mass but also potentially more surface area for oil absorption. A plate of 10 pieces is roughly 375 calories and you can clear that in under 5 minutes while talking lah. If you're at a makan table, put a small handful on your plate and walk away from the serving tray. Pair it with plain water or teh-o kosong rather than syrup drinks. The kuih itself has very little protein, so don't fool yourself into thinking it's a snack that keeps you full. It won't.

How to eat it lighter

  • Count before you eat. Put 4 to 6 pieces on your plate (150 to 225 calories) and go sit somewhere away from the tray.
  • Eat it slowly. The crunch is the whole point and rushing means you lose track of how many you've had.
  • Balance it with something with protein, a hard-boiled egg or some satay nearby so you don't just snack on pure carbs and fat.
  • Skip the condensed milk dip if it's being served. That adds another 30 to 50 calories per dip and most people dip multiple times.
  • Drink a full glass of water first. It won't change the calories but it genuinely slows you down.

Common questions

How many calories in Kuih Ros (Kuih Loyang)?
Two pieces of Kuih Ros (20g) has about 75 calories, with 5g fat, 7g carbs, and 1g protein. These are estimate figures since hard data on home-fried kuih is thin, but it's a reasonable ballpark.
What exactly is Kuih Ros?
It's a traditional Malaysian deep-fried snack made by heating a decorative brass mould in oil, dipping it into a thin batter of rice flour, coconut milk, egg, and sugar, then lowering it back into the hot oil until the batter sets and releases as a crispy shell. The rose or honeycomb shape comes from the mould. It's common at Hari Raya and festive gatherings.
Is Kuih Ros a healthy snack?
Not really, bro. It's deep-fried, has no meaningful protein, and the portion sizes at actual events are way beyond 2 pieces. It's a festive treat, enjoy it as one. If you're tracking calories, count your pieces before you eat rather than trying to remember afterwards.
Berapa kalori dalam Kuih Ros?
Kuih Ros ada kira-kira 75 kalori untuk 2 keping (20g), dengan 1g protein, 7g karbohidrat dan 5g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
