How Many Calories in Kung Pao Chicken?

Stir-fried chicken cubes with dried chili, peanuts or cashews, onion and a savoury-sweet kung pao sauce.

480kcal / 1 plate
A plate of kung pao chicken sits around 480 calories, with 30g protein, 24g carbs and 29g fat. Compared to battered dishes it is actually one of the better zi char picks because the chicken is usually just diced and stir-fried, not deep fried in batter. The fat comes mostly from the wok oil and the nuts, and the sauce keeps the carbs moderate rather than crazy.

The real talk

Kung pao chicken is a solid order if you are watching things, and I do not say that about most zi char plates. The chicken is cubed and stir-fried, not battered and deep fried, so you skip that whole layer of flour and absorbed oil that wrecks dishes like sweet and sour. At around 480 calories with a genuine 30g of protein, the macros are actually not bad. The fat number looks high, but a good chunk of it is the wok oil and the handful of peanuts or cashews tossed in, which at least bring something to the table besides empty calories.

Where it goes wrong is oil-heavy versions and the nut load. Some shops are generous with the wok oil and the dish arrives swimming, which can add 100 calories easily. The cashew version is richer than the peanut one because cashews are denser. And like every dish here, the rice is the real trap, not the chicken. If you treat the kung pao as your protein anchor and keep the rice to one scoop, you are eating well. Pick this over butter prawns or marmite chicken any day if you want flavour without the deep-fry tax.

How to eat it lighter

  • Choose kung pao over the battered dishes. Stir-fried beats deep fried every time on calories.
  • Ask them to go light on the oil. A drier wok finish can save you 80 to 100 calories per plate.
  • Eat the chicken and veg, go easy on scooping up the loose nuts and oil pooled at the bottom.
  • Use it as your protein main and keep rice to one scoop, that is where this meal stays reasonable.

Common questions

How many calories in kung pao chicken?
A standard plate is about 480 calories, with 30g protein, 24g carbs and 29g fat. Oil-heavy or cashew versions can reach 520 to 560.
Is kung pao chicken healthier than sweet and sour chicken?
Generally yes. Kung pao chicken is stir-fried, not battered and deep fried, so you skip the flour coating and a lot of absorbed oil. It also has more protein and fewer carbs per plate.
What makes kung pao chicken high in fat?
Mostly the wok oil and the peanuts or cashews. The chicken itself is lean since it is diced and stir-fried, so if the plate arrives swimming in oil, that is where the extra fat is hiding.
Berapa kalori dalam Kung Pao Chicken?
Kung Pao Chicken ada kira-kira 480 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (260g), dengan 30g protein, 24g karbohidrat dan 29g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
