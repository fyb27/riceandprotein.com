How Many Calories in Kway Chap?

Also known as Kueh Chap, 粿汁, Kway Chap with Braised Pork.

Kway chap is the Teochew hawker set of broad rice sheets in a dark herbal soy broth, served with a plate of braised pork belly, intestines, pig skin, tau kwa, tau pok and a braised egg.

650kcal / 1 set for one (kway in soup + braised platter)
A standard one person kway chap set is about 650 calories for a 611g serving, with around 26g of fat. The bowl of kway in broth is only about 190 calories. The braised platter with pork belly, intestines, egg and beancurd is the other 460.

The real talk

The bowl of kway is the cheap part of the calorie bill. 300g of flat rice sheets in braised broth is around 190 calories, and the broth itself adds very little. The plate next to it is the problem. Three slices of braised pork belly are about 250 calories on their own. Add 60g of intestines and pig skin at about 90, a braised egg at 75, and some tau kwa and tau pok at 50, and you are at 650 before you have finished counting.

The pork belly is the swing item. A S$5 set usually comes with 2 to 3 slices. Ask for lean meat or pork shoulder instead and the same set drops to roughly 520 calories. Ask for extra belly and it can pass 850. Nobody weighs it, so what lands on the plate depends on the auntie and how much she likes you.

One honest thing about kway chap. The offal gets blamed, but 60g of braised intestines is about 90 calories, less than one slice of belly. The intestines are mostly protein and some fat. If you like them, keep them. Drop a slice of belly instead and you will save more.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for lean pork or pork shoulder instead of belly. That alone cuts around 130 calories from a standard set.
  • Get the kway plain with just tau kwa, egg and one piece of meat. A set like that is about 400 calories.
  • Skip the extra soup ladle over the platter. The braised sauce is salty and oily, roughly 30 calories per spoon.
  • Share the S$10 platter between two people and each order your own bowl of kway. Same taste, half the meat.

Common questions

Is kway chap fattening?
The braised pork belly is. A standard 650 calorie set has 26g of fat and most of it comes from 3 slices of belly. Swap to lean pork and the same set is around 520 calories with half the fat.
How many calories in kway chap without meat?
The bowl of kway in broth is about 190 calories. Add a braised egg and tau kwa and you are at roughly 310, which is a light meal with about 15g of protein.
Are the intestines high in calories?
Not really. 60g of braised intestines is about 90 calories. One slice of pork belly is around 85. The intestines are higher in cholesterol, but for calories they are not the main issue.
How much protein is in kway chap?
A standard set gives about 30g of protein from the pork, egg and beancurd. It is one of the higher protein hawker meals, which is the argument for eating it with the lean cuts.
Berapa kalori dalam Kway Chap?
Kway Chap ada kira-kira 650 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (611g), dengan 30g protein, 68g karbohidrat dan 26g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
