How Many Calories in Lala Masak?

Lala Masak is stir-fried clams cooked with garlic, cili padi or ginger, oyster sauce and a generous pour of oil at your average hawker stall.

250kcal / 1 hawker plate (approx 400g shell-on clams, ~150g edible serving)
A standard hawker plate of Lala Masak comes in at around 250 calories for the edible portion, which is roughly 150g of clam meat and sauce from a 400g shell-on serving. The clam meat itself is actually quite lean at about 74 calories per 100g cooked, but the cooking oil alone accounts for around 136 calories, so the shells are doing you a favour by keeping portion sizes honest. Protein sits at 18g, which is decent for a seafood side dish.

The real talk

Lala are small saltwater clams and this dish is a Malaysian hawker staple. You order a plate, you get a heap of shells, you dig out the little nuggets of clam meat and you mop up the sauce with whatever carbs you ordered alongside. The clam meat itself is genuinely low calorie and high protein, very similar to other shellfish. The 250 calories here is a ballpark estimate, honestly, because reliable nutrition data for hawker-cooked lala is thin. Different stalls use wildly different amounts of oil and sauce, so treat this as a reasonable middle-ground guess rather than a precise number.

What actually swings the calories up or down is the oil. The hawker version uses around 1.2 tablespoons of oil in the wok, which contributes about 136 of those 250 calories. Kam Heong lala, which adds dried prawns, curry leaves and more sauce, runs closer to 300 calories. Sambal lala pushes to around 320 because sambal paste is oil-heavy by nature. The halia version with ginger and spring onion is the lightest at about 220 calories because the sauce is simpler and the oil tends to be more restrained. If you are eating lala as a side to white rice, the lala itself is not your problem. Watch the rice portion and whatever sauce you pour over it.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order the masak halia version if available. The ginger and spring onion base is simpler, uses less oil, and saves you around 30 calories per plate.
  • Ask for kurang minyak if you can. Some stalls will actually listen. Even a small reduction in oil cuts 50 to 100 calories.
  • Skip soaking up the leftover sauce with bread or rice. The sauce sitting at the bottom of the plate carries a good chunk of that oil.
  • Pair lala with a steamed or boiled side rather than fried rice or noodles. The clam meat is lean enough that your overall meal stays reasonable.
  • Stick to one plate as a shared side rather than eating a full plate solo. At 250 calories it is fine, but lala is meant to be shared at the table anyway.

Common questions

How many calories in Lala Masak?
A hawker plate of Lala Masak is around 250 calories for the edible portion (roughly 150g of clam meat and sauce from a 400g shell-on plate). The clam meat is only about 74 calories per 100g cooked; the cooking oil is what pushes it up to 250.
What exactly is Lala Masak?
Lala is a type of small saltwater clam common at Malaysian seafood stalls. Masak just means cooked. You get a plate of clams stir-fried with garlic, oyster sauce, soy sauce and usually either cili padi or ginger. You crack them open at the table and eat the little piece of clam meat inside.
Is Lala Masak a good choice if I am trying to lose weight?
The clam meat itself is great: high protein, low fat, low calorie. The issue is the stir-fry oil used at the hawker stall. If you can go for the masak halia version or ask for less oil, it sits around 220 calories and 18g protein, which is a solid side dish. Just do not drown your rice in the leftover sauce.
Berapa kalori dalam Lala Masak?
Lala Masak ada kira-kira 250 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (150g), dengan 18g protein, 8g karbohidrat dan 15g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
