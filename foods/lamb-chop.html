How Many Calories in Lamb Chop?

A full plate is about 680 calories. Lamb is fattier than chicken, so the meat itself counts more.

680kcal / 1 plate
A mamak Western lamb chop plate is around 680 calories. You get two to three lamb chops (grilled, usually two ribs of meat), a pile of fries, a bit of coleslaw, and black pepper or mushroom sauce. That comes to roughly 40g protein, 45g carbs and 38g fat. Lamb is naturally fattier than chicken, so the meat carries more of the calories here.

The real talk

Unlike the chicken chop where the meat is lean, lamb brings its own fat to the party. There is marbling running through the chops and a strip of fat along the bone, so even grilled, the meat itself is a chunky 350 plus calories before you touch the sides.

Then you add fries and a ladle of black pepper sauce, which is usually butter and flour based, and you are at 680. It is a good protein meal at 40g, but it is not a light one. If you want it leaner, trim the fat strip off the chops and put the sauce on the side.

How to eat it lighter

  • Trim the visible fat strip off each chop before eating. Lamb fat is dense, this saves real calories.
  • Swap fries for a side salad or extra veg. The fries are the easiest 200 plus to drop.
  • Get the black pepper or mushroom sauce on the side and dip. Those sauces are butter heavy.

Common questions

How many calories in a lamb chop plate?
A full mamak lamb chop plate is around 680 calories with fries and sauce. Lamb is fattier than chicken, so the meat itself carries more of the calories, about 380.
Is lamb chop higher in calories than chicken chop?
The meat is, yes. Lamb has natural marbling and a fat strip on the bone, so it is denser than lean chicken thigh. The full plates end up close once you add the same fries and sauce.
How do I eat lamb chop with fewer calories?
Trim the fat strip off each chop, swap the fries for salad, and keep the sauce on the side. That can bring it down towards 500.
Berapa kalori dalam Lamb Chop?
Lamb Chop ada kira-kira 680 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (380g), dengan 40g protein, 45g karbohidrat dan 38g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
