How Many Calories in Lassi?

Lassi is a blended yogurt drink from South Asian cuisine, sold at mamak and Indian restaurants across Malaysia in sweet mango, rose, or simple salted versions.

260kcal / 1 glass (mango lassi)
A standard mango lassi (300ml glass) comes in at around 260 calories. That is not a small number for a drink bro, and most of it is coming from the sugar and mango puree, not the yogurt itself. Salted lassi (namkeen) is a completely different story at just 110 calories. These are ballpark estimates since detailed lab data for the version served at your local mamak is basically nonexistent, so treat the numbers as honest guesses, not gospel.

The real talk

Lassi is a blended yogurt drink, usually full-fat yogurt thinned out with milk or water and then sweetened. You see it a lot at mamak stalls and Indian restaurants, sitting next to the teh tarik and Milo ais. The mango version is the crowd favourite, and at 260 calories per 300ml glass, it is more like a light dessert than a drink. The yogurt brings some protein (8g), which is actually decent, but the 18g of sugar and the mango puree push the carbs up to 42g. To be upfront, these numbers are estimates based on standard recipes because there is no solid lab data for the specific version your uncle's mamak is blending up. The actual calories could be 20 to 30 calories higher or lower depending on how heavy-handed they are with the sugar.

The biggest variable here is sugar. Some places dump in two tablespoons of sugar per glass without blinking. That alone can add another 60 to 90 calories. The size matters a lot too: a generous restaurant pour at 400ml bumps you to 345 calories without changing anything else. If you want to keep it lighter, ask for the salted lassi (namkeen) instead, which is just yogurt, water, and salt at around 110 calories. Or go for sweet lassi without fruit at 175 calories, which is decent if you want that yogurt flavour without committing to a full mango lassi. The main lever is always the sugar, so asking for less sweet is worth doing lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order salted lassi (namkeen) instead of mango, you drop from 260 to 110 calories in one swap.
  • Ask them to make it less sweet, cutting even half the sugar saves around 35 calories.
  • Go for the smaller serve if you can, since a 300ml glass versus a 400ml restaurant pour is an 85-calorie difference.
  • Treat it as dessert, not a thirst-quencher, so you are not drinking one alongside a full meal.
  • If you are making it at home, use low-fat yogurt and skip the milk entirely to bring it closer to 180 to 200 calories.

Common questions

How many calories in Lassi?
A standard mango lassi (300ml glass) has around 260 calories. Salted lassi is much lower at about 110 calories, and a sweet lassi without fruit sits at around 175 calories.
What exactly is lassi?
Lassi is a yogurt-based drink blended smooth and served chilled. The sweet mango version is the most common in Malaysia, but the traditional salted version (namkeen lassi) is still popular at proper Indian restaurants. It is tangy, filling, and a lot more caloric than it looks when it is loaded with sugar.
Is lassi a good choice when dieting?
The salted version is genuinely decent at 110 calories and gives you some protein from the yogurt. Mango lassi at 260 calories is fine occasionally but it is basically a liquid dessert, so do not drink it every day thinking it is healthy just because it has yogurt in it.
Berapa kalori dalam Lassi?
Lassi ada kira-kira 260 kalori untuk 1 gelas (300g), dengan 8g protein, 42g karbohidrat dan 6g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
