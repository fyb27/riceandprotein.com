How Many Calories in Lechon Kawali?

Also known as Crispy Fried Pork Belly, Litson Kawali, Bagnet.

Pork belly boiled until tender, then deep fried until the skin puffs and cracks. Served chopped with lechon sauce or toyomansi.

520kcal / 1 serving
A carinderia serving of lechon kawali, about 130g or 6 to 8 chopped pieces, is around 520 calories. Deep fried pork belly runs about 400 calories per 100g. With 1 cup of rice the plate is 780. The dipping sauce adds another 20 to 40.

The real talk

Pork belly is about half fat by weight before it touches the pan. Boiling it first renders some of that out, then frying puts oil back in, and you end with roughly 400 calories per 100g. A 130g carinderia scoop is 520 calories, which is more than a Jollibee Chickenjoy with rice. Each chopped cube is around 70 calories, so counting cubes is the honest way to track it.

Bagnet, the Ilocano version, is fried twice and comes out even drier and crunchier. The second fry pushes it past 450 calories per 100g. Lechon kawali houses in Manila usually serve 200g portions for 180 to 220 pesos, which is 800 calories before rice. Add the standard cup and the sizzling plate crowd is at 1,060 for lunch.

The sauce choice moves the number less than people think. Mang Tomas is 40 calories per 30g, toyomansi is about 10, spiced vinegar is nearly zero. What actually matters is whether the pieces came from the thin end of the belly (more meat) or the thick end (more fat). Ask for the leaner end if the cook lets you pick.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order the small portion (90g) and pair it with a vegetable ulam like pinakbet. That keeps the plate under 600 with rice.
  • Dip in spiced vinegar or toyomansi instead of liver sauce.
  • Blot the pieces on a paper napkin. Surface oil on fried belly is 30 to 50 calories per serving.
  • Make it at home in an air fryer after boiling. The skin still puffs and it drops to about 320 calories per 100g.

Common questions

How many calories in lechon kawali with rice?
About 780 calories with 1 cup of rice, or 1,040 with 2 cups. The pork alone is 520 for a 130g serving.
How many calories in one piece of lechon kawali?
About 70 calories per chopped cube (roughly 18g). A 6 to 8 piece serving is 420 to 560.
Is bagnet higher in calories than lechon kawali?
Yes, by about 15 percent. Bagnet is fried twice and loses more water, so it lands near 450 calories per 100g versus 400 for regular lechon kawali.
Can I eat lechon kawali on a diet?
Occasionally, in small portions. A 90g serving is 350 calories and gives you about 20g of protein. It is the size, not the dish, that breaks a calorie budget.
