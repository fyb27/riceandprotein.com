How Many Calories in Lemang?

Lemang is glutinous rice cooked with coconut milk inside bamboo lined with banana leaf, roasted over open fire until fragrant and sticky, eaten almost exclusively at Raya alongside rendang or serunding.

290kcal / 2 slices (typical hawker/Raya serving)
Two slices of lemang (about 180g, a normal Raya serving) comes in at roughly 290 calories, with 45g carbs, 12g fat, and only 4g protein. That 12g of fat is all from the santan soaked up during roasting, which is what makes lemang taste so good and also why it is not exactly a diet food. On its own it is not catastrophic, but nobody eats lemang on its own, kan.

The real talk

Lemang is basically glutinous rice that has been sitting in coconut milk inside a bamboo tube over a fire, so the santan gets fully absorbed into every grain. The result is dense, sticky, slightly fatty rice that is genuinely delicious. At 290 calories for 2 slices, the rice itself is not outrageous, but the carb count is high (glutinous rice hits harder than regular rice gram for gram) and there is almost no protein to speak of. If you are tracking, note that these numbers are a ballpark estimate as there is very little hard nutritional data published for lemang specifically, and fat absorption from coconut milk varies depending on how long it is roasted and the ratio used.

The number that actually matters is what you put next to it. Two slices of plain lemang at 290 cal is fine. Add 2 tablespoons of serunding kelapa and you are at 380. Add a full piece of beef rendang and you are at 490 calories in one plate. The rendang is worth it calorie-wise because it adds real protein, but the serunding is basically just fat and coconut with very little upside. Portion is everything here: one slice of lemang (roughly 90g) cuts you to about 145 calories, which leaves way more room for rendang.

How to eat it lighter

  • Stick to one slice instead of two. You get the flavour hit at half the carbs, and honestly one slice with rendang is already a solid plate.
  • Go for rendang over serunding as your pairing. Rendang adds about 200 calories but also real protein. Serunding adds 90 calories of mostly fat with almost no protein.
  • Balance the plate with a pile of ulam, raw vegetables, or at least some cucumber on the side to slow down the carb spike from the glutinous rice.
  • Skip the nasi and just let the lemang be your carb for that meal. Do not double up on white rice and lemang at the same Raya spread.
  • If you are making it at home, reducing the coconut milk ratio by a third cuts fat absorption meaningfully without killing the texture completely.

Common questions

How many calories in Lemang?
Two slices of lemang (180g, a typical Raya serving) is approximately 290 calories, with 45g carbs, 12g fat, and 4g protein. These are estimates as published data for lemang is limited.
What exactly is lemang?
Lemang is glutinous rice cooked with coconut milk inside bamboo tubes lined with banana leaf, then roasted over an open fire. It is a traditional Malay dish eaten mainly during Hari Raya, almost always paired with beef rendang or dry coconut serunding.
Is lemang healthy for someone trying to lose weight?
Not the easiest choice, bro. It is high in simple carbs from glutinous rice and has decent fat from coconut milk, with almost no protein. It is a once-a-year Raya food, so enjoy it, but keep the portion to one or two slices and make sure your protein for that meal comes from the rendang, not a second helping of lemang.
Berapa kalori dalam Lemang?
Lemang ada kira-kira 290 kalori untuk 2 keping (180g), dengan 4g protein, 45g karbohidrat dan 12g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
