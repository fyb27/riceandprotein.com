How Many Calories in Lempeng?

Lempeng is a traditional Malay griddle pancake made with flour and grated coconut, eaten plain with sugar or paired with sambal, and comes in a few tasty variants including banana and corn.

155kcal / 1 piece (medium, ~10cm diameter)
One medium lempeng (about 95g, roughly 10cm across) comes in at 155 calories. That is for the standard kelapa version cooked on a griddle with a bit of oil. Add sambal on the side and you are looking at around 175 calories. The banana version is actually lighter at about 130 calories since the pieces tend to be smaller and thinner.

The real talk

Lempeng is basically a coconut pancake, bro. Flour, fresh grated coconut, a splash of water, and then fried on a griddle with oil. It is a kampung-style breakfast or afternoon snack that a lot of people outside the Peninsula or rural areas might not have had before. At 155 calories per piece it is actually pretty reasonable for a snack, and the grated coconut is doing most of the calorie work at 60 kcal out of the 155. Worth flagging though: these numbers are a ballpark estimate because there is basically no proper lab data on lempeng out there. Different grandmothers, different coconut ratios, different amounts of oil on the griddle. Treat it as a solid guide, not gospel.

What actually moves the number is the oil on the griddle and what you eat it with. A griddle-cooked pancake absorbs maybe 1 to 2g of oil, which is about 15 kcal, but a heavier-handed cook re-greasing the pan constantly can push that higher. The berlauk version with rendang or curry chicken on top can jump to 220 calories or more because the lauk alone adds 40 to 70 kcal. If you are tracking, eat it plain or with just a sprinkle of sugar, skip the second piece lah, and go for the pisang version if you can find it since it saves you about 25 calories per piece.

How to eat it lighter

  • Go for the pisang version when available. At around 130 calories it is the lightest of the lot, and the banana adds natural sweetness so you do not need to dump sugar on top.
  • Ask the person making it to go easy on the griddle oil. Absorbed oil accounts for around 15 calories and is entirely in the cook's hands.
  • Eat it plain with just white sugar instead of sambal. You save about 20 calories per piece and it is honestly how a lot of people eat it anyway.
  • One piece is usually enough as a snack. It is 95g of food, not 30g, so it does fill you up more than you expect.
  • Pair it with a black coffee or teh-o kosong instead of teh tarik. The pancake itself is fine; it is the sweet drinks beside it that wreck your count.

Common questions

How many calories in Lempeng?
One medium lempeng is about 155 calories. The banana version (lempeng pisang) is lighter at around 130 calories, while the corn version (lempeng jagung) is slightly higher at 180 calories. Add a heavy lauk topping and it can reach 220 calories.
What exactly is lempeng?
Lempeng is a traditional Malay pancake made from flour, fresh grated coconut, and a little water, cooked flat on a griddle with oil. It is a common kampung breakfast or teatime snack, usually eaten with white sugar, kaya, or sambal on the side.
Is lempeng a healthy snack?
It is decent for a snack at 155 calories per piece, not amazing but not terrible either. The main thing to watch is the oil used on the griddle and what you eat it with. Plain with minimal accompaniment it fits fine into a calorie-controlled day. Just do not eat three of them.
Berapa kalori dalam Lempeng?
Lempeng ada kira-kira 155 kalori untuk 1 keping (95g), dengan 3g protein, 22g karbohidrat dan 6g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
