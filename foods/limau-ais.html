How Many Calories in Limau Ais?

Iced lime juice with sugar, no milk, around 100 calories from the sugar.

100kcal / 1 glass
Limau ais is iced lime with sugar, around 100 calories a glass, every one of them from the sugar syrup. There is no milk and no fat, so it is genuinely one of the lighter mamak drinks. Ask for less sugar and it drops fast.

The real talk

Limau ais is just lime, water, ice and sugar. No milk, no cream, nothing fatty. The only calories come from the sugar syrup they squeeze in, around 100 worth. Compared to a teh ais or a Milo, this is a featherweight.

The catch is some stalls go heavy on the sugar to balance the sour, so it can creep higher than you think. Just say kurang manis. The lime keeps it refreshing even with way less sugar, so you barely notice the difference.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order kurang manis, the lime still tastes great with half the sugar
  • Ask for plain lime soda water with a tiny bit of sugar instead
  • Skip the sugar fully for limau ais kosong, basically 10 calories

Common questions

How many calories in limau ais?
About 100 calories a glass, all from the sugar. No milk means no fat calories.
Is limau ais good for dieting?
Yes, it is one of the lighter mamak drinks. Order kurang manis and it is barely 55 calories.
Is limau ais just lime juice?
Lime juice, water, ice and sugar. The sugar is the only real calorie source.
Berapa kalori dalam Limau Ais?
Limau Ais ada kira-kira 100 kalori untuk 1 gelas (300g), dengan 0g protein, 25g karbohidrat dan 0g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
