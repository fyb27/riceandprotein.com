How Many Calories in Linopot?

A Kadazan-Dusun rice parcel from Sabah, pressed into a ball with hill or red rice, sometimes mixed with yam, and wrapped in fragrant wild leaf.

300kcal / 1 parcel (rice ball, plain, no accompaniments)
One plain linopot parcel (230g, rice and yam mix, no sides) comes in at around 300 calories. That's the traditional version with hill or red rice and some yam mixed in. If you're eating the modern white rice only version with no tuber, it's closer to 250 calories. Add a grilled fish set and you're looking at roughly 510 calories for the whole meal. Fair warning though: these numbers are ballpark estimates because linopot doesn't exactly have a mountain of lab data behind it, so treat them as a solid guide rather than gospel.

The real talk

Linopot is a Sabahan thing and if you haven't grown up in KK or the Kadazan-Dusun kampung circuit, you've probably never touched one. Picture this: cooked rice, often the nutty local hill rice or red/purple rice, sometimes mixed with a bit of yam or tapioca, then compressed into a tight ball and wrapped in doringin or tarap leaf. That leaf gives it a faint herbal aroma and it's traditionally eaten with grilled fish or meat. No oil in the rice itself, no coconut milk, nothing. So at 300 calories for a 230g parcel with the yam mixed in, it's honestly pretty clean as rice portions go. To be upfront about it though, the numbers here are a genuine estimate because hard nutritional data for linopot specifically is basically non-existent. So take 300 calories as a well-reasoned ballpark, not a lab result.

What actually moves the number is what you eat with it. The rice parcel alone, 250 to 300 calories depending on whether it's pure white rice or the traditional rice-plus-yam mix. The moment you add ikan bakar, a sambal, some ulam on the side, you're at 510 calories for the full set. That's still a pretty reasonable meal. The protein from a 120g grilled whole fish adds maybe 100 to 120 calories but bumps your protein up significantly, which matters if you're trying to stay full. The sambal is where you want to pay attention, because a heavy coconut-based sambal can add another 50 to 100 calories on top without you noticing. Go lighter on the sambal, load up on ulam, and the set meal stays well under 550 calories for a pretty satisfying feed.

How to eat it lighter

  • Eat the traditional yam-mix version rather than a plain white rice parcel, the yam adds fiber and keeps you full longer for about the same calories.
  • Skip the coconut-heavy sambal or ask for it on the side and use it sparingly, that alone can save 50 to 80 calories.
  • Pair it with grilled fish rather than fatty meat like pork belly or rendang, the ikan bakar set stays around 510 calories total.
  • Load up on ulam and raw vegetables on the side, zero calories and they genuinely fill the gaps.
  • One parcel is the right serving size, the rice is compressed so it's denser than it looks. Two parcels pushes you to 500 to 600 calories on the rice alone before any protein.

Common questions

How many calories in Linopot?
One plain linopot parcel (230g, traditional rice and yam mix) is about 300 calories. The white rice only version without yam is closer to 250 calories. A full linopot set with ikan bakar, sambal, and ulam comes to roughly 510 calories.
What exactly is linopot?
Linopot is a Kadazan-Dusun traditional food from Sabah. It's cooked rice, sometimes hill rice or red rice, pressed into a ball and wrapped tightly in doringin or tarap leaf. The leaf gives it a faint herbal smell and the parcel stays compact for eating on the go or at gatherings. It's usually eaten with grilled fish or meat.
Is linopot a healthy choice for weight loss?
It's one of the cleaner rice options out there because there's no added oil or coconut milk in the rice itself. At 250 to 300 calories per parcel, it's a reasonable carb portion. Pair it with grilled fish and ulam instead of heavy sambal and you've got a pretty decent meal under 550 calories. The yam version also has more fiber than plain white rice, which helps with fullness.
Berapa kalori dalam Linopot?
Linopot ada kira-kira 300 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (230g), dengan 7g protein, 65g karbohidrat dan 1g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
