How Many Calories in Longsilog?

Also known as Longganisa Sinangag Itlog, Longganisa with Garlic Rice and Egg, Longsi.

Two sweet Filipino pork sausages, a mound of garlic fried rice and a fried egg. The silog with the most sugar and the most fat, and the one most people order.

700kcal / 1 plate
A standard longsilog plate with 2 pieces of longganisa is about 700 calories. Two sweet longganisa (90g) are around 290 calories, the 200g of sinangag is 320, and the fried egg is 90. Three sausages with extra rice, a common tapsihan order, is 1,100.

The real talk

Longganisa is pork, pork fat, sugar and garlic in a casing. The sweet Pampanga style (hamonado) is about 320 calories per 100g cooked, and a lot of that is sugar, 8 to 10g per sausage. The fat renders out in the pan and most tapsihan cooks tip it back over the rice. That is why longsilog beats tapsilog by 50 calories even though the sausages weigh less than the tapa.

The style of longganisa changes the plate a lot. Vigan longganisa is garlicky, sour and leaner, about 240 calories per 2 pieces. Lucban is similar at 260. The skinless sweet ones sold at supermarkets are small, about 110 each, so 2 pieces is only 220. The tapsihan default is the plump sweet kind at 145 each, and the plate assumes that.

A longsilog at a Manila tapsihan is 75 to 110 pesos and usually comes with a cup of sinangag that is more like 250g than 200g. If the plate looks generous, count 400 for the rice instead of 320. Add a cup of 3-in-1 coffee at 90 calories and breakfast is close to 900 before 8am.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order 1 longganisa instead of 2 and add a second egg. That drops the plate to about 630 with more protein.
  • Choose Vigan or Lucban longganisa when the tapsihan has it. About 50 calories less per pair, and less sugar.
  • Plain rice instead of sinangag saves 60. Ask them not to drizzle the sausage fat over it.
  • Skip the 3-in-1 coffee. Black kape barako is 5 calories.

Common questions

How many calories in one longganisa?
About 145 calories for a standard sweet longganisa (45g cooked). Skinless supermarket ones are about 110, Vigan about 120.
Is longsilog or tapsilog higher in calories?
Longsilog, at about 700 versus 650 for tapsilog. Longganisa has more fat and sugar than beef tapa, and its rendered fat usually ends up on the rice.
How much sugar is in longganisa?
Sweet hamonado longganisa has about 8 to 10g of sugar per piece. Vigan and Lucban styles have almost none, which is why they taste sour and garlicky instead.
How many calories in longsilog without rice?
About 380 calories for 2 longganisa and a fried egg. The 200g of sinangag is 320 of the standard plate.
