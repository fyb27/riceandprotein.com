How Many Calories in Lontong Sayur?

Also known as Lontong Sayur Betawi, Lontong Sayur Padang, Lontong Gulai.

Lontong sayur is compressed rice cake sliced into a bowl of coconut milk vegetable gravy, usually chayote or young jackfruit, topped with a boiled egg, kerupuk and fried shallots.

450kcal / 1 bowl (lontong, coconut vegetable gravy, telur balado, kerupuk)
A standard bowl of lontong sayur with an egg and kerupuk is about 450 calories. FatSecret Indonesia lists the lontong and gravy alone at 357 calories for a 400g bowl. Add a telur balado at 71 and a kerupuk at 50 and you are at 450.

The real talk

Lontong is lighter than rice, and most people do not know this. Because it is compressed and cooked with a lot of water, lontong is about 105 calories per 100g against 130 for cooked rice. A bowl with 200g of lontong is about 210 calories of rice, where a plate of nasi is 260 to 325.

The gravy is where the coconut milk lives. Sayur labu siam in santan is around 120 calories for a 150ml ladle, and it looks like soup so nobody counts it. The Padang version with gulai nangka and extra chili oil on top is thicker and closer to 180 per ladle.

The default breakfast at a Betawi stall is Rp 10,000 to 15,000 and includes one egg and one kerupuk, which is 450 calories with 15g protein. That is honestly one of the cheaper and lighter breakfasts on the street, as long as you do not add the semur tahu, the tempe orek and the second kerupuk.

The real trap is the lontong cap go meh and the lontong sayur with ayam opor and sambal goreng ati. Those are festival versions at 750 calories or more, and eating them like a daily breakfast is how people gain during Lebaran week.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for the gravy 'jangan terlalu banyak kuah'. Less coconut gravy is 40 to 60 calories saved per bowl.
  • Keep the egg, lose the kerupuk. The egg is your protein; the kerupuk is 50 calories of fried starch.
  • Choose the labu siam gravy over the nangka gulai if there is a choice. It tends to be thinner and less oily.
  • Skip the extra sambal goreng on top. That red oil layer is pure fat, about 45 calories per teaspoon.
  • Do not add lontong. Half a lontong extra is another 100 calories.

Common questions

How many calories in lontong sayur?
About 450 calories for a bowl with lontong, coconut gravy, one egg and a kerupuk. Without the egg and kerupuk it is about 357.
Is lontong lower in calories than rice?
Yes, per 100g lontong is about 105 calories versus 130 for cooked rice, because it holds more water. A bowl with 200g lontong is about 210 calories.
How many calories in lontong sayur Padang?
Around 520 calories because the gulai nangka is thicker and comes with a spoon of chili oil on top.
Is lontong sayur good for weight loss?
It is a reasonable breakfast at 450 calories with 15g protein. Keep the egg, skip the kerupuk and ask for less gravy and it drops under 400.
Berapa kalori Lontong Sayur?
Lontong Sayur mengandung sekitar 450 kalori per 1 mangkuk (420g), dengan 15g protein, 58g karbohidrat dan 18g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
