How Many Calories in Lontong?

Also known as Lontong Sayur Lodeh, Lontong Singapore, Lontong Goreng.

Lontong is compressed rice cakes cut into cubes and served in sayur lodeh, a thick coconut milk vegetable curry, topped with a hard boiled egg, tempeh or tau kwa, sambal and serunding.

798kcal / 1 bowl with sayur lodeh
A standard hawker bowl of lontong is about 798 calories for a 600g serving, with around 42g of fat. The 200g of rice cakes is about 260 calories, but the 250ml of sayur lodeh coconut gravy is around 260 on its own, before the egg, tempeh, sambal and serunding.

The real talk

798 calories is more than a plate of char kway teow, and it is sold as breakfast. The rice cubes are not the problem. 200g of lontong is about 260 calories, similar to a scoop of rice. The sayur lodeh is. It is vegetables cooked in thick coconut milk, and a 250ml ladle is about 260 calories with almost all of it from coconut fat. The egg is 75, the tempeh or tau kwa about 100 because it is fried, the sambal 40, and the serunding on top another 60.

The hawker stalls that sell lontong usually sell mee soto next to it. Mee soto is about 433. Same counter, same S$4, and one bowl is nearly double the other. The difference is entirely the coconut gravy versus the clear turmeric broth. If you want the Malay breakfast and you are counting, mee soto is the easier choice by about 365 calories.

Lontong goreng, the fried version with the rice cubes tossed with sambal and vegetables, is around 550 because there is no coconut gravy, but it does take on frying oil. If you want the rice cakes and the sambal without the lodeh, that is the middle option.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less gravy, more vegetables. Half a ladle of sayur lodeh saves about 130 calories.
  • Skip the serunding and the fried tempeh. Together they are about 160 calories and they are toppings, not the meal.
  • Ask for tau kwa instead of tempeh if the tau kwa is not fried. Plain tau kwa is about 60 calories for the same portion.
  • Order mee soto instead. Same stall, 433 calories, and you still get the spiced broth and the sambal.
  • Eat the egg and the vegetables first, then decide how much of the rice and gravy you want. Most people leave a third once they slow down.

Common questions

Why is lontong so high in calories?
The sayur lodeh. A 250ml ladle of coconut milk vegetable gravy is about 260 calories, and the fat content is around 25g. The rice cakes are only 260 for 200g. Take away the gravy and lontong is a normal meal.
Is lontong healthy?
Not as a regular breakfast. At 798 calories and 42g of fat it is heavier than most hawker lunches. It does have vegetables and 22g of protein, but the coconut fat outweighs that.
How many calories in lontong goreng?
About 550 calories per plate. It is the same rice cubes stir fried with sambal, egg and vegetables. No coconut gravy, so it is around 250 calories lighter than lontong sayur lodeh.
Is lontong the same as ketupat?
Similar. Ketupat is rice cooked in woven palm leaf pouches, lontong is rice compressed in banana leaf. Both are about 130 calories per 100g. The calorie difference comes from what they are served with.
Berapa kalori dalam Lontong?
Lontong ada kira-kira 798 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (600g), dengan 22g protein, 80g karbohidrat dan 42g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
