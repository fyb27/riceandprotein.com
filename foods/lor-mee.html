How Many Calories in Lor Mee?

Thick noodles in dark starchy gravy, around 520 kcal a bowl.

520kcal / 1 bowl
A bowl of lor mee is around 520 calories. It is thick yellow noodles sitting in a dark, cornstarch-thickened gravy with bits of pork, fish cake, and egg. The noodles and the starchy gravy are the bulk of it, and toppings like fried items or extra egg can push it past 600.

The real talk

The real culprit in lor mee is that seductive thick gravy bro. It looks harmless, like a thin soup, but it is thickened with starch and carries oil and a beaten egg through it, so that brown sauce is quietly a chunk of the 520 calories.

It is not the worst hawker bowl out there honestly, because there is no coconut milk and the protein from pork, egg, and fish cake lands around 20g. The trap is the fried toppings, throw in some fried fish or extra deep-fried bits and you jump to 650 fast.

How to eat it lighter

  • Skip the deep-fried toppings, the fried fish and crispy bits are where the extra fat hides
  • Ask for more of the lean toppings like lean pork and egg, less of the fatty stuff
  • Add a side of vegetables or beansprouts to bulk the bowl without adding much to the count

Common questions

Is lor mee a healthy hawker choice?
It is mid, leaning okay. There is no coconut milk and it gives you around 20g protein, which is decent. The downside is the starchy gravy and any deep-fried toppings. Order it with lean toppings and skip the fried fish and it is a reasonable 450 to 500 calorie bowl.
Why is lor mee gravy so high in calories?
It is thickened with cornstarch and usually has a beaten egg and some oil stirred through. That makes the dark gravy more than just flavoured water, it is carrying real calories, roughly 130 in a standard bowl.
How much protein is in a bowl of lor mee?
About 20g from the pork, fish cake, and egg toppings. You can push it higher by asking for extra egg or more lean meat, which also makes the bowl more filling without much added fat.
Berapa kalori dalam Lor Mee?
Lor Mee ada kira-kira 520 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (450g), dengan 20g protein, 72g karbohidrat dan 16g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
