How Many Calories in Macaroni Chicken Soup?

Also known as Macaroni Soup, Chicken Macaroni, 通心粉, Hawker Macaroni, Macaroni in Soup.

Macaroni chicken soup is the Singapore hawker and kopitiam bowl of elbow macaroni in a clear chicken broth with shredded chicken, a few vegetables, fried shallots and a dash of pepper. The comfort food of the sick day and the school canteen.

277kcal / 1 bowl
A standard bowl of macaroni chicken soup is about 277 calories for a 450g bowl, with around 18g of protein and 6g of fat. The 120g of cooked macaroni is about 160 calories, the shredded chicken about 80, and the clear broth around 25.

The real talk

This is the bowl to order when you want a hot meal for under 300. The macaroni is plain boiled pasta, about 160 calories for 120g cooked. The chicken is poached and shredded, 50g at about 80. The broth is clear stock, not thickened and not creamy, about 25 for a full bowl. A few slices of cabbage or carrot and a spoon of fried shallots add 15. That is 277 with 18g of protein, which is more protein per calorie than almost any noodle bowl at the hawker centre.

The reason it stays light is what is not in it. No oil dressing like bak chor mee. No coconut like laksa. No frying like Hokkien mee. The stalls that sell it usually also sell chicken porridge and fish soup, and it sits in the same range, 215 to 350. It is filling because it is mostly hot water with pasta soaking it up.

It gets heavier in two ways. Some stalls add a fried egg or a slice of luncheon meat, which is about 90 or 100 each. And some kopitiam versions add a spoon of margarine or a splash of evaporated milk to the broth, which adds about 50 and makes it taste like the school canteen version. Ask for it plain and it stays at 277.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for extra chicken. It is about 40 calories for another 25g and takes the protein over 25g.
  • Skip the fried egg or luncheon meat add on. Each is about 90 to 100 calories.
  • Ask for no margarine and no milk in the soup if the stall does that. That is about 50 calories.
  • Add the chilli padi and soy dip instead of pepper sauce. The dip is about 10 calories.
  • Get extra vegetables. Most stalls will add cabbage or lettuce for free.

Common questions

Is macaroni chicken soup healthy?
Yes, it is one of the best hawker bowls for the calories. About 277 calories with 18g of protein and 6g of fat. The broth is clear, nothing is fried, and the portion is large enough to fill you.
How many calories in hawker macaroni soup?
About 277 calories for a standard bowl with shredded chicken. Without chicken it is around 200. With a fried egg and luncheon meat it climbs to about 470.
Is macaroni soup good for weight loss?
It is a good regular lunch. 277 calories leaves room for a proper dinner, and the 18g of protein plus a big bowl of hot broth keeps you full for a few hours.
Is macaroni soup or chicken porridge lower in calories?
Chicken porridge at about 215 is slightly lower. Macaroni soup at 277 has more protein and more chew, so it tends to hold you longer for the extra 60 calories.
Berapa kalori dalam Macaroni Chicken Soup?
Macaroni Chicken Soup ada kira-kira 277 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (450g), dengan 18g protein, 38g karbohidrat dan 6g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
