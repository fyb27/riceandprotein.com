Malaysian Food Calories

Malaysian food is where this site started, and it is still the biggest section. Every dish here is priced against a normal hawker, mamak or kopitiam portion, not a lab plate. Nasi lemak, roti canai, char kuey teow, teh tarik, the kuih from the pasar, the tai chow dishes, the Sabah and Sarawak specialities: all of it, ranked lightest to heaviest inside each category.

Nothing on this page is banned. I ate at mamak and kopitiam the whole way from 128kg to 86kg. The only thing that changed was that I knew what each plate cost before I ordered it. Click any dish for the macro breakdown, where the calories actually hide, and how to order it lighter.

Malaysian Food at a glance

There are 291 dishes ranked on this page. The lightest is Butod at 20 kcal per 1 grub (grilled), the heaviest is Nasi Ambeng at 1080 kcal per 1 portion (single-pax share from communal platter), and the average lands around 339 calories a serving. 143 of them come in under 300 calories, and 40 are 600 or more.

That spread is the whole point. Two dishes off the same menu can be 1060 calories apart, which is more than most people's entire daily deficit. Knowing which is which costs you nothing and changes the outcome.

Common questions

What is the highest calorie malaysian food here?
Nasi Ambeng tops the list at 1080 calories per 1 portion (single-pax share from communal platter). 40 of the 291 dishes here are 600 calories or more, so it is worth checking before you order.
How many calories are in a typical malaysian food serving?
Across the 291 dishes on this page the average is about 339 calories per serving, with the middle of the pack around 300 (Ikan Bakar). Figures are estimates for a normal portion.
Berapa kalori dalam malaysian food?
