How Many Calories in Mango Sticky Rice (Pulut Mangga)?

Mango sticky rice is around 380 calories a plate. Tastes like fruit, eats like a carb bomb.

380kcal / 1 plate
A plate of mango sticky rice is about 380 calories. The glutinous rice cooked in coconut milk is the bulk of it, the mango adds natural sugar, and then they pour more salted coconut sauce on top. It is delicious but it is closer to a meal than a light snack.

The real talk

Bro do not let the mango fool you into thinking this is a fruit dessert. The fruit is maybe 60 calories of the whole thing. The real heavyweight is the glutinous rice, which they cook in coconut milk and sugar, then top with even more santan sauce. That is how you get to 380.

This is one of the heavier desserts on the menu, easily matching a small meal. It is worth it once in a while because it is genuinely shiok, just do not pretend it is light because there is fruit on the plate. Share it with someone and you both walk away happy at 190 each.

How to eat it lighter

  • Split one plate between two people, instant halving to 190 calories
  • Eat more of the mango and less of the rice, the fruit is the light part
  • Ask for the coconut sauce on the side and use just a little

Common questions

Is mango sticky rice fattening?
It is calorie dense at around 380 a plate, mostly from coconut glutinous rice. The mango is the small part. Enjoy it occasionally and account for it like a small meal.
Why is mango sticky rice so high in calories?
Glutinous rice cooked in coconut milk plus a santan sauce on top. That combination is rich and sugary. The mango itself only adds about 40 calories.
Can I eat mango sticky rice on a diet?
Yes, but treat it like a meal-sized treat. Split a plate or eat half and you keep it around 190 calories.
Berapa kalori dalam Mango Sticky Rice?
Mango Sticky Rice ada kira-kira 380 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (230g), dengan 6g protein, 68g karbohidrat dan 11g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
