How Many Calories in Marmite Chicken?

Deep-fried chicken pieces coated in a sticky, sweet and savoury glaze made from Marmite, sugar and soy.

580kcal / 1 plate
A zi char plate of marmite chicken lands around 580 calories, with 28g protein, 40g carbs and 32g fat. This one is a double hit. The chicken is deep fried first, then tossed in a glossy glaze built on Marmite, sugar, honey or maltose and soy. So you pay for the frying oil and the sugary glaze at the same time. Tasty as hell, but not light.

The real talk

Marmite chicken is one of those dishes that tastes way too good for what it does to your day. The chicken gets battered and deep fried until crispy, then it is tossed in that dark sticky glaze made from Marmite, sugar, honey or maltose and a bit of soy. That glaze is where the magic and the damage both live, because it is essentially caramelised sugar clinging to fried chicken. At around 580 calories a plate, it is one of the heavier zi char chicken dishes, with the fat coming from the deep fry and the carbs coming straight from the sweet glaze.

The glaze thickness is everything here. A light Marmite coating is one thing, but some shops drench it so thick and glossy that every piece is candied, and that easily adds 100 to 150 calories of pure sugar. Because it is deep fried, you also cannot pull skin or batter off to save much, the calories are baked into the whole piece. If you love it, the move is to treat it as a shared table dish, take two or three pieces for the taste, and not make it your personal protein main. Pair it with steamed veg and one scoop of rice and you keep the meal from running away from you.

How to eat it lighter

  • Share it. Two or three pieces for the flavour hit, then get your actual protein from a steamed or plain stir-fried dish.
  • Go for the version with a thinner glaze. The thicker and glossier the coating, the more sugar you are eating.
  • Do not pour the leftover glaze from the plate over your rice, that pooled sauce is concentrated sugar and oil.
  • Balance the plate with a green veg like sawi or kangkung instead of ordering a second fried dish.

Common questions

How many calories in marmite chicken?
A standard zi char plate is roughly 580 calories, with 28g protein, 40g carbs and 32g fat. A heavily glazed version can push past 650.
Why is marmite chicken so high in calories?
It is a double hit. The chicken is deep fried first, then coated in a glaze made from Marmite, sugar and honey or maltose. So you get the fat from frying and the sugar from the glaze in the same bite.
Is marmite chicken worse than sweet and sour chicken?
Slightly, yes. Both are battered and deep fried, but the marmite glaze tends to be richer and more sugar-heavy than the sweet and sour sauce, so the plate usually lands a bit higher at around 580 calories.
Berapa kalori dalam Marmite Chicken?
Marmite Chicken ada kira-kira 580 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (260g), dengan 28g protein, 40g karbohidrat dan 32g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
