How Many Calories in Masak Merah?

Masak merah is chicken (or fish, prawns, or beef) fried then simmered in a sweet, slightly spicy tomato and chili sauce, a fixture at kenduri spreads and everyday warung alike.

340kcal / 1 piece bone-in chicken with sauce (ayam masak merah, no rice)
One bone-in piece of ayam masak merah with sauce comes in at around 340 calories for roughly 220g. That breaks down to 27g protein, 14g carbs, and 20g fat. The fried chicken itself accounts for most of it at 200 calories, but the sauce piles on another 140 or so from oil, ketchup, and sugar combined. Not a light lauk by any stretch, but not outrageous either if you are only having one piece.

The real talk

Masak merah is one of those dishes that sounds healthier than it is because it looks saucy and tomatoey, not greasy. But that sauce has two calorie traps: cooking oil and sweet tomato ketchup. The fried chicken underneath adds another layer of fat from the pre-frying step before everything gets simmered together. At 340 calories for one bone-in piece, it is decent protein for the calorie cost (27g), but you are also getting 20g fat. Worth noting these numbers are a ballpark estimate since hard nutrition data for this dish is thin, and every mak cik's recipe is a bit different in how much oil and sugar goes in.

What really moves the number is two things: the protein you pick and how generous the sauce is. Swap chicken for udang masak merah and you drop to around 220 calories for a similar serving because prawns carry almost no fat. Ikan merah sits in the middle at about 260 calories. Go the other way with daging lembu and you are looking at 380 calories because beef cubes are fattier. For the chicken version, kenduri or catering style preparation can hit 430 calories from extra oil and sometimes santan thrown in. The sauce itself is only 45 calories, but the oil share per serving adds 55 calories on its own, so ladles of extra gravy over your rice are not free.

How to eat it lighter

  • Go for udang or ikan merah instead of chicken and you save 80 to 120 calories per serving without touching the sauce at all.
  • Ask for less gravy, or scrape some off before eating. The oil in the sauce is where a big chunk of calories hide.
  • If you are cooking at home, cut the oil in the sauce by half and skip the ketchup, using fresh tomatoes instead. You can trim 50 to 80 calories per serving that way.
  • One piece only, lah. The chicken itself is already 200 calories before the sauce. Two pieces with rice and you are well past 800 calories for just that lauk and carb.
  • Pair it with plain steamed rice rather than nasi minyak or nasi tomato. Those festive rices add another 100 to 150 calories on top and they are usually served together at kenduri.

Common questions

How many calories in Masak Merah?
A standard serving of ayam masak merah, one bone-in chicken piece with sauce and no rice, is around 340 calories. The fried chicken contributes about 200 of those, with the oil and sweet tomato sauce making up the rest.
What exactly is masak merah?
Masak merah literally means 'cooked red'. It is a Malaysian dish where protein (most commonly chicken, but also red snapper, prawns, or beef) is first fried, then simmered in a sauce made from blended chili paste, tomatoes, tomato ketchup, and spices. It is a staple at kenduri (feasts) and everyday warung, and it sits somewhere between a curry and a sambal in terms of heat level.
Is masak merah a good choice if I am watching my calories?
It is okay but not the lightest option. At 340 calories per chicken piece you get solid protein (27g), which is decent value. The issue is the 20g fat from the fried chicken and oil-heavy sauce. If you want a lower-calorie version of the same dish, order udang masak merah instead, prawns drop the count to around 220 calories for a similar portion.
