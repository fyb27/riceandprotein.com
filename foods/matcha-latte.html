How Many Calories in Matcha Latte?

Also known as 抹茶ラテ, Matcha Tea Latte, Green Tea Latte, 抹茶 ティー ラテ.

Sweetened matcha powder whisked into steamed milk, sold hot or iced at every Starbucks, Doutor and konbini chiller in Japan. The green colour reads as healthy. The sugar in the powder does not.

240kcal / 1 tall (350ml)
A tall (350ml) hot matcha tea latte with regular milk is 232 calories on Starbucks Japan's published nutrition sheet, with 35.8g of carbs. The iced version is 214. The matcha is about 10 calories; the 300ml of milk is 200 and the sugar pre-mixed into the powder is 30.

The real talk

Matcha on its own is one of the lowest calorie drinks in Japan. A bowl of usucha, whisked with water, is about 10 calories and 1g of tea. The latte is a different product. It is 300ml of full milk at 200 calories with a sweetened green tea powder stirred in, and the powder at most chains is roughly 40% sugar. You are drinking a small glass of milk with 2 teaspoons of sugar and a pinch of tea, and the colour makes it feel like a vegetable.

The sizes climb fast. Short is 170, tall 240, grande 320 and venti about 350. Whipped cream adds 80 to 100 on top. A venti matcha latte with whip is 450 calories, more than a bowl of kake udon, and it is a drink you have next to a meal, not instead of one. The konbini carton versions from Lawson and 7-Eleven are lighter at 120 to 150 for 240ml because they are thinner, but they are also sweeter per sip.

The lever is the milk, not the matcha. The same tall with non-fat milk is about 150 calories, soy milk about 200, oat about 210. Almond drops it to about 140. Sugar cannot be removed because it is inside the powder at most chains, but you can ask for one pump less of the syrup at shops that sweeten separately. Or drink the real thing. Hot matcha with water at a tea counter in Kyoto is 10 calories and tastes more of tea than any latte.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order the short. It is 170 calories against 240 for the tall and it is the size matcha was meant to be.
  • Switch to non-fat milk. Same tall cup, about 150 calories instead of 240.
  • No whipped cream. It is 80 to 100 calories of nothing.
  • Ask whether the shop sweetens with syrup or pre-sweetened powder. If syrup, ask for half.
  • Plain matcha whisked with water is 10 calories. Most cafes will make it if you ask for usucha.

Common questions

How many calories in a Starbucks matcha latte in Japan?
232 for a tall hot with regular milk and 214 iced, per the official sheet. Short is about 170 and venti about 350.
Is matcha latte healthy?
The matcha is. The latte is 300ml of milk with about 8g of added sugar mixed into the powder, so it is a 240 calorie drink. Plain matcha with water is 10.
How much sugar is in a matcha latte?
About 20 to 25g in a tall, roughly half from the milk's own lactose and half from the sweetened powder.
Does matcha latte have caffeine?
Yes, around 60 to 80mg in a tall, less than a coffee latte at about 150mg.
