How Many Calories in Mee Bandung Muar?

Also known as Mee Bandung.

Sweet-spicy dried shrimp gravy noodles from Muar, Johor, loaded with prawns, beef, and a half-cooked egg in a thick tomato-based sauce.

580kcal / 1 bowl
A standard bowl of Mee Bandung Muar comes in at around 580 calories for a 480g serving with yellow noodles, thick dried shrimp gravy, 2-3 prawns, beef slices, and a half-cooked egg. That puts it in the same ballpark as a plate of char kuey teow, which is honestly not bad for how filling this bowl is. The gravy is the key variable here, not the noodles.

The real talk

580 calories for a bowl of Mee Bandung Muar is honestly reasonable, bro. You're getting 28g of protein from the prawns, beef, and egg combined, decent carbs from the yellow noodles, and a gravy that tastes like it should be way worse than it is. The thick dried shrimp and tomato gravy clocks in at around 120 calories for 200ml, which is manageable. What pushes this dish up is the yellow noodles at 250 calories for 180g, they're the biggest single chunk. Fair warning though: these numbers are a ballpark estimate. Hard nutrition data for Mee Bandung Muar specifically is thin on the ground, so treat 580 as a solid working figure, not a lab result.

What actually changes the maths is how generous the stall is with the gravy and whether they finish the bowl with a splash of oil. Some places ladle on 250-300ml of gravy instead of 200ml, that alone can add another 60-80 calories. Ordering the version with double prawns and extra beef brings you to around 680 calories, still fine if that's your main meal of the day. If you swap the yellow noodles for bihun you drop to roughly 490 calories because rice vermicelli is a bit lighter. The egg is basically free at 75 calories, so no reason to skip it, you want that protein.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less gravy or request it on the side so you control how much goes in. Going from 200ml to 150ml saves you roughly 30-40 calories.
  • Order bihun (rice vermicelli) instead of yellow noodles to knock about 90 calories off the bowl without changing the flavour experience at all.
  • Skip the extra oil drizzle that some stalls add at the end. You won't miss it and it can save 50-80 calories depending on how heavy-handed they are.
  • Keep the egg and the prawns, they're your best protein per calorie in this dish. If you're cutting something, cut extra noodles, not the protein toppings.
  • Pair it with plain water or teh kosong instead of teh tarik. That one swap saves you another 100-150 calories and the bowl is already tasty enough on its own.

Common questions

How many calories in Mee Bandung Muar?
A standard bowl of Mee Bandung Muar with yellow noodles, thick dried shrimp gravy, 2-3 prawns, beef slices, and a half-cooked egg has around 580 calories for a 480g serving.
Is Mee Bandung Muar fattening?
It's not a light dish but it's not a disaster either. At 580 calories with 28g of protein, it works fine as a main meal if you're watching your daily total. The gravy portion and any added oil at the end are the main things that can push it higher.
What's the difference between Mee Bandung Muar and Mee Rebus?
Both are gravy noodle dishes but Mee Bandung uses a dried shrimp and tomato-based gravy that's thicker and slightly sweet-spicy, while Mee Rebus uses a sweet potato-thickened gravy with a more starchy texture. Calorie-wise they're in a similar range, around 500-600 per bowl.
Berapa kalori dalam Mee Bandung Muar?
Mee Bandung Muar ada kira-kira 580 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (480g), dengan 28g protein, 65g karbohidrat dan 18g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
