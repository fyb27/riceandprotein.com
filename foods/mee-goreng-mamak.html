How Many Calories in Mee Goreng Mamak?

Yellow noodles fried with sweet sauce, egg, tofu and potato. The sauce and oil do most of the calorie damage.

660kcal / 1 plate
A standard plate of mamak mee goreng is around 660 calories. The noodles, oil and sweet kicap sauce make up most of it, with the egg and tofu adding a bit of protein.

The real talk

Mamak mee goreng tastes sweet for a reason. There is real sugar in that sauce, plus the oil it is all fried in. The yellow noodles themselves are already calorie dense, and then potato and fried tofu pile on more carbs and fat. It adds up to a heavy plate that does not actually keep you full very long because the protein is low relative to the calories.

It is not the worst mamak option but it is not a smart one either. If you are choosing at the mamak and trying to be good, a plate of chicken with rice or even a tosai beats mee goreng on protein per calorie almost every time.

The yellow mee arrives at the stall already oiled. Fresh yellow noodles are parboiled and coated in oil so they do not stick, before the wok even starts. Then comes the frying oil, then the sweet sauce, which is genuinely two to three teaspoons of sugar hiding in your dinner. Sweet plus oily is the whole flavour, and the whole 660.

If you like mamak fried noodles but want a lighter plate, bihun goreng is the quiet winner at around 540. Rice vermicelli is lighter than yellow mee and absorbs less oil. Kuey teow goreng sits in between. Same wok, same taste direction, real difference in calories.

And check the protein column: 20g for 660 calories is a bad deal. That is why you are hungry again two hours after mee goreng. The plate is nearly all carbs and fat. One extra egg or a side of ayam goreng fixes the hunger problem better than a second plate of noodles ever will.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for extra egg for protein instead of extra noodles.
  • Request less oil and less sweet sauce if the stall will do it.
  • If protein matters, order ayam or a side of egg rather than relying on the noodles.
  • Bihun goreng instead of mee goreng saves you around 120 calories with the same satisfaction.

Common questions

How many calories in mamak mee goreng?
A standard plate is about 660 calories. Most of it is the noodles, the oil they are fried in, and the sweet sauce.
Is mee goreng healthy?
Not really. It is high in calories and refined carbs but low in protein for the calorie cost, so it does not keep you full long. Adding egg helps a little.
Why does mee goreng taste sweet?
There is real sugar in the kicap manis based sauce. That sweetness is part of the calorie load, on top of the frying oil.
Mee goreng or bihun goreng, which is lower calorie?
Bihun goreng, at around 540 calories versus 660 for mee goreng. Vermicelli holds less oil than yellow noodles and the plate usually carries less sweet sauce.
How many calories in mee goreng special?
Around 780 calories. The special usually adds egg, chicken or extra everything on top of the standard plate.
Why does mee goreng make me hungry again so fast?
Low protein. Only about 20g in a 660 calorie plate, so your body burns through the carbs, the sugar dip hits, and you are eyeing the menu again. Adding an egg or chicken makes it last much longer.
Berapa kalori dalam Mee Goreng Mamak?
Mee Goreng Mamak ada kira-kira 660 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (350g), dengan 20g protein, 85g karbohidrat dan 26g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
