How Many Calories in Mee Jawa?

Javanese-style noodles in a thick sweet-potato gravy, served with egg, fried tofu and a prawn fritter, adding up to more calories than the bowl looks like it holds.

570kcal / 1 bowl
A standard bowl of Mee Jawa comes in at around 570 calories for a 420g serving with the full works: egg noodles, sweet potato gravy, prawn fritter, fried tauhu, hard-boiled egg and bean sprouts. That puts it in the same ballpark as a plate of nasi lemak, which surprises people because it looks like just noodles in gravy. The sweet potato base adds more calories than you'd expect, and that one cucur udang alone is 105 calories before you even think about the noodles.

The real talk

Mee Jawa is one of those dishes where the calories sneak up on you. The gravy looks watery but it's thickened with sweet potato and sometimes potato too, so the ~200ml of gravy alone clocks in at 125 calories before you've touched the noodles. Add 150g of egg noodles (180 cal), fried tauhu (80 cal), a hard-boiled egg with bean sprouts (80 cal), and then that cucur udang on top (105 cal by itself), and you're at 570 calories for the full bowl. It's not a disaster, but it's not light either. Fair warning though: hard data on Mee Jawa is thin compared to more famous dishes like char kuey teow, so treat this as a solid ballpark estimate, not a lab result.

What actually swings the number is the gravy and the fritters. Hawker stalls vary a lot in how thick and sweet they make the gravy, and some add a proper spoon of sambal that pushes the bowl closer to 650 calories. Drop the prawn fritter and you're immediately at 450 calories, which is a much more manageable lunch. The protein is decent at 22g thanks to the egg, tauhu and prawns, but if you want to make this a proper high-protein meal, ask for an extra egg instead of the extra fritter. You'll get more satiety for fewer calories, and the stall auntie probably won't even charge you much more.

How to eat it lighter

  • Skip the prawn fritter and save 105 calories instantly, bringing the bowl to 465 cal.
  • Ask for less gravy or request it on the side so you control how much sweet potato sauce ends up in your bowl.
  • Add an extra hard-boiled egg instead of a second fritter for more protein without the deep-fried calories.
  • Go easy on or skip any added sambal, which can add 30 to 50 calories depending on the stall.
  • Eat the bean sprouts and tauhu first to take the edge off hunger before hitting the noodles and gravy.

Common questions

How many calories in Mee Jawa?
A standard hawker bowl of Mee Jawa with egg noodles, sweet potato gravy, prawn fritter, fried tauhu, hard-boiled egg and bean sprouts comes to around 570 calories for a 420g serving.
Is Mee Jawa fattening?
It depends on toppings. Without the prawn fritter you're at around 465 calories, which is a reasonable lunch. The full bowl at 570 cal is fine if you're eating within your daily target. The main things to watch are the thick sweet gravy and any extra fritters.
What is the difference between Mee Jawa and Mee Rebus?
Both are noodles in a thick gravy with similar toppings, but Mee Jawa is the Javanese version with a sweeter gravy often thickened with sweet potato, while Mee Rebus uses a more savoury potato-based sauce with stronger curry notes. Calorie counts are roughly similar between the two.
Berapa kalori dalam Mee Jawa?
Mee Jawa ada kira-kira 570 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (420g), dengan 22g protein, 72g karbohidrat dan 20g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
