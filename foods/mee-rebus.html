How Many Calories in Mee Rebus?

Yellow noodles in a thick sweet potato gravy with egg and tofu. The starchy sweet gravy is the calorie multiplier.

500kcal / 1 plate
A plate of mee rebus is around 500 calories. The thick sweet-potato-and-spice gravy is starch thickened, sweetened and oil bound, so unlike a clear soup it adds real calories on top of the noodles.

The real talk

Mee rebus is yellow noodles drowned in a thick, sweet, savoury gravy made from sweet potato, taucu and dried shrimp, topped with a boiled egg, tofu, green chilli and lime. The gravy is the catch. It is not a free clear broth, it is starchy, a bit sweet and carries oil, so it acts as a calorie multiplier on top of the noodles. A plate is around 500 calories.

The egg and tofu give you a little protein but this is mostly a carb and gravy dish. Johor style portions run heavy. It is fine occasionally, just do not assume noodles in soup means light, because this soup is doing work.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less gravy. The thick sweet gravy is the calorie multiplier here.
  • The egg and tofu are your protein, lean on those.
  • Skip the sweet drink alongside, the gravy already brings the sugar.

Common questions

How many calories in mee rebus?
Around 500 calories a plate. The thick sweet-potato gravy is the main reason it is heavier than a clear noodle soup, on top of the yellow noodles.
Is mee rebus healthy?
It is a carb-and-gravy dish with modest protein from the egg and tofu, around 500 calories a plate. Ask for less gravy to keep it lighter.
Why is the gravy so calorie dense?
It is thickened with sweet potato and starch, sweetened, and carries oil and taucu, so unlike a clear broth it adds real calories rather than just flavour.
Berapa kalori dalam Mee Rebus?
Mee Rebus ada kira-kira 500 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (400g), dengan 16g protein, 70g karbohidrat dan 15g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
