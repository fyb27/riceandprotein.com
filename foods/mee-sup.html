How Many Calories in Mee Sup?

A comforting bowl of yellow noodles in clear chicken or beef broth, served with meat, vegetables, and fried shallots. A Sabah staple and one of the lighter noodle options around.

350kcal / 1 bowl
One bowl of Mee Sup is about 350 calories for a standard 450g serving with yellow noodles and chicken. That is pretty decent for a full bowl of noodles, honestly. The broth keeps things light, the noodles give you the carb hit (185 cal just from the noodles alone), and a decent 18g of protein from the chicken pieces means you are not going to be hungry again in an hour.

The real talk

Mee Sup is honestly one of the more diet-friendly noodle dishes you can order at a Sabah stall. 350 calories for a full bowl, 18g protein, and the fat is only 10g because the base is clear broth instead of coconut milk or thick gravy. The noodles are doing the heavy lifting calorie-wise at 185 cal for 150g, which is just how yellow noodles work. But 350 total is still pretty manageable if you are watching your intake. The chicken version is the lightest. Daging bumps it to 380, tulang goes to 400 because the marrow adds fat to the broth whether you suck it out or not.

What actually moves the number is the noodle portion and how the cook prepares the broth. A generous hand with the noodles can easily push you from 150g to 200g, which adds another 60 to 80 calories before you even pick up your spoon. Some stalls also finish the bowl with a small drizzle of oil or extra fried shallots, and that garnish alone is 35 calories for just 10g. The broth itself is only around 30 calories for 250ml when it is made properly, so that is not the problem lah. If you want to keep it light, ask for ayam, go easy on the noodles, and skip the extra shallots.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less noodles and more chicken. Dropping from 150g to 100g of yellow noodles saves you roughly 60 calories right there.
  • Skip the fried shallots or ask for them on the side. Those 10g of shallots add 35 calories of pure fried oil that you barely taste anyway.
  • Go for the ayam version instead of tulang. You save about 50 calories just by avoiding the marrow fat in the bone broth.
  • Drink the broth last and stop before you finish it. Restaurant broth can be saltier and slightly oilier than it looks.
  • Add extra choy sum or bean sprouts if the stall allows it. Vegetables are basically free calories and they help you feel full.

Common questions

How many calories in Mee Sup?
A standard bowl of Mee Sup is about 350 calories for a 450g serving with yellow noodles and chicken. The tulang version with bone broth and marrow pushes it to around 400 calories.
Is Mee Sup good for weight loss?
It is one of the better noodle options, bro. Clear broth instead of coconut milk or thick gravy keeps the fat low at around 10g. You get 18g protein and a full bowl of food for 350 calories. Just watch the noodle portion since that is where most of the calories sit.
What is the difference in calories between Mee Sup Ayam, Daging, and Tulang?
Mee Sup Ayam is the lightest at 350 calories. Daging (beef) comes in around 380 calories because beef adds a bit more fat. Tulang (bone marrow) is the heaviest at around 400 calories as the marrow fat adds roughly 50 extra calories compared to the chicken version.
Berapa kalori dalam Mee Sup?
Mee Sup ada kira-kira 350 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (450g), dengan 18g protein, 48g karbohidrat dan 10g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
