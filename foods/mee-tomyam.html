How Many Calories in Mee Tomyam?

Spicy, sour tom yam broth over yellow noodles with chicken or seafood, fishballs, tofu, and a handful of veg.

460kcal / 1 bowl
One bowl of Mee Tomyam is about 460 calories for a standard 450g hawker serving. That number sits somewhere between "not bad" and "watch yourself" depending on what's in it. The broth with coconut milk and chili oil accounts for around 115 calories on its own, the 130g of yellow noodles adds 185, and your protein (chicken or seafood) brings in another 110. So it's a real meal, not a light snack, but it's also not a disaster if you're counting.

The real talk

460 calories for a bowl of Mee Tomyam is honestly pretty reasonable for a full hawker meal, but let's be honest about what's driving that number. The yellow noodles alone are 185 calories for 130g, which is just the nature of noodles, carbs are carbs lah. The broth is the sneaky one. Coconut milk and chili oil in a 280g pour adds 115 calories of mostly fat, and some stalls are more generous with that than others. Your protein at 75g adds another 110. So the meal itself is balanced, 22g of protein is decent, but it's not a "light" soup just because it's spicy. These numbers are honest ballpark estimates by the way, hard calorie data for hawker mee tomyam is thin and every stall cooks differently.

What actually changes the maths is the broth. Ask for less kuah or a soup-side serve, and you could shave 30 to 50 calories easily. The ayam version comes in at 430 calories because chicken is leaner and the cook tends to go easier on the coconut milk. The seafood version nudges up to 490 because some stalls tend to use a richer broth, and fishballs have more sodium and fat than they look. If you're eating this three times a week, those 30 to 60 calorie differences across variants do add up over a month. The noodle portion is also worth watching, a generous stall might throw in 160g of mee instead of 130g, which pushes you past 500 calories without any visible sign. Order campur if you want the default hawker experience at 460, go ayam if you want to trim a bit.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less kuah or request the broth on the side so you control how much coconut milk and oil you actually consume.
  • Order the ayam version at 430 calories instead of the seafood one at 490, the difference is small but consistent if you eat this regularly.
  • Skip or reduce the fishballs and tofu extras if the stall charges add-on price for them, your 40g of those items adds 35 calories without much protein payoff.
  • Load up on the bean sprouts, mushroom, and tomato if the stall lets you add more veg, it adds volume and barely any calories.
  • Don't finish the broth. Drink half, leave the rest. The bottom of the bowl is where the oil and coconut milk collect.

Common questions

How many calories in Mee Tomyam?
A standard hawker bowl of Mee Tomyam is about 460 calories for a 450g serving. The ayam version is slightly less at 430 calories, and the seafood version comes in around 490 calories.
Is Mee Tomyam good for weight loss?
It can fit into a calorie deficit, 460 calories with 22g of protein is a solid ratio. The issue is the coconut milk broth adds up, so ask for less gravy and go for the ayam version to keep it on the lighter side.
What is the difference in calories between mee tomyam ayam and seafood?
Ayam comes in at around 430 calories, campur (mixed) is 460, and seafood is about 490. The gap is mainly from the type of protein and how rich the broth is, not a huge difference but it adds up if you eat this often.
Berapa kalori dalam Mee Tomyam?
Mee Tomyam ada kira-kira 460 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (450g), dengan 22g protein, 58g karbohidrat dan 14g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
