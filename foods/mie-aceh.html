How Many Calories in Mie Aceh?

Also known as Mi Aceh, Mie Aceh Goreng, Mie Aceh Daging, Mie Aceh Kepiting.

Mie Aceh is thick yellow noodles cooked in a curry-like paste of chili, cardamom, cumin and turmeric with beef, goat, shrimp or crab. It comes goreng (dry), tumis (moist) or kuah (soup), with emping and pickled shallots.

600kcal / 1 plate (mie Aceh goreng with beef, emping)
A restaurant plate of mie Aceh goreng with beef is about 600 calories. FatSecret Indonesia lists a lighter 205g version at 238 calories, but a real Rp 25,000 plate has around 200g of noodles, 60g of beef, 15g of oil and emping, which lands at 600.

The real talk

Mie Aceh noodles are the thick, chewy kind, and a plate holds about 200g cooked, roughly 260 calories. Then the spice paste. It is fried in oil until the red separates, and a plate carries about 15g of oil, 130 calories, more than any other noodle dish on this site except the mamak ones. The 238 calorie figure on FatSecret is for a very small, wet portion and not what a warung gives you.

The protein depends on what you order. Daging (beef) is the standard, about 60g for 130 calories and 15g protein. Kambing (goat) is similar. Udang (shrimp) is leaner at about 80 calories for the same weight. Kepiting (crab) is the leanest meat but the plate costs Rp 45,000 to 60,000 and the crab is often fried first.

The three styles matter. Goreng (dry) is the heaviest at 600 because all the oil stays in the noodles. Tumis (moist) has some broth and is about 540. Kuah (soup) is about 480 because part of the oil floats on the soup and stays in the bowl. Same price, 120 calories difference.

Emping on the side is 50 calories for a small pile, and the acar bawang (pickled shallots) is free. Mie Aceh comes with a lot of chili already, so the extra sambal is not needed.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order kuah (soup) instead of goreng. About 120 calories less, and the flavour is the same paste.
  • Pick udang or kepiting over daging. Shrimp saves about 50 calories against beef for the same portion.
  • Ask for 'jangan terlalu berminyak'. Some warungs will drain the paste more before tossing.
  • Leave the emping. 50 calories of cracker on a plate that already has 70g of carbs.
  • Share a plate with extra beef instead of two plates. Mie Aceh portions are generous.

Common questions

How many calories in mie Aceh?
About 600 calories for a plate of mie Aceh goreng with beef. The soup (kuah) version is around 480.
Is mie Aceh spicy enough to burn calories?
No. Chili raises metabolism by a trivial amount. The plate is 600 calories whether you sweat or not.
Which mie Aceh has the fewest calories?
Mie Aceh kuah with shrimp, around 440 calories. Goreng with beef or crab is the heaviest at 600 to 650.
How much fat is in mie Aceh goreng?
About 26g per plate. The spice paste is fried in a lot of oil and the thick noodles hold on to all of it.
Berapa kalori Mie Aceh?
Mie Aceh mengandung sekitar 600 kalori per 1 piring (330g), dengan 24g protein, 70g karbohidrat dan 26g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
