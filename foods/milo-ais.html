How Many Calories in Milo Ais?

Also known as Milo Peng.

Iced Milo. The Milo powder is only half the story. The condensed milk on top is what makes it a dessert.

250kcal / 1 glass (iced)
A standard glass of kopitiam milo ais is around 250 calories. The Milo powder alone would be moderate, but kopitiam versions add condensed milk and sometimes sugar, which roughly doubles it.

The real talk

People think Milo is a health drink because of the ads with kids playing sports. The Milo powder itself is mostly malt and sugar, and the kopitiam version makes it worse by adding condensed milk on top. That is why a glass of milo ais lands around 250 calories, closer to a dessert than a drink.

It is not evil, but be honest about what it is. A milo ais is a 250 calorie sweet treat, not a free drink to wash down your nasi lemak. Pairing a 250 calorie drink with a 556 calorie nasi lemak turns an 800 calorie breakfast into a thing you did not plan for.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for kurang manis or no condensed milk. The condensed milk is a big chunk of the calories.
  • Have it hot and plain (milo o) rather than iced with condensed milk piled on.
  • Treat it as a treat, not a default drink with every meal.

Common questions

How many calories in milo ais?
About 250 calories a glass at a kopitiam. The Milo powder is part of it, but the condensed milk and sugar added on top are what push it up.
Is Milo a healthy drink?
The powder is mostly malt and sugar, and the kopitiam version adds condensed milk, so milo ais is closer to a dessert than a health drink. One glass is around 250 calories.
How do I make milo lower calorie?
Ask for no condensed milk and less or no sugar, or have it as milo o. The condensed milk is the easiest calorie to cut.
Berapa kalori dalam Milo Ais?
Milo Ais ada kira-kira 250 kalori untuk 1 gelas (300g), dengan 6g protein, 38g karbohidrat dan 8g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
