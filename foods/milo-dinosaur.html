How Many Calories in Milo Dinosaur?

Iced Milo with condensed milk and a heap of dry Milo dumped on top, about 340 calories.

340kcal / 1 glass
A Milo Dinosaur runs around 340 calories a glass, sometimes more depending on how heavy the hand was with the condensed milk. The base iced Milo is already sweet, then they pile undissolved Milo powder on top, which is just more sugar and malt. That heap alone can be 50 to 80 calories of pure powder.

The real talk

Milo Dinosaur is iced Milo that got greedy. Normal Milo ais is already milky and sweet, then someone decided to bury it under a small mountain of dry Milo powder. That powder is not free, bro. It is sugar and malt, and the heap on top can add 60 odd calories before it even melts into your drink.

People think because it is Milo it is healthy, like some sports drink. Nah. With condensed milk it can hit 40 to 50 grams of sugar, more than a can of Coke. It is a tasty treat, no shame, but treat it like dessert, not a daily energy boost.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less condensed milk, that is where most of the calories hide
  • Skip the dry Milo topping, saves you 50 to 80 calories instantly
  • Use evaporated milk instead of condensed, lighter but still creamy

Common questions

How many calories in a Milo Dinosaur?
Around 340 calories a glass. The dry Milo heap and the condensed milk are the heavy hitters.
Is Milo Dinosaur healthy?
Not really. It is tasty but it can carry more sugar than a can of Coke. Drink it as a treat, not a daily thing.
What is the difference between Milo ais and Milo Dinosaur?
Milo ais is just iced Milo. The Dinosaur adds a big heap of undissolved Milo powder on top, which is extra sugar and calories.
Berapa kalori dalam Milo Dinosaur?
Milo Dinosaur ada kira-kira 340 kalori untuk 1 gelas (350g), dengan 7g protein, 52g karbohidrat dan 11g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
