How Many Calories in Miso Soup?

Also known as 味噌汁, みそ汁, Misoshiru, Tofu Wakame Miso Soup.

Dashi stock with miso paste stirred in, plus tofu cubes and wakame seaweed, or whatever vegetables are in season. It comes with almost every teishoku in Japan and costs 100 to 150 yen on its own.

40kcal / 1 bowl (tofu and wakame)
A standard bowl of miso soup with tofu and wakame is about 40 calories. Matsunoya publishes its plain miso soup at 28 calories. The miso paste is most of that, the tofu adds about 15, and the dashi and seaweed are close to zero. Tonjiru, the pork and root vegetable version, is 140 to 180.

The real talk

Miso soup is the cheapest way to feel like you ate more. A bowl is 180ml of hot salty liquid for 40 calories, and it slows the meal down. The miso paste is 12 to 15g per bowl at about 2 calories per gram. Tofu is 25g at 14 calories. Wakame, dried, is 1g and rounds to nothing. Even if you double the tofu, you are at 55.

The trade-off is salt. A bowl carries 1.2 to 1.6g of salt, and Matsunoya lists theirs at 1.6g. Japan's health ministry suggests under 7.5g a day for men, so 2 bowls at breakfast and dinner is nearly half the budget. Nobody gains weight from miso soup, but if you are watching blood pressure, one bowl a day is the sensible ceiling. Reduced-salt miso pastes (genen miso) cut that by about a third.

Watch for the upgraded versions. Tonjiru with pork belly, daikon, carrot and konnyaku is 140 to 180 calories a bowl and 7g of fat, still reasonable as a side. Miso soup with a whole fried tofu pouch or nameko mushrooms in a thick broth is 80 to 100. The instant cup versions from the konbini are 30 to 45 and fine, just salty.

How to eat it lighter

  • There is nothing to cut. At 40 calories, drink the bowl and enjoy it.
  • Use it as the starter. A bowl of hot soup before a teishoku means you eat the rice slower and often leave some.
  • If salt is the concern, pick genen (reduced salt) miso at home, about 1g of salt per bowl instead of 1.5.
  • Add more tofu and wakame, not more miso. Extra tofu is 15 calories and 2g of protein per 25g.
  • Tonjiru at 150 calories with a small rice bowl is a proper 400 calorie lunch on its own.

Common questions

How many calories in a bowl of miso soup?
About 40 calories for a standard bowl with tofu and wakame. A plain bowl is around 28, tonjiru with pork is 140 to 180.
Is miso soup good for weight loss?
Yes. At 40 calories a bowl it adds volume and warmth to a meal for almost nothing, which makes it easier to eat less of the rice. The only caution is salt at about 1.5g per bowl.
How much salt is in miso soup?
About 1.2 to 1.6g per bowl. Two bowls a day is roughly 40 percent of the 7.5g daily limit Japan recommends for men.
Does miso soup have protein?
A little. About 3g per bowl from the miso and tofu. It is not a protein source, it is a low calorie side.
