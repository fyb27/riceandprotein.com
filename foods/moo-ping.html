How Many Calories in Moo Ping?

Also known as หมูปิ้ง, Moo Bing, Thai Grilled Pork Skewers, Mu Ping.

Thin slices of pork shoulder marinated in coconut milk, palm sugar, garlic, coriander root and soy, threaded on bamboo and grilled over charcoal. Sold at every morning market and BTS station exit, 10 baht a stick.

130kcal / 1 skewer
One skewer of moo ping is about 130 calories for roughly 35g of pork, per foodmany.com. The classic breakfast of two skewers plus a small bag of sticky rice is about 430 calories. The pork is fattier than it looks because the marinade uses coconut milk and the cut is shoulder with fat left on.

The real talk

Moo ping is small, so it feels harmless, and at 130 calories per stick one or two is fine. The trouble is the maths of a 10 baht stick: five sticks is 50 baht and 650 calories, which is a full meal eaten standing up at a bus stop. Most of the calories are fat, because moo ping is made from pork shoulder or neck with the fat kept on for tenderness, then brushed with coconut milk while it grills.

The marinade also has sugar. Palm sugar and sweet soy are why the edges caramelise, and there is about 4 to 5g of sugar per stick. Not a lot on its own, but it is why moo ping tastes sweet and why you can eat six without thinking. The charcoal grill drips some fat off, which is the one thing working in your favour.

The sticky rice is the other half of the breakfast. A small 100g bag is about 170 calories, a regular 150g bag is 260. Two moo ping and a small bag is 430 calories with 16g of protein, which is a perfectly reasonable breakfast. Four moo ping and a big bag is 780, which is not.

How to eat it lighter

  • Set the number before you order: 2 sticks is 260 calories, 4 is 520.
  • Ask for the small bag of khao niao, or share one bag between two people.
  • Pick the leaner looking sticks. Shops thread fatty and lean pieces together; the ones with visible white fat are 20 to 30 calories more.
  • Pair it with som tam instead of a second bag of sticky rice.

Common questions

How many calories in moo ping with sticky rice?
Two skewers (260) with a 100g bag of sticky rice (170) is about 430 calories. With a regular 150g bag it is around 520.
Is moo ping healthy?
It is a reasonable protein snack at 130 calories and 8g of protein a stick. The fat is 9g a stick, so it stops being reasonable somewhere around stick four.
Moo ping vs satay, which is lighter?
Satay. A chicken satay stick is about 50 calories because it is smaller and made from lean meat. Moo ping is bigger, fattier and glazed with coconut milk, so 130 per stick.
