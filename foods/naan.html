How Many Calories in Naan?

Naan is a leavened flatbread from the tandoor oven, a staple at Indian Muslim restaurants and mamak stalls, eaten with curry, dal, or just dipped in whatever gravy is on the table.

270kcal / 1 piece (plain)
One plain naan (about 90g) is roughly 270 calories, with 45g carbs, 8g protein, and 6g fat. That's before any butter hits it. Order butter naan and you're looking at around 370 calories. Cheese naan pushes up to 430. The numbers here are ballpark estimates since mamak portions vary a fair bit, but they give you a solid working figure.

The real talk

Naan is essentially a refined flour flatbread, leavened with a bit of yogurt and cooked in a super-hot tandoor clay oven. It's softer and fluffier than roti canai, and at 270 calories for a plain piece it's not outrageous on its own, but it's almost pure carbs with very little to slow down digestion. The dough has a small amount of oil or ghee baked in, which accounts for a chunk of the calories. Most of the calorie damage actually happens after it leaves the oven, when the mamak uncle slathers butter on top. That's where butter naan at 370 cal and cheese naan at 430 cal come from. These are estimates though, honest, because every mamak has a slightly different recipe and a very different definition of how much butter is "enough".

The thing that really changes the maths is what you pair it with. One naan with a bowl of dhal (around 150 cal) is a reasonable meal. One naan with a heavy mutton curry or korma, plus another piece because the first one went too fast? You can easily hit 700 to 900 calories without thinking about it. Swap butter naan for plain, you save roughly 100 calories. Order one piece instead of two and you save another 270. If you're eating naan as part of a restaurant meal, that's probably your entire carb budget for the sitting, so maybe skip rice on the side, bro.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order plain instead of butter or garlic naan. That single swap saves you 70 to 160 calories per piece.
  • Stick to one piece and load up on the protein side instead, like tandoori chicken or a dal that's not swimming in oil.
  • Use naan to scoop dal or a light lentil curry rather than dunking it repeatedly in a heavy coconut-based gravy.
  • Ask if you can get it without the butter brush on top. Some mamak places will do this if you ask nicely.
  • Split one naan between two people as a side and let the main dish do the work.

Common questions

How many calories in Naan?
One plain naan (90g) is about 270 calories. Garlic naan goes up to around 340 cal, butter naan to 370 cal, and cheese naan to 430 cal. These are estimates based on typical mamak portions.
What actually is naan and how is it different from roti canai?
Naan is a leavened flatbread made with wheat flour, yogurt, and a little oil, then cooked in a tandoor clay oven at very high heat. Roti canai is unleavened, made with a lot more oil or ghee, and cooked on a flat griddle. Naan is softer and less flaky. Both are carb-heavy but naan generally has less fat than roti canai if you get the plain version.
Is naan a good choice when you're trying to lose weight?
Plain naan is not terrible at 270 calories, but it's mostly refined carbs with not much fibre or protein to keep you full. The real problem is portion control at the mamak, where it's easy to eat two pieces plus a heavy curry without realising the total is pushing 900 calories. If you're watching your intake, one plain piece with a high-protein side is the move.
Berapa kalori dalam Naan?
Naan ada kira-kira 270 kalori untuk 1 keping (90g), dengan 8g protein, 45g karbohidrat dan 6g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
