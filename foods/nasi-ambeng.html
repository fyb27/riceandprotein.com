How Many Calories in Nasi Ambeng?

A Javanese communal rice platter piled with ayam masak kicap, sambal goreng, serunding kelapa, urap, bihun goreng, and bergedel. Big, filling, and worth knowing the numbers before you dig in.

1080kcal / 1 portion (single-pax share from communal platter)
Nasi Ambeng comes in at around 1080 calories per portion, based on a single-pax share from a communal platter (about 700g total). That covers your rice, ayam masak kicap, sambal goreng, serunding kelapa, urap, bihun goreng, and bergedel all in one go. It is a full meal, not a snack, and the numbers reflect that.

The real talk

1080 calories is a lot for one plate, but here is the thing: Nasi Ambeng is not just rice and one lauk. You are getting ayam masak kicap, sambal goreng tempeh and tofu, a small mountain of serunding kelapa, urap, bihun goreng, and a bergedel all at once. That is a proper Javanese spread, bro. The protein is decent at 50g, which is actually the silver lining here. The real calorie culprits are the serunding (coconut floss is calorically brutal for its size), the fried bihun, and the bergedel. Worth flagging too: this estimate is a ballpark. Hard nutritional data for Nasi Ambeng specifically is thin on the ground, so treat the 1080 figure as a reasonable estimate, not a lab result. Different stalls will portion things differently, and the amount of santan or coconut in the sides can swing the total by 100 to 200 calories easily.

What actually changes the maths is how generously the sides are scooped. The serunding is only 25g in a standard portion but hits 115 calories because dried spiced coconut is mostly fat. If the uncle gives you a bigger scoop, you could be at 150 calories just from that condiment. The bergedel and bihun goreng together are sitting at a big chunk of that 325-calorie combined side estimate. If you want to bring this meal down, those two are the easiest targets. Swap to rendang and the whole plate jumps to about 1200 calories, so the ayam masak kicap version at 1080 is already the lighter choice. At the end of the day this is a communal dish that is meant to be shared, and if you are splitting it properly four ways from the original platter, the numbers look a lot friendlier than eating a single-pax portion alone.

How to eat it lighter

  • Go easy on the serunding. It is only 25g but packs 115 calories. Take half the usual amount and you save around 55 to 60 calories without noticing much.
  • Skip or halve the bergedel (potato fritter). It is fried and mostly starch, so it does not do much for you calorie-wise compared to the protein sides.
  • Ask for less bihun goreng and more urap (the vegetable salad). The urap is lighter because it is mostly vegetables with grated coconut, whereas bihun goreng has added oil.
  • If you have a choice of protein, pick the ayam masak kicap over rendang. The rendang version adds about 120 calories per portion.
  • Eat it without a sugary drink. Nasi Ambeng is already a full meal. Adding a Milo Ais or sirap limau puts you well over 1200 calories for one sitting.

Common questions

How many calories in Nasi Ambeng?
A standard single-pax portion of Nasi Ambeng is around 1080 calories, covering roughly 700g of food including rice, chicken, sambal goreng, serunding, urap, bihun goreng, and bergedel.
Is Nasi Ambeng suitable for someone on a calorie deficit?
It can fit, but you have to be honest with yourself. At 1080 calories it is a big meal. If your daily target is around 1600 to 1800 calories, this single plate takes up more than half. Have it as your main meal, skip the sugary drink, and keep dinner light.
What makes Nasi Ambeng so high in calories?
The serunding kelapa alone is 115 calories from just 25g because dried coconut floss is dense with fat. The bergedel (fried potato fritter) adds to it, and the bihun goreng has oil in there too. It is the combination of fried sides and coconut-based condiments that stacks the total up.
