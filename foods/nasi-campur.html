How Many Calories in Nasi Campur (Economy Rice)?

Also known as Cai Png, Cai Fan, Economy Rice.

Rice plus 3 dishes lands around 720 kcal. Fried picks are the trap.

720kcal / 1 plate (rice + 3 dishes)
A standard nasi campur plate with rice and three dishes is around 720 calories. The rice alone is about 280, then your three lauk pile on the rest depending on what you point at. Pick fried chicken plus a fried egg plus a fried veg and you are quietly at 850. Go steamed fish, sayur, and tofu and you can drop it under 550.

The real talk

Economy rice is a trap dressed as a budget meal bro. The rice is cheap, sure, but you point at three lauk and half of them are deep fried, so your RM9 plate is quietly 800 calories before you even add the curry gravy on top.

The killer is the auntie ladle of kuah. That free splash of curry or masak lemak over your rice is pure coconut oil and easily adds 100 to 150 calories you never counted. You think it is free flavour but your waistline is paying for it.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick two veg dishes and one protein instead of three fried meats, easily saves 200 calories
  • Go steamed or grilled fish over fried chicken, that one swap alone is about 150 calories down
  • Skip the curry gravy ladle or ask for just a little, that drizzle is sneaky coconut oil

Common questions

Is nasi campur healthy?
It can be if you choose well. Go for a steamed protein and two vegetable dishes and you land around 520 calories with decent protein. The problem is most people stack fried lauk and drown it in curry gravy, which pushes it past 800.
Why does my economy rice cost so much?
Because you pointed at the expensive fried stuff and the auntie eyeballed your portions. Funny enough the pricier plates are usually the more fattening ones since fried meat costs more and carries more oil. Cheaper veg picks are also lighter.
How much protein is in nasi campur?
Roughly 30g for a standard plate with one meat dish and an egg. If you want more, ask for a double portion of chicken or fish instead of a second veg, gets you to 40g plus without much extra fat.
Berapa kalori dalam Nasi Campur?
Nasi Campur ada kira-kira 720 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (450g), dengan 30g protein, 85g karbohidrat dan 28g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
