How Many Calories in Nasi Goreng Cina?

Chinese-style fried rice cooked in a wok with egg, vegetables, char siew, and a generous pour of oil that most people don't think about.

630kcal / 1 plate
One plate of Nasi Goreng Cina comes in at around 630 calories for a standard 380g hawker portion with char siew. That puts it solidly in the "you need to know this exists" zone if you're tracking. The bulk of those calories come from the rice base (325 cal) and the oil absorbed during wok frying (120 cal), not the egg or veg. It's a fairly calorie-dense meal for its size, but it's also genuinely filling, so most people stop at one plate.

The real talk

630 calories for a plate of nasi goreng cina is honestly not terrible, but it's also not as innocent as it looks. The rice is the base at 325 calories, and that's fine. The real silent killer is the wok oil. You can't see it, you won't taste it separately, but 120 calories of absorbed cooking oil is sitting in that plate doing absolutely nothing for your fullness. The egg adds 90 calories and at least gives you protein. The char siew pieces and soy seasoning? Another 55 calories. Add it all up and you're at 630 for a pretty average hawker portion. Fair warning: these numbers are a ballpark estimate because there's basically no hard lab data for Malaysian hawker fried rice. Every uncle fries it differently, some woks drink more oil than others, and portion sizes vary a lot stall to stall.

What actually swings the maths is the oil and the extras. A basic egg-only version drops to around 530 calories because you lose the char siew and the extra wok work. The moment you ask for seafood, you're looking at 750 calories, partly from the proteins but also because more ingredients means more time in the wok means more oil absorbed. Portion size matters too. A generous uncle giving you 300g of rice instead of 250g adds another 65 calories before anything else changes. If you're watching your numbers, asking for less rice (not a smaller plate, specifically less rice) and eating the egg-based version instead of seafood will save you 100 to 200 calories without feeling like you're on a diet.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order the egg-only version instead of char siew or seafood, saves you roughly 100 calories and you'll barely notice the difference
  • Ask the uncle for less rice specifically, half a scoop less is about 60 to 80 fewer calories
  • Skip the fried egg on top if the stall offers it as an add-on, that's another 90 calories you don't need
  • Pair it with plain soup or cucur-free sides instead of ordering a second dish with more oil
  • If you cook at home, use a non-stick pan and measure your oil, most home cooks pour in 2 to 3 times more than a hawker would

Common questions

How many calories in Nasi Goreng Cina?
A standard hawker plate of Nasi Goreng Cina (around 380g with char siew) is approximately 630 calories. The egg-only version is closer to 530 calories, while a seafood version can hit 750 calories.
Is Nasi Goreng Cina good for weight loss?
It can fit into a calorie deficit, but you need to be honest about the oil. At 630 calories a plate it's not low-calorie, but it's filling and has decent protein from the egg and char siew. Order the egg-only version and ask for less rice if you want to keep it under 550 calories.
What is the difference between Nasi Goreng Cina and Nasi Goreng Kampung?
Nasi Goreng Cina is Chinese-style fried rice, typically cooked with soy sauce, sesame oil, egg, vegetables, and char siew. Nasi Goreng Kampung is the Malay village-style version using belacan, anchovies, and chilli, giving it a spicier, more pungent flavour. Calorie-wise they're in a similar range, both sitting around 600 to 700 calories a plate.
Berapa kalori dalam Nasi Goreng Cina?
Nasi Goreng Cina ada kira-kira 630 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (380g), dengan 18g protein, 78g karbohidrat dan 24g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
