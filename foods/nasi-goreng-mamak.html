How Many Calories in Nasi Goreng Mamak?

Mamak fried rice, sweeter and oilier than the kampung version. Big plate of carbs with not much protein.

660kcal / 1 plate
A plate of mamak nasi goreng is around 660 calories. Like the mee goreng next to it on the menu, the sweet sauce and frying oil are where most of the calories hide, while the protein stays low.

The real talk

Mamak nasi goreng tastes sweet and rich for a reason, there is sugar in the sauce and a good amount of oil in the wok. It is basically a big plate of rice carrying sauce and oil. The egg and the bits of chicken or seafood help, but the protein is low compared to the calorie load, so you are hungry again in a couple of hours.

If you are at the mamak deciding what to order, plain chicken with rice or even a couple of telur beats nasi goreng mamak on protein per calorie. Does not taste as good, I know. But that is the trade.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for extra egg or extra chicken for protein instead of a bigger portion of rice.
  • Request kurang minyak and less of the sweet sauce if the cook will do it.
  • Skip the keropok and fried sides that come on the plate.

Common questions

How many calories in nasi goreng mamak?
About 660 calories for a standard plate. Most of it is the rice, the oil it is fried in, and the sweet sauce.
Is mamak nasi goreng healthy?
Not really. It is high in calories and refined carbs but low in protein for the cost, so it does not keep you full long. Adding egg and chicken helps.
What is the difference from nasi goreng kampung?
Mamak style is usually sweeter and oilier with a darker sauce, while kampung style leans on belacan, anchovies and chilli. Calorie wise they land close, around 630 to 660 a plate.
Berapa kalori dalam Nasi Goreng Mamak?
Nasi Goreng Mamak ada kira-kira 660 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (350g), dengan 17g protein, 86g karbohidrat dan 25g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
