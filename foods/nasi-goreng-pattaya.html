How Many Calories in Nasi Goreng Pattaya?

Fried rice wrapped in an omelette, about 700 kcal a plate.

700kcal / 1 plate
A plate of nasi goreng pattaya is around 700 calories. It is basically mamak fried rice wrapped in a thin omelette blanket with a squiggle of chilli sauce on top. The fried rice underneath is already 500 plus, then the egg omelette and the oil it is fried in add another 150 to 200 on top.

The real talk

Nasi goreng pattaya is just nasi goreng wearing a jacket bro. The fried rice inside is the same 500 calorie business as usual, and then they wrap it in a fried egg omelette which adds fat and another splash of oil from the pan.

The chilli sauce squiggle on top looks innocent but it is mostly sugar. Combine the oily fried rice, the omelette, and the sweet sauce and you are at 700, which is more than the plain nasi goreng you thought you were upgrading from.

How to eat it lighter

  • Eat half the rice inside, the omelette and a smaller scoop of fried rice still feels like a full meal
  • Skip or scrape off the sweet chilli sauce drizzle, it is basically sugar syrup
  • Ask them to go light on oil for both the rice and the omelette, easily 100 calories saved

Common questions

Is nasi goreng pattaya more fattening than normal nasi goreng?
A bit, yes. The omelette blanket and the extra oil to fry it add roughly 150 to 200 calories over a plain plate. You do get a little more protein from the egg, around 24g, but the calorie cost outweighs it if you are counting.
How many calories is the omelette part alone?
The egg wrap is about 160 calories, mostly from the two eggs and the oil used to fry the thin omelette. It is the main thing separating pattaya from regular fried rice on the calorie sheet.
Can I make nasi goreng pattaya lighter at home?
Yes. Use a non-stick pan with minimal oil for the omelette, load the fried rice with more vegetables and less rice, and skip the sweet sauce. You can bring it down near 450 to 500 with the same look and feel.
Berapa kalori dalam Nasi Goreng Pattaya?
Nasi Goreng Pattaya ada kira-kira 700 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (350g), dengan 24g protein, 80g karbohidrat dan 30g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
