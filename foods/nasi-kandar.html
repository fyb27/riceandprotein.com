How Many Calories in Nasi Kandar?

Also known as Nasi Kandaq.

Rice flooded with mixed curries and a fried lauk. The kuah banjir is the calorie bomb people never count.

950kcal / 1 plate (banjir + fried chicken)
A standard nasi kandar plate (rice with mixed curry flood and a piece of fried chicken) is around 950 calories. The oil-rich kuah banjir and the deep-fried chicken are where most of the calories hide, not the rice.

The real talk

Nasi kandar is the king of underestimated calories bro. People think it is just rice and curry. But that kuah banjir, the flood of mixed gravies poured over the rice, is loaded with oil. The gravy alone can be hundreds of calories. Add a piece of ayam goreng berempah, which is deep fried, and you are at around 950 calories for a plate that looks innocent.

And that is the standard plate. Start adding sotong, salted egg, extra lauk and a sweet drink, like most people do, and you blow past 1,300 calories easy. It is delicious and the protein is genuinely high, around 42g. Just respect what it is and do not eat it daily.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less kuah (kurang kuah). The curry flood is the biggest hidden calorie source.
  • Pick a grilled or curried lauk over deep fried ayam goreng.
  • Skip the second and third lauk. One protein plus vegetables is plenty.

Common questions

How many calories in a nasi kandar plate?
A standard plate with curry flood and fried chicken is around 950 calories. A loaded plate with extra lauk can pass 1,300. The kuah banjir and fried lauk are the main calorie sources.
Why is nasi kandar so high in calories?
The kuah banjir, the flood of mixed curries poured over the rice, is rich in oil, and the popular lauk like ayam goreng are deep fried. The rice is actually the smaller part of the total.
How do I make nasi kandar lighter?
Ask for less kuah, choose a grilled or curried lauk instead of fried, and stick to one protein plus vegetables. That can cut a plate from 950 down toward 650.
Berapa kalori dalam Nasi Kandar?
Nasi Kandar ada kira-kira 950 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (450g), dengan 42g protein, 95g karbohidrat dan 42g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
