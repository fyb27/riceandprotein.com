How Many Calories in Nasi Kerabu?

Blue herb rice piled with ulam, sambal and grilled protein. One of the lighter, fresher rice dishes.

480kcal / 1 plate with ayam percik
A plate of nasi kerabu with ayam percik is around 480 calories. The pile of raw herbs and ulam is almost free, so the calories sit mostly in the blue rice and the protein.

The real talk

Nasi kerabu is genuinely one of the smarter rice dishes for someone watching their weight, and it is gorgeous too with that blue bunga telang rice. Half the plate is raw ulam and herbs, which is basically free calories but loads of fibre and flavour. The rice is a normal scoop and the grilled ayam percik gives you real protein. At around 480 calories with 30g of protein, the numbers are good.

What changes the maths is the protein and the kerisik. Grilled ikan bakar keeps it light. Switch to fried chicken or pile on the toasted coconut kerisik and sweet sambal and it climbs toward 650. Order it grilled and you are winning.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick grilled protein, ikan bakar or ayam percik, over anything fried.
  • The ulam is free volume, pile it on without guilt.
  • Go easy on the kerisik (toasted coconut) and sweet sambal if you want the lightest plate.

Common questions

How many calories in nasi kerabu?
Around 480 calories a plate with ayam percik. It is one of the lighter rice dishes because much of the plate is raw herbs and ulam, which are almost calorie free.
Is nasi kerabu healthy?
Yes, it is one of the better rice options. The big pile of ulam gives you fibre for almost no calories, and grilled protein keeps it lean. Fried protein and heavy kerisik are what push it up.
What makes nasi kerabu lighter than other rice dishes?
Most of the plate is raw herbs and vegetables rather than oil-rich gravy or fried lauk, so the calories sit mainly in the rice and the protein.
Berapa kalori dalam Nasi Kerabu?
Nasi Kerabu ada kira-kira 480 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (400g), dengan 30g protein, 55g karbohidrat dan 16g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
