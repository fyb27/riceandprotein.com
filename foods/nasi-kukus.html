How Many Calories in Nasi Kukus?

Steamed rice served with ayam goreng berempah and sambal, a classic at mamak stalls and East Coast hawker spots where the crispy spiced fried chicken is the whole point.

700kcal / 1 plate (steamed rice + 1 piece ayam goreng berempah + sambal + sides)
A standard plate of Nasi Kukus comes in at around 700 calories: 220g of steamed rice (285 kcal), one piece of ayam goreng berempah at 150g (300 kcal), sambal at about 55 kcal, and the usual sides of ikan bilis, cucumber and tauge adding another 50 kcal. The fried chicken alone is doing nearly half the work here, so that is where the number lives.

The real talk

Nasi Kukus is basically steamed rice served with a piece of heavily spiced, deep-fried chicken (ayam goreng berempah), sambal, and a small pile of sides. It sounds simple but that one piece of fried chicken is pulling 300 calories on its own because it is coated in rempah paste and deep-fried. The rice and sambal are pretty tame by comparison. At 700 calories for a full plate with decent protein at 36g, it is not a disaster of a meal, honestly. If you were 128kg and eating two of these plates, yes, that is a problem. But one plate fits into a sensible day without much drama. Fair warning though: calorie data for nasi kukus is thin and the numbers here are a ballpark estimate based on component weights. Different stalls use different amounts of oil and rempah, so treat 700 kcal as a reasonable middle ground, not a lab-certified figure.

The single biggest lever is the gravy. If you go kuah banjir style, where the uncle floods your rice with curry gravy, you are looking at around 1,100 calories instead of 700. That extra ladle of curry adds close to 400 kcal in one move. Ordering a second piece of ayam goreng berempah bumps you straight to 980 calories. If you want to eat lighter, skip the ikan bilis (saves you roughly 70 calories and a bit of fat from the frying), ask for less sambal, and stick to one piece of chicken. The steamed rice portion is actually quite reasonable at 220g for 285 kcal, so the rice is not your enemy here. It is the oil in the chicken and any add-on gravy that pushes the number up fast.

How to eat it lighter

  • Stick to one piece of ayam goreng berempah and skip any extra chicken. Going from one to two pieces adds 280 calories.
  • Say no to kuah banjir. That curry gravy pour-over adds close to 400 calories in one move and you barely notice it taste-wise.
  • Skip the ikan bilis goreng if you are watching calories. It is fried anchovies, which adds fat you do not really need when the chicken already has plenty.
  • Load up on the cucumber and tauge sides instead. They add almost no calories and actually help you feel fuller.
  • If the stall lets you choose your chicken cut, go for whatever you prefer. Drumstick, thigh, the fat difference between cuts is small either way.

Common questions

How many calories in Nasi Kukus?
A standard plate of Nasi Kukus is around 700 calories: 285 kcal from the steamed rice, 300 kcal from one piece of ayam goreng berempah, 55 kcal from the sambal, and about 50 kcal from the ikan bilis and vegetable sides.
What exactly is Nasi Kukus?
Nasi Kukus means steamed rice, but the dish is really about the ayam goreng berempah, which is a deep-fried chicken piece coated in a blend of spices like turmeric, lemongrass, and galangal. It is popular at mamak stalls and East Coast hawker places in Malaysia, and you will also find it commonly in Sabah. The rice is plain steamed, but the chicken is anything but.
Is Nasi Kukus healthy for someone trying to lose weight?
One plate at 700 calories with 36g of protein is actually a decent meal for weight loss, as long as you stop at one plate. The fried chicken is high in fat from the oil, so it is not the leanest protein source, but the portion is not huge. The danger is adding curry gravy (jumps to 1,100 kcal) or ordering two pieces of chicken (980 kcal). Keep it to one piece, no gravy flood, and it fits into a calorie deficit without feeling like punishment.
Berapa kalori dalam Nasi Kukus?
Nasi Kukus ada kira-kira 700 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (450g), dengan 36g protein, 79g karbohidrat dan 27g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
