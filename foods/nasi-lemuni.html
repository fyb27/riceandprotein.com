How Many Calories in Nasi Lemuni?

A fragrant east coast herbal rice cooked with daun lemuni and light coconut milk, served with sambal, ulam, ikan bilis, and egg.

580kcal / 1 plate (rice + sambal + ulam + ikan bilis + egg)
A standard plate of Nasi Lemuni comes in at around 580 calories for a full set with rice, sambal, ulam, ikan bilis, and a hard boiled egg. The herbal rice itself accounts for 350 of those calories, so the base is already reasonably filling for the portion size. Fair warning though: these numbers are a ballpark estimate because Nasi Lemuni is niche enough that solid nutritional data is pretty thin out there.

The real talk

Nasi Lemuni is one of those Kelantanese and Terengganu things most Malaysians outside the east coast have never tried, which is a shame because it is genuinely good. A full set comes in at around 580 calories, which is honestly not bad for a plate of rice with protein. The herbal rice cooked with daun lemuni and light santan is the main calorie driver at 350 kcal, with the sambal adding another 90. The hard boiled egg and ikan bilis bring the rest. One honest caveat though: these figures are a rough estimate, bro. Nasi Lemuni is niche enough that there is basically no formal nutritional data on it, so treat these as a ballpark rather than a lab result.

What actually changes the maths here is the sambal and what protein you add. A basic set with ulam, egg, and ikan bilis sits at 580 calories, which is manageable. But the moment you upgrade to ayam goreng berempah, that jumps to roughly 750 calories. Sambal portion matters too as 2 tablespoons is already 90 calories, and some stalls pour it on heavy. The ulam is basically free at 15 calories for 60g, so eat as much of that as you want. If you want to keep this under 500, skip the ikan bilis, go easy on sambal, and stick to the egg as your only protein. The herbal rice itself is the part worth eating lah, so do not cut that.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less sambal or take it on the side. Two tablespoons of sambal already adds 90 calories, and some stalls are more generous than that.
  • Skip the ikan bilis goreng. At 55 calories for 15g of deep fried anchovies, they add up fast and you barely notice them calorie-for-calorie.
  • Load up on ulam instead of asking for more rice. The raw herb vegetables are basically 15 calories for 60g, so they are almost free volume.
  • If you are adding protein, go grilled or rebus rather than ayam goreng berempah. A fried spiced chicken leg adds around 170 calories on top of your 580 base.
  • Request the rice without extra santan if the stall cooks per order. Nasi lemuni with no coconut milk drops to around 270 calories for the rice portion alone.

Common questions

How many calories in Nasi Lemuni?
A standard plate of Nasi Lemuni with sambal, ulam, ikan bilis, and a hard boiled egg is around 580 calories. The rice itself (about 210g with a light coconut milk cook) makes up roughly 350 of those calories.
Is Nasi Lemuni healthier than Nasi Lemak?
They are in a similar calorie range for a basic set. Nasi Lemuni uses lighter coconut milk in the rice and comes with ulam (raw herbs) instead of the fattier Nasi Lemak condiments, so it can edge out slightly cleaner. But pile on the sambal and fried chicken and the gap closes fast.
What is daun lemuni and does it add calories?
Daun lemuni is a herb from Kelantan and Terengganu traditionally used for its fragrance and medicinal properties. The leaf itself contributes almost no calories. The calories in the rice come from the rice grain and the small amount of santan used during cooking.
Berapa kalori dalam Nasi Lemuni?
Nasi Lemuni ada kira-kira 580 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (360g), dengan 17g protein, 62g karbohidrat dan 22g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
