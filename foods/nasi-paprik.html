How Many Calories in Nasi Paprik?

Spicy paprik chicken and veg over rice, around 680 kcal a plate.

680kcal / 1 plate
A plate of nasi paprik is around 680 calories. You get rice topped with paprik, which is chicken stir-fried with vegetables in a spicy thick sauce, so the protein is solid at about 32g. The catch is the sauce carries oil and a bit of sugar, and the wok-fry adds more, so it climbs higher than you would guess for a stir-fry.

The real talk

Nasi paprik is one of the better mamak rice plates honestly bro, because it is loaded with actual chicken and a pile of vegetables, so you walk away with 32g protein. That is more than most nasi goreng will ever give you.

The sauce is where it sneaks up on you though. That glossy spicy paprik gravy is wok oil plus a spoon of sugar to balance the chilli, and they pour it generously over the rice. The protein and veg are great, the sauce is the part quietly adding 150 calories.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less rice, the paprik chicken and veg already fill you up so half the rice still satisfies
  • Request less sauce or get it on the side, that thick gravy is the oil-and-sugar part
  • It is already protein and veg heavy, so just controlling the rice and sauce makes it a genuinely lean plate

Common questions

Is nasi paprik a good choice for dieting?
Actually yes, it is one of the smarter mamak rice picks. It packs around 32g protein from the chicken and a real serving of vegetables. Just go easy on the rice and ask for less of the oily sauce and you have got a lean 520 calorie plate.
Why is nasi paprik more calories than plain stir-fried chicken?
The rice and the sauce. The paprik itself is reasonable, but a full scoop of rice is 280 calories and the glossy sauce is wok oil plus sugar, around 140 more. Together they push a healthy-looking stir-fry up to 680.
How much protein is in nasi paprik?
About 32g for a standard plate, which is genuinely good for a single rice meal. If you want more, ask for extra chicken, most stalls will load you up for a small top-up.
Berapa kalori dalam Nasi Paprik?
Nasi Paprik ada kira-kira 680 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (400g), dengan 32g protein, 80g karbohidrat dan 26g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
