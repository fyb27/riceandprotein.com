How Many Calories in Nasi Uduk?

Also known as Nasi Uduk Betawi, Nasi Uduk Komplit, Coconut Rice Jakarta.

Nasi uduk is Jakarta's coconut rice, sold from morning stalls with a lineup of side dishes: telur balado, tempe orek, bihun goreng, fried chicken and kerupuk.

600kcal / 1 plate (nasi uduk, telur balado, tempe orek, bihun goreng, kerupuk)
A typical breakfast plate of nasi uduk is about 600 calories: 200g of coconut rice at 325 calories, a telur balado at about 70, tempe orek at 90, a spoon of bihun goreng at 60 and one kerupuk at 50. Add fried chicken and you pass 900.

The real talk

FatSecret Indonesia lists plain nasi uduk at 260 calories for a 160g bowl, which works out to about 163 per 100g. Plain white rice is 130 per 100g. So the coconut milk costs you roughly 25% more per scoop, and a stall scoop is closer to 200g than 160g. That is why the rice alone on this plate is 325 calories before any side dish.

The side dishes are where the plate swings. The default Rp 12,000 to 15,000 breakfast set with egg, tempe orek, bihun and kerupuk is around 600 calories. Swap the egg for a piece of ayam goreng and you add about 390 calories. Add semur jengkol and another 150. The nasi uduk with ayam goreng, telur and everything is easily 1,000 calories and it is 7am.

Tempe orek looks innocent but it is fried tempe tossed in kecap manis and sugar. A small spoon of 40g is about 90 calories, and a lot of that is sugar. Bihun goreng is 60 calories per spoon, again mostly oil. Neither one is the enemy on its own, they just stack.

For 600 calories you get only 18g of protein, which is the actual weak point. The rice and bihun are 80g of carbs. If you want this to hold you until lunch, the fix is more egg or more tempe, not more rice.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for half rice (nasi setengah). Coconut rice is dense, and cutting 100g saves 160 calories.
  • Take two telur balado instead of the fried chicken. Two eggs are about 140 calories with 14g protein; a piece of ayam goreng is 390.
  • Skip the bihun goreng. It is rice noodles fried in oil sitting next to rice, so you are paying twice for the same carb.
  • One kerupuk, not three. Each one is about 50 calories and they are all air and oil.
  • Ask for the sambal kacang on the side. The peanut sauce is about 40 calories per spoon and the stall tends to be generous.

Common questions

How many calories in nasi uduk komplit?
Around 600 calories for the usual set with telur balado, tempe orek, bihun goreng and one kerupuk. With fried chicken it is closer to 1,000.
Is nasi uduk more fattening than white rice?
Per 100g, nasi uduk is about 163 calories versus 130 for plain rice, because it is cooked in coconut milk. It is about 25% more, so half rice is the easy fix.
How many calories in nasi uduk with ayam goreng?
About 990 calories once you add a bone-in piece of fried chicken (around 390 calories) to the standard 600 calorie plate.
Is nasi uduk good for breakfast on a diet?
It can work at half rice with two eggs, around 370 calories and 16g protein. The full plate with chicken is a whole day's worth of fat before 8am.
Berapa kalori Nasi Uduk?
Nasi Uduk mengandung sekitar 600 kalori per 1 piring (340g), dengan 18g protein, 80g karbohidrat dan 24g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
