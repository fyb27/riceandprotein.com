How Many Calories in Neslo Ais (Nescafe + Milo)?

Nescafe and Milo mixed together with condensed milk, around 250 calories.

250kcal / 1 glass
Neslo ais is Nescafe plus Milo in one glass, usually with condensed milk, landing around 250 calories. You are basically stacking a coffee drink and a chocolate malt drink together, so the calories add up faster than either one alone. The condensed milk is the heaviest part.

The real talk

Neslo is what happens when you cannot decide between coffee and Milo, so you order both in one glass. Nes for Nescafe, lo for Milo. Sounds genius until you realise you just combined two sweet drinks into one, plus condensed milk on top.

That stacking is why it hits around 250 calories, heavier than a plain Milo ais or a kopi. It is a tasty kaw kaw drink, no doubt, but treat it like the indulgence it is. Ask for less condensed milk and you can knock 50 odd calories off without losing the flavour.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less condensed milk, the biggest calorie cut
  • Order it kurang manis to control the sugar
  • Use evaporated milk instead of condensed for a lighter version

Common questions

How many calories in neslo ais?
Around 250 calories a glass. Combining Nescafe and Milo with condensed milk stacks it up.
What is neslo?
Nescafe plus Milo mixed in one glass. Nes from Nescafe, lo from Milo.
Is neslo worse than Milo ais?
A bit heavier, since you add coffee and often more condensed milk. Around 250 versus 200 for plain Milo ais.
Berapa kalori dalam Neslo Ais?
Neslo Ais ada kira-kira 250 kalori untuk 1 gelas (350g), dengan 6g protein, 38g karbohidrat dan 8g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
