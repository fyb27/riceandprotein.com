How Many Calories in Nestum Chicken?

Crispy deep-fried chicken tossed in buttery Nestum cereal, sweet and dangerously easy to overeat.

700kcal / 1 plate (2-3 pieces)
One plate of Nestum Chicken (2-3 bone-in pieces, roughly 220g) comes in at around 700 calories. That is not a typo. The cereal coating sounds innocent but it is deep-fried chicken tossed in butter and Nestum, so the calories add up fast. The chicken itself accounts for about 235 calories, and then you have 135 calories from the Nestum cereal plus another 150 calories from the butter used for tossing. Order this at a kopitiam and you are looking at a solid 700-calorie lauk before you even touch your rice.

The real talk

Nestum Chicken is one of those dishes that tricks you. It looks like a moderate-sized chicken dish, not a bowl of laksa or a plate of nasi lemak with everything on top. But at 700 calories for 2-3 pieces, it is punching way above its weight. The culprit is not the chicken itself. It is the combination of deep frying plus a proper butter toss with the Nestum cereal. About 150 of those calories come from the butter alone, and another 135 from the Nestum cereal coating. That sweet, fragrant crunch you love? That is where a huge chunk of the damage comes from. To be fair, these are ballpark estimates since hard nutritional data for this dish is basically nonexistent, so treat the 700 figure as a solid educated guess rather than a lab-verified number.

What actually changes the maths here is prep method and portion. A baked or air-fried version drops this to around 430 calories because you skip most of the frying oil and cut the butter way down. If you are ordering at a zi char place that goes heavy on the buttery Nestum sauce, you could easily hit 850 calories for a larger portion. Pair this lauk with a full plate of white rice and you are probably sitting at 1,000+ calories for one meal. If you want to make it work for weight loss, share the plate between two people or limit yourself to one piece and load up on plain veg on the side. The protein is decent at 35g, so the chicken part of this is genuinely useful. It is just the cereal-butter party around it that you need to manage lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order one piece, not two or three. One bone-in piece is roughly 230-250 calories. That alone makes a big difference.
  • Ask for less butter when ordering at a restaurant. Some places will accommodate, especially at zi char stalls.
  • Eat it with plain steamed rice instead of fried rice. You are already at 700 calories on the lauk alone.
  • Skip the extra gravy or sauce at the bottom of the plate. That pooled butter-Nestum mixture is where a lot of extra fat hides.
  • Make it at home with an air fryer and half the butter. A homemade version can come in near 430 calories and still tastes solid.

Common questions

How many calories in Nestum Chicken?
A standard plate of Nestum Chicken with 2-3 bone-in pieces (about 220g total) comes to roughly 700 calories. The main calorie drivers are the butter used for tossing (150 calories) and the Nestum cereal coating (135 calories), not just the chicken itself.
Is Nestum Chicken healthy or is it just junk food?
It is somewhere in between. The chicken gives you a decent 35g of protein per serving, which is actually pretty good. But the deep frying plus butter-Nestum toss means the fat is at 38g for one plate. It is fine to eat occasionally, but it is not a low-calorie dish you want to have every day if you are watching your weight.
Can I make Nestum Chicken lower calorie at home?
Yes, and it makes a big difference. Using an air fryer instead of deep frying and cutting the butter down to 10-15g can bring the whole plate to around 430 calories. You lose a bit of the richness but the Nestum crunch is still there. Worth trying if you want to eat this more regularly without the calorie hit.
Berapa kalori dalam Nestum Chicken?
Nestum Chicken ada kira-kira 700 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (220g), dengan 35g protein, 45g karbohidrat dan 38g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
