How Many Calories in Okonomiyaki?

Also known as お好み焼き, おこのみやき, Japanese Savoury Pancake, Buta-tama, Osaka Okonomiyaki.

A griddle pancake of shredded cabbage, flour batter and egg with pork belly slices on top, finished with sweet brown sauce, mayonnaise, aonori and bonito flakes. Osaka mixes everything, Hiroshima layers it with noodles.

550kcal / 1 pancake (buta-tama, Osaka style)
One Osaka-style buta-tama okonomiyaki is about 550 calories. The pancake itself, cabbage, batter, egg and pork, is about 450. The sauce, mayo and toppings add another 100. A Hiroshima-style one with a layer of yakisoba noodles is closer to 750.

The real talk

The name means 'grilled how you like it', which is exactly the problem. The base is fine. A 120g pile of cabbage is 28 calories and an egg is 80. The 50g of flour batter is 180. What pushes it past 500 is the 40g of pork belly at 160 calories and then the table work. Okonomi sauce is 35 calories per tablespoon and mayo is 70 per tablespoon, and most people do not stop at one of each.

Hiroshima style changes the maths. The same pancake gets a full serving of yakisoba noodles griddled in, another 200 calories, and often a fried egg on top for 90 more. A 'Hiroshima special' with noodles, pork and extra egg is 750 to 800 calories for around 1,200 yen, roughly the same as a large katsudon. It also weighs close to 500g, so at least you feel it.

The upside nobody mentions is the cabbage. A single okonomiyaki holds 100 to 150g of cabbage, which is more vegetable than most Japanese meals manage in a day. Made at home with a lighter hand, half the flour, thin pork loin instead of belly, sauce brushed on rather than poured, the same size pancake comes in at 350 to 400 calories and still tastes like the real thing.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for sauce only, no mayo. The mayo zigzag alone is 70 to 140 calories.
  • Choose seafood (ika or ebi) over pork belly. Squid is 40 calories for the same weight that pork belly is 160.
  • Skip the noodle layer. Osaka style over Hiroshima style saves about 200 calories.
  • At home, halve the flour and double the cabbage. The pancake still holds together with 1 egg.
  • Share one pancake and add a plate of grilled vegetables from the same teppan. A half is 275 calories.

Common questions

How many calories in an okonomiyaki?
About 550 for a standard Osaka-style pork okonomiyaki with sauce and mayo. Hiroshima style with noodles is 750 to 800.
Is okonomiyaki healthy?
Healthier than it looks. It contains 100 to 150g of cabbage and 20g of protein. The calories come from pork belly, flour and mayo, all of which can be cut. A lighter home version is 350 to 400.
How many calories in okonomiyaki sauce and mayo?
Okonomi sauce is about 35 calories per tablespoon, Japanese mayo about 70 per tablespoon. A typical restaurant finish uses 2 tablespoons of sauce and 1 of mayo, around 140 calories.
Osaka or Hiroshima okonomiyaki, which has fewer calories?
Osaka, by about 200 calories. Hiroshima style adds a full serving of yakisoba noodles and usually a fried egg.
