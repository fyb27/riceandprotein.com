How Many Calories in Omurice?

Also known as オムライス, Omu-rice, Omelette Rice, Japanese Omelette Rice.

Ketchup-fried rice with chicken and onion wrapped in a thin omelette, or topped with a soft runny one that is split open at the table. Finished with ketchup or demi-glace sauce. A yoshoku classic at family restaurants, kissaten and Pomunoki.

750kcal / 1 plate
A standard plate of omurice is about 750 calories. The chicken rice inside is about 500 calories on its own because it is 220g of rice fried in butter with ketchup. The 2 egg omelette and the butter it is cooked in add 210, and the ketchup on top another 20. Demi-glace or cheese versions run 850 to 950.

The real talk

Omurice is fried rice wearing a disguise. The rice inside is cooked in butter with ketchup, and ketchup is a quarter sugar by weight. A 220g portion of chicken rice has 10g of butter and 25g of ketchup in it, which is 100 calories of fat and sugar hidden inside what looks like plain orange rice. The omelette on top is 2 eggs at 150 plus another knob of butter to get it glossy. Then more ketchup on top.

The fancier the sauce, the higher the number. Ketchup is about 20 calories for the squiggle on top. Demi-glace sauce, the dark one at yoshoku restaurants, is 100 to 150 for a ladle because it is stock reduced with butter and flour. A cheese omurice with demi-glace and a cream sauce is 900 to 950 calories. Pomunoki's large size is about 500g of rice and clears 1,200.

There is not much protein for the size. A 750 calorie plate has about 22g of protein, most of it from the 2 eggs, because the chicken in chicken rice is 30g of diced thigh at most. Compare that with a 468 calorie plate of 5 karaage carrying 28g. Omurice is comfort food with a big carb load, and it is best treated as the whole meal rather than a main with sides.

How to eat it lighter

  • Choose the ketchup version over demi-glace or cream sauce. Saves 100 to 200 calories.
  • Skip the cheese. Melted cheese inside or on top is another 100.
  • At home, cook the rice in 1 teaspoon of oil instead of a tablespoon of butter, and use 150g of rice. The plate drops to about 500.
  • Order the small size where offered. Pomunoki's S size is about 550 versus 750 for the regular.
  • Add a side salad and eat it first. The plate has no vegetables apart from the onion.

Common questions

How many calories in omurice?
About 750 for a standard plate with ketchup. Demi-glace versions are 850 to 900, and cheese with cream sauce around 950.
Is omurice healthy?
It is a large carb plate with 2 eggs. About 90g of carbs and 32g of fat with 22g of protein. Fine as a full meal, heavy as a side. A home version with less butter and rice is 500.
Why is omurice so high in calories?
The rice is fried in butter with ketchup, which adds fat and sugar, and the omelette is cooked in more butter. About 160 of the 750 calories come from butter and ketchup alone.
How many calories in Pomunoki omurice?
The regular size lands around 750 to 850 depending on the sauce. The L size uses roughly double the rice and clears 1,200.
