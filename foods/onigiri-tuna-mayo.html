How Many Calories in Tuna Mayo Onigiri?

Also known as ツナマヨおにぎり, ツナマヨネーズおむすび, シーチキンマヨ, Tuna Mayonnaise Rice Ball, Konbini Onigiri.

A triangle of white rice around a spoon of canned tuna mixed with mayonnaise, wrapped in a sheet of crisp nori. The top-selling onigiri filling at every convenience chain, 200 to 270 yen.

197kcal / 1 onigiri
A tuna mayo onigiri from 7-Eleven is 197 calories, with 37g of carbs, 4g of protein and 4g of fat. That is the published figure for their soy-sauce rice tuna mayo. A salmon onigiri from the same shelf is 174, and a plain salt one about 170.

The real talk

One tuna mayo onigiri is 197 calories, which is about the same as a slice and a half of bread. The rice is 90g and makes up 140 of the calories. The tuna mayo filling is only 15g, about 45 calories, and the nori and seasoning are the last 10. It is a small, dense carb snack with almost no protein, 4g, because the tuna is mostly mayo by the time it goes in.

The 'mayo' part gets blamed, but the difference between tuna mayo and grilled salmon onigiri at 7-Eleven is 23 calories. The filling is too small to matter much. What matters is how many you eat. A konbini lunch of 2 onigiri, a karaage-kun and a sweet milk tea is about 750 calories with 20g of protein, which is a poor deal. Two onigiri and a salad chicken breast instead is 520 with 30g of protein for the same 700 yen.

Onigiri are a decent snack in a country where the alternatives are 400 calorie melon pan and 350 calorie sandwiches. The trouble is the rice is refined, the protein is low, and hunger comes back in 90 minutes. Pair one with a boiled egg (70) or a stick of cheese (60) and the 260 calorie combination holds you much longer than a second onigiri at 394.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick salmon, kombu or ume over tuna mayo. Saves 20 to 40 calories per onigiri.
  • One onigiri plus a salad chicken breast (110 calories, 24g protein) beats 2 onigiri for the same fullness.
  • Skip the extra-large 'omusubi' sizes. They are 130 to 150g of rice and 270 to 300 calories.
  • Look for the mochi-mugi or brown rice versions. Similar calories, more fibre, slower hunger.
  • Drink unsweetened tea with it. The sweet milk tea in the same basket is 150 to 200 calories.

Common questions

How many calories in a tuna mayo onigiri?
About 197 at 7-Eleven, around 190 to 230 across chains depending on rice weight and how much mayo is in the filling.
Is onigiri healthy for weight loss?
It is a controlled portion of rice at under 200 calories, which is useful. It has almost no protein though, 4g, so pair it with an egg, salad chicken or edamame rather than a second onigiri.
Which konbini onigiri has the fewest calories?
Ume, kombu and plain salt onigiri at 160 to 170. Anything with mayo, tempura or fried filling is 200 to 250.
How much rice is in one onigiri?
About 90 to 100g of cooked rice in a standard konbini onigiri, which is roughly 140 to 150 calories on its own.
