How Many Calories in Opor Ayam?

Also known as Opor Ayam Putih, Opor Ayam Kuning, Opor Ayam Lebaran, Chicken in Coconut Gravy.

Opor ayam is chicken simmered in a mild coconut milk gravy with galangal, lemongrass, candlenut and coriander. It is the Lebaran dish, served with ketupat and sambal goreng.

390kcal / 1 bowl (1 large piece bone-in chicken with gravy, no rice or ketupat)
A serving of opor ayam is about 390 calories: one large bone-in piece of chicken (around 150g) with 90ml of coconut gravy. FatSecret Indonesia lists it at 163 calories per 100g and 392 for a 240g bowl, with 34g protein.

The real talk

Opor is milder than gulai but it is still coconut milk, and that is where the fat comes from. Of the 24g fat in a bowl, roughly half is the chicken skin and half is the santan. A 150g piece of thigh with skin is about 250 calories; the 90ml of gravy it sits in is about 130. If you eat breast without skin and only a spoon of gravy, the same bowl is 260.

The protein is the good part. 34g per serving is high, because the piece is big and it is simmered rather than fried, so nothing is lost to a crust. On a normal day at a warung this is Rp 15,000 to 20,000 with rice, and the meal is about 650 calories, which is a fair trade for that much protein.

Lebaran is when it gets out of hand. Two ketupat (290), opor with the fattiest piece (450), sambal goreng ati (200) and kerupuk (50) is a 1,000 calorie plate, and then the relatives serve it again at the next house. Three houses is 3,000 calories, and that is where the Lebaran kilo comes from. Not the opor, the repetition.

Opor putih (white, no turmeric) and opor kuning (yellow) are the same in calories. The Solo style with thicker santan is about 40 calories heavier per bowl than the thinner Jakarta version.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick the breast piece (dada). About 60 calories less than the thigh, same gravy.
  • Take the chicken out of the gravy and leave most of the santan in the pot. Half the gravy is 65 calories saved.
  • One ketupat, not two. Each one is 145.
  • Skip the sambal goreng ati at Lebaran. Liver in coconut chili is about 200 calories per spoon and it is the fattiest thing on the table.
  • At home, use light santan (santan encer) or half the coconut milk and top up with stock.

Common questions

How many calories in opor ayam?
About 390 calories for one large piece of chicken with coconut gravy. FatSecret Indonesia lists 163 calories per 100g.
How many calories in ketupat opor?
About 680 calories for two ketupat and a serving of opor. With sambal goreng ati and kerupuk the Lebaran plate is around 1,000.
Is opor ayam high in fat?
Moderate, about 24g per serving. Half is from the chicken skin and half from the coconut milk. Breast without skin cuts it to about 12g.
How much protein in opor ayam?
About 34g per serving, which is one of the highest of any Indonesian lauk because the piece is large and simmered, not fried.
Berapa kalori Opor Ayam?
Opor Ayam mengandung sekitar 390 kalori per 1 mangkuk (240g), dengan 34g protein, 10g karbohidrat dan 24g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
