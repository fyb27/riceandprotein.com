How Many Calories in Otak-Otak?

Grilled spiced fish paste wrapped in banana leaf, one of those snacks that actually earns its place on a diet plate.

80kcal / 1 piece (grilled, banana leaf)
One piece of otak-otak is 80 calories. That's for a standard 50g grilled piece wrapped in banana leaf, the kind you grab off the grill at the night market. It's actually pretty reasonable for a fish-based snack with a bit of coconut milk in the mix. Eat three of these and you're still under 250 calories, which is nothing. The protein is decent too, 8g per piece from real mackerel, not filler.

The real talk

Honestly, otak-otak is one of the better snacks you can eat if you're watching calories. Eighty calories per piece, 8g protein, and the main ingredient is mackerel, which is real food. The coconut milk adds 18 calories and the aromatics (shallots, chili, lemongrass, belacan) contribute about 13 more, but none of that is crazy. The banana leaf grilling gives it that smoky char without you adding any oil. Compare this to pisang goreng or karipap and otak-otak wins every time, bro.

What actually changes the math is the cooking method and how many pieces you eat, because nobody stops at one. The steamed Nyonya version drops to 65 calories per piece. The recipe typically uses slightly less coconut milk than the grilled version, so the overall fat content is a bit lower. The fried version (otak goreng) jumps to 110 calories from the oil absorbed during shallow frying. That's a 37% increase from doing basically nothing different. If you're buying from a hawker stall and they're selling by the stick, three to four pieces grilled is 240 to 320 calories, decent protein hit of around 24 to 32g, and it won't ruin your day. Five or six pieces starts adding up fast so count before you eat.

How to eat it lighter

  • Choose grilled over fried. Otak goreng adds roughly 30 extra calories per piece from absorbed oil, that's meaningless fun tax.
  • Go Nyonya steamed style if you can find it. Saves about 15 calories per piece and still tastes great.
  • Eat it without extra sambal or sauce on the side. The otak itself already has belacan and chili built in, it doesn't need the dip.
  • Count your pieces before you start. Three is a snack. Six is a side dish. Know which one you're eating.
  • Pair it with plain rice instead of nasi lemak or coconut rice. The otak is already rich enough, you don't need more fat underneath it.

Common questions

How many calories in Otak-Otak?
One standard grilled piece of otak-otak in banana leaf is 80 calories. That's for a 50g piece, which is typical hawker size. The steamed version is 65 calories and the fried version hits 110 calories.
Is otak-otak good for weight loss?
It's one of the better hawker snacks for dieting, honestly. Eighty calories per piece with 8g protein and it's mostly fish. The main thing to watch is how many pieces you eat and whether they're grilled or fried. Three grilled pieces is 240 calories and 24g protein, which is a proper snack.
What makes otak-otak so high in fat?
It's not that high really, 4g fat per piece. The fat comes mostly from coconut milk, which is used as a binder in the fish paste. Compared to most fried snacks at the same stall, otak-otak is actually on the leaner side because the banana leaf wrapping keeps it from soaking up extra oil during grilling.
Berapa kalori dalam Otak-Otak?
Otak-Otak ada kira-kira 80 kalori untuk 1 keping (50g), dengan 8g protein, 4g karbohidrat dan 4g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
