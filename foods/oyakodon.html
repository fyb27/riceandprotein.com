How Many Calories in Oyakodon?

Also known as 親子丼, おやこどん, Chicken and Egg Rice Bowl, Parent and Child Bowl.

Chicken thigh and onion simmered in sweet dashi soy, bound with soft egg and poured over rice. The 'parent and child' bowl, cheap at every soba shop and family restaurant.

631kcal / 1 bowl
A standard bowl of oyakodon is about 631 calories, per calorie.slism.jp for a 460g bowl. It carries about 30g of protein from the chicken and egg, with only 16g of fat because nothing is fried. The rice is about 390 of the calories.

The real talk

Oyakodon is the donburi to pick when you want to stay on plan. It is 631 calories, nearly the same as a gyudon, but with 30g of protein instead of 20 and 16g of fat instead of 24. Nothing in it is fried. The chicken thigh is about 70g, the eggs are one and a half, and the rest is rice and a sweet dashi sauce. At Nakau or a soba shop it costs 500 to 700 yen.

The rice is still 60 percent of the bowl. At 250g and 390 calories, the rice outweighs the chicken and egg combined. Ordering 'gohan sukuname' or eating half the rice takes the bowl down to 430 to 500 calories with the same protein, which is a very good lunch. The egg and chicken on top are only about 230 calories together.

Home versions and restaurant versions differ mostly in sugar. A restaurant warishita has sugar and mirin, about 30 calories per serving. Some shops also finish with a third egg yolk on top for richness, another 55 calories. Neither is a big deal. The bigger jump is the 'omori' rice, which adds about 150 calories, and the pickles and miso soup set, which adds about 50.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less rice or leave a third of it. The chicken and egg are only 230 of the 631 calories.
  • Order oyakodon over katsudon every time. Same shop, about 235 fewer calories, similar protein.
  • Skip the extra yolk topping some shops offer. It is 55 calories of pure fat.
  • Add the miso soup. It is 40 calories and it stretches the meal without adding rice.

Common questions

Is oyakodon healthy?
It is one of the better donburi at 631 calories, 30g protein and 16g fat, because the chicken is simmered rather than fried. The main weakness is 88g of carbs from the rice.
Oyakodon or gyudon, which has fewer calories?
They are close: oyakodon 631, Yoshinoya gyudon 633. Oyakodon has 10g more protein and 8g less fat, so it is the better pick.
How much protein is in oyakodon?
About 30g per bowl, from roughly 70g of chicken thigh and one and a half eggs.
