How Many Calories in Pad Thai?

Also known as ผัดไทย, Phad Thai, Pad Thai Goong Sod, Thai Fried Noodles.

Thin rice noodles stir fried with egg, shrimp or chicken, bean sprouts, chives and a tamarind and palm sugar sauce, finished with crushed peanuts. Thailand's most exported plate of food.

577kcal / 1 plate
One plate of pad thai with shrimp and egg is about 577 calories for a 250g street stall serving, based on the Thai Department of Health food table. The noodles and the sweet sauce carry most of it. Shrimp adds almost nothing, the oil and peanuts add a lot.

The real talk

The 577 figure is for a modest stall plate. Sit down in a tourist area restaurant and the plate grows to 350g with a second egg and a heavier pour of oil, and you are at 750 to 800 calories without changing the recipe. The rice noodles alone are about 215 calories per plate, the sauce is another 70, and the cook's ladle of oil is 120 or more depending on how generous they are.

The sauce is the sneaky part. Pad thai sauce is tamarind, fish sauce and a lot of palm sugar, about 15g of sugar per plate. That is why it tastes sweet even when it looks savoury. Squeezing the lime over it does not cancel anything, it just balances the taste. The crushed peanuts on the side are 60 calories per tablespoon, so if you spoon on three, that is a whole extra egg's worth.

Compared with the other stall noodle plates, pad thai sits in the middle. Pad see ew is heavier at 679, rad na is lighter at 397. If you are picking one plate a day from the noodle cart, pad thai at 577 is fine. It only becomes a problem when it is the 40 baht second dinner after a full meal, which is how a lot of people actually eat it.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for goong sod (fresh shrimp) rather than chicken or pork; shrimp is about 50 calories for 4 pieces versus 100 to 130 for the same weight of pork.
  • Say mai sai nam man yer (not much oil). Most cooks will happily use half a ladle instead of a full one, which saves 60 to 80 calories.
  • Skip the extra peanuts and extra sugar packet on the table. The plate already has 15g of sugar in the sauce.
  • Order pad thai hor kai (wrapped in a thin omelette) only if you count the extra egg; it adds around 100 calories.

Common questions

Is pad thai healthy?
It is a balanced plate for the calories: 577 calories with about 20g protein and some vegetables. The weak points are 15g of sugar in the sauce and 22g of fat, mostly from oil. One plate is a reasonable meal, two plates is not.
How many calories in pad thai without egg?
Take off about 60 to 70 calories, so around 510 for a standard plate. Keep the egg though, it is the cheapest protein on the plate.
Is pad thai or pad see ew higher in calories?
Pad see ew. The Thai Department of Health lists pad see ew with pork and egg at 679 calories versus 577 for pad thai with shrimp, because wide rice noodles soak up more oil and dark soy.
