How Many Calories in Panang Curry?

Also known as พะแนง, พะแนงหมู, Phanaeng, Panaeng Moo, Penang Curry.

A thick, dry style coconut curry with sliced pork or beef, kaffir lime leaf and a touch of peanut in the paste. Less sauce than green curry, but what sauce there is comes from coconut cream rather than milk.

320kcal / 1 bowl (curry only)
A 200g bowl of panang moo is about 320 calories, using the foodmany.com figure of 158 calories per 100g. It is thicker than green or red curry because it is made with coconut cream, so even a smaller ladle carries more fat. With a plate of rice it is about 580 calories.

The real talk

Panang looks like the small portion at a curry shop and it is, by weight. The catch is that it is reduced coconut cream rather than coconut milk, so 100g of panang is about 158 calories while 100g of green curry is under 100. A 200g bowl of panang and a 250g bowl of green curry end up at about the same 240 to 320 range, but the panang has far less liquid, so people eat all of it.

It is also the highest protein curry per spoon. Sliced pork or beef is the main ingredient with almost no vegetables, so a 200g bowl has about 20g of protein. That makes it a decent topping over rice if you stop at one ladle. The Thai table has panang at roughly 158 per 100g; a restaurant panang nuea with 150g of beef is nearer 450.

Watch the khao rad gaeng plate. Panang is often paired with a fried egg or with pad pak, and because the panang itself is dry, shops give a generous ladle. A plate of rice with panang and a fried egg is about 690 calories, and it is a small looking plate.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for one ladle (took diaw); panang is dense, so a second ladle is another 320.
  • Pick chicken or fish panang where offered; pork belly and beef versions run 20 to 30 percent higher.
  • Skip the fried egg on the side; the plate is already 23g of fat.
  • Use panang as the only topping and fill the rest of the plate with a clear vegetable stir fry.

Common questions

Is panang curry high in calories?
For its size, yes. About 320 calories for a 200g bowl, which is denser than green curry because it uses coconut cream instead of milk. With rice, expect around 580.
Panang vs massaman, which is heavier?
Close. Massaman is 325 a cup with potato and peanuts; panang is about 320 for a smaller 200g bowl. Per gram, panang is the richer one.
Can I eat panang curry on a diet?
One ladle over 150g of rice is about 515 calories with 20g of protein. That works as a meal. Two ladles plus a fried egg does not.
