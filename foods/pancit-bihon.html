How Many Calories in Pancit Bihon?

Also known as Pansit Bihon, Bihon Guisado, Filipino Rice Noodles.

Thin rice vermicelli stir-fried with soy sauce, chicken or pork, cabbage, carrot and celery. The noodle that shows up at every birthday because it is cheap and feeds a crowd.

380kcal / 1 plate
A carinderia plate of pancit bihon, about 250g, is around 380 calories. Dry bihon is 60g of that at 215 calories, plus about 70 calories of oil and 95 from chicken, vegetables and soy. It is roughly 70 calories lighter than the same plate of pancit canton.

The real talk

Bihon is rice flour and water, nothing else. Dry, it is 360 calories per 100g with less than 1g of fat, which is why it is the lightest pancit on the menu even after the wok. The 60g of dry noodles in a plate swells to about 170g cooked. The oil is the only fat source, and a careful cook uses one tablespoon (about 70 to 100 calories) for a family batch, while a careless one uses a quarter cup.

The toppings are where carinderia bihon and party bihon differ. Carinderia bihon has 40g of shredded chicken and cabbage, about 80 calories of extras. Party bihon from a caterer gets pork belly slices, shrimp, chicharon crumbs and a boiled egg per bilao, which pushes a slice to 500. Same noodle, different economics.

A plate of bihon at a carinderia is 35 to 50 pesos and comes with a pandesal or a cup of rice if you ask. The pandesal adds 138, the rice adds 260. Bihon with rice is a carb-on-carb plate at 640 calories with about 18g of protein, which is a poor deal. Bihon alone with an egg on the side is a better lunch at 450.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for it without rice or pandesal. The noodles are the carbohydrate.
  • Add a boiled egg or a piece of grilled chicken for protein. Bihon on its own is low at 14g.
  • Squeeze calamansi over it instead of adding more soy sauce. It brightens the plate with zero calories.
  • At home, swap half the noodles for shredded cabbage. The texture holds and the plate drops to about 280.

Common questions

How many calories in pancit bihon per cup?
About 250 calories per cup (170g cooked). A carinderia plate is about 1.5 cups, or 380 calories.
Is pancit bihon good for weight loss?
It is one of the lower calorie pancit at 380 per plate, but it is light on protein at about 14g. Add an egg or chicken and skip the rice to make it a proper meal.
How many calories in pancit bihon with rice?
About 640 calories with 1 cup of rice. Bihon is already noodles, so the rice is a second carbohydrate with no extra protein.
Which pancit has the least calories?
Pancit bihon at 380 per plate, then sotanghon at a similar level, then canton at 450. Palabok and malabon are higher because of the shrimp sauce and toppings.
