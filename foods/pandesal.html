How Many Calories in Pandesal?

Also known as Pan de Sal, Filipino Bread Roll, Pandesal Roll.

A slightly sweet, breadcrumb-dusted bread roll sold warm from neighbourhood bakeries every morning at 3 to 5 pesos each. Eaten plain, dipped in coffee, or stuffed with anything.

110kcal / 1 piece (bakery size, about 35g)
One standard bakery pandesal is about 110 calories for a 35g roll, or about 310 calories per 100g. FatSecret lists a larger 45g pandesal at 138. Plain, it is a small amount of bread. The usual breakfast of 3 pandesal with butter and coffee is closer to 500.

The real talk

A pandesal on its own is modest. Bread is bread: about 310 calories per 100g, and a corner bakery roll is 30 to 40g, so 100 to 125 calories. FatSecret's entry is 138 for a 45g roll, which is the bigger supermarket size. Kawaling Pinoy's recipe card agrees at 138 per roll from a 24-roll batch. The problem has never been one pandesal.

It is the count and the palaman (filling). Nobody eats one. Three pandesal is 330 calories, and that is before anything goes inside. A teaspoon of butter or margarine per roll is 35, a tablespoon of peanut butter is 95, a slice of cheese 70, coconut jam about 60. Three pandesal with butter is about 440. Three with peanut butter is about 615. A pandesal with a fried egg and a slice of Spam is about 300 by itself.

Then the coffee. 3-in-1 sachets are about 100 calories each, so a normal Filipino breakfast of 3 buttered pandesal and one 3-in-1 is around 540 calories, mostly refined flour and sugar, with about 12g of protein. It is not a terrible breakfast, but it is a bigger one than it looks, and it does not hold you until lunch.

How to eat it lighter

  • Two pandesal instead of three saves 110 calories and you will not notice by 10am.
  • Fill it with egg, tuna or kesong puti instead of butter or peanut butter. Same calories, 3 to 4 times the protein.
  • Black coffee or kapeng barako instead of 3-in-1. About 100 calories saved per cup.
  • Whole wheat or malunggay pandesal is the same calories but keeps you full longer.

Common questions

How many calories in 2 pandesal?
About 220 calories for 2 standard 35g rolls, plain. With a teaspoon of butter on each it is about 290.
Is pandesal good for weight loss?
It can fit. Two pandesal with egg or tuna is about 300 calories with 20g of protein. Three with peanut butter and 3-in-1 coffee is over 700, which is the version to avoid.
Is pandesal healthier than white bread?
About the same. Pandesal is white flour with a bit of sugar and fat, roughly 310 calories per 100g, the same as a sliced loaf. Whole wheat pandesal has more fibre at the same calories.
How many calories in ube pandesal with cheese?
About 250 to 300 per piece. Those are bigger rolls (70 to 80g) with ube jam and a cheese centre, so more than double a plain one.
