How Many Calories in Pasembur (Mamak Rojak)?

Pasembur is a Penang mamak classic of shredded veg, fried tofu, prawn fritters, cuttlefish and boiled egg all drenched in a sweet, spicy peanut-and-sweet-potato sauce that absolutely does not mess around.

700kcal / 1 plate (hawker portion)
A standard hawker plate of Pasembur comes in at around 700 calories for roughly 430g of food. The vegetables and egg are basically free calories, but those prawn fritters alone clock in at 210 calories and the peanut sauce adds another 170. Load up the plate and you are easily looking at 950 calories before you sit down.

The real talk

Pasembur is one of those dishes that looks lighter than it is because half the plate is shredded cucumber and jicama. Do not be fooled lah. The real calorie drivers are the deep-fried prawn fritters (210 cal for 80g) and that thick, sweet peanut-and-sweet-potato sauce poured generously over everything (170 cal for 90g alone). Fried tofu strips bring another 120. The veg, cuttlefish and boiled egg together? Only about 160 calories total. So you have this beautiful pile of food that feels healthy but lands at 700 calories for a standard plate. Worth knowing: these numbers are a ballpark estimate because solid lab data on hawker pasembur is basically nonexistent. Every stall makes the sauce differently, fritters vary in size and oil absorption, so treat this as a reasonable guide rather than gospel.

What actually moves the numbers is how many fritters end up on your plate and how heavy-handed the uncle is with the sauce. Ask for less sauce and you are shaving off maybe 50 to 80 calories right there. Skip the prawn fritters entirely and go veg plus tofu only with light sauce and you drop the whole plate down to around 450 calories. That is a genuinely decent snack at that point. If you are ordering this as a full meal, know that at 700 calories it is on the heavier side for a snack, comparable to a full plate of char kuey teow. Better to treat it as a shared starter or a light solo meal rather than a side dish.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less sauce. The peanut sauce is the sneaky one, 170 calories in 90g. Half the pour, maybe 80 fewer calories.
  • Skip or reduce the prawn fritters. They are the single biggest calorie item at 210 calories. Go without and you have already cut nearly a third of the plate calories.
  • Load up on the shredded cucumber, jicama and bean sprouts. They are 50 calories for 120g. Ask the uncle to give you more veg.
  • Eat it as a standalone snack, not a side. At 700 calories it is already a meal on its own. Pairing it with rice or noodles is how you accidentally eat 1,200 calories at the hawker stall.
  • Share the plate. Split it two ways and it is a 350-calorie snack each, which is perfectly reasonable.

Common questions

How many calories in Pasembur (Mamak Rojak)?
A standard hawker plate of Pasembur is around 700 calories for approximately 430g. A lighter version with less sauce and no prawn fritters drops to about 450 calories, while a loaded plate with extra fritters and double sauce can hit 950 calories.
What exactly is Pasembur?
Pasembur is a Penang hawker dish, sometimes called Mamak Rojak. It is a pile of shredded cucumber, jicama, bean sprouts, fried tofu, prawn fritters, cuttlefish and boiled egg, all topped with a thick sweet and spicy sauce made from peanuts and sweet potato. It is a mamak stall staple, especially in Penang.
Is Pasembur a healthy choice?
The vegetables and protein from the egg and cuttlefish are solid, but the deep-fried fritters and thick peanut sauce push the calorie count up fast. At 700 calories it is not a light snack. If you go easy on the fritters and sauce it becomes a much more reasonable option, but ordering the full loaded version regularly is going to make your deficit hard to maintain.
