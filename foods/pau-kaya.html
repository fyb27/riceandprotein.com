How Many Calories in Kaya Pau (Steamed Kaya Bun)?

One steamed kaya pau is about 215 kcal. Soft and sweet, but it is flour plus sugar coconut jam.

215kcal / 1 bun
One kaya pau comes in around 215 calories. It is a steamed white flour bun filled with kaya, which is coconut jam made from sugar, egg, and coconut milk. Steamed means no frying oil, that is the good news, but the soft dough plus sweet filling is still a solid chunk of carbs and sugar.

The real talk

People think kaya pau is light because it is steamed and fluffy, not fried. Fair, no frying oil. But that fluffy bun is basically sweet white bread, and the kaya inside is pure sugar and coconut. One bun is 215 calories of mostly carbs.

It is not a disaster, it is actually one of the gentler snacks on this list since there is almost no frying involved. Just do not eat it thinking it is a free pass. One pau with your kopi is fine, two is creeping toward a meal worth of sugar.

How to eat it lighter

  • Have one pau, not two, and skip the condensed milk in your kopi to balance it
  • Pick a smaller pau if the stall has different sizes, they vary a lot
  • Pair with a black coffee or plain tea so you are not stacking more sugar on top

Common questions

How many calories in a kaya pau?
Roughly 215 calories for a standard bun. Larger ones or extra kaya can push it to 250 plus.
Is kaya pau better than fried snacks?
Calorie for calorie it is gentler because it is steamed, not fried, so the fat is lower. The catch is the sugar in the kaya filling.
Can I eat kaya pau on a diet?
Yes, one bun fits fine in a deficit, especially since it is steamed. Just count it as a real 215 calories, not a freebie.
Berapa kalori dalam Kaya Pau?
Kaya Pau ada kira-kira 215 kalori untuk 1 biji (80g), dengan 5g protein, 38g karbohidrat dan 5g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
