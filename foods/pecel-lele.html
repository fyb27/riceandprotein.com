How Many Calories in Pecel Lele?

Also known as Pecel Lele Lamongan, Lele Goreng, Fried Catfish with Sambal, Penyetan Lele.

Pecel lele is whole catfish deep fried until crisp, smashed with sambal terasi and served with lalapan (raw cabbage, cucumber, basil) at tents with the famous Lamongan banner, usually with rice.

330kcal / 1 whole fried catfish with sambal and lalapan (no rice)
One whole fried catfish with sambal and lalapan is about 330 calories. FatSecret Indonesia lists pecel lele at 292 calories per 150g serving, with 53% of the energy from fat. Add a plate of rice and the standard Rp 15,000 to 20,000 meal is about 590 calories.

The real talk

Catfish itself is a lean fish, around 100 calories per 100g raw. Deep frying a whole 200g fish until the tail goes crispy roughly doubles that, because the skin and the fins become oil sponges. FatSecret's 150g cooked portion at 292 calories works out to 194 per 100g, and that is a fair number for a Lamongan tent fish. So half the calories on the fish are oil.

The good news is the protein: about 24g per fish. The lalapan is basically free at 10 calories, and sambal terasi is around 30 for a spoon, mostly from the terasi and a little oil. If you eat fish, sambal and lalapan without rice it is a genuinely reasonable 330 calorie dinner. The tents also sell tempe and tahu, about 95 and 70 a piece, and those add up fast if you order the 'komplit'.

Rice is 260 for the normal plate, which puts the standard pecel lele meal at 590. Ask for nasi tambah (rice refill) and it is 850. Add es teh manis at 90 and you are at 940 for a tent dinner that felt light.

The lele are usually small farmed fish, 150 to 250g each. Some tents sell 'lele jumbo' at 350g, which is about 550 calories on its own once fried.

How to eat it lighter

  • Fish, sambal and lalapan with half rice is 460 calories and 26g protein. That is a good meal.
  • Ask for the fish 'jangan terlalu kering' (not too crispy). Less time in the oil, a little less oil absorbed.
  • Skip the fried tempe and tahu that come with the komplit set. Together they are about 165 calories.
  • Eat all the lalapan. Cabbage and cucumber cost nothing and fill the same space as rice.
  • Order teh tawar instead of es teh manis. Saves 90 calories.

Common questions

How many calories in pecel lele?
About 330 calories for one fried catfish with sambal and lalapan, and about 590 with a plate of rice.
Is pecel lele healthy?
The fish has 24g protein, but deep frying roughly doubles its calories. Grilled lele (lele bakar) is about 220 calories versus 290 fried.
How many calories in fried catfish?
Around 194 calories per 100g cooked according to FatSecret Indonesia, so 290 for a normal 150g tent-sized fish.
How many calories in pecel lele with rice and es teh?
About 680 calories: 330 for the fish plate, 260 for rice and 90 for the sweet iced tea.
Berapa kalori Pecel Lele?
Pecel Lele mengandung sekitar 330 kalori per satu porsi biasa (200g), dengan 24g protein, 10g karbohidrat dan 22g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
