How Many Calories in Pempek?

Also known as Empek-Empek, Pempek Palembang, Pempek Kapal Selam, Pempek Lenjer, Mpek-Mpek.

Pempek is Palembang's fish and tapioca cake, boiled then fried, served in a bowl of cuko, a dark sweet and sour sauce of palm sugar, tamarind and chili. Kapal selam is the big one with a whole egg inside.

400kcal / 1 kapal selam with cuko
One pempek kapal selam with a bowl of cuko is about 400 calories. FatSecret Indonesia lists pempek at 195 calories per 100g and 39 calories per small piece, so a 150g fried kapal selam with its egg is about 310, and the 100ml of cuko you pour over it adds another 90.

The real talk

Pempek is half fish, half tapioca, and that ratio decides the calories. A good Palembang pempek is 195 calories per 100g according to FatSecret with about 13g protein, which is more protein than most fried snacks because the fish is real. The cheap street version with more sago than fish is closer to 220 per 100g and has half the protein. Price tells you: Rp 15,000 to 25,000 for a kapal selam at a proper place, Rp 8,000 at a cart.

Kapal selam is the heaviest single piece because it is 150g of pempek wrapped around a whole egg and deep fried. About 310 calories with 16g protein. Lenjer (the log) cut into slices is about 40 per slice. Adaan (the round fried ball) is 60 each because it is fried from raw and the dough has more oil in it. Pempek kulit (fish skin) is 70 each and the crispiest, which is the warning.

The cuko is the part nobody counts. It is palm sugar, tamarind, garlic and chili boiled down to a dark syrup, and 100ml is about 90 calories, all of it sugar. The bowl you get with your pempek holds more than that, and people top it up. A generous soak of cuko is easily 150 calories, which is more than three small pempek.

A 'pempek campur' plate with 5 small pieces, one kapal selam kecil, bihun and cucumber under cuko is about 550. The pempek is not the problem there, it is the count and the sauce. Pempek rebus (boiled, not fried) is about 30% lower per piece, and some places will do it if you ask.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for pempek rebus or bakar. Boiled or grilled pempek skips the frying oil, about 40 calories less per 100g.
  • Cuko on the side and dip, do not soak. Halving the sauce saves 45 calories of pure sugar.
  • One kapal selam is a meal. Five small pieces plus a kapal selam is two meals.
  • Add the cucumber and ebi. The shredded cucumber is free and the dried shrimp powder is 10 calories of flavour.
  • Pick lenjer or kapal selam over adaan and kulit. The boiled-then-fried types absorb less oil than the fried-from-raw ones.

Common questions

How many calories in pempek?
About 40 calories per small piece, 195 per 100g according to FatSecret Indonesia. A kapal selam with cuko is about 400.
How many calories in pempek kapal selam?
About 310 for the fried pempek with its egg, and about 400 once you add the usual bowl of cuko.
Is pempek healthy?
Better than most fried snacks, because half of it is fish and a kapal selam has about 18g protein. The cuko is the issue: 90 calories of sugar per bowl and it is easy to drink two.
How many calories in cuko pempek?
About 90 calories per 100ml, nearly all from palm sugar. A heavy pour can be 150.
Berapa kalori Pempek?
Pempek mengandung sekitar 400 kalori per satu porsi biasa (250g), dengan 18g protein, 46g karbohidrat dan 15g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
