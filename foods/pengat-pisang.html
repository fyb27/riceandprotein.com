How Many Calories in Pengat Pisang?

Pengat pisang is around 280 calories a bowl. Banana, coconut milk and palm sugar, a rich combo.

280kcal / 1 bowl
A bowl of pengat pisang is about 280 calories. It is bananas stewed in coconut milk and gula melaka, so the fruit is healthy but the santan and palm sugar are where the calories live. Warm, sweet, and heavier than it looks.

The real talk

Pengat pisang sounds innocent, it is just banana right. But the banana gets simmered in a pool of coconut milk and gula melaka until it is basically a sweet santan stew. The fruit contributes maybe 90 calories, the coconut milk and palm sugar bring the rest up to 280.

It is a proper comfort dessert, warm and rich, the kind of thing you want during puasa or a rainy evening. Nothing wrong with it, just know it is a 280 calorie bowl and not a guilt-free banana snack. Lighter santan is the easiest way to trim it down.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less coconut milk to cut around 70 calories
  • Use ripe banana sweetness and request less added gula melaka
  • Have a smaller bowl, half a portion is a cosy 140 calories

Common questions

Is pengat pisang healthy?
The banana gives you potassium and fibre, but the dish overall is around 280 calories because of coconut milk and palm sugar. Treat it as a dessert, not a fruit serving.
What makes pengat pisang high in calories?
Coconut milk and gula melaka. The banana is the light part at around 90 calories. The santan and sugar push it up to 280.
Can I eat pengat pisang and still lose weight?
Yes, just budget for it. Ask for less santan and keep the bowl small and it fits into a normal day fine.
Berapa kalori dalam Pengat Pisang?
Pengat Pisang ada kira-kira 280 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (220g), dengan 2g protein, 44g karbohidrat dan 10g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
