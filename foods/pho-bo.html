How Many Calories in Pho Bo?

Also known as Phở bò, Beef Pho, Vietnamese Beef Noodle Soup, Pho Tai.

Flat rice noodles in a clear beef bone broth with sliced beef, onion, and a plate of herbs on the side. Sold from 5am at every street corner in Hanoi and Saigon for 40,000 to 60,000 dong.

450kcal / 1 bowl
A standard bowl of pho bo is about 450 calories for roughly 500g of noodles, beef and broth. Rare beef (tai) lands around 430 and well done brisket (chin) around 455. Order it with fatty brisket, tendon and a side of quay and you are past 700.

The real talk

The 450 calories in a bowl of pho splits about like this: 200g of cooked banh pho noodles is 220, the 80g of beef is 160, and 300ml of broth is only 50 even with the fat floating on top. The herbs, onion and bean sprouts add maybe 10. So half the bowl is noodles. That is the part to control if you want to eat pho five mornings a week without gaining weight.

The beef cut changes the number more than people expect. Tai (rare sliced round) is lean, about 130 calories per 80g. Nam (flank) and gau (fatty brisket) run 200 to 230 for the same weight. A bowl of tai nam gau with a spoon of the rendered fat from the pot is closer to 600. The quay, those fried dough sticks, are 120 to 150 each and most people eat two.

Pho is one of the better street breakfasts if you order it plain. You get 28g of protein, the broth fills you up with almost nothing, and there is no oil beyond what is on the beef. The problem is a 60,000 dong bowl at a busy quan is often 250g of noodles, not 200, and the free refill of broth comes with more fat skimmed from the pot.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for it bớt bánh (less noodles). That alone drops the bowl from 450 to about 350 with no loss of beef.
  • Order tai instead of gau or nam. Same protein, 60 to 90 fewer calories per bowl.
  • Skip the quay. Two sticks are 250 to 300 calories, more than half the pho itself.
  • Do not add the spoon of fat from the pot that some quan offer. It is 45 calories per teaspoon.
  • Load up on the herb plate and bean sprouts. They are basically free and slow you down.

Common questions

Is pho good for weight loss?
It can be. A plain bowl is 450 calories with 28g protein and a lot of water volume, which is a reasonable breakfast or lunch. Order less noodles and lean beef and it drops to about 350.
How many calories in pho broth alone?
About 15 to 20 calories per 100ml, so a full 300ml ladle is around 50. Almost all of it is from fat that rises to the top; skim it and the broth is close to zero.
Does pho have a lot of sodium?
Yes. One bowl typically carries 1,500 to 2,000mg of sodium from the broth and fish sauce, most of a day's limit. Not a calorie issue but worth knowing if you drink every drop.
Pho bo or pho ga, which has fewer calories?
Pho ga with breast meat is about 400 versus 450 for pho bo. The difference is small; the noodle portion matters more than the meat choice.
