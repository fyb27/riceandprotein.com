How Many Calories in Pho Ga?

Also known as Phở gà, Chicken Pho, Vietnamese Chicken Noodle Soup.

The chicken version of pho: rice noodles in a lighter chicken broth with shredded or sliced free range chicken, spring onion and lime leaf. Hanoi does it with skin on and a pile of fried shallots.

400kcal / 1 bowl
A standard bowl of pho ga is around 400 calories, built from 200g of rice noodles, 80g of chicken and 300ml of broth. Shredded breast keeps it near 380; a thigh with the skin on pushes it to about 450. The dry mixed version, pho ga tron, is 500 plus because of the oil and shallots.

The real talk

Pho ga is about 50 calories lighter than pho bo, and the difference is almost entirely the meat. Chicken breast is 110 calories per 80g against 160 for the same weight of beef. The noodles are identical at 220 for 200g, and chicken broth is a little leaner than beef broth, around 45 for the bowl. So you are looking at 400 for a normal quan portion.

The trap is the Hanoi style bowl. Many quan use ga ta, free range chicken, served with skin on and chopped into bone-in pieces. The skin on an 80g portion adds 60 to 80 calories. Then there is the spoonful of fried shallots in oil that goes on top, another 40. Ask for a lot of chicken and you often get 120g instead of 80, which is fine for protein but now the bowl is 480.

Pho ga tron, the dry version tossed in soy, chili oil and fried shallots with the broth on the side, is the one to watch. The dressing is 80 to 100 calories of oil and the noodle portion is usually bigger because there is no broth to fill the bowl. Expect 500 to 550 for the same price.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for uc (breast) rather than dui (thigh). Same bowl, about 60 fewer calories and more protein.
  • Skip the skin. Most quan will happily give you skinless if you say khong da.
  • Order bớt bánh, less noodles. Takes the bowl to about 320.
  • Stick with the soup version. Pho ga tron adds 100 to 150 calories of dressing.
  • The lime, chili and herb plate are free. Use them instead of the shallot oil.

Common questions

Is pho ga healthier than pho bo?
Marginally. Pho ga is about 400 calories versus 450 for pho bo, with similar protein around 26 to 28g. Both are decent meals if you control the noodles.
How much protein is in a bowl of pho ga?
About 26g from 80g of chicken plus a little from the broth. Ask for extra chicken and it climbs to 35g for around 480 calories.
How many calories in pho ga tron?
Roughly 500 to 550. The dry version uses more noodles and a dressing of oil, soy and fried shallots that adds over 100 calories compared with the soup version.
