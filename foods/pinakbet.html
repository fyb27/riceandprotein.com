How Many Calories in Pinakbet?

Also known as Pakbet, Pinakbet Tagalog, Pinakbet Ilocano.

Squash, ampalaya, eggplant, okra and sitaw stewed with shrimp paste and pieces of pork belly or bagnet. The Ilocano vegetable dish that every region has its own version of.

230kcal / 1 carinderia serving (about 180g)
One carinderia serving of pinakbet is about 230 calories for a 180g scoop. The mixed vegetables are only about 60 calories, the pork belly pieces about 130 and the bagoong and cooking oil about 40. FatSecret lists a leaner cup at 152 calories.

The real talk

Pinakbet is one of the better ulam choices, but the pork decides it. Squash, ampalaya, eggplant, okra and sitaw together are about 40 calories per 100g, so the 130g of vegetables in a scoop is 60 calories. Then the cook adds pork belly for flavour, about 30g per serving at 130 calories, and a tablespoon of oil to fry the bagoong. Kawaling Pinoy's Tagalog version comes to 434 per serving with half a pound of belly for 4 people, which is a very pork heavy home portion.

The Ilocano version with bagnet on top is a different number. Bagnet is deep fried pork belly, about 500 calories per 100g, and a proper Ilocano pinakbet has a handful of it crumbled over the top. That pushes a serving to 350 or more. The Tagalog version with kalabasa and a little belly is the 230 calorie one, and the carinderia version with mostly vegetables and a few pork scraps can be as low as 170.

At 40 to 50 pesos a scoop it is the cheapest way to fill half your plate. Pinakbet with 1 cup of rice is about 490 calories, and the fibre from the ampalaya and okra makes it more filling than the number suggests. The bagoong is salty rather than caloric, about 15 calories per tablespoon, so the taste does not cost much.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for it without the bagnet or with the pork on the side. Vegetables and bagoong alone are about 110 calories per scoop.
  • Scoop from the vegetable side of the tray, not where the pork settled at the bottom.
  • Use pinakbet as your vegetable next to a lean protein like inasal or grilled bangus. Together with rice that is about 800 for a full plate.
  • At home, use shrimp instead of pork belly. Same bagoong flavour at about 150 per serving.

Common questions

Is pinakbet good for weight loss?
Yes, as far as carinderia ulam goes. About 230 calories for a scoop with pork, and about 110 without. High fibre, decent protein, and it makes rice go slower.
How many calories in pinakbet with bagnet?
About 360 per serving. Bagnet is deep fried belly at about 500 calories per 100g, and a serving gets 25 to 30g of it.
Is pinakbet high in sodium?
Yes, from the bagoong. A serving can carry 800 to 1,000mg of sodium. Calories are low but it is a salty dish.
Pinakbet or laing, which is lower in calories?
Pinakbet, at about 230 versus 350 for laing. Pinakbet has no coconut milk, which is what makes laing heavy.
