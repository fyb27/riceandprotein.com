How Many Calories in Pork BBQ?

Also known as Filipino Pork Barbecue, Pork BBQ Skewer, Inihaw na Baboy, BBQ Stick.

Thin slices of pork marinated in soy, banana ketchup, calamansi, brown sugar and 7-Up, threaded on a stick and grilled over charcoal. Sold from street carts for 20 to 40 pesos.

130kcal / 1 stick
One standard street stick of pork BBQ is about 130 calories for roughly 45g of grilled pork with its sweet glaze. About 100 calories is the pork and 30 is the sugar in the marinade and basting sauce. Three sticks with 1 cup of rice is about 650 calories.

The real talk

Pork BBQ is fairly reasonable per stick. Most vendors use kasim (shoulder) with a bit of fat, which grills to about 220 calories per 100g, so a 40 to 45g stick is around 100 calories of meat. The marinade is where the sugar lives: brown sugar, banana ketchup and 7-Up, then a basting sauce with more of the same. About 8g of sugar sticks to each skewer, which is 30 calories. Kawaling Pinoy's recipe card shows 175 per stick, but that counts a full 3 cups of brown sugar across 50 skewers as if all of it ended up on the meat.

The stick size is the real variable. A 20 peso street stick is thin, maybe 35g, and lands near 100 calories. The fat 40 peso sticks outside a restaurant can be 80g of pork with visible fat chunks between the slices, and those are 230 to 250 calories each. Ask yourself whether it is 3 bites or 6 bites and you have your answer.

Nobody eats one. The normal order is 3 to 5 sticks with rice, and vendors sell them in a pile for a reason. Five standard sticks is 650 calories before rice. With 1 cup of rice that is 910 calories, and the sweet glaze makes rice go down fast. Compared to a 150g slab of liempo at 600 calories, BBQ is actually the leaner ihaw choice if you stop at 3 sticks.

How to eat it lighter

  • Three sticks, 1 cup rice, about 650 calories. That is a real meal and easy to remember.
  • Pick the sticks with less white fat between the slices. A lean stick is closer to 100 calories.
  • Skip the extra basting. Ask for it without the last brush of sauce and save about 15 calories per stick.
  • Chicken BBQ sticks are about 90 calories each if the vendor has them.

Common questions

How many calories in 5 sticks of pork BBQ?
About 650 calories for 5 standard street sticks, or around 1,200 for 5 jumbo sticks. With rice add 260 per cup.
Is pork BBQ healthy?
Per stick it is fine: about 9g of protein for 130 calories. The problems are the sugar glaze (about 8g per stick) and the habit of eating 5 with 2 cups of rice.
How much sugar is in pork BBQ?
About 8g per stick from the brown sugar, banana ketchup and 7-Up in the marinade and basting sauce. Five sticks is 40g of sugar, roughly a can of soft drink.
Pork BBQ or liempo, which is lower in calories?
Pork BBQ. Three sticks is about 390 calories, while a standard 150g liempo slab is about 600, because BBQ uses shoulder and liempo uses belly.
