How Many Calories in Prawn Noodle Soup?

Also known as Prawn Mee Soup, Hae Mee, 虾面, Prawn Noodles Singapore.

Singapore prawn noodle soup is yellow noodles or bee hoon in a deep prawn head and pork bone broth with prawns, kangkong and bean sprouts, served soup or dry with a side of broth.

294kcal / 1 bowl (soup)
A standard bowl of prawn noodle soup is about 294 calories for a 500g bowl, with only 5g of fat. The dry version tossed in chilli, lard and soy is about 461 calories. The 167 calorie difference is almost entirely the dressing.

The real talk

This is one of the best value hawker bowls for anyone counting. 150g of noodles is about 180 calories, five prawns about 60, and the broth, even though it tastes rich, is around 40 calories a bowl because the stall skims most of the fat off. Add kangkong and bean sprouts at 10 and some fried shallots at 15. That is 294 for a full bowl that keeps you full until dinner.

The dry version is a different dish for calorie purposes. Same noodles, same prawns, but tossed in chilli paste, lard, ketchup and soy. That dressing is about 150 to 170 calories, which takes the bowl to 461. The small bowl of broth on the side is still nearly free. So dry prawn mee costs you about 55 percent more than soup for the same amount of food.

Watch the add ons. Pork ribs are the usual upgrade and two ribs add around 150 calories. The S$8 jumbo prawn version is fine, because prawns are lean, and two big prawns are only about 60 calories more. Pig tail and intestines also stay under 100. The rule is simple. Add seafood, not pork fat.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order soup, not dry. The dry dressing is about 167 calories for the same bowl.
  • Choose bee hoon over yellow noodles. It is about 30 calories lighter per bowl and takes less alkaline flavour.
  • Upgrade with prawns, not pork ribs. Two extra prawns are 30 calories, two ribs are around 150.
  • Ask for extra kangkong and bean sprouts. Most stalls give it free and it fills the bowl for under 20 calories.
  • Skip the fried shallots if the stall spoons them on heavily. It is a small saving, about 15 calories, but it is free to skip.

Common questions

Is prawn noodle soup good for weight loss?
It is one of the better hawker choices. At 294 calories with 22g of protein and 5g of fat, a soup bowl is filling for the calories. Order dry and it becomes 461, which is still fine but no longer light.
How many calories in dry prawn mee?
About 461 calories for a standard bowl. The chilli, lard and soy dressing is about 167 calories on top of the 294 soup version.
Is the prawn broth high in calories?
No. Most stalls skim the fat, so a bowl of broth is about 40 calories. The richness comes from prawn heads and bones, which flavour the stock without adding much fat.
How is Singapore prawn mee different from Penang Hokkien mee?
Same idea, different details. Penang Hokkien mee at about 420 calories has a spicier broth with more chilli oil and often a hard boiled egg. The Singapore soup version at 294 is lighter and usually comes without egg.
Berapa kalori dalam Prawn Noodle Soup?
Prawn Noodle Soup ada kira-kira 294 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (500g), dengan 22g protein, 42g karbohidrat dan 5g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
