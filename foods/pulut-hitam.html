How Many Calories in Bubur Pulut Hitam?

Also known as Black Glutinous Rice Dessert.

Bubur pulut hitam is around 290 calories a bowl. Black rice sounds healthy, the coconut milk says otherwise.

290kcal / 1 bowl
A bowl of bubur pulut hitam is about 290 calories. The black glutinous rice and sugar make up most of it, and then the coconut milk drizzle on top adds roughly 100 calories on its own. It tastes like a healthy dessert but that santan is doing real work.

The real talk

Pulut hitam tastes like a healthy dessert because it is black rice, full of fibre and antioxidants, but bro that santan they drown it in is where 100 plus calories are hiding. The rice and sugar already put you around 190, then the coconut milk topping shoves you to 290.

It is not a bad dessert at all. You actually get some fibre and a bit of protein out of it. Just respect that it is a 290 calorie bowl and not the free health food it pretends to be. If you want it lighter, the move is simply less santan on top.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less coconut milk on top, that alone saves around 80 to 100 calories
  • Have a smaller bowl, half a portion is a satisfying 145 calories
  • Skip the extra scoop of sugar, the rice is already sweetened in the pot

Common questions

Is bubur pulut hitam healthy?
It has good things going for it, black rice gives you fibre and antioxidants. But at 290 calories a bowl, mostly from coconut milk and sugar, treat it as a dessert and not a free pass.
Why is pulut hitam so high in calories?
The coconut milk is the main reason. That topping alone is around 100 calories. Glutinous rice and palm sugar do the rest.
Can I eat pulut hitam on a diet?
Yes, just account for it. Ask for less santan and keep the portion reasonable and a bowl fits fine into a normal day.
Berapa kalori dalam Bubur Pulut Hitam?
Bubur Pulut Hitam ada kira-kira 290 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (250g), dengan 5g protein, 48g karbohidrat dan 9g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
