How Many Calories in Pulut Panggang?

2 rolls of pulut panggang are about 290 kcal. Grilled sticky rice with spicy coconut inside.

290kcal / 2 rolls
Two rolls of pulut panggang come in around 290 calories, so roughly 145 each. It is glutinous rice cooked in coconut milk, wrapped around a spiced coconut or dried shrimp filling, then grilled in banana leaf. The grilling keeps oil out, but glutinous rice plus coconut milk is dense, so two rolls is a proper snack not a nibble.

The real talk

Pulut panggang smells amazing off the charcoal and the banana leaf char makes you want three immediately. But glutinous rice is heavy, bro, and it is cooked in coconut milk, so each roll is about 145 calories. Two rolls is 290, basically a small meal.

It is grilled so there is no frying oil, which is a plus, and the rempah filling actually gives it flavour and a little protein. Just know that two rolls fills you up like rice because it is rice. Treat it as a mini meal, not a between-meal snack you stack on top of lunch.

How to eat it lighter

  • Count 2 rolls as part of a meal, not an extra on top of your rice
  • Pick rolls with more filling and a thinner rice layer if you can
  • It is grilled already, so just skip any sweet sauce and you are good

Common questions

How many calories in pulut panggang?
About 145 calories per roll, so 290 for two. Bigger rolls with more coconut filling go higher.
Is pulut panggang fattening?
It is calorie dense because glutinous rice and coconut milk are heavy, but it is grilled not fried. Treat 2 rolls like a small meal and it fits fine.
Is pulut panggang a meal or a snack?
Two rolls is really a light meal worth of carbs. If you eat it as a snack on top of full meals, that is where the calories pile up.
Berapa kalori dalam Pulut Panggang?
Pulut Panggang ada kira-kira 290 kalori untuk 2 gulung (130g), dengan 6g protein, 44g karbohidrat dan 10g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
