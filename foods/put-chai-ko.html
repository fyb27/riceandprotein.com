How Many Calories in Put Chai Ko (Red Bean Pudding Cake)?

Also known as 缽仔糕, 砵仔糕, Put Chai Ko, Bowl Pudding, Hong Kong Red Bean Rice Cake.

Put chai ko is a small steamed pudding of rice flour, wheat starch and brown or white sugar set in a porcelain bowl, studded with red beans, then popped out and speared on two bamboo sticks. Street stalls in Sham Shui Po and Yuen Long sell them for HK$5 to HK$10.

130kcal / 1 piece
One put chai ko of about 100g is around 130 calories, based on the Centre for Food Safety figure of 130 kcal per 100g. It is nearly all carbohydrate: about 29g, of which 13g is sugar, with 2g of protein and 1g of fat. The Macau Sports Bureau food guide gives a similar 140 calories per piece.

The real talk

This is one of the few Hong Kong street snacks that is not fried, and it shows. At 130 calories a piece, a put chai ko is a third of a sheet of egg waffle (390) and less than half a fish siu mai skewer (294). There is no oil in the recipe at all; the 1g of fat comes from the red beans. What you are eating is rice starch and sugar set with steam, which is why it is chewy and translucent.

The sugar is the whole story. CFS puts it at 13g per 100g, so one piece is about 3 teaspoons, and the brown sugar version (黃糖) has slightly more than the white. That is a fast hit of carbs with no protein or fibre to slow it down, which is why dietitians in Hong Kong flag it for people managing blood sugar. Two pieces is 26g of sugar, over half the WHO daily suggestion of 50g, for a snack that takes 3 minutes to eat.

Size is the variable. The old style bowl cakes from the Sham Shui Po stalls are about 100g and land at the 130 figure. The mini versions sold in packs of 6 at Taipo and Yuen Long markets are 40 to 50g each, so around 60 calories a piece, which is the number the Centre for Health Protection used in its snack post. Check which one you are holding before you assume it is a light snack.

How to eat it lighter

  • One piece is a fair 130 calorie snack. Two is a sugar bomb.
  • Choose the red bean version over the plain. The beans add about 1g of fibre and a little protein for the same calories.
  • Skip the sweet sauce some stalls brush on. It adds another 20 calories of pure sugar.
  • Eat it after a meal with protein rather than on an empty stomach. The 13g of sugar lands softer.

Common questions

How many calories in a put chai ko?
About 130 calories for a standard 100g piece, according to Centre for Food Safety data. The mini versions are around 60.
Is put chai ko healthy?
It is low in fat and calories for a street snack, but about 13g of sugar per piece with no protein. Fine as an occasional snack, not a daily one if you are watching blood sugar.
Is put chai ko gluten free?
Mostly rice flour, but many recipes add wheat starch for the chewy texture, so not reliably. Ask the stall if it matters to you.
