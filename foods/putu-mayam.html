How Many Calories in Putu Mayam?

Also known as Putu Mayong, String Hoppers, Idiyappam, Puttu Mayam, Putu Mayam with Orange Sugar.

Putu mayam is the Indian Singaporean breakfast of steamed rice flour string noodles pressed into lacy nests, served with freshly grated coconut and orange coloured sugar or gula melaka, sold from hawker stalls and pasar malam carts.

210kcal / 1 plate (2 nests with coconut and sugar)
A standard plate of putu mayam with 2 nests, grated coconut and orange sugar is about 210 calories for a 140g serving, with around 4g of fat. The two nests are about 130 calories, the grated coconut around 55, and the sugar about 25.

The real talk

The noodle itself is steamed rice flour and water, about 65 calories per 50g nest. Two nests is 130. Nothing is fried and nothing has oil in it. The coconut is 20g of fresh grated white coconut at about 55, and the orange sugar is 12g at 25. That is the 210 plate for about S$1.50. It is a breakfast with fewer calories than a single kaya toast slice with butter.

The stall decides the sugar. Some pour the sugar on with a small spoon, some with a big one. Two spoons of the orange sugar and a heap of coconut takes the same plate to about 260. Gula melaka, which is sold as the premium option, is the same calories as white sugar per gram but the stalls use more of it because it is softer, so a gula melaka plate is usually 20 to 30 higher.

Where it goes wrong is quantity. Putu mayam is sold in stacks of 5 or 10 nests for the family, and eating 5 nests with coconut and sugar is about 480. It is light per nest and dangerous per stack. The Malay version with a thicker noodle, called putu mayong, is about the same per nest.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less sugar. Half the orange sugar saves about 12 calories, and the coconut carries the flavour anyway.
  • Eat 2 nests, not 4. Each nest is about 65 calories before toppings.
  • Skip the gula melaka upgrade. The stall uses more of it than white sugar, and it adds around 25 calories.
  • Add a boiled egg from the drinks stall. Putu mayam has only 4g of protein per plate.
  • Choose putu mayam over appam with sugar if you want the lighter Indian breakfast. Two appam is about 276.

Common questions

How many calories in putu mayam?
About 210 calories for a plate of 2 nests with grated coconut and orange sugar. Each nest on its own is around 65 calories. A stack of 5 with toppings is about 480.
Is putu mayam healthy?
It is one of the lighter hawker breakfasts. Steamed rice noodles with no oil, about 4g of fat per plate. The downside is low protein at 4g and about 20g of sugar from the topping.
What is the orange sugar on putu mayam?
Plain white sugar coloured orange, about 4 calories per gram like any sugar. A hawker spoon of about 12g adds around 25 calories to the plate.
Is putu mayam the same as idiyappam?
Yes, same string hopper. In South India idiyappam is eaten with curry or coconut milk. In Singapore and Malaysia putu mayam is usually the sweet breakfast version with coconut and sugar.
Berapa kalori dalam Putu Mayam?
Putu Mayam ada kira-kira 210 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (140g), dengan 4g protein, 40g karbohidrat dan 4g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
