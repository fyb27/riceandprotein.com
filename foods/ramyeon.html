How Many Calories in Ramyeon?

Also known as 라면, Korean Instant Noodles, Shin Ramyun, 신라면, Ramyun.

Ramyeon is Korean instant noodles, boiled with the soup powder in one pot and usually finished with an egg, a slice of cheese or kimchi. Shin Ramyun is the benchmark pack.

575kcal / 1 pack cooked with 1 egg
A 120g pack of Shin Ramyun is 500 calories on the Nongshim label, with 79g of carbs, 16g of fat and 1,790mg of sodium. Add the egg most people crack in and a bowl of ramyeon is about 575 calories. Add a slice of cheese and it is 640.

The real talk

The noodle block is fried before it is dried, which is why 110g of noodles carries 440 calories and 16g of fat. The soup powder is only about 60 calories, but it holds most of the 1,790mg of sodium, which is 90 percent of the daily limit in one bowl. The calories are the noodles. The salt is the soup.

Then there are the extras. An egg is 75 calories, a slice of cheese is 65, half a cup of leftover rice dunked into the broth is 150. A convenience store ramyeon with egg, cheese and rice is 790 calories, which is more than a bowl of jajangmyeon. The pack itself is not the problem. The habit of turning it into a three-course meal is.

Prices are still low, around 1,000 to 1,500 won a pack at a mart and 3,500 to 4,500 won cooked at a bunsik shop. At that price it is an easy late-night default. Two packs in one sitting, which plenty of people do, is 1,000 calories and 3,600mg of sodium before any toppings.

How to eat it lighter

  • Drink half the broth or less. That saves close to 900mg of sodium and about 30 calories from the fat floating on top.
  • Use half the soup powder. The noodles taste the same, and the sodium drops by 45 percent.
  • Keep the egg, skip the cheese and rice. The egg adds 6g of protein for 75 calories; the cheese and rice add 215 calories and no benefit.
  • Throw in 100g of bean sprouts, mushrooms or cabbage. About 25 calories and it doubles the bowl.
  • Look for the non-fried versions (건면). Shin Ramyun's dry noodle version is 350 calories for a 97g pack.

Common questions

How many calories in one Shin Ramyun?
500 calories per 120g pack on the Nongshim label. Cooked with an egg it is about 575.
Why is ramyeon so high in calories?
The noodle block is deep fried before drying. 110g of noodles is 440 calories and 16g of fat. The soup is only about 60.
Is ramyeon bad for weight loss?
One pack at 500 calories fits a diet if it is your meal, not a snack. The bigger issue is 1,790mg of sodium, which causes water retention the next day.
How much sodium is in Shin Ramyun?
1,790mg per pack, about 90 percent of the 2,000mg daily recommendation. Leaving the broth cuts that roughly in half.
