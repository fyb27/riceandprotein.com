How Many Calories in Rendang?

Slow cooked dry coconut and spice curry. The toasted coconut and santan are the calorie engine, not the meat.

200kcal / 1 lauk portion (100g)
Rendang is around 200 calories per 100g serving (the lauk only, no rice). The calories come from the coconut milk and kerisik (toasted coconut) it is slow cooked in, not so much the meat. The drier it is, like rendang tok, the higher the calories per gram.

The real talk

Everyone assumes rendang is heavy because of the beef. Wrong. It is the coconut bro. Rendang is meat slow cooked in santan and kerisik (toasted ground coconut) until the gravy dries into the meat. That dried coconut fat is the calorie engine. A 100g lauk portion is around 200 calories, and most of the fat is coconut, not beef.

Because it is concentrated, the drier versions hit harder per gram. Rendang tok, cooked down longest with the most kerisik, is the richest at around 250 to 270 per 100g. The seafood and organ versions like rendang kerang are leaner. A normal kenduri scoop of about 150g puts you near 300 calories, so portion is everything with rendang.

How to eat it lighter

  • Watch the portion. Rendang is calorie dense per gram, so a moderate scoop goes a long way.
  • Leaner versions like rendang kerang (cockles) sit lower than the dry beef rendang tok.
  • Do not also drench your rice in extra kerisik and gravy. That is pure coconut fat.

Common questions

How many calories in rendang?
Around 200 calories per 100g for beef rendang (lauk only). The coconut milk and toasted coconut (kerisik) are the main calorie source, not the meat.
Why is rendang so calorie dense?
It is slow cooked in coconut milk and kerisik until the gravy dries into the meat, concentrating coconut fat. The drier the rendang, like rendang tok, the higher the calories per gram.
Which rendang is lowest in calories?
Rendang kerang (cockles) at around 150 per 100g is among the leanest. Rendang tok is the highest near 260 because it is the driest and uses the most kerisik.
