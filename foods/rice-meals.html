Rice Meals Calories

Rice is not the enemy. I lost 42kg eating rice almost every day, so this page is never going to tell you to quit nasi. What matters is which rice dish and what goes on top of it. A plate of steamed chicken rice and a nasi kandar with three lauk can be 700 calories apart, and both look like "just rice with stuff".

Every rice meal below is ranked from lightest to heaviest. Click any dish for the full macro breakdown, where the calories actually hide, and how to order it lighter without eating sad food.

Rice Meals at a glance

There are 27 dishes ranked on this page. The lightest is Ketupat at 145 kcal per 1 piece (ketupat nasi), the heaviest is Nasi Ambeng at 1080 kcal per 1 portion (single-pax share from communal platter), and the average lands around 624 calories a serving. 3 of them come in under 300 calories, and 18 are 600 or more.

That spread is the whole point. Two dishes off the same menu can be 935 calories apart, which is more than most people's entire daily deficit. Knowing which is which costs you nothing and changes the outcome.
