How Many Calories in Roast Goose Rice?

Also known as 燒鵝飯, Siu Ngo Faan, Roasted Goose with Rice, Hong Kong Roast Goose.

Roast goose rice is a plate of steamed rice with chopped roast goose, skin on, drizzled with the sweet roasting juices. Every siu mei shop in Hong Kong hangs a few birds in the window, and a plate runs about HK$50 to HK$70.

970kcal / 1 plate
One plate of roast goose rice (about 530g) is around 970 calories, per Centre for Food Safety testing. The goose portion with skin is roughly 500 calories, the rice about 390, and the gravy adds 60. Fat is about 50g, most of it from the skin and the layer underneath.

The real talk

Goose is a fatty bird by nature and roasting it with the skin on keeps every gram. A 180g portion of roast goose with skin is about 500 calories and 40g of fat, compared to roast duck at roughly 400 for the same weight and a steamed chicken portion at 250. The meat underneath is good protein, around 35g per plate, but you cannot get to it without eating the skin unless you peel it off yourself.

The rice underneath is 300g steamed, about 390 calories, and the shop ladles goose drippings over it. That gravy is rendered fat mixed with soy and sugar, around 60 calories per spoonful. CFS lists goose rice at 970 for the plate, slightly under the average siu mei rice figure of 922 for the leaner meats but nearly the same as a char siu rice, because the fat in goose skin does the same job as the honey glaze on char siu.

For what it is, roast goose rice still beats baked pork chop rice (1,300) or a plate of salted fish chicken fried rice (1,400) at the same shop. It is an honest plate: real meat, real rice, no batter, no cheese. The lever you have is the skin. Peel it off half the pieces and you drop the plate to roughly 800. Order the leg (鵝髀) and it is more meat, less fat, than the breast pieces with the thick skin.

How to eat it lighter

  • Peel the skin off half the pieces. That alone saves about 150 calories.
  • Ask for less rice (少飯). About 130 calories saved and you will not miss it.
  • Order the goose with a side of plain blanched choy sum (油菜) instead of extra rice. Around 60 calories and a lot more volume.
  • Skip the extra gravy. The default drizzle is fine; a second ladle is another 60 calories of goose fat.
  • If you are choosing between siu mei, soy sauce chicken rice is about 180 calories per 100g versus 200 for goose or char siu.

Common questions

How many calories in roast goose rice?
About 970 calories for a 530g plate, according to Centre for Food Safety testing of Hong Kong siu mei shops.
Is roast goose fattier than roast duck?
Yes. Roast goose with skin is roughly 280 calories per 100g versus about 240 for roast duck. Goose has a thicker fat layer under the skin.
How can I make roast goose rice lighter?
Remove the skin from half the pieces and ask for less rice. That brings a 970 calorie plate down to around 700 without changing the dish.
