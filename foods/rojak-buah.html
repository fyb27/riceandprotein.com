How Many Calories in Rojak Buah?

Rojak buah is a Malaysian fruit and vegetable salad with jambu, mango, pineapple, cucumber, and sengkuang, tossed in thick sweet hae ko sauce and finished with crushed peanuts.

300kcal / 1 plate (hawker portion)
A standard hawker plate of rojak buah is around 300 calories for roughly 250g. That number is a ballpark estimate since lab data for this dish is thin, but it's a reasonable figure for a typical portion with standard sauce and peanuts. The fruit itself is light; it's the hae ko sauce and the peanut topping doing most of the calorie work.

The real talk

Rojak buah looks like a fruit salad, so people assume it's basically free calories. It's not, but it's also not a disaster. The dish is fresh mixed fruit and vegetables, usually jambu air, mango, pineapple, cucumber, and sengkuang, tossed in a thick sauce made from hae ko (sweet fermented prawn paste), tamarind, and a fair amount of sugar. The whole plate comes in at around 300 calories for 250g, but here's the thing: the 160g of fruit only accounts for about 75 of those calories. The 55g of sauce contributes 130 calories, and the 22g of crushed peanuts on top adds another 125. So more than 80% of the calories come from the sauce and nuts, not the fruit. Fair heads up that these numbers are estimates because solid nutritional data for rojak buah is genuinely hard to find, so treat the figures as a useful ballpark rather than gospel.

What actually moves the needle here is how heavy-handed the uncle is with the sauce ladle and the peanut scoop. Ask for light sauce and less peanuts and you can realistically drop the plate down to around 200 calories. On the flip side, the Chinese-style version that comes with a stick of youtiao fried dough dunked in jumps to about 430 calories. The fruit portion itself barely changes things, so if you're watching numbers, your negotiation is entirely with the sauce and the peanuts. Order without youtiao, tell them to go light on the hae ko, and you've turned a 300-calorie snack into a 200-calorie one without feeling shortchanged.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less sauce. Seriously just say it. A light drizzle of hae ko instead of a full dunk can save you close to 60 to 70 calories on its own.
  • Skip the youtiao. The fried dough stick adds around 130 calories and is the single biggest upgrade to the calorie count.
  • Ask for less peanuts or skip them entirely. The 22g topping is adding 125 calories; even halving it makes a real difference.
  • Eat it as a proper snack, not a side dish stacked on top of a full meal. At 200 to 300 calories it fits fine as a standalone afternoon eat.
  • If you're making it at home, use a small measured amount of hae ko and go heavy on the sengkuang and cucumber since those are basically water with crunch and barely register calorically.

Common questions

How many calories in Rojak Buah?
A standard hawker plate of rojak buah is around 300 calories for a 250g serving. This is an estimate since hard data is thin for this dish, but it's a solid working figure. Light on sauce and peanuts brings it down to about 200 calories; add youtiao and you're looking at roughly 430 calories.
What exactly is rojak buah?
Rojak buah is a Malaysian mixed fruit and vegetable dish. You get a pile of fresh fruit (jambu air, mango, pineapple, cucumber, sengkuang) tossed in a thick, dark, sweet-savoury sauce made from hae ko (fermented prawn paste), tamarind, and sugar, then topped with crushed peanuts. It's tangy, sweet, a bit funky from the paste, and genuinely good. Common at pasar malam stalls and hawker centres across the country.
Is rojak buah healthy?
It's not bad, but it's not as light as it looks. The fruit base is fine but the hae ko sauce is high in sugar and the peanuts add a solid chunk of fat. At 300 calories it's a reasonable snack if you're keeping track. The main thing to watch is sodium from the prawn paste and the sugar load in the sauce, especially if you're having a big portion.
Berapa kalori dalam Rojak Buah?
Rojak Buah ada kira-kira 300 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (250g), dengan 7g protein, 42g karbohidrat dan 12g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
