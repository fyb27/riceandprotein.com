How Many Calories in Roti Canai?

Flaky fried flatbread. One plain piece is fine. The trouble starts at roti telur, roti tisu and the second piece.

301kcal / 1 plain piece + dhal
One plain roti canai with dhal is about 301 calories. The flour is only part of it. The real calories come from the margarine or ghee it is cooked and folded with.

The real talk

One plain roti canai is genuinely not a disaster. 301 calories with some dhal is a reasonable light meal. The problem is nobody stops at one, and nobody orders it plain. Roti telur, roti planta, roti tisu, roti bom, each upgrade is just more fat and sugar folded into the same dough.

The margarine is the part people miss. That flaky, layered texture comes from fat worked into the dough and more fat on the hot plate. The flour gives you carbs. The margarine gives you the calories. Two plain rotis plus a teh tarik is already a 750 calorie supper, and most people treat that as a light snack.

How to eat it lighter

  • One piece, not two. Pair it with dhal or sambal for some protein and fibre.
  • Skip roti telur, planta and tisu. Those are fat and sugar upgrades on the same dough.
  • Have it with teh o kosong instead of a condensed milk drink to save 100 plus calories.

Common questions

How many calories in one roti canai?
One plain roti canai is about 270 calories on its own, or roughly 301 with a serving of dhal. The margarine and ghee are the main calorie source, not the flour.
Is roti canai bad for weight loss?
One plain piece is fine. The problem is the upgrades (telur, planta, tisu) and ordering two or three, which quickly turns a light meal into a 700 plus calorie one.
Which roti is the lowest calorie?
Plain roti canai or roti kosong. Every upgrade (egg, margarine, sugar, condensed milk) adds fat or sugar on top of the same base dough.
