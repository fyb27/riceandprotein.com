How Many Calories in Roti Gluay?

Also known as โรตีกล้วย, Thai Banana Roti, Banana Pancake, Roti Kluay, Thai Roti.

Stretched dough fried on a flat griddle in a big knob of margarine, folded around sliced banana, then cut into squares and drowned in condensed milk and sugar. The night market dessert at every tourist strip and most local ones, 30 to 60 baht.

500kcal / 1 roti
A standard roti gluay, about 180g cut into squares, is roughly 500 calories. The MGR calorie table puts the egg, sugar and condensed milk version at 590. The banana is only 70 of it. The dough is 240, the margarine it fries in is 110 and the condensed milk and sugar on top add 85.

The real talk

Watch the cart for 30 seconds and you see where the calories are. The cook drops a lump of margarine the size of a golf ball on the griddle, and it all goes into the dough. That is 15g of pure fat, 110 calories, before the banana touches it. The dough itself is 80g of white flour made with oil and egg, another 240. Then the condensed milk goes on with a heavy hand, 20g for 65 calories, and a spoon of white sugar for 20 more. The banana is the healthiest thing on the plate and it is 14% of the total.

The number climbs fast with the extras. An egg cracked into the dough adds 75. Chocolate sauce or Nutella is 100 for the drizzle. Roti gluay kai with chocolate is 650 to 700 calories, more than a plate of pad kra pao with rice. It is a 10 minute walk to buy and 3 minutes to eat, and it comes after dinner, not instead of it.

A plain roti with nothing on it is about 350 and it is genuinely good, crisp and salty. If you want the banana, ask for no sugar on top (mai sai nam tan) and the vendor skips the white sugar and often goes lighter on the condensed milk too. That puts banana roti at about 430. Still a dessert, but 100 calories less than the default, and you did not have to give anything up that you would actually miss.

How to eat it lighter

  • Say mai sai nam tan (no sugar). The white sugar is pure calories and the condensed milk already makes it sweet.
  • Skip the egg. It sounds like protein but it adds 75 calories to something that is 90% dough and fat.
  • No chocolate sauce. That drizzle is 100 calories and covers the banana flavour anyway.
  • Share one. Half a banana roti is 250 and you get the full experience.
  • Plain roti at 350 is the lowest option on the cart and it is the version Thais eat most.

Common questions

How many calories in Thai banana roti?
About 500 for a standard one with condensed milk and sugar. With egg it is 590 and with chocolate sauce on top around 670.
Is roti gluay the same as roti canai?
Same dough family. Thai roti is fried in margarine and served sweet at 350 to 500 calories. Malaysian roti canai is about 300 plain and comes with curry.
Is banana roti healthy?
No. It is white flour, margarine, condensed milk and sugar, with a banana in the middle. A dessert at 500 calories.
What is the lowest calorie roti at a Thai cart?
Plain roti with a little condensed milk, about 350. Every filling and sauce adds 70 to 100 on top.
