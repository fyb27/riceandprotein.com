How Many Calories in Roti John?

One whole roti john is about 650 calories. The bread soaks up the egg and oil like a sponge.

650kcal / 1 whole
A whole roti john runs about 650 calories. It is a long bun split open, smeared with an egg and minced meat mix, then pressed face down onto an oily griddle so the bread fries and soaks everything up. Add the chilli and mayo drizzle on top and you get roughly 28g protein, 60g carbs and 33g fat. A loaded mutton version can push past 850.

The real talk

Roti john looks healthy because there is egg and meat in it, but bro the bread is the problem. That long bun gets pressed onto a griddle slicked with oil, face down, so it drinks up the fat like a sponge. By the time it is golden and crispy it has soaked in way more oil than you think.

Then they finish it with a zigzag of mayo and chilli sauce on top, which is another 100 plus calories of pure topping. One whole roti john is a proper meal, around 650, and the loaded mutton ones the hawkers do can hit 850. That is not a snack lah, that is dinner.

How to eat it lighter

  • Share a whole one or order a half. Half a roti john is a much more sensible 325 or so.
  • Skip the mayo drizzle on top and just have the chilli sauce. The mayo is pure add-on fat.
  • Ask them to go easy on the oil when they griddle it. Less oil in the bread is the single biggest saving.

Common questions

How many calories in a roti john?
A whole standard roti john is around 650 calories. The bread soaks up the griddle oil, and the mayo drizzle on top adds even more. Mutton versions can hit 850.
Is roti john a healthy breakfast?
Not really. The egg and meat give you protein, but the long bun fried in oil and topped with mayo makes it a heavy, fatty meal. Treat it as one full meal, not a light start.
How do I make roti john lower calorie?
Order a half instead of a whole, skip the mayo drizzle, and ask for less oil on the griddle. That can easily cut it down by 200 or more.
Berapa kalori dalam Roti John?
Roti John ada kira-kira 650 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (280g), dengan 28g protein, 60g karbohidrat dan 33g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
