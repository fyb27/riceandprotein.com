How Many Calories in Sabah Beef Noodles (Ngiu Chap)?

Also known as Ngiu Chap, Ngiu Chap Sabah.

Sabah's beef noodle soup, loaded with beef, beef balls and offal. High protein, and the clear broth keeps it lean.

520kcal / 1 bowl (soup)
A bowl of Sabah beef noodles (ngiu chap) is around 520 calories with a big 38g of protein. The clear beef broth keeps the fat moderate, and the beef and offal make it one of the higher protein noodle bowls going.

The real talk

Ngiu chap is Sabah's beef noodle soup, a bowl loaded with beef slices, springy beef balls, tripe, tendon and sometimes liver, in a clear or herbal beef broth over your choice of noodles. For your goals this is actually a smart bowl, around 520 calories but with a massive 38g of protein and moderate fat, because the broth is clear rather than oily.

The protein per calorie here beats almost every other noodle dish on this site. The versions to watch are the herbal braised broth with fattier cuts and the dry tossed version, which climb toward 620. But the standard clear soup bowl fits the high protein, high volume eating angle perfectly.

How to eat it lighter

  • Choose the clear soup broth over the richer herbal or dry tossed versions.
  • Load up on the lean beef slices and beef balls for protein.
  • Go easy on the fattier offal cuts if you are watching fat.
