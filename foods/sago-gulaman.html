How Many Calories in Sago't Gulaman?

Also known as Sago at Gulaman, Samalamig, Palamig, Sago Gulaman Drink.

Brown sugar syrup (arnibal) flavoured with banana essence or pandan, poured over ice with tapioca pearls and cubes of gulaman jelly. The 15 to 25 peso drink from the samalamig cart on every corner.

200kcal / 1 large cup (about 400ml)
A large 400ml cup of sago't gulaman is about 200 calories. The arnibal syrup is about 150 of that, the sago pearls about 35 and the gulaman cubes about 10. The ice and water are free. Kawaling Pinoy's recipe card lands at 248 per serving with a heavier syrup pour.

The real talk

Sago't gulaman is brown sugar water with chewy things in it, and the calories follow the sugar. A vendor ladles about 40g of arnibal (brown sugar boiled with water and a drop of banana extract) into a large cup, which is 150 calories. The sago pearls are cooked tapioca starch, about 30g at 35 calories. The gulaman is agar jelly set with a little sugar, about 40g at 10 calories. Then ice and water to the top. Kawaling Pinoy and Panlasang Pinoy both land around 250 per serving, with a cup of sugar for 6 servings.

The cart drinks are all the same sugar in different colours. Melon juice, buko pandan, black gulaman, the sago't gulaman with a squeeze of calamansi: 150 to 250 per large cup, and the difference is mostly how heavy the vendor's hand is. A large cup is about the same as a can of Coke (140) plus a bit, and people drink 2 in an afternoon because it is 20 pesos and it is hot.

The mall versions with cream or condensed milk are a step up. A large buko pandan with cream is about 280, and the 'special' sago't gulaman with extra sago and a milk float is around 300. Sago't gulaman is a fine drink to have instead of a soft drink, but it is not water with jelly in it. It is 49g of carbs, all of it sugar and starch.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for konting arnibal (less syrup). A vendor will halve it and the cup drops to about 120.
  • Small cup instead of large. About 120 versus 200, and it is the same drink.
  • Skip the extra sago. Each spoon of pearls is about 20 calories of starch.
  • If you want the cold sweet hit, a calamansi juice with less sugar is about 80 for a large cup.

Common questions

Is sago't gulaman healthy?
No, it is sugar water with tapioca and jelly. About 200 calories and 45g of sugar in a large cup. The gulaman itself is nearly free; the arnibal is the problem.
How many calories in the sago alone?
About 35 calories for the 30g of cooked pearls in a large cup. Sago is pure starch, about 120 calories per 100g cooked.
Is sago't gulaman lower in calories than Coke?
Not really. A large cup is about 200 calories versus 140 for a 330ml can of Coke. With less syrup it drops to 120, which is when it becomes the better choice.
How many calories in gulaman?
Almost none. Gulaman is agar, about 5 to 10 calories per 40g cube portion once the sugar in the jelly is counted. It is the syrup and sago around it that add up.
