How Many Calories in Sai Krok Isan?

Also known as ไส้กรอกอีสาน, Isan Sausage, Thai Fermented Sausage, Sai Krok Isaan, Sai Krok.

Sour fermented pork sausage from Isan, minced pork mixed with cooked rice, garlic and pork fat, stuffed into casings as small balls and left to sour for a day before it hits the charcoal. Eaten with raw cabbage, ginger, bird's eye chilli and peanuts.

300kcal / 5 balls (1 skewer)
A skewer of 5 grilled sai krok isan balls, about 100g, is roughly 300 calories, or 60 per ball. About half of that is pork fat. The rice inside adds 35 and the lean pork about 130. The cabbage and chilli it comes with are free, the peanuts are not.

The real talk

Sai krok is a fat delivery system with a sour flavour. The recipe is roughly 3 parts pork to 1 part pork fat to 1 part rice, and the fat is what makes it juicy after the sourness. Per ball that is about 8g of lean pork at 20 calories, 3g of fat at 26 calories, and a spoon of rice at 7. Sixty a ball. Five on a skewer is 300, the same as a bowl of rice, and it is gone in 2 minutes at the bus stop.

The MGR table lists one sai krok at 90 calories, which is the longer single sausage some stalls sell, about 30g. The 5 ball skewer at 20g each lands at the same number per gram. What changes the count is the grill. Balls that sit long enough for the fat to render and drip lose 10 to 15 calories each. The ones served pale and quick keep it all. Pick the charred skewer.

The sides are the smart part of this snack. Raw cabbage, sliced ginger and bird's eye chilli cost nothing and slow you down. The roasted peanuts are about 6 calories each and a normal handful is 20 of them, so 120 more. Two skewers with peanuts is 720 calories standing on a pavement. One skewer with the cabbage and no peanuts is 300 and you feel like you ate something.

How to eat it lighter

  • One skewer, not two. The second is 300 calories and you only bought it because it was 10 baht.
  • Ask for the well charred balls. Rendered fat that dripped into the coals is fat you did not eat, about 50 calories a skewer.
  • Eat the cabbage and ginger it comes with. Free volume, and it slows you down.
  • Leave the peanuts or take 5. A handful of 20 is 120 calories on top of the sausage.
  • Sai krok with sticky rice is 540 calories. Sai krok with cabbage is 300. Choose based on whether it is a meal or a snack.

Common questions

How many calories in one sai krok isan ball?
About 60 for a 20g grilled ball. Half of it is pork fat.
Is sai krok isan safe to eat?
The fermentation is short, about a day, and it is grilled through. Buy from a busy stall with high turnover and eat it hot.
Is sai krok isan high in protein?
Moderate. About 14g of protein per 5 ball skewer, against 18g of fat. It is more of a fat snack than a protein one.
What do you eat sai krok isan with?
Raw cabbage, fresh ginger, bird's eye chilli and roasted peanuts. The vegetables are free, the peanuts add about 120 for a handful.
