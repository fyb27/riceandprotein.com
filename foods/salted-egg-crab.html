How Many Calories in Salted Egg Crab?

Half a mud crab drowning in rich salted egg butter sauce, where the sauce does most of the calorie work.

500kcal / 1 serving (about half a crab)
One serving of Salted Egg Crab (about half a mud crab, roughly 320g with shell) comes in at around 500 calories. That number sounds manageable until you realise the crab meat itself is only about 115 calories, and the other 385 calories are basically the sauce: butter, salted egg yolks, oil. So yes, you are essentially eating a very delicious stick of butter that happens to have a crab in it.

The real talk

Honestly, 500 calories for half a crab sounds almost reasonable for a restaurant dish. But let's be real about what's in there. The actual crab meat, about 120g edible, is only 115 calories. The other 385 calories are butter (180 cal), salted egg yolks (110 cal), and cooking oil (65 cal). You're basically paying restaurant prices to eat fat with a crab-shaped delivery mechanism. That said, 26g of protein is genuinely good, and if you're the type who orders this at dinner and keeps the rest of the day clean, it does fit. This is a treat dish, not a daily lauk. Also worth flagging: these numbers are a ballpark estimate, because there is very little hard nutritional data for Malaysian restaurant dishes like this. The actual calorie count swings a lot depending on how heavy-handed the kitchen is with butter and how big the crab is.

What actually changes the maths is the sauce volume. A hawker stall doing smaller crabs with a thinner sauce coat can land at 380 calories. A restaurant that drowns the crab in thick creamy salted egg sauce and sends you a whole 1kg crab solo is looking at 950 calories. The crab itself barely moves the needle, bro. It's all about how much sauce ends up on your plate. If you're eating this with mantou buns and scooping up every last bit of sauce, you could easily add another 300 calories to your count without realising. The version you order at a Chinese seafood restaurant in KL or Penang is almost always the heavier end. A good rule: eat the crab, go light on the sauce mopping.

How to eat it lighter

  • Skip the mantou buns entirely. Each deep-fried bun adds 150 to 200 calories and people use them to soak up sauce, which doubles your sauce intake without thinking.
  • Ask for less butter in the sauce if you're at a restaurant that does custom orders. A thinner sauce coat cuts maybe 80 to 100 calories per serving.
  • Share the dish properly. Salted Egg Crab is meant to be shared among 3 to 4 people. If you eat half a crab solo you're treating a shared dish like a personal main.
  • Load up on the plain steamed vegetables or plain rice on the table first. You'll eat less of the sauce by the time the crab arrives.
  • Pick the crab over other salted egg dishes like salted egg prawns or salted egg fish skin. At least with crab you're getting 26g of protein for the calorie spend.

Common questions

How many calories in Salted Egg Crab?
A standard restaurant serving of half a mud crab with salted egg butter sauce is around 500 calories. Hawker-style with a smaller crab and lighter sauce can drop to about 380 calories, while a whole 1kg crab ordered solo is closer to 950 calories.
Is Salted Egg Crab high in fat?
Yes, very. About 40g of fat per half-crab serving, almost all of it coming from butter and salted egg yolks in the sauce. The crab meat itself is actually lean, around 115 calories for 120g. It's the sauce that makes this a high-fat dish.
Can I eat Salted Egg Crab on a diet?
You can, but you have to be honest with yourself about the calories. At 500 calories for half a crab, it fits into a calorie deficit if the rest of your day is light. The protein from the crab (26g) is solid. Just do not go scooping up extra sauce with mantou buns on top.
Berapa kalori dalam Salted Egg Crab?
Salted Egg Crab ada kira-kira 500 kalori untuk 1 hidangan (320g), dengan 26g protein, 7g karbohidrat dan 40g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
