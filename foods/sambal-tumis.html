How Many Calories in Sambal Tumis?

Chilli paste fried in oil. Small portion, but oil dense. The chilli is free, the oil and sugar are not.

150kcal / 2-3 tablespoons
A serving of sambal tumis (2 to 3 tablespoons, around 40g) is about 150 calories. The chilli itself is nearly free. The calories come from the oil it is fried in and the sugar used to balance it.

The real talk

Sambal is sneaky because the portion looks tiny but it is oil dense. That deep red glossy look? That is oil. A couple of tablespoons of sambal tumis is around 150 calories, almost all of it from the frying oil and the sugar that balances the heat. The chilli, shallots and tamarind are basically free.

It is a condiment, so the damage depends entirely on how much you scoop. A little dab to lift your rice is fine. Half a plate of sambal because it is so good, plus petai or ikan bilis on top, and you have quietly added 300 calories to your meal. Spicy does not mean low calorie bro, never has.

How to eat it lighter

  • Use it as a dab to flavour rice, not as a main scoop. The oil is concentrated.
  • Sambal with ikan bilis adds a little protein. Sambal goreng jawa is the heaviest.
  • Remember spicy is not the same as light. The heat is free, the oil is not.

Common questions

How many calories in sambal tumis?
About 150 calories for a 2 to 3 tablespoon serving. Most of it is the oil it is fried in and the sugar, not the chilli.
Is sambal fattening?
It is oil dense, so a small dab is fine but big scoops add up fast. Spicy does not mean low calorie. The heat is free but the frying oil and sugar are not.
Which sambal is highest in calories?
Sambal goreng jawa tends to be heaviest at around 200 per serving because it is sweeter and uses more oil. Sambal petai is around 190. Plain sambal tumis and sambal ikan bilis are around 140 to 150.
Berapa kalori dalam Sambal Tumis?
Sambal Tumis ada kira-kira 150 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (40g), dengan 3g protein, 8g karbohidrat dan 12g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
