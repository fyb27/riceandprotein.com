How Many Calories in Sambal Udang?

Spicy sambal-coated prawns, a staple lauk at any nasi campur counter, where the prawns are lean but the oil in the sambal does most of the calorie work.

300kcal / 1 plate (hawker/kopitiam portion, ~8-10 medium prawns)
Sambal Udang at a hawker stall comes in at around 300 calories for a standard plate of 8-10 medium prawns. The prawns themselves are only about 115 calories, but the sambal paste and cooking oil push the total up to 300 kcal. Not bad for a lauk, actually, considering you're getting 22g of protein out of it.

The real talk

Sambal Udang is actually one of the better lauk options at the nasi campur counter, bro. A full hawker plate sits around 300 calories, which sounds okay until you realise that most of those calories, about 160 of them, come from the cooking oil alone. The prawns are 115 kcal, the sambal paste is only 20 kcal. So the dish itself is not the problem; it's the wok technique and how heavy-handed the cook is with the oil. To be honest, these are ballpark estimates because there's basically no solid published calorie data for Malaysian hawker sambal udang. Different cooks, different oil amounts, different prawn sizes. Take the 300 figure as a reasonable middle ground, not a hard measurement.

The thing that really changes the maths is portion size and how much gravy you take. A small nasi campur scoop can be as low as 190 calories if you get a lighter ladle. Ask the aunty for 'kurang kuah' and you drop maybe 40 to 60 kcal straight away. On the other end, if you go butter prawn or cereal prawn, you're looking at 420 calories because those are deep-fried in batter with a butter-egg coating. Udang goreng tepung is even worse at 480 calories due to the batter absorbing oil. Stick to sambal and you keep the protein-to-calorie ratio decent, 22g protein at 300 kcal is genuinely not bad for a lauk.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less sambal gravy at nasi campur, you can shave off 30 to 50 calories just by reducing the oil-heavy sauce.
  • Order the small nasi campur scoop instead of a full plate, that drops you from 300 to around 190 calories.
  • Skip the butter prawn or cereal prawn versions, those are 420-480 calories and the frying adds a lot of fat fast.
  • Shell-on prawns mean you naturally eat slower and feel fuller on less, so embrace the peeling process.
  • If you cook at home, use a non-stick pan and measure your oil to 1 teaspoon instead of eyeballing it from the bottle.

Common questions

How many calories in Sambal Udang?
A standard hawker or kopitiam plate of Sambal Udang with 8-10 medium prawns is around 300 calories. A smaller nasi campur scoop is closer to 190 calories. The prawn meat itself is lean; most of the calories come from the oil used to fry the sambal.
Is Sambal Udang good for weight loss?
It can be, honestly. Prawns are high protein and low calorie. The issue is the oil in the sambal, which adds up fast. If you ask for less gravy or less sambal sauce, you can keep it under 250 calories. Pair with plain rice and some veggies and it's a solid meal.
How many calories in Sambal Udang Petai?
Sambal Udang Petai (with bitter beans) is roughly 280 calories for a standard portion. The petai itself adds maybe 30 kcal, but the dish is often cooked with extra oil, so the total still lands a bit below a full hawker plate at around 280 calories.
Berapa kalori dalam Sambal Udang?
Sambal Udang ada kira-kira 300 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (180g), dengan 22g protein, 10g karbohidrat dan 19g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
