How Many Calories in Samgyeopsal?

Also known as 삼겹살, Korean Grilled Pork Belly, Samgyupsal, Samgyeopsal-gui.

Samgyeopsal is thick-cut pork belly grilled at the table and eaten wrapped in lettuce with garlic, ssamjang and kimchi. One 1인분 order is 150g to 200g of raw meat.

630kcal / 1 serving (1인분, 200g raw belly, grilled, with ssam)
A standard 200g serving of samgyeopsal is about 630 calories after grilling, based on the fatsecret.kr figure of 349 kcal per 100g for grilled belly. Around 52g of that is fat, because pork belly is roughly one third fat by weight. The lettuce, garlic and ssamjang add less than 50 calories on top.

The real talk

Pork belly is about 33 percent fat, so a 200g order starts at 660 calories raw. Some of that drips into the grill tray, which is why the grilled number sits closer to 630. The problem is nobody eats one order. Two people at a samgyeopsal restaurant usually put away 3 servings between them, which is 950 calories each before the rice, the doenjang jjigae and the drinks.

The wrap is nearly free. A lettuce leaf, a perilla leaf, a clove of grilled garlic and a smear of ssamjang come to about 20 calories per ssam. Eating 10 wraps costs you 200 calories of vegetables and sauce, and it slows you down, which is the real benefit. The bowl of rice (300 calories) and the fried rice at the end (about 450) are where the meal gets out of hand.

At 15,000 to 18,000 won per 1인분 in Seoul now, samgyeopsal is not cheap protein either. 36g of protein for 630 calories is a poor ratio compared with the 목살 (pork neck) on the same menu, which gives you about 45g protein for 430 calories per 200g.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order 목살 (pork neck) for half the table instead of all belly. 200g of neck is about 430 calories versus 630 for belly.
  • Let the fat render fully and keep the meat at the edge of the grill so it drips, not sits in oil. That is easily 50 calories saved per serving.
  • Wrap everything. A ssam with lettuce, perilla and garlic is about 20 calories and makes you eat slower.
  • Skip the fried rice at the end. That 볶음밥 is around 450 calories and you are already full.
  • Grill the kimchi and mushrooms in the fat instead of ordering a second serving of meat.

Common questions

How many calories in 1인분 of samgyeopsal?
About 630 for a 200g serving after grilling. Shops that serve 150g portions come in closer to 480.
Is samgyeopsal good for weight loss?
Not really. It is about 33 percent fat, so 52g of fat per serving. If you want grilled pork on a diet, moksal (neck) has half the fat for the same weight.
Does grilling remove fat from pork belly?
Some. Rendering drops the raw 660 calories to roughly 630 for a 200g serving. It does not turn belly into a lean cut.
How many calories in a full samgyeopsal meal?
Two servings of meat, a bowl of rice, jjigae and half a bottle of soju is around 1,700 calories. That is a full day for many people.
