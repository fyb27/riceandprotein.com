How Many Calories in Samosa?

Also known as Samosas, Aloo Samosa.

A deep-fried triangular pastry with spiced potato and pea filling, a mamak staple that looks small but packs a solid calorie punch thanks to the oil-heavy shell.

210kcal / 1 piece
One samosa (about 70g) comes in at around 210 calories, with 4g protein, 24g carbs, and 11g fat. That's for the standard vegetable version with potato and pea filling. The chicken version sits a bit higher at roughly 220 calories. These are ballpark estimates, not lab numbers, so take them as a good guide rather than gospel.

The real talk

Samosa is one of those snacks that feels innocent because it's small. It's a triangle of fried dough stuffed with spiced potato and peas, super common at mamak stalls and pasar malam. You grab one for RM1.50 and think, okay small snack, cannot be that bad. But that little triangle is 210 calories, and here's why: the maida pastry shell contributes 90 calories and the absorbed frying oil adds another 65 on top of that. The actual potato filling? Only 55 calories. So you're basically paying the calorie price for pastry and oil, not the vegetables inside. The filling is almost the healthy part. Worth knowing, not worth stressing over, but worth knowing.

The honest maths problem with samosas is how fast they stack up. One piece at 210 calories is fine. But nobody eats just one, kan? Two pieces is 420 calories before you've even looked at your teh tarik. Chicken samosa nudges it to around 220 calories per piece, roughly the same range. The oil absorption is the big variable here since freshly fried samosas that have been sitting in the oil longer will soak up more than a well-drained one. If you're ordering at a stall, try to grab one that came out recently and has been left to drain a bit. There's no meaningful way to make samosa low-calorie because the deep-frying is the whole point, but knowing it's 210 a piece means you can budget for it instead of being surprised later. Also worth flagging: all the numbers here are estimates. Hard data on Malaysian mamak snacks is thin, so treat these figures as a solid ballpark rather than exact science.

How to eat it lighter

  • Eat one, not three. Sounds obvious but two samosas plus teh tarik is already a 600-calorie snack session before you've had lunch.
  • Pair it with something that has actual protein and volume, like a bowl of clear soup or a plate of ulam, so you're not hungry again in 45 minutes.
  • If you're at a stall with options, pick the freshly fried batch and let it drain on the rack for a minute. Samosas that have been sitting in oil absorb noticeably more.
  • Chicken samosa at 220 calories gives you slightly more protein than the vegetable version, which makes it marginally better value calorie-for-calorie if you can find a good one.
  • Skip the extra chutney dips. Green chutney is fine, but the sweet tamarind chutney adds sugar calories fast and you barely notice it.

Common questions

How many calories in Samosa?
One samosa (70g) is about 210 calories. That breaks down to roughly 90 calories from the fried pastry shell, 55 from the spiced potato and pea filling, and 65 from absorbed frying oil. Chicken samosa is slightly higher at around 220 calories.
What exactly is a samosa?
It's a deep-fried triangular pastry, super common at mamak stalls across Malaysia. The outside is a thin, crispy shell made from maida (white flour), and the inside is usually spiced potato with peas. Chicken samosa uses spiced minced chicken instead. Served hot, usually with chutney on the side.
Is samosa a healthy snack?
Not really, but not a disaster either. The problem is that most of the calories come from the oil-fried pastry, not the filling. One piece fits into a normal day fine. The issue is portion control, because they're small, cheap, and easy to eat three before you notice.
Berapa kalori dalam Samosa?
Samosa ada kira-kira 210 kalori untuk 1 keping (70g), dengan 4g protein, 24g karbohidrat dan 11g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
