How Many Calories in Sata (Terengganu)?

2 sticks of sata are about 230 kcal with 14g protein. Grilled fish, not fried, surprisingly solid.

230kcal / 2 sticks
Two sticks of sata land around 230 calories with a respectable 14g of protein. It is spiced fish meat mixed with grated coconut, wrapped in banana leaf and grilled over charcoal. Because it is grilled not fried, the calories come mostly from the fish and coconut rather than soaked oil, which makes it one of the better snack choices here.

The real talk

Sata is actually one of the smarter picks on this list, bro. It is fish, it is grilled over charcoal, and 2 sticks give you 14g of real protein. The coconut adds fat so it is not zero calorie, but you are getting actual nutrition, not just fried air.

The thing to watch is the grated coconut, that is where the fat comes from, about 16g across two sticks. Still, compared to muruku or kacang putih, sata fills you up and feeds your muscles. If you are choosing a snack on the east coast, this one is a green light.

How to eat it lighter

  • It is grilled already, so just go easy on any sweet dipping sauce on the side
  • Pair 2 sticks with cut cucumber or ulam instead of rice if you want it lighter
  • Choose sticks with more fish and less coconut paste if the stall lets you see

Common questions

How many calories in sata?
About 115 calories per stick, so 230 for two. It varies with how much coconut is packed in.
Is sata good for weight loss?
Pretty good actually. It is grilled fish with 14g protein per 2 sticks, so it is filling and not fried. The coconut adds fat but it is still a smart snack.
Is sata high in protein?
Yes, around 14g for 2 sticks since it is mostly fish. That is one of the highest protein snacks in this category.
Berapa kalori dalam Sata?
Sata ada kira-kira 230 kalori untuk 2 cucuk (90g), dengan 14g protein, 8g karbohidrat dan 16g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
