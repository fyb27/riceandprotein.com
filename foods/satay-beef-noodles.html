How Many Calories in Satay Beef Noodles?

Also known as 沙嗲牛肉麵, 沙爹牛肉公仔麵, Sa De Ngau Yuk Min, Satay Beef Instant Noodles, Cha Chaan Teng Satay Beef.

Satay beef noodles are instant noodles in broth topped with sliced beef stir fried in a peanut and shrimp based satay sauce. It is the most ordered cha chaan teng breakfast in Hong Kong, about HK$35 to HK$45 with a drink.

670kcal / 1 bowl
One bowl of satay beef noodles is around 670 calories, based on a Hong Kong dietitian's tally of cha chaan teng breakfasts (667 kcal). About 470 calories is the fried instant noodle cake, 120 is the beef, and the satay sauce and oil add another 80. Fat is about 35g.

The real talk

The beef is not the problem. A cha chaan teng gives you about 70g of sliced beef, roughly 120 calories and 18g of protein. The instant noodles are the problem: one cake of 公仔麵 or 出前一丁 is deep fried at the factory and carries about 470 calories and 18g of fat before it touches water. That is the same as a bo lo yau and a half, sitting at the bottom of your breakfast bowl.

Satay sauce is the second hit. Hong Kong satay sauce is peanut, dried shrimp, shallot and a lot of oil. The kitchen fries the beef in it, then spoons more over the noodles, so you get around 80 calories of sauce and most of it is fat. One dietitian who ranked cha chaan teng items said satay beef noodles should be a once a week thing at most, and at 667 calories with about 1,300mg sodium, that is reasonable advice.

This is a breakfast, remember. 670 calories at 8am, with a milk tea (110) and the buttered toast that comes in the set (250), is over 1,000 calories before you sit down at work. For most people trying to lose weight, that is half the day gone. The same beef on rice vermicelli in clear soup would be about 350.

How to eat it lighter

  • Swap the instant noodles for rice vermicelli (米粉) or flat rice noodles (河粉). Saves about 250 calories.
  • Ask for the satay sauce on the side or less sauce (少醬). Saves 40 to 60 calories.
  • Skip the buttered toast in the set. That is 250 calories on its own.
  • Order it as a lunch instead of breakfast if you are going to have it, and go lighter at dinner.
  • Drink half the soup. The satay broth is where a lot of the sodium and oil settle.

Common questions

How many calories in satay beef noodles?
About 670 calories per bowl, according to a Hong Kong registered dietitian's cha chaan teng breakfast ranking. Some estimates run to 700 or 800 for bigger bowls.
Why are satay beef noodles so high in calories?
The instant noodle cake is deep fried at the factory and carries about 470 calories on its own. The satay sauce is oil based and adds another 80.
Is satay beef noodles healthy for breakfast?
Not really. It has good protein (28g) but 35g of fat and about 1,300mg sodium. Swapping to rice vermicelli brings it down to around 420 calories.
