How Many Calories in Sate Kambing?

Also known as Sate Kambing Tegal, Sate Kambing Muda, Sate Kambing Balibul, Goat Satay.

Sate kambing is young goat cut into chunks with the fat left on, grilled over charcoal and served with kecap manis, sliced shallot, tomato, cabbage and rawit. Tegal and Solo argue about who does it best.

370kcal / 10 skewers with kecap sambal (no rice or lontong)
Ten skewers of sate kambing with the kecap and shallot dressing come to about 370 calories. FatSecret Indonesia lists one skewer at 32 calories and the meat at 216 calories per 100g, so ten skewers of meat is about 320, and the kecap manis and sambal add 50. With lontong or rice it is about 620.

The real talk

Goat sate is fattier than it looks because the Tegal style deliberately alternates lean meat with a cube of fat on the skewer. That is the point of it. FatSecret puts the meat at 216 calories per 100g with 57% of it from fat. A skewer is 15 to 20g so it is about 32 calories, and ten skewers is 320. There is no peanut sauce, which is why ten goat skewers cost about the same as ten sate ayam with sauce.

The kecap manis dressing is not zero. The sweet soy pooled on the plate with sliced shallot, tomato and rawit is about 50 calories per plate, and people mop it up with the lontong. Lontong is 145 per piece and most places serve two, so the Rp 35,000 to 50,000 sate kambing meal with lontong is around 620 calories. With rice instead it is about 630.

Sate kambing muda (young goat) and balibul (under five months) are leaner than older goat, about 10% lower per skewer, and more tender, which is why the price is higher. Sate buntel, the Solo version with minced goat wrapped in caul fat, is much heavier at about 90 calories per skewer because the fat wrapping melts into the meat.

The protein is the reason to order this over most fried lauk: 28g in ten skewers. Goat also has less fat than lamb of the same cut. The cholesterol reputation is real but it is about portion. Ten skewers at dinner is fine; the Idul Adha day where you eat thirty skewers, then tongseng, then gulai, is not.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for 'daging semua, tanpa gajih'. Skewers without the fat cube are about 25 calories each instead of 32.
  • One lontong, not two. That is 145 calories saved and ten skewers is enough protein without it.
  • Kecap on the side and dip lightly. Most of the 50 calories of dressing is sugar from the sweet soy.
  • Eat the raw cabbage, tomato and shallot. They are free and they are what keeps goat sate from being all meat.
  • Skip the gulai or tongseng that comes as a set. Sate plus a bowl of gulai kambing is 700 before rice.

Common questions

How many calories in sate kambing?
About 32 calories per skewer, so 320 for ten skewers of meat and about 370 with the kecap dressing. With two lontong it is around 660.
Is sate kambing healthier than sate ayam?
Per skewer the goat is fattier, but sate ayam comes drowned in peanut sauce. Ten sate kambing with kecap is about 370; ten sate ayam with sauce is about 500. Goat wins on protein too.
Does sate kambing raise cholesterol?
Goat meat has less fat and cholesterol than beef or lamb per 100g. The issue is the fat cubes on the skewer and the portion size at celebrations, not the meat itself.
How many calories in sate buntel?
About 90 calories per skewer, nearly three times a normal goat skewer, because the minced meat is wrapped in caul fat that melts in.
Berapa kalori Sate Kambing?
Sate Kambing mengandung sekitar 370 kalori per satu porsi biasa (170g), dengan 28g protein, 8g karbohidrat dan 25g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
