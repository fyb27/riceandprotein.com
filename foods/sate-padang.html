How Many Calories in Sate Padang?

Also known as Satai Padang, Sate Padang Pariaman, Sate Padang Panjang.

Sate Padang is beef, tongue or offal skewers from West Sumatra, boiled in spices then grilled, served under a thick yellow or red sauce made from rice flour and stock, with ketupat and fried shallots.

500kcal / 1 plate (10 skewers, thick sauce, 1 ketupat)
A plate of sate Padang with 10 skewers, sauce and one ketupat is about 500 calories. FatSecret Indonesia lists the meat at 24 calories per skewer and 161 per 100g, so 10 skewers is about 240. The thick sauce adds about 120 and the ketupat 145.

The real talk

The meat on sate Padang is actually lean. It is beef, tongue and sometimes tripe or heart, boiled in spiced stock first and then briefly grilled without the coconut or peanut marinade that Javanese sate uses. One skewer is around 24 calories and 2.7g protein. Ten skewers is 240 calories and 27g protein, which is a better ratio than almost any other street meat.

The sauce is the surprise. That thick yellow gravy (or red in the Pariaman style) is the boiling stock thickened with rice flour, so it is starch, not fat. A 100g ladle is about 120 calories. It is poured generously and it is what you mop up with the ketupat, and one ketupat is 145 calories. So the meat is less than half the plate.

At Rp 20,000 to 30,000 for ten sticks in Jakarta or Padang, it is a decent evening meal at 500 calories with 30g protein. The version with two ketupat is 645. The kerupuk kulit (fried cow skin) that some carts add on top is another 90 for one big piece, and it goes soggy in the sauce so you eat it fast.

Sate Padang Panjang (yellow, more turmeric) and Pariaman (red, more chili) are about the same in calories. The Padang Kota version uses a darker, thinner sauce with less flour and comes out about 40 calories lighter per plate.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less sauce. Half the gravy saves 60 calories and the skewers are already seasoned from the boil.
  • One ketupat, not two. Each is 145 calories and it is just compressed rice.
  • Ask for daging (beef) skewers rather than lidah (tongue), which is fattier.
  • Skip the kerupuk kulit. One piece is about 90 calories and it is pure fried skin.
  • Order 15 skewers with one ketupat instead of 10 with two. More protein, same calories.

Common questions

How many calories in sate Padang?
About 500 calories for a plate of 10 skewers with sauce and one ketupat. The skewers alone are about 24 calories each.
Is sate Padang healthier than sate ayam?
The meat is leaner, since it is boiled and grilled without peanut sauce. Ten sate Padang skewers are about 240 calories versus roughly 500 for ten chicken sate with peanut sauce.
What is the sate Padang sauce made of?
Beef stock thickened with rice flour and spices. It is about 120 calories per 100g ladle, mostly from the starch, not fat.
How many calories in one sate Padang skewer?
About 24 calories and 2.7g protein per skewer of meat, before sauce.
Berapa kalori Sate Padang?
Sate Padang mengandung sekitar 500 kalori per 1 piring (350g), dengan 30g protein, 52g karbohidrat dan 18g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
