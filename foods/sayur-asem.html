How Many Calories in Sayur Asem?

Also known as Sayur Asam, Sayur Asem Betawi, Sayur Asem Sunda, Tamarind Vegetable Soup.

Sayur asem is a sour tamarind broth with young jackfruit, corn, long beans, chayote, melinjo and peanuts. It is served next to fried fish and sambal at Sundanese and Betawi warungs and on every warteg counter.

80kcal / 1 bowl
A bowl of sayur asem is about 80 calories. FatSecret Indonesia lists a 240g bowl at 80 calories with 3g fat, 13g carbs and 3g protein. There is no coconut milk in it, so the broth is close to zero and almost all the calories are in the corn, jackfruit and peanuts.

The real talk

This is the soup you want to be greedy with. The broth is water, tamarind, galangal, a little terasi and palm sugar, and a 200ml ladle of it is under 15 calories. Everything else in the bowl is vegetables. The corn cob piece is the biggest single item at about 30 calories, the young jackfruit is 15, the chayote and long beans together are 10, and the melinjo and raw peanuts are 15 to 20 depending on how many the cook threw in.

Compare that to sayur lodeh, which is the same vegetables in coconut milk at about 160 for the same bowl, or gulai nangka at 200 plus. A warteg will put either in the same size ladle for the same Rp 3,000 to 5,000, and the sayur asem version is half the calories. If you are trying to fill up on volume at a warteg, this is the ladle to ask for twice.

The Sundanese version served with ikan asin, tempe and sambal terasi is where the meal gets its calories, not the soup. A plate of rice at 260, a piece of ikan asin at 80, tempe goreng at 95 and the sayur asem at 80 is a 515 calorie lunch, and the soup is the smallest part of it. The Betawi version with more peanuts and a heavier hand on the palm sugar runs about 100 per bowl.

The one warning is the sugar. Some warungs sweeten sayur asem hard, and a bowl with 2 teaspoons of gula merah in the ladle is 110, not 80. It still does not matter much, but if it tastes like dessert that is why.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for two ladles. At 80 calories a bowl, doubling the soup is the cheapest way to make a warteg plate feel like more.
  • Use it to replace half the rice. A bowl of sayur asem and 100g rice is 210 calories, against 260 for a plain full plate of rice.
  • If the broth tastes sweet, ask for the version 'jangan terlalu manis'. Saves about 30 calories of palm sugar.
  • Leave the melinjo seeds if you are counting closely. They are the densest thing in the bowl at about 5 calories each.
  • Pair it with ikan bakar or a boiled egg, not the fried ikan asin, and the whole meal stays under 500.

Common questions

How many calories in sayur asem?
About 80 calories per 240g bowl according to FatSecret Indonesia, with only 3g fat. Most of that is corn and jackfruit, not the broth.
Is sayur asem good for diet?
Yes. It is one of the lowest calorie dishes in Indonesian food, no coconut milk, mostly vegetables and water. Eat as much of it as the warung will give you.
Sayur asem or sayur lodeh, which is lower in calories?
Sayur asem by a lot. About 80 calories a bowl against 160 for sayur lodeh, because lodeh is cooked in coconut milk.
Does sayur asem have sugar?
A little. Most recipes use a teaspoon or two of palm sugar per pot to balance the tamarind. A sweet warung version can have 8 to 10g of sugar per bowl.
Berapa kalori Sayur Asem?
Sayur Asem mengandung sekitar 80 kalori per 1 mangkuk (240g), dengan 3g protein, 13g karbohidrat dan 3g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
