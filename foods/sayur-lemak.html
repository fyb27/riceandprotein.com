How Many Calories in Sayur Lemak?

Vegetables cooked in coconut milk gravy, where the santan does most of the calorie heavy lifting.

190kcal / 1 hawker serving
A standard hawker serving of Sayur Lemak (about 180g) comes in at roughly 190 calories. Most of that is from the santan, not the vegetables. The coconut milk alone accounts for about 125 of those calories, which is the trade-off you make every time you scoop up that rich, yellowish gravy. As a veg side dish, 190 calories is moderate, though it can swing quite a bit depending on how heavy-handed the cook is with the coconut milk.

The real talk

Sayur Lemak is one of those dishes that tricks you into thinking you're eating healthy because, hey, it's vegetables. And technically you are. But that pale yellow gravy is basically coconut milk, and coconut milk gets around 85% of its calories from fat. Out of 190 calories in a normal hawker scoop, roughly 125 come from the santan alone. The vegetables themselves, whether pumpkin, pucuk manis, or cabbage, contribute maybe 45 calories total. So you're not eating a low-calorie dish. You're eating a moderate-calorie dish that happens to have some vegetables in it. Fair to flag here: these numbers are a genuine ballpark estimate because detailed lab data for sayur lemak specifically is thin. MOH figures and food tracker baselines vary quite a bit depending on the variant, so treat 190 as a reasonable middle ground, not gospel.

What actually moves the number is the santan ratio. A labu masak lemak can be as low as 120 calories for the same 180g serving, mainly because pumpkin itself is low-calorie so even with a standard santan gravy the overall dish comes in lighter. A rich sayur lemak campur with extra santan can push past 240 calories. The vegetable choice matters less than you'd think. It's the gravy that decides. Practically speaking, if you're eating this at a nasi campur stall, ask for less gravy and more solid veg, and you can probably shave 40 to 60 calories off that serving. If you cook at home, using reduced-fat santan or diluting with water drops the fat content significantly. As a side dish at lunch with plain rice and a piece of grilled chicken, 190 calories is absolutely fine. The problem is when you take two scoops and pour all that gravy over your rice lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask the stall for less gravy and more solid vegetable pieces, which can cut 40 to 60 calories per serving.
  • At home, use reduced-fat coconut milk or dilute full-fat santan with water at a 1 to 1 ratio to roughly halve the fat from the gravy.
  • Pick the labu (pumpkin) version when available, it typically runs around 120 calories per serving because pumpkin itself is low-calorie so even with a standard santan gravy the overall dish comes in lighter.
  • Pair it with plain steamed rice rather than nasi lemak or nasi goreng, no point stacking coconut dishes on top of each other.
  • Treat it as a side, not a main. One scoop alongside a lean protein like grilled chicken or tofu keeps your total meal reasonable.

Common questions

How many calories in Sayur Lemak?
A typical hawker serving of Sayur Lemak (180g) has around 190 calories. The range is roughly 120 to 240 calories depending on how rich the santan gravy is and which vegetable is used.
Is Sayur Lemak healthy for weight loss?
It can fit into a weight loss diet as a side dish, but it is not a free food. The coconut milk adds significant fat. Stick to one scoop, ask for less gravy, and pair it with a lean protein to keep the overall meal balanced.
Which version of Sayur Lemak is lowest in calories?
Labu (pumpkin) masak lemak is the lowest, coming in around 120 calories per serving. Pumpkin itself is low-calorie, so even with a standard santan gravy the overall dish comes in lighter than leafy green versions.
Berapa kalori dalam Sayur Lemak?
Sayur Lemak ada kira-kira 190 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (180g), dengan 4g protein, 12g karbohidrat dan 14g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
