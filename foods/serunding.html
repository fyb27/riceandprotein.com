How Many Calories in Serunding?

Dry spiced meat floss eaten as a topping. Tiny portion, but concentrated coconut and oil make it dense.

95kcal / 2-3 tablespoons
A serving of serunding (2 to 3 tablespoons, around 20g) is about 95 calories. It is eaten in small amounts as a topping, but it is very calorie dense per gram because it is fried dry with grated coconut and oil.

The real talk

Serunding is that fragrant meat floss you get with ketupat and lemang during raya. A normal topping portion is only 2 or 3 tablespoons, around 95 calories, so it is not a big deal in small amounts. But per gram it is dense, close to 470 calories per 100g, because all the moisture is fried out and what is left is meat, coconut and oil.

The trap is treating it like a free sprinkle and piling it on, especially next to lemang or pulut which are already calorie dense. A little goes a long way on flavour, so use it like the condiment it is and you are fine.

How to eat it lighter

  • Use it as a small topping, not a main scoop. It is concentrated.
  • Chicken serunding is slightly leaner than beef.
  • Mind what you pair it with. Lemang and pulut are heavy on their own.

Common questions

How many calories in serunding?
About 95 calories for a 2 to 3 tablespoon serving of beef serunding. It is small but dense, close to 470 calories per 100g, because it is fried dry with coconut and oil.
Is serunding fattening?
In a small topping portion, no. The risk is piling it on like a free sprinkle, especially next to already heavy lemang or pulut. Use it as a condiment.
Beef or chicken serunding, which is lower?
Chicken serunding is slightly leaner at around 85 calories per serving versus 95 for beef, though both share the same coconut and oil base.
Berapa kalori dalam Serunding?
Serunding ada kira-kira 95 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (20g), dengan 7g protein, 4g karbohidrat dan 6g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
