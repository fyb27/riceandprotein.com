How Many Calories in Sinangag?

Also known as Garlic Fried Rice, Sinangag na Kanin, Filipino Garlic Rice.

Day-old rice fried in oil with a lot of crushed garlic and salt. It is the sin in tapsilog, longsilog and every other silog plate in the Philippines.

300kcal / 1 cup
One cup of sinangag is about 300 calories for a 200g carinderia scoop. That is 260 calories from the rice itself and about 40 from the tablespoon of oil and the fried garlic. It is only a little more than plain steamed rice, but the oil adds up fast if the cook is generous.

The real talk

The rice is the whole story. A cup of cooked white rice is 200g and about 260 calories before anything touches it. Sinangag adds roughly 1 tablespoon of oil per cup, which is another 120 calories in the pan, but only part of that sticks to the rice. Kawaling Pinoy's recipe card puts it at 247 calories per cup with a quarter cup of oil across 4 cups of rice. A carinderia uses more oil than a home cook, so 300 is the fair number.

Where people go wrong is the second cup. Silog plates come with 1 cup, and an extra cup of sinangag is 20 to 25 pesos. That second scoop turns a 650 calorie tapsilog into a 950 calorie tapsilog, and the tapa did not change at all. The garlic and salt make it very easy to keep eating, which is the point of the dish and the problem with it.

The oil varies a lot by cook. Some carinderias fry the garlic in lard or in the fat left over from frying longganisa, which makes the rice taste better and pushes a cup closer to 350 calories. If the rice looks shiny and leaves a film on the plate, count it as 350, not 300.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for plain rice instead of sinangag. You save about 40 to 90 calories per cup and the ulam still tastes the same.
  • Order half rice. A half cup of sinangag is about 150 calories and is plenty next to a fried egg and tapa.
  • If you make it at home, use 1 teaspoon of oil per cup instead of 1 tablespoon. That is 260 plus 40 instead of 260 plus 120.
  • Skip the extra scoop. The second cup is where a silog plate goes from lunch to two lunches.

Common questions

Is sinangag higher in calories than plain rice?
Yes, but not by much. A cup of plain rice is about 260 calories and a cup of sinangag is about 300. The difference is the oil and garlic. The problem is usually the portion, not the frying.
How many calories in a silog plate?
Between 550 and 750 depending on the ulam. Tapsilog is about 650, longsilog about 700. Sinangag is 300 of that and the fried egg is about 90.
Can I eat sinangag while losing weight?
Yes. Keep it to 1 cup, which is 300 calories, and pair it with a lean ulam like chicken inasal or tinola. The second cup is what breaks the budget.
Is sinangag the same as fried rice?
It is the plainest version of fried rice. No egg, no vegetables, no soy sauce, just garlic, oil and salt. That makes it lower in calories than Chinese fried rice, which is about 400 to 450 per cup.
