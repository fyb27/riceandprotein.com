How Many Calories in Sinh To Bo?

Also known as Sinh tố bơ, Vietnamese Avocado Smoothie, Avocado Shake, Sinh To.

Half a Dak Lak avocado blended with condensed milk, a splash of fresh milk, sugar and a glass of crushed ice until it is thick enough to hold a straw upright. Sold at every sinh to cart for 20,000 to 35,000 dong, usually next to the sugarcane press.

340kcal / 1 glass
A standard glass of sinh to bo is around 340 calories. The 120g of avocado in it is 190 on its own because avocado is 160 calories per 100g, the 30g of condensed milk is 100, and the fresh milk and sugar make up the last 60. Long Chau's range is 300 to 400 per glass; a version with fresh milk and no condensed milk is about 230.

The real talk

Avocado is the one fruit that is mostly fat, 15g per 100g, so a sinh to bo starts heavy before anything sweet goes in. A cart uses about half a large avocado, 120g, which is 190 calories. Then 30g of condensed milk at 100, 60ml of fresh milk at 40 and a spoon of sugar at 20. That is 350 before the ice, which Long Chau rounds to 300 to 400 per glass. It is the heaviest sinh to on the cart by a wide margin.

It is also the most filling. A glass has 7g of fibre and 22g of fat, and it takes 10 minutes to get through with a spoon. People who replace a banh mi (500) with a sinh to bo (340) for breakfast usually stay full longer, which is unusual for a drink. The trap is having it as a drink with a meal, because 340 on top of a 700 calorie com tam is a 1,040 calorie lunch.

The sweetener choice is the lever. Huggies works out a glass with 100g of avocado, 120ml of fresh milk and a little sugar at 220 to 250. The same glass made with condensed milk, which is what most carts do because it keeps without a fridge, is 340. Sinh to bo sau rieng, with durian blended in, is 480. Kem bo, the Da Lat version with a scoop of ice cream on top, is 500.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for sua tuoi thay sua dac (fresh milk instead of condensed). Drops the glass to about 230.
  • Khong duong, no added sugar. The avocado and milk are sweet enough for most people.
  • Have it as a meal, not with one. At 340 with 7g of fibre it works as breakfast on its own.
  • A small glass (ly nho) at most carts is about 230.
  • Skip the durian and ice cream versions. Each adds 140 to 160.

Common questions

How many calories in a sinh to bo?
About 340 for a standard cart glass with condensed milk. Long Chau gives a range of 300 to 400; a fresh milk, no sugar version is around 230.
Is sinh to bo good for weight loss?
As a meal replacement, yes: 340 calories with 7g of fibre and healthy fat keeps you full for hours. As a drink next to a full meal, no. It is the highest calorie sinh to on the menu.
How many calories in avocado alone?
About 160 per 100g, so the half avocado in a glass is 190 before any milk or sugar. The fat is mostly monounsaturated, the same kind as olive oil.
Sinh to bo or nuoc mia, which has more calories?
Sinh to bo at 340 versus about 220 for a glass of sugarcane juice. The avocado has fat and fibre; the sugarcane is only sugar.
