How Many Calories in Sirap Bandung?

Also known as Bandung.

The iconic pink drink at every mamak and kenduri: rose syrup mixed with evaporated milk over ice, sweet and creamy and deceptively calorie-dense for something that feels like just a drink.

190kcal / 1 glass (iced, hawker)
Sirap Bandung comes in at 190 calories per standard hawker glass (300ml). Most of that comes from two things: rose syrup at around 90 calories and evaporated milk at around 95 calories. The ice and water make up the rest, bringing the total to roughly 190 calories for what is essentially a sweet pink drink. Not catastrophic, but definitely not free.

The real talk

190 calories for a drink sounds okay until you realise you're ordering two of these with your nasi lemak and roti canai. The calories here come from both the rose syrup and the evaporated milk, and they contribute almost equally: about 90 calories from the syrup and 95 from the milk. Rose syrup alone is 90 calories for just 55g, which is basically cordial with red dye. Add evaporated milk at 95 calories per 100g and you've got a proper calorie bomb in a glass. The ice and water dilution save you a bit, but not enough to pretend this is a light choice. If you're counting, 190 calories for a drink is the same as a small bowl of plain rice, lah.

What actually changes the number is how heavy-handed the uncle is with the syrup and milk. Some hawkers go really generous with both, which can push this to 220 or even 250 calories easily. The soda version (Bandung Soda) swaps some of that evaporated milk for soda water or sarsi, bringing it down to around 160 calories, but the sugar stays roughly the same because the syrup doesn't change. If you're trying to cut calories without giving up the drink entirely, ask for less syrup, get the soda version, or just drink half and leave the rest. Both the syrup and the milk contribute roughly equally here, so cutting back on either one will make a real difference.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask the uncle to go light on the syrup. That alone can save 30 to 50 calories.
  • Order the soda version (Bandung Soda) instead. Around 160 calories versus 190, same drink experience.
  • Stop at one glass. Sounds obvious, but most people casually order a refill without thinking.
  • Skip the Sirap Bandung if you already have another sweet drink coming. Teh tarik plus Bandung in one meal is 400 calories just from drinks.
  • Drink it slow, make it last. You enjoy it more and you won't feel like you need a second one.

Common questions

How many calories in Sirap Bandung?
A standard hawker glass of Sirap Bandung is about 190 calories. The main sources are rose syrup (90 cal) and evaporated milk (95 cal), with ice and water making up the rest of the 300ml glass.
Is Sirap Bandung fattening?
It depends on how many you drink. One glass at 190 calories is manageable. Two glasses with your meal is 380 calories from drinks alone, which adds up fast. The sugar content (36g of carbs per glass) is the bigger concern if you're watching your diet.
What is the difference between Sirap Bandung and Bandung Soda?
Bandung Soda replaces some or all of the evaporated milk with soda water or sarsi. It comes out lighter at around 160 calories because there's less fat from the milk. The sugar content stays similar since the rose syrup amount doesn't really change.
Berapa kalori dalam Sirap Bandung?
Sirap Bandung ada kira-kira 190 kalori untuk 1 gelas (300g), dengan 3g protein, 36g karbohidrat dan 3g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
