How Many Calories in Sisig?

Also known as Pork Sisig, Sizzling Sisig, Sisig Kapampangan.

Chopped pig face, ears and belly, boiled, grilled, then fried on a sizzling plate with onion, chili, calamansi, a raw egg and a squeeze of mayonnaise. Pampanga's gift to every beer house.

530kcal / 1 sizzling plate
A standard sizzling plate of pork sisig, about 200g with egg and mayo, is around 530 calories. FatSecret puts plain pork sisig at 215 calories per 100g, and the egg (70) and mayo (100) push a restaurant plate past 500. With 1 cup of rice it is 790.

The real talk

Sisig is pig face. Ears, jowl and snout are mostly skin, fat and cartilage, and the belly some cooks add is half fat. FatSecret lists pork sisig at 515 calories per cup, with 35g of fat and 39g of protein, and that is before the sizzling plate toppings. The protein is real, which surprises people, but the fat is higher.

The toppings are 170 of the 530. A raw egg cracked on top is 70 calories. The squiggle of mayonnaise most restaurants add is 15g, and mayo is 100 calories per 15g. Chicharon crumbled on top is another 55 per 10g. A plain sisig with just calamansi and onion is about 360 calories for the same 200g, which is a meaningful difference for the same dish.

A sisig plate in a Manila beer house is 150 to 250 pesos and is priced as pulutan for a group. In practice one person clears it with 2 cups of rice, which is 1,050 calories, plus two San Miguel Pale Pilsens at 150 each. That is a 1,350 calorie sitting. The sisig was never the whole problem, the setting was.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it without mayo and without egg. Same flavour from the calamansi and chili, 170 calories less.
  • Try chicken sisig (about 380) or bangus sisig (about 330). Same plate, half the fat.
  • Stick to 1 cup of rice. Sisig is salty and pushes you toward a second cup.
  • Make it at home with pork shoulder instead of face. Lean pork sisig is about 300 calories per 200g.

Common questions

How many calories in sisig with rice?
About 790 calories for a standard plate with 1 cup of rice, or 1,050 with 2 cups. Sisig alone is around 530.
How many calories in sisig without mayo and egg?
About 360 calories for a 200g plate. The egg is 70 and the mayo is 100, so leaving both off saves 170.
Is sisig high in protein?
Yes, about 34g per plate. FatSecret lists 39g of protein per cup of pork sisig. The catch is the 35g of fat that comes with it.
Is chicken sisig healthier than pork sisig?
It is lower in calories, about 380 versus 530 for the same plate, because thigh meat has far less fat than pig face and belly.
