How Many Calories in Siu Mei Rice (Roast Pork Rice)?

Also known as 燒味飯, 燒肉飯, Siu Yuk Fan, Roast Meat Rice, Crispy Pork Rice, 雙拼飯.

Siu mei rice is a plate of steamed rice with roast meat chopped over it from the window of a siu mei shop: crispy roast pork (siu yuk), char siu, roast goose, roast duck or soy sauce chicken. Around HK$42 to HK$58 for a single meat, more for a double combo.

930kcal / 1 plate (roast pork on rice)
One plate of roast pork (siu yuk) rice is about 930 calories, based on Centre for Food Safety analysis of a 470g plate. Char siu rice from the same study is 1,000 calories, roast goose rice 970, plain poached chicken rice 940 and soy sauce chicken rice 770. The meat is 40g of fat on its own; the rice is around 110g of carbs.

The real talk

Siu yuk is pork belly with the skin blistered. A 130g portion on rice is about 500 calories before you count the rice, because belly is roughly a third fat by weight. CFS measured 12g of saturated fat in a plate of roast pork rice, more than the 10g the WHO considers a reasonable daily ceiling. Char siu is leaner meat but it is glazed in maltose and the fatty 半肥瘦 cut is what most shops chop by default, which is why it comes out slightly higher at 1,000.

The rice is the quiet half of the plate. Most siu mei shops put 280 to 300g of cooked rice under the meat, which is about 360 calories and more than the meat's carb-free 500. Then the shop spoons roast juices and sweet sauce over it, another 30 to 50. Ask for less rice (少飯) and the plate drops by around 120 calories with the same amount of meat, which is the cheapest calorie cut in Hong Kong.

Soy sauce chicken is the one to know about. The CFS figure for 油雞飯 is 770 calories versus 930 for roast pork, a 160 calorie gap for the same plate size. Peel the skin and it is closer to 650. The double combo (雙拼) plates that stack char siu and roast pork are 1,100 or more, and the roast goose leg on rice with skin can pass 1,000 on its own. Pick one meat, pick it lean, and this is a decent 30g plus protein lunch.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for lean char siu (瘦叉燒) or soy sauce chicken without skin. Either saves 200 to 300 calories versus roast pork belly.
  • Order less rice (少飯). About 120 calories off with no change to the meat.
  • Skip the sweet sauce and the extra ladle of roast juices. That is 30 to 50 calories and a lot of sodium.
  • Add a plate of blanched choy sum for HK$12. Around 30 calories and it makes the plate a real meal.

Common questions

How many calories in a plate of siu mei rice?
Between 770 and 1,000 depending on the meat, per Centre for Food Safety data. Roast pork rice is about 930, char siu rice 1,000, roast goose rice 970 and soy sauce chicken rice 770.
Which siu mei is the lowest calorie?
Soy sauce chicken at about 770 calories on rice, or closer to 650 with the skin removed. Roast pork belly is the highest in fat at about 40g per plate.
Is siu yuk or char siu higher in calories?
On a rice plate they are close: roast pork 930 and char siu 1,000 in the CFS study. Char siu is glazed with maltose and usually chopped from the fatty cut, which is why it edges ahead.
