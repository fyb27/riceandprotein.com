How Many Calories in Sliced Fish Bee Hoon Soup?

Also known as Fish Soup Bee Hoon, 鱼片米粉, Sliced Fish Soup, Fish Bee Hoon Singapore.

Sliced fish bee hoon is the Singapore hawker soup of rice vermicelli in a clear fish bone broth with slices of batang or snakehead fish, tomato, lettuce or cabbage, and fried shallots. The fried fish and milk versions are the same bowl with the lights off.

349kcal / 1 bowl
A standard bowl of sliced fish bee hoon soup is about 349 calories for a 500g bowl, with 25g of protein and around 7g of fat. The fish soup alone without noodles is about 113 calories per 241g serving. The fried fish version with evaporated milk is about 730.

The real talk

This is the hawker bowl dietitians keep pointing people to, and the number backs it up. 150g of bee hoon is about 165 calories, 80g of sliced batang fish about 90, the clear broth 50, vegetables and tomato 15, and the fried shallots and a drizzle of oil around 25. That is 349 for a full bowl with 25g of protein. Nothing else at the hawker centre gives you that ratio for S$5.

The same stall sells a bowl that is more than double. Fried fish bee hoon with milk is the sliced fish battered and deep fried, then the broth whitened with evaporated milk. That bowl is about 730 calories. The fried fish coating and oil add about 250, the milk another 100, and the stalls usually put in more fish because the fried version is the premium order. Same counter, same bee hoon, 380 calories apart.

The plain version is not bland if you use the chilli padi and soy dip properly. That dip is about 15 calories. A spoon of the fried shallot oil from the top of the pot is 40. Ask the stall to go light on the shallot oil and you keep the taste at a lower price.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order sliced fish, not fried fish. That single choice is about 250 calories.
  • Say no to the evaporated milk. It is about 100 calories per bowl and it hides the fish flavour anyway.
  • Ask for extra fish instead of extra noodles. 40g more fish is about 45 calories and 9g more protein.
  • Ask for extra vegetables. Most stalls add cabbage or lettuce free and it is under 15 calories.
  • Get the soup without noodles and eat it with a small bowl of rice if you want to control the carbs. Fish soup alone is about 113 calories.

Common questions

Is sliced fish soup good for weight loss?
Yes, it is one of the best hawker options. About 349 calories with 25g of protein and only 7g of fat for a full bowl of bee hoon. Without noodles the soup is about 113 calories.
How many calories in fried fish bee hoon soup?
About 600 calories without milk and 730 with evaporated milk. The battered fried fish adds around 250 calories over the sliced version, and the milk adds about 100 more.
Does the milk in fish soup add a lot of calories?
About 100 calories per bowl. Evaporated milk is roughly 130 calories per 100ml and the stalls add about 80ml. It also adds 6g of fat to a soup that otherwise has very little.
Which fish is used in sliced fish soup?
Usually batang, which is Spanish mackerel, or toman, the snakehead. Both are lean, about 110 calories per 100g raw, which is why the sliced version stays light.
Berapa kalori dalam Sliced Fish Bee Hoon Soup?
Sliced Fish Bee Hoon Soup ada kira-kira 349 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (500g), dengan 25g protein, 45g karbohidrat dan 7g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
