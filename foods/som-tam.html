How Many Calories in Som Tam?

Also known as ส้มตำ, ส้มตำไทย, Som Tum, Green Papaya Salad, Tam Thai.

Shredded green papaya pounded in a clay mortar with lime, fish sauce, palm sugar, chili, garlic, tomato, long bean, dried shrimp and peanuts. The most ordered salad in Thailand, usually eaten with sticky rice and grilled meat.

150kcal / 1 plate
A plate of som tam thai is about 150 calories for a 150g serving. The Thai Department of Health lists the classic recipe at 105 calories per 100g. Green papaya itself is under 40 calories per 100g; the peanuts and palm sugar in the dressing are what add up.

The real talk

Som tam on its own is one of the lightest plates in Thai food. 100g of green papaya is about 40 calories, and the lime, chili, garlic and fish sauce add nothing meaningful. The 150 calorie total comes from about a tablespoon of palm sugar (55 calories) and a small handful of roasted peanuts (45 to 60 calories). Ask for no peanuts and less sugar and you are eating an 80 calorie plate.

The problem was never the som tam. It is the table. A standard isaan order is som tam, a bag of sticky rice and gai yang or moo ping. The sticky rice bag is 260 calories, the grilled chicken thigh is 300, and the whole meal lands around 700. That is still a decent dinner, but people think of it as a salad and go back for a second bag of rice.

Variants matter. Tam pu pla ra (with fermented fish and salted crab, no peanuts, less sugar) is about 80 calories. Tam khai khem (with salted egg) is around 210. Tam thai at 150 is the middle. If you want the most food for the least calories, pu pla ra wins, as long as you can handle the smell.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for mai wan (not sweet) or wan noi (less sweet). The palm sugar is a third of the calories.
  • Skip the peanuts, or ask for half. Each tablespoon is 60 calories.
  • Order it with grilled chicken and one bag of sticky rice, not two. That keeps the full meal around 700 calories.
  • Tam pu pla ra without peanuts is the lightest version at about 80 calories.

Common questions

Is som tam good for weight loss?
Very. At 150 calories for a full plate with lots of fibre and crunch it fills you up for almost nothing. The meal only gets heavy from the sticky rice and grilled meat next to it.
How many calories in som tam with sticky rice?
A plate of tam thai (150) plus a 150g bag of khao niao (260) is about 410 calories. Add a grilled chicken thigh and the set is around 700.
Which som tam has the fewest calories?
Tam pu pla ra without peanuts, around 80 calories a plate. Tam thai is 150 because of sugar and peanuts. Anything with salted egg, pork rind or instant noodles goes above 200.
