How Many Calories in Sop Buntut?

Also known as Sup Buntut, Sop Buntut Goreng, Sop Buntut Bakar, Indonesian Oxtail Soup.

Sop buntut is oxtail simmered for hours in a clear broth with carrot, potato, tomato, nutmeg and clove, served with fried shallots, emping and sambal. Hotel restaurants made it famous; warungs made it affordable.

380kcal / 1 bowl (about 3 pieces of oxtail, broth, vegetables, no rice)
A bowl of sop buntut with about three pieces of oxtail is roughly 380 calories without rice. TKPI lists the soup at about 71 calories per 100g as served, which for a 550g restaurant bowl gives 390, and building it from the oxtail meat, broth and vegetables lands in the same place. Sop buntut goreng is about 500.

The real talk

Oxtail is not lean. Once the bone is out, cooked oxtail meat is about 260 calories per 100g, and roughly half of that is fat and gelatin. A restaurant bowl has 180 to 200g of bone-in pieces, which is about 100g of actual meat and fat, so 250 calories before the soup. The long simmer pulls fat into the broth, and 300ml of that broth with the shine on top is another 90. The carrot, potato and tomato are 30.

That makes the plain bowl about 380 calories with 28g protein, which is a good ratio. The problem is the price and the format. At Rp 60,000 to 120,000 a bowl in a Jakarta restaurant, it comes with rice, emping, kerupuk and sambal, and the full set is 700 calories. At a warung it is Rp 30,000 to 45,000 with a smaller piece of tail and closer to 300.

Sop buntut goreng is the popular variant: the oxtail is boiled, then deep fried, then served with the broth on the side. The frying adds 100 to 120 calories and the crispy edges are exactly where the fat is. Sop buntut bakar (grilled with kecap) is a bit lighter than fried but the kecap glaze adds about 40 calories of sugar.

Emping is the sneaky add. A small plate of the melinjo crackers next to the bowl is 80 to 100 calories, and they are there to be eaten with the soup. Two rounds of emping is more than the vegetables in the bowl.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for the broth 'tanpa lemak' or skim the top yourself. The fat layer on a bowl of sop buntut is 40 to 60 calories.
  • Plain sop buntut, not goreng or bakar. The frying step alone adds about 120 calories.
  • Half rice. The bowl has 28g protein and the vegetables, so 100g of rice is enough to make it a meal at about 510.
  • Skip the emping and kerupuk. Together they are easily 130 calories and neither one is filling.
  • Eat the meat off the bone and leave the fat pockets. Oxtail has visible fat between the segments and it is about 9 calories per gram.

Common questions

How many calories in sop buntut?
About 380 calories for a restaurant bowl with three pieces of oxtail, without rice. With rice and emping it is about 700.
Is sop buntut high in fat?
Yes, about 26g per bowl. Oxtail is a fatty cut and the long simmer pushes that fat into the broth. Skimming the top and leaving the fat pockets cuts it a lot.
How many calories in sop buntut goreng?
About 500 calories. Deep frying the boiled oxtail adds roughly 120 calories on top of the plain version.
Is sop buntut good for diet?
It can be. 28g protein, real vegetables, and no coconut milk. Order it plain, skim the broth, half rice, no emping, and it is a 500 calorie meal that fills you.
Berapa kalori Sop Buntut?
Sop Buntut mengandung sekitar 380 kalori per 1 mangkuk (550g), dengan 28g protein, 8g karbohidrat dan 26g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
