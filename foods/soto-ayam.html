How Many Calories in Soto Ayam?

Also known as Soto Ayam Lamongan, Soto Ayam Kuning, Soto Ayam Koya, Indonesian Chicken Soup.

Soto ayam is turmeric chicken soup with shredded chicken, glass noodles, cabbage, a boiled egg and fried shallots. The Lamongan version adds koya, a powder of fried garlic and prawn crackers.

310kcal / 1 bowl (no rice)
A bowl of soto ayam is about 310 calories on its own. FatSecret Indonesia lists a 241g bowl at 312 calories with 24g protein. The rice most people order with it adds another 260, and a heavy spoon of koya adds 60.

The real talk

Soto ayam is one of the better street meals in Indonesia if you stop at the bowl. 310 calories, 24g protein, and the broth is turmeric and stock rather than coconut. The chicken is shredded, so 60g looks like a lot in the bowl, and it is 100 calories. The half boiled egg is 35. The bihun is 40g, about 45 calories, and the cabbage and tomato are close to nothing.

The number moves when you add the plate of rice, and everybody does. Nasi at 200g is 260 calories, so 'soto ayam plus nasi' is 570 before anything else. Some places put the rice in the bowl (soto campur), some serve it separate (soto pisah), same calories either way. A Rp 15,000 to 20,000 bowl becomes a 600 calorie lunch.

Koya is the Lamongan twist and it hits harder than it looks. It is fried garlic and crushed prawn crackers, about 60 calories per heaped spoon, and the taste is good enough that people take two. The oily yellow film on top of Surabaya-style soto is chicken fat and turmeric oil, maybe another 40 calories if the ladle catches it.

The side dishes on the table are the real trap. Sate usus, sate telur puyuh, perkedel and tempe goreng are all 60 to 100 calories each, and the bill only shows you the sticks afterwards.

How to eat it lighter

  • Skip the rice or take half. Soto with 100g rice is 440 calories and the broth fills the gap.
  • One spoon of koya, not two. Each one is about 60 calories.
  • Ask for the ladle from below the oil line. The yellow film on top is fat.
  • Add a whole egg instead of the half. 35 extra calories for 3g more protein.
  • Leave the sate usus and perkedel on the table. They are small but 60 to 100 calories each.

Common questions

How many calories in soto ayam?
About 310 calories per bowl without rice. Add a plate of rice and it is around 570.
Is soto ayam good for weight loss?
Yes, one of the better options. 24g protein for 310 calories, clear broth, and it is filling. Just watch the rice and the koya.
How many calories in soto ayam with rice?
Around 570 calories with 200g of rice, or 440 with half rice.
How many calories in koya?
About 60 calories per heaped tablespoon. It is fried garlic and crushed prawn crackers, so it is mostly oil and starch.
Berapa kalori Soto Ayam?
Soto Ayam mengandung sekitar 310 kalori per 1 mangkuk (330g), dengan 24g protein, 20g karbohidrat dan 15g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
