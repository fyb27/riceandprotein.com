How Many Calories in Soto Betawi?

Also known as Soto Betawi Daging, Soto Betawi Susu, Jakarta Beef Soto.

Soto Betawi is Jakarta's rich beef soup made with coconut milk, sometimes milk too, with chunks of beef and offal, tomato, fried potato and emping crackers.

365kcal / 1 bowl (no rice)
A bowl of soto Betawi is about 365 calories without rice. FatSecret Indonesia lists a 270g bowl at 363 calories, with 56% of it from fat. With a plate of rice at 260 and a handful of emping at 50, the meal is about 675.

The real talk

This is the fatty soto, and the broth is the reason. Coconut milk, sometimes evaporated milk on top of that, and the fat that renders out of beef brisket and offal. A 200ml ladle of the broth alone is about 135 calories, more than the meat in the bowl. The white creamy look is not a trick of the light, it is 27g of fat per bowl.

The meat is usually a mix: 80g of daging, babat (tripe), paru (lung) or usus, about 150 calories. Daging only is the leanest choice. Paru goreng versions are heavier because the lung is fried first, adding maybe 60 calories. The fried potato and tomato are 30. The emping crackers on top or on the side are another 50 for a small pile.

It is a Rp 25,000 to 40,000 bowl in Jakarta, and it is nearly always eaten with rice, so plan on 630 to 700 calories for the meal. For that you get only about 20g protein, which is not great value. The Betawi soto is a comfort food, not a protein dish.

The trick is that the fat floats. If you eat the meat and half the broth, you are closer to 280 calories. If you drink every drop and dip the emping in it, you are at 415 before rice.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for daging only, no jeroan. Tripe and lung soak up broth and lung is often fried.
  • Leave half the broth. Most of the fat is in the liquid, not the meat.
  • Half rice. 130 calories saved, and the broth is rich enough to make it feel like plenty.
  • Emping is 50 calories per small pile. Skip it or take three pieces.
  • Squeeze the lime and add sambal. Both are free and cut the fatty taste so you need less broth.

Common questions

How many calories in soto Betawi?
About 365 calories per bowl without rice. With rice it is around 625, and with emping and extra broth about 700.
Is soto Betawi high in fat?
Yes, about 27g fat per bowl, which is more than half the calories. The coconut milk broth and the beef fat are the reason.
Soto Betawi or soto ayam, which has fewer calories?
Soto ayam by a lot. Soto ayam is about 310 calories with 24g protein; soto Betawi is 365 with 16g protein and nearly double the fat.
How many calories in soto Betawi susu?
Around 410 calories. The evaporated or fresh milk added to the coconut broth pushes it up by about 45 calories per bowl.
Berapa kalori Soto Betawi?
Soto Betawi mengandung sekitar 365 kalori per 1 mangkuk (300g), dengan 16g protein, 14g karbohidrat dan 27g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
