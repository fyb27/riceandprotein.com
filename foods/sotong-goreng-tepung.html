How Many Calories in Sotong Goreng Tepung (Fried Calamari)?

Squid rings coated in seasoned flour batter and deep fried until golden and crispy.

460kcal / 1 plate
A plate of sotong goreng tepung comes in around 460 calories, with 24g protein, 34g carbs and 24g fat. Here is the irony: squid by itself is one of the leanest, lowest-calorie proteins you can eat. But the moment you coat it in flour batter and deep fry it, you stack on carbs from the coating and fat from the absorbed oil. The squid is innocent, the batter and fryer are not.

The real talk

Sotong goreng tepung is a real what-could-have-been dish bro. Plain squid is incredibly lean, like 90 calories per 100g lean, high protein, almost no fat. It should be a diet dream. But then you coat each ring in seasoned flour batter and drop it in the deep fryer, and suddenly that innocent squid is wearing a calorie jacket. The batter brings the carbs and the absorbed frying oil brings the fat. At around 460 calories a plate, it is not the worst thing on the table, but it is a long way from the lean protein the squid started as. The crispy coating is the entire story here.

The variables are batter thickness and how well it was drained. A light, thin tepung coating absorbs less oil and keeps the plate closer to 400, while a thick beer-batter style or a heavily clumped coating can push it past 550. Squid also goes rubbery fast if overcooked, so some places batter it thick to hide that, which works against you. Dipping it in mayo or thousand island sauce, which a lot of places serve it with, can quietly add another 100 calories. If you want the squid without the deep-fry tax, sotong masak gives you the same lean protein for far fewer calories. But if you are ordering the fried version, blot the oil, skip the mayo, and share it as a starter rather than a main.

How to eat it lighter

  • If you can choose, get sotong stir-fried or in sambal instead of battered. Same lean squid, way fewer calories without the batter and oil.
  • Skip the mayo or thousand island dip. That sauce can add 100 calories fast and you do not need it.
  • Blot the rings with a tissue before eating. Surface oil removal genuinely shaves a bit off a deep-fried plate.
  • Share it as a starter for the table, not as your protein main, since the protein-to-calorie ratio gets wrecked by the batter.

Common questions

How many calories in fried calamari (sotong goreng tepung)?
A standard plate is around 460 calories, with 24g protein, 34g carbs and 24g fat. A thin-coated version is closer to 380, while a thick batter can push past 550.
Isn't squid supposed to be low calorie?
It is. Plain squid is one of the leanest proteins around, roughly 90 calories per 100g. The calories in fried calamari come almost entirely from the flour batter and the oil absorbed during deep frying, not the squid itself.
What is a lighter way to eat squid?
Get it stir-fried or cooked in sambal instead of battered and fried. You keep the lean high-protein squid but skip the flour coating and the frying oil, which is where most of the calories come from.
Berapa kalori dalam Sotong Goreng Tepung?
Sotong Goreng Tepung ada kira-kira 460 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (200g), dengan 24g protein, 34g karbohidrat dan 24g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
