Soups Calories

Soup is your best friend when you are cutting. Water has zero calories, it takes up space in your stomach, and a big bowl of sup ayam, tom yum or samgyetang keeps you full for hours at a very low cost. I ate soup constantly on my way from 128kg to 86kg.

The catch: some Asian soups are soups in name only. Anything built on coconut milk, or served with a mountain of rice on the side, plays by different rules. The ranking below sorts the genuinely light bowls from the ones wearing a disguise.

Soups at a glance

There are 48 dishes ranked on this page. The lightest is Miso Soup at 40 kcal per 1 bowl (tofu and wakame), the heaviest is Samgyetang at 918 kcal per 1 pot (whole young chicken), and the average lands around 359 calories a serving. 17 of them come in under 300 calories, and 4 are 600 or more.

That spread is the whole point. Two dishes off the same menu can be 878 calories apart, which is more than most people's entire daily deficit. Knowing which is which costs you nothing and changes the outcome.
