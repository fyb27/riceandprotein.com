How Many Calories in Soya Cincau (Michael Jackson)?

Also known as Michael Jackson, Soya Grass Jelly.

Sweet soya milk with grass jelly, the black-and-white Michael Jackson, about 200 calories.

200kcal / 1 glass
Soya cincau, nicknamed Michael Jackson for the black grass jelly in white soya milk, runs around 200 calories a glass. The soya milk is usually sweetened and the grass jelly comes with sugar syrup, so most of the calories are sugar. The soya itself gives you a little protein, around 6 grams.

The real talk

Michael Jackson, black and white, get it? Grass jelly sitting in soya milk. It sounds healthy because soya, but the soya milk at most stalls is sweetened heavily and the cincau is soaked in sugar syrup. So you are drinking maybe 30 grams of sugar in a glass.

It is not the worst choice lah, at least you get a bit of protein from the soya, around 6 grams. But do not kid yourself that it is a health drink. Ask for the unsweetened soya version if they have it and you cut a big chunk of the calories straight away.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for unsweetened or less-sweet soya milk if available
  • Order it without extra sugar syrup on the cincau
  • Have it as a snack replacement, not on top of a full meal

Common questions

How many calories in soya cincau?
About 200 calories a glass. Most of it is sugar from the sweetened soya and the cincau syrup.
Why is it called Michael Jackson?
Because of the look, black grass jelly in white soya milk. Black and white, get it.
Is soya cincau healthy?
It has a little protein from the soya, but it is usually loaded with sugar. Order unsweetened soya to make it genuinely lighter.
Berapa kalori dalam Soya Cincau?
Soya Cincau ada kira-kira 200 kalori untuk 1 gelas (350g), dengan 6g protein, 34g karbohidrat dan 5g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
