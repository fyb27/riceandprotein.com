How Many Calories in Sundubu Jjigae?

Also known as 순두부찌개, Soft Tofu Stew, Soondubu Jjigae, Sundubu.

Sundubu jjigae is a bubbling pot of silken tofu in a red chili broth, cracked with a raw egg at the table and served with rice and banchan.

260kcal / 1 pot
One restaurant pot of sundubu jjigae is about 260 calories (FatSecret Korea lists the generic serving at 259). Add the bowl of rice that always comes with it and the meal is around 570. The tofu and egg supply 16g of protein, so it is one of the more filling stews for the calories.

The real talk

The pot is mostly water and silken tofu. A 200g block of soft tofu is about 110 calories, the egg cracked in is 70, and whatever seafood or minced pork the kitchen adds is 40 to 60. The rest of the 260 is the chili oil the broth is built on, which is the one ingredient that varies wildly between shops.

That chili oil matters more than people think. A kitchen that starts the pot with 2 tablespoons of gochu-gireum is adding about 240 calories before anything else goes in. Most lunch places use a lot less, which is why the average lands around 260, but a very red, glossy surface on the broth is a sign you are at the higher end.

The rice is the bigger lever. A 210g bowl is around 310 calories, more than the stew. Order the stew with half rice and you are at roughly 415 for a complete lunch with 20g of protein. The Gangneung-style versions with beef and extra fat listed by packaged brands run 450 to 475 per serving, closer to a full meal on their own.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order the seafood (해물) version rather than beef or pork. It typically lands 40 to 60 calories lower.
  • Ask for less chili oil (기름 적게). It is a normal request and can save 100 calories.
  • Half rice instead of full. The stew is filling enough that most people do not miss it.
  • Do not drink all the broth. The flavour is in the top half, the oil settles there too.

Common questions

Is sundubu jjigae healthy?
It is one of the better stews: about 260 calories, 16g protein, and most of the volume is tofu and water. The only thing to watch is the chili oil and the sodium.
How many calories in sundubu jjigae with rice?
About 570 calories with a standard 210g bowl of rice. The rice is more than half the total.
Is sundubu jjigae high in protein?
Decent, not high. The tofu and egg give around 16g. Add a side of gyeran-mari or order the beef version to push it past 25g.
Does the egg add a lot of calories?
About 70. It also adds 6g of protein, so it is worth keeping.
