How Many Calories in Sup Daging?

Sup Daging is a clear, warmly spiced Malaysian beef soup, usually served with tender beef shank or short rib chunks and eaten with bread for dipping.

400kcal / 1 bowl
A standard hawker bowl of Sup Daging is around 400 calories for roughly 510g of soup, beef, and veg. Most of that comes from the beef itself (about 300 kcal for 150g of shank or short rib), with the spiced broth adding another 60 kcal and the potato and carrot rounding out the rest. On its own it is actually a pretty reasonable meal, but add two pieces of roti for dipping and you are looking at closer to 580 kcal total.

The real talk

Sup Daging is a clear spiced beef soup you find at Malay hawker stalls and warung, usually ordered alongside roti canai or a crusty French loaf. The broth is light and fragrant, flavoured with things like star anise, cinnamon, and cloves, but do not let "clear soup" fool you into thinking it is low calorie automatically. At 400 kcal a bowl, it is solidly in the middle range, and that is because beef shank and short rib are fatty cuts by nature. The rendered fat from the meat bleeds into the broth, and bone marrow versions (Sup Berempah) push it to 460 kcal. Fair warning though: these numbers are genuine ballpark estimates. Hard lab data for Malaysian hawker soups is thin, so take them as a reasonable guide rather than gospel.

What actually swings the number is the cut of beef and how much fat ends up in your bowl. Lean cuts like topside instead of shank can shave off 80 to 100 kcal without you noticing much. The broth itself at around 60 kcal per 300ml is not the villain, but stalls that skim less fat off the top will give you a noticeably richer, higher-calorie soup. Potato and carrot add about 45 kcal from 60g, which is fine. The real jump comes from the roti. Two pieces of roti canai or French loaf adds roughly 180 kcal and takes you to 580 kcal total. If you are watching calories, eat the soup on its own first. You might not even want the bread after that.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it without bread, or ask for just one piece of roti instead of two. That one swap saves around 90 to 180 kcal right there.
  • Ask for leaner cuts if you can. Topside or silverside instead of short rib means less fat rendered into the broth.
  • Skip the bone marrow version if calories matter. Marrow adds 60 to 80 kcal to an already decent bowl.
  • Drink the broth but leave some of the oilier layer at the bottom. Sounds fussy but the last third of a fatty soup has more calories than the first two thirds.
  • This soup is high in protein at 38g per bowl, so it keeps you full. Eat it slowly and you probably do not need rice or bread on the side.

Common questions

How many calories in Sup Daging?
A standard hawker bowl of Sup Daging is around 400 calories for roughly 510g with beef chunks, spiced broth, potato, and carrot. The lean version without potato comes in closer to 300 kcal, and the berempah version with marrow can reach 460 kcal. Add roti for dipping and the total goes up to about 580 kcal.
What is Sup Daging exactly?
It is a Malaysian clear beef soup, spiced with aromatics like star anise, cloves, and cinnamon. The beef is usually shank or short rib, slow-cooked until tender. You get a clean, warmly spiced broth with soft beef chunks, sometimes potato and carrot, and it is almost always eaten with bread for dipping.
Is Sup Daging a healthy choice for someone trying to lose weight?
It is one of the better hawker options, bah. You get 38g of protein in a 400 kcal bowl, which is a solid ratio. The broth keeps you full, it does not come with rice by default, and you control whether you add bread. Main thing to watch is the cut of beef and how fatty the broth is. Skip the roti or have just one piece, and it is a genuinely good diet meal.
Berapa kalori dalam Sup Daging?
Sup Daging ada kira-kira 400 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (510g), dengan 38g protein, 12g karbohidrat dan 20g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
