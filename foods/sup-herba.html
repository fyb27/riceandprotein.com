How Many Calories in Sup Herba (Herbal Chicken Soup)?

Herbal chicken soup, about 300 kcal a bowl and high protein.

300kcal / 1 bowl
A bowl of sup herba is around 300 calories. It is chicken simmered in a herbal broth with ingredients like wolfberry, red dates, and dang gui, so you are basically eating lean protein in flavoured water. The protein lands around 28g and the carbs are tiny, which makes this one of the cleaner things you can order.

The real talk

Sup herba is genuinely one of the best things on this whole site bro. It is chicken in a herbal broth, so you get around 28g of protein for only 300 calories, with almost no carbs and no oily gravy to sabotage you. Order this and you are winning.

The only thing to watch is the skin and the fattier chicken parts. If your bowl is loaded with chicken skin and fatty drumstick the fat creeps up, but pick a leaner cut or skim the oil layer on top and this stays a lean, filling, high-protein bowl.

How to eat it lighter

  • Go for chicken breast or thigh without skin to keep the fat down, drops it toward 240 calories
  • Skim the layer of chicken oil floating on top of the broth before you drink it
  • Pair it with vegetables instead of rice if you want a full meal that stays light

Common questions

Is sup herba good for weight loss?
Honestly one of the best picks you can make. Around 28g protein for only 300 calories, low carb, and very filling thanks to the warm broth. Order this over a fried rice any day and your deficit thanks you.
Why is herbal chicken soup low in calories?
Because it is just chicken and herbs simmered in water. There is no coconut milk, no thickened gravy, no frying. Most of the calories come from the chicken itself, and the broth is basically flavoured water.
Does sup herba have enough protein for after the gym?
Yes, around 28g in a standard bowl, which is a solid post-workout amount. Add an extra piece of chicken or pair it with rice if you trained hard and need more total food.
Berapa kalori dalam Sup Herba?
Sup Herba ada kira-kira 300 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (400g), dengan 28g protein, 10g karbohidrat dan 16g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
