How Many Calories in Sup Kambing?

Also known as Mutton Soup.

A rich, warming spiced mutton soup served with French bread, the kind you find at mamak stalls at midnight when you need something real.

520kcal / 1 bowl with 2 slices French bread
A standard bowl of Sup Kambing with 2 slices of French bread comes to around 520 calories. That breaks down as roughly 220 kcal from the bone-in mutton, 85 kcal from the spiced broth, 185 kcal from the bread, and 30 kcal from the veg and garnish. If you skip the bread and just have the soup, you're looking at about 340 calories instead, which is actually a pretty decent protein hit for the calories.

The real talk

520 calories for a full bowl with bread is honestly not bad for how satisfying this soup is. The mutton itself is doing the heavy lifting on protein, about 32g per bowl, which is solid. The fat comes mostly from two places: the mutton itself (bone-in cuts like ribs and shank have good marbling) and the rendered fat sitting in the broth. That broth is around 85 kcal for 300ml, which sounds innocent until you realize some stalls are ladling in way more oil than others. Fair warning: these are ballpark estimates. Hard nutrition data for Sup Kambing is thin, so treat this as a reasonable middle-ground figure rather than a lab result.

The biggest variable in the maths is what you eat it with. Skip the 2 slices of French bread (70g, 185 kcal) and your bowl drops from 520 to around 340 calories, which is genuinely low for how full you feel. Swap to roti canai instead and you're pushing 620 kcal. The other lever is the broth. Some stalls serve it cleaner, some are swimming in ghee. If you're watching intake, ask for less oil in the soup, it actually works at the good stalls. The mutton portion also varies a lot. The 200g bone-in weight here gives you roughly 110g of edible meat, so if your stall is more generous, add roughly 20 kcal per extra 10g of meat.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order soup only, no bread. You cut 185 kcal straight away and the soup is still filling on its own.
  • Ask the uncle to go easy on the oil when ladling. A less greasy broth can save you 30 to 50 kcal without losing flavour.
  • Avoid dunking the bread in the broth repeatedly. Bread soaks up oil fast and those calories add up quick.
  • If you want something to dip, ask if they have plain buns or less oily bread options. The French bread is pretty calorie-dense for its size.
  • Eat the meat, leave some of the fattier broth at the bottom of the bowl. The spice flavour is in the early sips anyway.

Common questions

How many calories in Sup Kambing?
A standard bowl of Sup Kambing with 2 slices of French bread is around 520 calories. If you have just the soup and mutton without any bread, it drops to about 340 calories.
Is Sup Kambing good for weight loss?
The soup itself is actually decent for dieting. 340 calories with around 26-27g of protein is a solid deal. The bread is where the extra calories pile in. Skip or reduce the bread and it becomes one of the more filling low-calorie options at the mamak.
Why is Sup Kambing high in fat?
Mutton, especially bone-in cuts like ribs and shank, naturally carries more fat than chicken. On top of that, the broth is cooked with rendered fat and sometimes ghee or oil added at the stall. The fat count per bowl is around 28g, most of it coming from the meat and the oily broth.
Berapa kalori dalam Sup Kambing?
Sup Kambing ada kira-kira 520 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (550g), dengan 32g protein, 30g karbohidrat dan 28g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
