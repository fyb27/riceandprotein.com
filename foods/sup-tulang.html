How Many Calories in Sup Tulang?

Slow-cooked mutton or beef leg bones in a deeply spiced broth, with rich marrow that you suck straight out of the bone.

350kcal / 1 bowl (2-3 bones with broth)
A bowl of Sup Tulang comes in at around 350 calories for a standard serving of 2 to 3 bones with broth (about 400g total). Most of that comes from the bone marrow alone, which clocks in at roughly 170 calories from just 22g of the stuff. The meat on the bone adds another 140 calories, and the spiced broth keeps things relatively lean at 50 calories. For a full bone soup with that much richness and flavour, 350 calories is honestly pretty reasonable.

The real talk

Sup tulang sits at around 350 calories per bowl for the clear version, and honestly that's not bad at all for how satisfying it is. You're getting 26g of protein, which is solid, but the fat is also 26g and that's mostly coming from the bone marrow. That 22g of marrow packs 170 calories on its own, bro. It's basically concentrated fat in a hollow bone, which is exactly why it tastes so good when you suck it out. The meat off the bone adds another 140 cal, the broth is a light 50 cal, and the tendon and cartilage contribute 30 cal. Worth noting: these are ballpark estimates because detailed nutritional data for sup tulang specifically is quite thin, so treat the 350 cal figure as a reasonable guide rather than a precise count.

The version that actually messes with your tracking is sup tulang merah, which runs around 480 calories per bowl. That extra 130 calories comes from the red rempah sauce made with ghee, tomato puree, chilli paste, and sugar. Same bones, completely different calorie story. So if you're at a gerai and they ask which one you want, the clear broth saves you a meaningful chunk. Practical tips: skip soaking your bread in the broth (that soaked-in roti adds calories fast), ask for less sauce on the merah version, and don't go back for a second helping of the marrow bones if you're watching intake. The soup itself is actually your friend here. It fills you up, it's warm, and the protein from the meat is real.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order the clear broth version (sup tulang) instead of merah to save around 130 calories per bowl.
  • Skip dipping bread or roti into the broth. It tastes great but adds calories quickly and there's no fixed amount people stop at.
  • Eat the meat off the bone but go easy on how much marrow you suck out. Each bone has roughly 7 to 8g of marrow, and it adds up fast.
  • Ask the stall to go light on the ghee or oil if you're getting the merah version. Some places are more generous than others.
  • Pair it with plain rice rather than nasi goreng or roti on the side. The soup is already filling enough on its own.

Common questions

How many calories in Sup Tulang?
A standard bowl of sup tulang (clear broth, 2 to 3 bones with broth, around 400g) is approximately 350 calories. The sup tulang merah version with red rempah sauce is higher, around 480 calories.
Is Sup Tulang good for weight loss?
It's not bad, honestly. 350 calories for a bowl that's warm, filling, and has 26g of protein is a reasonable deal. The issue is the bone marrow, which is pure fat. If you're in a deficit, clear broth sup tulang can fit into your day. Avoid the merah version if you're being strict.
Why does Sup Tulang Merah have so many more calories?
The bones themselves are the same, so the protein and marrow content is identical. The extra calories come from the rempah sauce: ghee, tomato puree, chilli paste, and a bit of sugar. That sauce adds around 130 calories to the bowl compared to the clear broth version.
Berapa kalori dalam Sup Tulang?
Sup Tulang ada kira-kira 350 kalori untuk 1 mangkuk (400g), dengan 26g protein, 4g karbohidrat dan 26g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
