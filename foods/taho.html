How Many Calories in Taho?

Also known as Tahô, Silken Tofu with Arnibal, Tahoooo.

Warm silken tofu ladled into a cup with brown sugar syrup (arnibal) and sago pearls, sold from two metal buckets by the magtataho walking past your house at 7 in the morning.

220kcal / 1 cup (regular, about 250g)
A regular cup of taho is about 220 calories for a 250g serving. The soft tofu is only about 80 calories, the sago pearls about 30, and the arnibal syrup about 110. The syrup is the whole difference between a healthy breakfast and a sugary one.

The real talk

The tofu part is genuinely good for you. Silken tofu is about 55 calories per 100g with 5g of protein, so the 150g in a regular cup is 80 calories and 7g of protein. Kawaling Pinoy's recipe card comes out at 213 calories per serving, which matches a vendor cup. The rest is arnibal: brown sugar boiled into a syrup. A vendor ladles in about 40g of it, which is 110 calories of straight sugar.

Ask for 'konting arnibal' (less syrup) and the cup drops to about 150 calories. Most magtataho will do it without blinking. The sago is minor at about 30 calories for a spoonful, though it is also just starch. A large cup at 30 to 35 pesos is about 350 calories, because the vendor scales the syrup with the tofu.

The strawberry taho from Baguio is a different animal. The strawberry syrup is sugar with fruit flavour and they tend to pour more of it, so a regular cup is closer to 260 calories. Same for the ube and pandan versions at mall stalls, which also add condensed milk. Taho is a 20 peso breakfast that is fine at 220 calories. It is not fine as a 350 calorie large on top of pandesal and coffee.

How to eat it lighter

  • Say 'konting arnibal' and save about 60 calories. Half the syrup, same tofu.
  • Regular cup, not large. The large is 350 calories, and the extra is mostly syrup.
  • Skip the sago if you do not care about it. About 30 calories saved and nothing lost.
  • Make it your protein for the morning instead of an extra: taho plus 1 pandesal is about 330 calories.

Common questions

Is taho healthy?
The tofu is, with about 7g of protein per cup. The arnibal is 110 calories of sugar. Ask for less syrup and it becomes a decent 150 calorie breakfast.
How many calories in taho without syrup?
About 110 calories for the tofu and sago alone. Plain silken tofu is 55 calories per 100g.
How much sugar is in a cup of taho?
About 28 to 30g in a regular cup, nearly all from the arnibal. A large cup is closer to 45g.
Is taho good for weight loss?
It works as a breakfast if it replaces something, not if it is added on top. A regular cup with less syrup is 150 calories and keeps you full for a couple of hours.
