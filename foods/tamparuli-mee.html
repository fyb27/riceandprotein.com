How Many Calories in Tamparuli Mee?

Sabah's handmade springy yellow noodles fried in dark soy sauce, topped with roasted pork belly, BBQ pork, egg roll and scrambled egg, a kedai kopi classic from Tamparuli town.

630kcal / 1 plate
A plate of Tamparuli Mee comes in at around 630 calories for a standard 340g serve. That is for the classic dry version with handmade egg noodles, siew yoke (roasted pork belly), char siew, chun kien (egg roll), and scrambled egg. It is a solid, filling plate and not something you would eat on a deficit without thinking about it, but it is also not some obscene blowout. The 630 figure is a ballpark estimate since hard nutrition data for this dish is basically nonexistent, so take it as a reasonable guide rather than gospel.

The real talk

Tamparuli Mee is a fried noodle dish from Tamparuli, a small town about an hour from Kota Kinabalu in Sabah. For anyone not from Sabah: the noodles here are handmade, thick, springy egg noodles, closer in texture to a good wantan mee noodle but chewier, and they hold the dark soy sauce coating really well. The full plate comes with siew yoke (roasted pork belly), char siew (BBQ pork), and chun kien (a Sabah-style egg roll), all sitting on top. It is not halal. The 630 calorie estimate for a standard plate is exactly that, an estimate, because nobody has actually lab-tested this dish and we are working backwards from the components. The real number could sit anywhere from 580 to 700 depending on how heavy-handed the cook is with oil and how thick they slice the siew yoke. Take it as a reasonable ballpark, not a precise count.

The biggest calorie drivers on this plate are the noodles themselves at around 255 calories and the siew yoke at about 145 calories for just 50g. Pork belly is fatty by nature, so if you get a generous stall that loads it up, you can easily push past 700 calories. The scrambled egg and stir-fry oil together add another 90 calories, which sounds low but oil accumulates fast in a wok. If you are watching the numbers, the practical moves are: eat the standard portion rather than upsizing (saves you roughly 120 calories right there), ask for less oil if the stall allows it, and load up on the veg portion since that whole 70g of greens is only contributing about 40 calories. Swapping siew yoke for a leaner protein like steamed chicken, if the stall offers it, would also knock off 50 to 80 calories without making the plate feel empty.

How to eat it lighter

  • Stick to the standard portion and skip the upsized plate, which saves around 120 calories
  • Ask for less oil when ordering, wok frying adds significant calories and some stalls are more generous than others
  • Go easy on or skip the siew yoke topping if you want the biggest single calorie reduction, pork belly at 145 calories per 50g is the heaviest item on the plate
  • Eat the vegetables first so you fill up on the lowest-calorie part before reaching for more noodles
  • Skip sugary drinks on the side, a kopi susu or teh tarik adds another 100 to 150 calories to what is already a substantial meal

Common questions

How many calories in Tamparuli Mee?
A standard plate of Tamparuli Mee has around 630 calories for approximately 340g. That covers the handmade egg noodles, siew yoke, char siew, chun kien and scrambled egg. An upsized or extra-pork version can push up to 750 calories.
What is Tamparuli Mee?
Tamparuli Mee is a fried noodle dish from Tamparuli town in Sabah, Malaysia. It uses thick handmade springy egg noodles tossed in dark soy sauce, topped with siew yoke (roasted pork belly), char siew (BBQ pork) and chun kien (egg roll). It is a non-halal dish commonly found at Chinese kedai kopi in Sabah.
Is Tamparuli Mee healthy?
It is not a low-calorie dish at 630 calories a plate, but it is not a disaster either. You get 26g of protein which is decent, and the portion is filling. The fat count of 28g is the part to watch, driven mainly by the pork belly and wok oil. If you are on a calorie deficit, it fits fine as a main meal as long as you keep the rest of the day lighter.
Berapa kalori dalam Tamparuli Mee?
Tamparuli Mee ada kira-kira 630 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (340g), dengan 26g protein, 65g karbohidrat dan 28g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
