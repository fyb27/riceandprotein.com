How Many Calories in Tangsuyuk?

Also known as 탕수육, Korean Sweet and Sour Pork, Tangsooyuk.

Tangsuyuk is pork strips in a thick starch batter, deep fried and served with a sweet and sour sauce, poured over or on the side depending on which side of the bumeok versus jjikmeok argument you are on.

590kcal / 1 person's share (about 200g, a third of a small plate)
A single person's share of tangsuyuk, about 200g, is roughly 590 calories, matching the fatsecret.kr 1인분 figure of 591. A whole small plate (소, about 600g) is close to 1,800 calories, which is why it is meant to be shared by 3 people. The batter and sauce are about two thirds of the total.

The real talk

Nobody orders tangsuyuk alone, and that is the problem. A small plate is 1,800 calories, a large one about 2,600, and it arrives next to two bowls of jajangmyeon at 797 each. Split a small plate three ways and each person adds 590 calories to an 800 calorie noodle bowl. That is a 1,400 calorie lunch on a Tuesday.

The pork itself is lean. 130g of loin strips would be about 180 calories before battering. The potato starch batter soaks up oil during the double fry and turns that into 420, and then 70g of the sweet and sour sauce adds another 150 with roughly 25g of sugar. The pineapple, cucumber and onion in the sauce are the only things you can eat freely.

Whether you dip or pour makes a small difference, about 40 calories. Dipping (찍먹) means you use less sauce and the batter stays crisp, so you eat slower. Pouring (부먹) soaks the batter and most people eat the whole puddle. At 18,000 to 25,000 won for a small plate, either way it is an expensive way to eat 600 calories of batter.

How to eat it lighter

  • Share a small plate between 3 or 4 people, not 2. Your share drops from 900 to 450 to 590.
  • Dip, do not pour. You use about a third less sauce, and that is 40 to 50 calories and 8g of sugar.
  • Pick the biggest pieces of meat and leave the batter crumbs and sauce pool on the plate.
  • If it comes with jjamppong or jajangmyeon, eat half the noodles. The set is the calorie problem, not the tangsuyuk alone.
  • Eat all the vegetables and pineapple in the sauce. They are about 20 calories and fill space.

Common questions

How many calories in a small plate of tangsuyuk?
About 1,780 for a whole 소 plate of roughly 600g. That is meant for 3 people; one share is about 590.
Is tangsuyuk high in sugar?
Yes. The sauce is sugar, vinegar and starch, about 25g of sugar in a 70g portion. Dipping instead of pouring cuts that by a third.
Does jjikmeok (dipping) have fewer calories than bumeok (pouring)?
Slightly, about 40 to 50 calories less per share because you use less sauce. It is not a big enough gap to argue about.
How many calories in tangsuyuk with jajangmyeon?
About 1,400 for a bowl of jajangmyeon (797) plus one share of tangsuyuk (590). It is a full day of food for a lot of people.
