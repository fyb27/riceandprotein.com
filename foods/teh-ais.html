How Many Calories in Teh Ais?

Also known as Teh Peng.

Iced milk tea with condensed milk and sugar, around 150 calories a glass.

150kcal / 1 glass
A standard glass of teh ais is about 150 calories. The tea is nothing, but the condensed milk and added sugar do all the work, easily 120 of those calories combined. Order it teh o ais (no milk) and it drops to around 60.

The real talk

Bro that teh ais you order without thinking? Condensed milk plus sugar, that is 150 calories of pure sweet before you even touch food. The tea leaves contribute basically nothing, it is the sweetened condensed milk that turns it into a calorie drink.

Three teh ais a day is a meal you drank by accident, around 450 calories you did not even chew. If you are trying to lose weight and cannot figure out why it is stuck, count your drinks first. Most people are shocked when they add it up.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order teh o ais (no milk) to cut about 90 calories
  • Ask for kurang manis to slash the sugar
  • Switch to teh o ais limau, lime tea with sugar only, light and refreshing

Common questions

How many calories in teh ais?
About 150 calories a standard glass, mostly from condensed milk and sugar.
Is teh ais bad for weight loss?
Not on its own, but two or three a day adds up to a hidden meal. Watch the count or switch to teh o ais.
Is teh o ais lighter?
Yes, much. No condensed milk means around 60 calories instead of 150.
Berapa kalori dalam Teh Ais?
Teh Ais ada kira-kira 150 kalori untuk 1 gelas (300g), dengan 3g protein, 28g karbohidrat dan 3g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
