How Many Calories in Teh C?

Tea with evaporated milk and sugar, about 130 calories, slightly lighter than teh ais.

130kcal / 1 glass
Teh C is tea made with evaporated milk instead of condensed, plus sugar, landing around 130 calories a glass. The evaporated milk is unsweetened, so the sweetness comes from the sugar they add separately. That makes it a touch lighter than teh ais if you control the sugar.

The real talk

Teh C is the tea with evaporated milk, the C is for Carnation, the old milk brand. Because the milk is unsweetened, you have more control here. The sugar is added on the side, so kurang manis actually makes a real dent, unlike condensed milk where the sweet is baked in.

Standard teh C is about 130 calories, so a little lighter than teh ais. Not a huge difference, but if you drink a few a day it adds up in your favour. Order it kosong, just tea and evaporated milk with no sugar, and you are down around 70.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order kurang manis, the sugar is added separately so this really helps
  • Go kosong (no sugar) and rely on the milk for body, around 70 calories
  • Switch to teh o limau for a sugar-only lime tea that feels lighter

Common questions

How many calories in teh C?
Around 130 calories a glass with sugar. Kosong (no sugar) drops to about 70.
Is teh C healthier than teh ais?
Slightly. Evaporated milk is unsweetened, so you control the sugar yourself. Order kurang manis and it beats teh ais.
What does the C in teh C mean?
It stands for Carnation, the evaporated milk brand. Teh C is tea with evaporated milk.
Berapa kalori dalam Teh C?
Teh C ada kira-kira 130 kalori untuk 1 gelas (300g), dengan 4g protein, 22g karbohidrat dan 3g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
