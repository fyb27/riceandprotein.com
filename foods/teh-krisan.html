How Many Calories in Chrysanthemum Tea (Teh Krisan)?

Brewed chrysanthemum flower tea with sugar, around 90 calories, light if you skip the syrup.

90kcal / 1 glass
Teh krisan, chrysanthemum tea, is around 90 calories a glass. The flower brew itself has almost no calories, so everything you are drinking is the sugar they add. Order it without sugar and it is basically a free drink, close to zero.

The real talk

Chrysanthemum tea is one of those drinks people order thinking it is healthy, and you know what, the base actually is. It is just brewed flowers, no calories at all. The only reason it shows up at 90 calories is the sugar or rock sugar they stir in.

So this one is fully in your control. Kosong, no sugar, and you are drinking something that is genuinely close to zero calories with a nice floral taste. If you want a sweet mamak drink that will not hurt you, this is one of the smartest picks on the menu.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it kosong (no sugar), drops to nearly zero calories
  • Ask for kurang manis if you still want a bit of sweetness
  • Drink it hot, you tend to need less sugar to enjoy it

Common questions

How many calories in chrysanthemum tea?
About 90 calories a glass with sugar. Without sugar it is nearly zero.
Is chrysanthemum tea good for weight loss?
Yes, the brew itself has no calories. Order it kosong and it is one of the lightest drinks around.
Does teh krisan have caffeine?
No, it is a flower tea, not a real tea leaf, so it is naturally caffeine-free.
Berapa kalori dalam Chrysanthemum Tea?
Chrysanthemum Tea ada kira-kira 90 kalori untuk 1 gelas (300g), dengan 0g protein, 22g karbohidrat dan 0g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
