How Many Calories in Teh O Limau Ais?

Also known as Teh O Ais Limau, Teh O Limau Ais.

Iced black tea with calamansi lime and sugar, no milk, making it one of the lighter drink options at any mamak or kopitiam.

70kcal / 1 glass (hawker, normal sweet)
A standard glass of Teh O Limau Ais is around 70 calories, almost entirely from the sugar the hawker adds. The black tea itself contributes basically nothing (about 2 calories), and the lime juice adds maybe 8. So the whole drink is basically just sweetened lime tea over ice. Order kurang manis and you are down to about 40 calories. These are ballpark estimates as hard data for this specific drink is thin, but the numbers are in the right range.

The real talk

Teh O Limau Ais is iced black tea with calamansi lime and sugar, no condensed milk involved. That is exactly why it is lighter than teh tarik, which can clock 130 to 180 calories from condensed milk alone. At 70 calories for a standard glass, it is honestly one of the better drink choices at a mamak. The problem is the word 'standard' does different things in different shops. Some hawkers have a heavy hand with the sugar, and a manis-heavy pour can push you to 100 calories easily. These numbers are estimates, bro, there is no lab testing happening here, so treat them as a ballpark.

What actually moves the calorie count is purely the sugar. The tea and lime are basically free calories. About three and a half teaspoons of sugar gets you to 70 calories. Four teaspoons lands at 100. One and a half teaspoons (kurang manis) puts you at about 40. If you are tracking, just say kurang manis every single time and you shave off 30 calories per glass. Over a day of two or three drinks, that adds up. Nothing else changes here, no oil, no protein, no noodle swap to worry about.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order kurang manis every time, drops you from 70 to about 40 calories per glass.
  • If you want zero sugar, ask for teh o limau kosong. Pure tea and lime, basically zero calories.
  • Stick to one glass per meal rather than getting free refills at those all-you-can-drink spots.
  • Compare to teh tarik at 130 to 180 calories or milo ais at 250 plus, and teh o limau ais is already the smart pick lah.
  • Squeeze extra lime yourself if the stall has fresh limes on the table. More lime flavour means you notice the sweetness less even with less sugar.

Common questions

How many calories in Teh O Limau Ais?
About 70 calories for a standard hawker glass at normal sweetness. Kurang manis brings it down to around 40 calories, and extra sweet can push it to 100. Almost all the calories come from sugar.
What exactly is Teh O Limau Ais?
It is iced black tea with calamansi lime juice and sugar, no milk. The 'O' means black (no milk) and 'limau' is lime. You will find it at pretty much every mamak, kopitiam, and hawker centre in Malaysia.
Is Teh O Limau Ais a healthy drink choice?
Compared to most Malaysian drinks it is one of the better options. No condensed milk, no Milo powder, no coconut milk. The only real calories come from sugar, so just say kurang manis and you have a 40-calorie drink that tastes good and keeps you hydrated. Not bad at all.
Berapa kalori dalam Teh O Limau Ais?
Teh O Limau Ais ada kira-kira 70 kalori untuk 1 gelas (250g), dengan 0g protein, 17g karbohidrat dan 0g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
