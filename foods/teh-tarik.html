How Many Calories in Teh Tarik?

Pulled milk tea. The tea is free. The condensed milk and sugar are the entire calorie count.

130kcal / 1 glass
A standard glass of teh tarik is about 130 calories. The tea itself has almost none. Every calorie comes from the condensed milk and added sugar, which is why teh o kosong is basically free.

The real talk

Here is the eye opener bro. The tea in teh tarik is zero calories. The 130 calories are entirely condensed milk and sugar. People sip three or four of these a day without thinking and that is 400 to 500 calories of pure liquid sugar that does not fill you up at all.

Drink calories are the most wasted calories in the Malaysian diet. They taste good for ten seconds and keep you full for zero minutes. If you do one thing for your waistline, swap your sweet drinks for kosong versions. Teh o kosong (tea, no milk, no sugar) is about 5 calories. Same caffeine, same ritual, none of the cost.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order teh o kosong (no milk, no sugar). Drops from 130 to about 5 calories.
  • Or teh tarik kurang manis (less sweet) for a middle ground.
  • Count your drinks. Three sweet teh tarik a day is nearly 400 calories you did not notice.

Common questions

How many calories in teh tarik?
About 130 calories a glass, almost all of it from condensed milk and sugar. The tea itself is essentially zero calories.
Is teh tarik fattening?
On its own one glass is not much, but the habit is the problem. Three or four a day is 400 to 500 liquid calories that do not fill you up at all.
What is the lowest calorie kopitiam drink?
Teh o kosong or plain water, both essentially zero calories. Teh o kosong gives you the same tea ritual without the condensed milk and sugar.
