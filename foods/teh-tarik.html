How Many Calories in Teh Tarik?

Pulled milk tea. The tea is free. The condensed milk and sugar are the entire calorie count.

130kcal / 1 glass
A standard glass of teh tarik is about 130 calories. The tea itself has almost none. Every calorie comes from the condensed milk and added sugar, which is why teh o kosong is basically free.

The real talk

Here is the eye opener bro. The tea in teh tarik is zero calories. The 130 calories are entirely condensed milk and sugar. People sip three or four of these a day without thinking and that is 400 to 500 calories of pure liquid sugar that does not fill you up at all.

Drink calories are the most wasted calories in the Malaysian diet. They taste good for ten seconds and keep you full for zero minutes. If you do one thing for your waistline, swap your sweet drinks for kosong versions. Teh o kosong (tea, no milk, no sugar) is about 5 calories. Same caffeine, same ritual, none of the cost.

Here is the yearly math that changed my behaviour. Two teh tarik a day is 260 calories, times 365 days is about 95,000 calories a year. That is roughly 12kg of body fat, from tea. Nobody gets fat from one glass. People get fat from the automatic twice a day habit that runs for years.

Know your kopitiam menu codes and you can save calories without giving anything up. Teh c uses evaporated milk instead of condensed, around 100 calories. Teh c kosong is evaporated milk with no sugar, around 40. Teh o is tea with sugar only, around 60. Teh o kosong is the champion at basically 5. Same tea, five different prices for your waistline.

The reason drink calories are so dangerous is that they do not register. Liquid sugar does not trip your fullness switch the way food does. Your body simply does not count the teh tarik, so you eat the same amount of food anyway, plus the 130.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order teh o kosong (no milk, no sugar). Drops from 130 to about 5 calories.
  • Or teh tarik kurang manis (less sweet) for a middle ground.
  • Count your drinks. Three sweet teh tarik a day is nearly 400 calories you did not notice.
  • Give yourself one sweet drink slot a day, with your favourite meal. Every other drink is kosong.

Common questions

How many calories in teh tarik?
About 130 calories a glass, almost all of it from condensed milk and sugar. The tea itself is essentially zero calories.
Is teh tarik fattening?
On its own one glass is not much, but the habit is the problem. Three or four a day is 400 to 500 liquid calories that do not fill you up at all.
What is the lowest calorie kopitiam drink?
Teh o kosong or plain water, both essentially zero calories. Teh o kosong gives you the same tea ritual without the condensed milk and sugar.
How many calories in teh tarik kurang manis?
Around 95 calories. Kurang manis usually means the same condensed milk but less added sugar, so it helps, but teh c kosong at around 40 calories is the bigger saving.
What is the difference between teh tarik and teh c?
Teh tarik uses condensed milk, which is milk plus a lot of sugar, around 130 calories a glass. Teh c uses evaporated milk, which is unsweetened, so around 100 calories with the standard sugar, or 40 if you order it kosong.
How much weight can I lose by switching to teh o kosong?
If you currently drink two teh tarik a day, switching saves about 250 calories daily. That is roughly 1kg of fat every five to six weeks, from changing nothing except your drink order.
Berapa kalori dalam Teh Tarik?
Teh Tarik ada kira-kira 130 kalori untuk 1 gelas (250g), dengan 3g protein, 22g karbohidrat dan 4g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
