How Many Calories in Telur Balado?

Also known as Telur Bumbu Balado, Telor Balado, Telur Sambal Merah, Egg in Chili Sambal.

Telur balado is a hard-boiled egg fried until the outside blisters, then tossed in a chili, shallot and tomato sambal. It sits in the window of every warteg and nasi Padang shop in Indonesia.

130kcal / 1 egg with sambal
One telur balado is about 130 calories: 72 for the egg, about 35 for the oil it picks up when fried, and around 25 for the sambal balado. FatSecret Indonesia lists it at 71 calories per egg, but that figure does not account for the frying, so 130 is the honest warung number.

The real talk

A hard-boiled egg is 72 calories. Warteg telur balado is not just a boiled egg. It goes into hot oil until the white blisters, which is what lets the sambal stick, and that step adds about 4g of oil, 35 calories. Then the sambal, which is chili, shallot and tomato ground and fried in oil, another 25 for a coating. So 130 per egg, and 7g protein.

That still makes it the cheapest protein on the shelf. At Rp 3,000 to 5,000 an egg, two telur balado is 260 calories and 14g protein, which is more protein than a piece of tempe goreng and less fat than a piece of ayam goreng at 390. With a plate of rice and a spoon of sayur, two eggs is a 500 calorie warteg lunch.

The Padang version (telur balado at a nasi Padang shop) is oilier because the balado is cooked longer with more oil and sits in a tray of it. Expect 150 per egg there. The Manado telur rica-rica version is similar. Telur dadar (omelette) is a different thing entirely, about 150 to 200 for one thick warteg slice because it is fried in far more oil.

The sambal is where the calories vary. A light coating is 25 calories. If the server gives you an extra spoon of the red oil from the bottom of the tray, that is 45 more.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for the egg 'tanpa minyak' or shake off the oil before it goes on the plate. The sambal oil is 45 calories a spoon.
  • Telur rebus or telur pindang (plain boiled or spiced boiled) is 72 to 75 calories. Same protein, no frying.
  • Two telur balado and no other fried lauk is a good warteg plate at 260 calories for 14g protein.
  • Pair with sayur bening or sayur asem instead of sayur lodeh. Clear vegetables are 60 to 80 per bowl versus 160.
  • At home, skip the frying step and put the sambal straight on the boiled egg. About 95 calories.

Common questions

How many calories in telur balado?
About 130 calories per egg at a warteg: the egg is 72, the frying adds about 35 and the sambal about 25. FatSecret lists 71 for the egg alone.
Is telur balado good for a diet?
Yes. It is 7g protein for 130 calories and costs Rp 3,000 to 5,000. Two eggs with rice and a clear vegetable soup is a balanced warteg meal around 500 calories.
How many calories in telur balado with rice?
About 390 calories for one egg with a 200g plate of rice, or 520 with two eggs.
Is telur balado high in fat?
About 10g fat per egg, 5g from the yolk and the rest from frying oil and sambal. Boiled egg in sambal without frying drops it to 6g.
Berapa kalori Telur Balado?
Telur Balado mengandung sekitar 130 kalori per satu porsi biasa (70g), dengan 7g protein, 3g karbohidrat dan 10g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
