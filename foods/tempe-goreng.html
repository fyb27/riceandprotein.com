How Many Calories in Tempe Goreng?

Also known as Tempeh Goreng, Tempe Goreng Tepung, Tempe Mendoan, Fried Tempeh.

Tempe goreng is sliced fermented soybean cake, marinated in garlic, coriander and salt, and shallow fried until golden. It is the default protein side at every warteg in Indonesia.

190kcal / 2 pieces (warung size)
Two warung-sized pieces of tempe goreng are about 190 calories with 12g protein. FatSecret Indonesia lists 225 calories per 100g fried and 192 for an 85g serving. Tempe mendoan with a thick flour batter is about 120 per piece.

The real talk

Raw tempe is about 190 calories per 100g and 19g protein, which is already one of the best protein deals in Indonesia at Rp 5,000 a block. Frying pushes that to about 225 per 100g because the slices soak up oil, roughly 5g per two pieces. So the frying costs you 45 calories and gives you the crunch. A warung piece is 35 to 45g, so two is about 190.

The version matters. Plain tempe goreng bumbu (marinated, no batter) is the lightest. Tempe goreng tepung has a thin batter that adds about 20 calories a piece. Tempe mendoan from Purwokerto is a thin slice in a thick soft batter, fried briefly, about 120 calories each, and the batter is more than the tempe. Tempe orek (fried shreds in kecap manis) is about 90 for a 40g spoon and a third of it is sugar.

At a warteg, tempe goreng is Rp 2,000 to 3,000 a piece and people take two or three next to rice and a vegetable. Three pieces is 285 calories with 18g protein, which is more protein than a piece of chicken skin and less fat. It is one of the few fried things here that is worth it.

The catch is the oil quality. Warteg oil gets reused a lot, and thick pieces fried in old oil at low heat absorb more. Thin, hot, quick fried tempe is both tastier and lighter.

How to eat it lighter

  • Tempe bacem or tempe rebus (braised or steamed) is about 60 calories per piece against 95 fried, though bacem has sugar.
  • Plain marinated tempe goreng over mendoan. The thick batter doubles the calories.
  • Two pieces is a good protein side. Four pieces is a fried meal.
  • At home, air fry or pan fry with a teaspoon of oil. Tempe crisps without deep frying.
  • Skip tempe orek if you are watching sugar. It is fried and then glazed in kecap manis.

Common questions

How many calories in tempe goreng?
About 95 calories per warung-sized piece and 190 for two, with 12g protein. FatSecret Indonesia lists 225 calories per 100g fried.
Is tempe goreng good for weight loss?
Yes, as fried food goes. Two pieces give 12g protein for 190 calories, better than most fried sides. Steamed or bacem tempe is lighter still.
How many calories in tempe mendoan?
About 120 calories per piece. The thick flour batter holds more oil than the tempe itself.
Does frying tempe destroy the protein?
No. Frying adds oil calories but the protein stays, around 6g per piece. What changes is the fat, from about 4g raw to 6g fried per piece.
Berapa kalori Tempe Goreng?
Tempe Goreng mengandung sekitar 190 kalori per 2 potong (80g), dengan 12g protein, 10g karbohidrat dan 12g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
