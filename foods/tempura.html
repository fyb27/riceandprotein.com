How Many Calories in Tempura?

Also known as 天ぷら, てんぷら, Tempura Moriawase, Assorted Tempura, Ebi Tempura.

Prawns, sweet potato, pumpkin, aubergine, shiso and other vegetables dipped in a cold flour and egg batter and fried in light oil. Served as a plate with tentsuyu dipping sauce, on rice as tendon, or on top of udon and soba.

480kcal / 1 assorted plate (2 prawns + 4 vegetables)
A standard assorted tempura plate with 2 prawns and 4 pieces of vegetable is about 480 calories. One prawn tempura is about 85 calories, one piece of kabocha or sweet potato about 90, and a kakiage fritter 120 to 180. On rice as a tendon, the meal reaches 750 to 850 calories.

The real talk

A raw prawn is 15 calories. The same prawn as tempura is 85. That is the whole story of tempura in one line. The batter is flour and egg, which adds about 30 calories per piece, and then the batter soaks up frying oil, which adds another 40. Tempura is 20 to 25 percent oil by weight when it comes out of the fryer, so the light and crispy texture is doing a lot of hiding.

Vegetables are worse than they look. A slice of kabocha or sweet potato is 90 calories as tempura because the batter clings to the flat surface and the vegetable itself is starchy. Shishito peppers and shiso leaves are the cheap ones at about 30 calories each because they are thin and barely weigh anything. Kakiage, the fritter of shredded vegetables and small shrimp, is the heaviest single item on the plate at 120 to 180 calories because it is basically a puck of batter.

The way tempura is served decides the number. A 480 calorie plate with grated daikon and tentsuyu is a reasonable main. The same pieces on 280g of rice with sweet tare sauce as a tendon is 800 calories. On a bowl of udon it is 370 to 450 calories. At a good Tokyo counter, a 10 piece omakase course runs 1,000 calories and about 8,000 yen, so at least the price slows you down.

How to eat it lighter

  • Pick the plate over the tendon. The rice and sweet tare add 300 to 350 calories.
  • Choose prawns, shishito and shiso over kabocha and sweet potato. Each swap saves 50 to 60 calories.
  • Skip the kakiage. It is the one piece that is mostly batter.
  • Use the grated daikon and tentsuyu generously. They add almost nothing and the acid cuts the oil feeling.
  • At Marugame or Hanamaru, one prawn tempura on a kake udon is about 380 calories, a filling lunch.

Common questions

How many calories in one ebi tempura?
About 85 calories for a standard prawn tempura. The prawn is 15 of that, the batter and oil are the other 70.
Is tempura unhealthy?
It is deep-fried, so it carries 20 to 25 percent oil by weight. A 480 calorie plate is fine as a main once a week. The issue is tendon at 800 calories or tempura as a side to a full meal.
Which tempura has the fewest calories?
Shiso leaf and shishito pepper at about 30 each. Then prawn and squid at 80 to 90, then kabocha and sweet potato at 90 to 100, and kakiage at 120 to 180.
How many calories in tempura sauce?
Tentsuyu is about 15 calories per small dish. Tendon tare, the sweet thick sauce on tendon, is closer to 60 to 80 because of the sugar.
