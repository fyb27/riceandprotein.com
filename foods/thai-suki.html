How Many Calories in Thai Suki?

Also known as สุกี้, Suki Nam, Thai Sukiyaki, Suki Haeng, MK Suki.

Thai style sukiyaki: glass noodles, egg, cabbage, morning glory and your choice of chicken, pork or seafood, cooked in a light broth (nam) or stir fried without it (haeng), then eaten with a red suki sauce made from chilli, garlic, sesame and fermented bean curd.

350kcal / 1 bowl (suki nam)
A one person bowl of suki nam (soup version) is about 350 calories, with 25g of protein from the chicken and egg. The dry stir fried suki haeng is about 450 because it picks up oil in the wok. At an MK style hotpot restaurant, one person's share of a table order usually lands at 600 to 800, mostly because of the sauce and the second round of meat.

The real talk

Suki is one of the better meals in Thailand for the number, because it is a vegetable and protein dish that happens to have some noodles in it. A bowl of suki nam has 120g of cabbage and morning glory at 30 calories, 80g of chicken at 100, an egg at 75 and 80g of cooked glass noodles at 100. The broth is 10. That is 315 before the sauce, and it fills a bowl the size of your head.

The suki sauce is where it goes wrong. It is sugar, chilli, garlic and sesame oil, about 40 calories per tablespoon, and nobody uses one tablespoon. At a hotpot restaurant the sauce bowl gets refilled 3 or 4 times, which is 150 to 200 calories of sauce alone. The MGR table lists suki gai nam at 345 for the bowl, which matches the street version with a normal spoon of sauce stirred in. The dry version adds 15g of oil in the wok, another 100.

MK and the hotpot chains are a different game. The food is the same, but the format is a table full of plates and a 2 hour sitting. A normal share per person is 200g of meat and seafood, 2 balls of fish paste, a plate of noodles and a lot of sauce. That lands at 700 to 800, with the fried tofu and crispy pork skin plates pushing it higher. The vegetables are still free. The problem is that nobody goes to MK for the vegetables.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order suki nam, not haeng. The soup version saves about 100 calories of wok oil.
  • One spoon of suki sauce stirred in, not a bowl on the side for dipping. Each refill is 100 to 150 calories.
  • Double the vegetables and drop the noodles to half. The cabbage and morning glory are 30 calories for the whole bowl.
  • At a hotpot place, load the pot with the greens, tofu and lean meat first and skip the fried tofu and fish balls.
  • Seafood suki (talay) is about 20 calories lower than chicken and 60 lower than pork belly.

Common questions

Is Thai suki healthy?
Suki nam is one of the better Thai meals at 350 calories with 25g of protein and a big pile of vegetables. The sauce is the thing to watch.
How many calories in MK suki?
A normal one person share of a table order is 600 to 800 depending on the meat, the fried extras and how many bowls of sauce you go through.
Suki nam or suki haeng, which is lower?
Suki nam at about 350. Haeng is stir fried and picks up about 100 calories of oil.
How many calories in suki sauce?
About 40 per tablespoon. It is sugar, chilli, garlic and sesame oil. A dipping bowl refilled 3 times is 150 or more.
