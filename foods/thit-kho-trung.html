How Many Calories in Thit Kho Trung?

Also known as Thịt kho trứng, Thịt kho tàu, Thịt kho hột vịt, Vietnamese Caramelised Pork Belly with Eggs.

Cubes of pork belly simmered with duck or chicken eggs in coconut water, fish sauce and nuoc mau caramel until the fat turns to jelly and the sauce goes dark and sweet. The Tet dish in the south, and the Tuesday dish at every com binh dan tray, 25,000 to 40,000 dong with rice.

460kcal / 1 serving (3 pieces belly, 1 egg)
A normal com binh dan serving of thit kho trung, about 100g of braised belly and one duck egg with sauce, is around 460 calories. Long Chau gives 350 to 550 per portion and works a family pot of 500g belly and 5 eggs out at about 487 per person. The belly is 300 of it, the egg 130, and the caramel sauce 30 to 40.

The real talk

Pork belly is 30 to 35 percent fat raw, and thit kho is cooked slowly enough that the fat softens rather than renders out, so you eat all of it. Long Chau's numbers: 100g of lean pork is 145 calories, belly is 260, and the fatty cuts used for kho are up to 394. A com binh dan scoop is 3 cubes, around 100g, at 300 calories once the sauce has gone in. Ask for nac (lean) pieces and the same scoop is 170.

The egg is the part people miscount. Thit kho in the south uses hot vit, duck eggs, at about 130 calories each, against 78 for a chicken egg and 14 for a quail egg. A serving with 2 duck eggs is 590. The sauce is coconut water (Coca Cola at some quan), fish sauce and 20g or so of caramelised sugar for the pot, so 30 to 40 calories per person, but it soaks into the rice and pushes you to eat more of it.

The meal is the problem more than the dish. Thit kho trung at 460, a com binh dan plate of rice at 300 and the free bowl of canh is 800 calories for lunch, and a lot of that is fat, 34g in the pork alone. Over Tet, when the pot sits on the stove for 3 days and people eat from it at every meal alongside banh tet, the 460 becomes 1,400 a day from one dish. That is why everyone comes back from Tet 2kg heavier.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for thit nac, the lean end of the belly or shoulder. About 170 per scoop instead of 300.
  • One egg, not two. A duck egg is 130; swap for a chicken egg at 78 if the quan has them.
  • Spoon the meat, leave the sauce. It carries most of the sugar and the fat that has melted into it.
  • Take it com, a smaller rice portion. The sweet sauce makes 300g of rice disappear.
  • Skim the fat off a home pot after it cools in the fridge. Saves 100 or more per serving.

Common questions

How many calories in thit kho trung?
About 460 for a serving with 100g of belly and one duck egg. Long Chau puts a portion at 350 to 550, with the range depending on how fatty the pork is and how many eggs you take.
How many calories in one piece of thit kho?
Around 100 for a 35g cube of belly with sauce, or 55 to 60 for a lean piece the same size.
Is thit kho trung healthy?
It is high in protein at 26g per serving but also 34g of fat, mostly saturated, plus sugar and salt in the sauce. Fine once a week with a lot of vegetables; not a daily dish if you are trying to lose weight.
Thit kho trung or ca kho to, which is lighter?
Ca kho to at 300 for a clay pot versus 460 for thit kho trung. Fish is leaner than belly, and both come with the same rice.
