How Many Calories in Three Layer Tea (Teh C Peng Special)?

Teh C with a gula melaka palm sugar bottom layer, around 210 calories.

210kcal / 1 glass
Three layer tea, or teh C peng special, is around 210 calories a glass. It is teh C with a thick layer of gula melaka palm sugar syrup sitting at the bottom, which is where the three layers come from. That gula melaka layer is basically concentrated sugar, so it pushes the calories well above a normal teh C.

The real talk

Three layer tea looks fancy, the gula melaka sinks to the bottom, evaporated milk in the middle, tea on top. Looks like art. But that beautiful bottom layer is a thick pour of palm sugar syrup, and that is the whole reason it costs more calories than a plain teh C.

You are looking at around 210 calories, mostly because of that gula melaka. It does taste amazing, the palm sugar gives it that deep caramel thing. Just know it is a sweeter, heavier cousin of teh C, so order it as a treat and not your everyday drink lah.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less gula melaka, the bottom layer is the calorie bomb
  • Order a plain teh C kurang manis instead for everyday drinking
  • Share a glass if you just want a taste of the palm sugar

Common questions

How many calories in three layer tea?
Around 210 calories a glass. The gula melaka bottom layer is the main reason it is high.
What are the three layers?
Gula melaka syrup at the bottom, evaporated milk in the middle, tea on top. It is a fancy teh C.
Is three layer tea worse than teh C?
Yes, the extra gula melaka adds about 80 calories over a normal teh C.
Berapa kalori dalam Three Layer Tea?
Three Layer Tea ada kira-kira 210 kalori untuk 1 gelas (350g), dengan 4g protein, 38g karbohidrat dan 4g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
