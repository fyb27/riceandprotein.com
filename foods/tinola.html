How Many Calories in Tinola?

Also known as Tinolang Manok, Chicken Tinola, Ginger Chicken Soup.

Chicken simmered in ginger broth with green papaya or sayote, malunggay or chili leaves, and fish sauce. The sick-day soup, the rainy-day soup, and the cheapest ulam at the carinderia.

300kcal / 1 bowl
A carinderia bowl of tinolang manok, about 400g with one bone-in chicken piece, papaya and leaves, is around 300 calories. The chicken is 200 of that, the ginger broth about 30, the papaya 30, and the oil used to sauté the ginger and garlic 45. It is the lightest of tinola, sinigang and bulalo.

The real talk

Tinola broth is water, ginger, garlic, onion and patis. A cup of it is about 15 calories, and even after the chicken simmers in it for 30 minutes it stays under 30 because chicken thigh does not shed fat the way pork belly does. The one place calories sneak in is the start, when the cook fries the ginger and garlic in a tablespoon of oil (about 90 calories for the pot, 45 per bowl if the pot serves two).

The chicken piece is the whole number. A bone-in thigh or leg with skin, about 120g cooked, is 200 calories with 22g of protein. A skinless breast piece of the same weight is 140. Native chicken (manok na bisaya) is leaner and tougher and lands around 160 for the same piece, which is why the long-simmered Visayan tinola tastes different and runs lighter.

The green papaya and malunggay are practically free, about 30 calories per 80g of papaya and 10 per handful of leaves, and they carry most of the volume. A 400g bowl is mostly broth and vegetable by weight. With 1 cup of rice the meal is 560, which is one of the better carinderia lunches you can order for 60 to 80 pesos.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for the breast piece or take the skin off. About 60 calories less per bowl.
  • Ask for extra papaya or sayote. It is 30 calories a serving and it fills the bowl.
  • One cup of rice. Tinola broth on rice is good enough that people go back, and that second cup is 260.
  • Squeeze calamansi and add fresh chili instead of extra patis. Zero calories, more flavour.

Common questions

How many calories in tinola with rice?
About 560 calories for a standard bowl with 1 cup of rice. The soup alone is around 300.
Is tinola good for weight loss?
It is one of the best carinderia choices. About 300 calories a bowl with 26g of protein, and most of the volume is broth, papaya and leaves. Pick the breast piece and it drops to 230.
How many calories in tinola broth only?
About 15 to 30 calories per cup. Ginger, garlic, onion and fish sauce add almost nothing, and chicken thigh sheds very little fat into it.
Which is lower in calories, tinola or sinigang?
Tinola, at about 300 per bowl versus 320 for pork sinigang. The gap widens if the sinigang uses pork belly, which can push it past 400.
