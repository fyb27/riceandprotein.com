How Many Calories in Tod Mun Pla?

Also known as ทอดมันปลา, Thai Fish Cakes, Tod Man Pla, Thai Fried Fish Cake.

Fish paste pounded with red curry paste, long beans and kaffir lime leaf, shaped into flat discs and deep fried until they puff. Sold by the piece at markets with a cucumber, peanut and sweet chilli dip (ajad).

300kcal / 5 pieces
A 5 piece order of street tod mun pla, about 25g each, is roughly 300 calories including the ajad dip. That is about 60 calories per fish cake. The restaurant size pieces are bigger and heavier, around 230 calories for a single 80g cake, so count what you are actually holding.

The real talk

Fish paste on its own is about 130 calories per 100g and mostly protein. Tod mun pla ends up at 240 per 100g because the cakes go into a wok of oil and soak up about 12g of it per 5 pieces. That is 105 calories of frying oil in a 300 calorie snack, a third of the total. The curry paste and the egg that bind it add another 35. The dip is 30 for a small bowl, and most of that is the sugar in the vinegar and the crushed peanut on top.

Size is the whole story. The market pieces are 25g, thin and crisp, 60 calories each. The restaurant version served on a plate with a proper ajad is 60 to 80g per cake and the MGR calorie table puts one at 230. Five of those is over 1,000. Same name, four times the number. If you are ordering at a sit down place, 2 pieces is a portion, not 5.

As a snack tod mun pla is a decent trade. Twenty grams of protein in 300 calories beats almost anything else at the same cart, where a bag of fried bananas is 400 with 3g of protein. The mistake is eating it as a side to a full meal, a plate of fried fish cakes next to khao pad and tom yum. Then it is a 300 calorie garnish.

How to eat it lighter

  • Buy 3 pieces, not 5. Three market size cakes is 180 calories and enough to kill the craving.
  • Ask for pieces from the front of the tray. They have drained longer and carry less oil.
  • Skip the peanut on the ajad or ask for the plain cucumber. Saves about 20 calories and the crunch comes from the cucumber anyway.
  • At a restaurant, share the plate. One large cake is about 230 on its own.
  • Grilled fish cakes on a stick (pla sadet yang) at the same market are about 40 calories each with no frying oil.

Common questions

How many calories in one Thai fish cake?
About 60 for a 25g market piece and around 230 for a large 80g restaurant cake. The size difference is bigger than most people expect.
Is tod mun pla high in protein?
Yes for a fried snack. Five small pieces carry about 20g of protein in 300 calories.
Is tod mun pla healthy?
It is deep fried, so a third of the calories are oil. As a 3 to 5 piece snack it is a reasonable choice. As a side to a full meal it is 300 extra calories.
What is the dip served with tod mun pla?
Ajad, a sweet vinegar dip with cucumber, shallot, chilli and crushed peanut. About 30 calories for a small bowl, mostly sugar.
