How Many Calories in Tom Saap?

Also known as ต้มแซ่บ, ต้มแซ่บกระดูกหมู, Tom Saep, Tom Zaab, Isan Spicy Pork Rib Soup.

The Isan cousin of tom yum: a clear, sour and very spicy broth with pork ribs or soft bones, lemongrass, galangal, lime leaf, lime juice, chili, toasted rice powder and a handful of sawtooth coriander. No coconut milk, no chili paste, no oil.

270kcal / 1 bowl
A 350g bowl of tom saap kraduk moo is about 270 calories, with 22g of protein. The broth is essentially free at under 20 calories; the pork ribs, roughly 90g of edible meat and fat once the bone is out, are around 230. Tom saap gai (chicken) is about 200 a bowl, and the big hot pot version served on a burner is closer to 400 per person.

The real talk

Tom saap is the lightest soup on an Isan menu that still counts as a meal. The broth is water, lemongrass, galangal, lime leaf, lime juice, fish sauce and chili, which comes to maybe 15 calories per bowl. Compare tom kha at 320 a bowl, where 180 calories are coconut milk, or tom yum nam khon with its chili paste and evaporated milk. Everything in a tom saap bowl that has calories is the meat.

The meat is pork ribs or kraduk on (soft cartilage bones). A stall bowl carries around 180g of bone in ribs, which gives roughly 90g of edible meat and fat at about 230 calories. Ribs are a fatty cut, so the fat number for the bowl is 18g even though nothing was fried. Pick tom saap moo sam chan (belly) and the bowl climbs to 380; pick the chicken version and it falls to about 200.

The catch is the rice. Tom saap comes with a bag of khao niao or a plate of rice, and that is where a 270 calorie bowl becomes a 520 calorie lunch. Still fine. The bowl also comes with toasted rice powder and chili flakes stirred in at the table, about 20 calories combined, so the numbers above already include them.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order tom saap gai or tom saap kraduk on (soft bone) instead of belly or thick ribs; that saves 70 to 110 calories a bowl.
  • Eat all the meat and mushrooms and drink the broth; it is nearly free and very filling on a hot day.
  • Pair it with a small bag of sticky rice (160 calories) or half a plate of rice rather than a full plate.
  • Ask for extra sawtooth coriander and mushrooms; they add volume and about 10 calories.

Common questions

Is tom saap healthy?
It is one of the leaner soups in Thai food. A bowl is about 270 calories with 22g of protein and no cooking oil or coconut milk. The sodium is high from fish sauce, but for calories it is a good choice.
Tom saap vs tom yum, which has fewer calories?
A clear tom yum gai is about 100 to 150 a bowl because it uses less meat. Tom saap is about 270 because it is built around pork ribs. Tom yum nam khon with chili paste and evaporated milk is about 250 to 300, so tom saap and creamy tom yum are close.
How many calories in tom saap with rice?
About 520 for a rib bowl with a 150g bag of sticky rice, or about 500 with a small plate of steamed rice.
