How Many Calories in Tom Yum Goong?

Also known as ต้มยำกุ้ง, Tom Yum Kung, Thai Hot and Sour Prawn Soup, Tom Yam Goong.

Hot and sour prawn soup with lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime leaf, chilli, mushrooms and lime juice. Comes clear (nam sai) or creamy (nam khon) with evaporated milk and roasted chilli paste stirred in.

180kcal / 1 bowl (clear)
A 400g restaurant bowl of clear tom yum goong (nam sai) is about 180 calories, mostly from 100g of prawns. The creamy nam khon version is about 280 because of the evaporated milk and the extra spoon of nam prik pao. A small cup at a rice stall is around 65.

The real talk

Tom yum is the closest thing to a free meal on a Thai menu, as long as you order it clear. The broth is water, herbs and lime, maybe 20 calories in the whole bowl. The prawns are 100 calories per 100g and nearly all protein. Mushrooms are 15. The only thing with real calories is the nam prik pao, the roasted chilli paste, which is fried in oil and runs 45 calories per tablespoon. One bowl at 180 fills you the way a 600 calorie plate of fried rice does.

Nam khon changes the maths. A ladle of evaporated milk adds 60 to 80 calories and most cooks double the chilli paste when they make it creamy, so a bowl lands at 280 to 300. Still a good deal for the size, but not the near zero people assume. The instant noodle version, Mama tom yum goong with a real prawn dropped in, is a different animal at about 500 because the fried noodle block alone is 350.

The trap is what tom yum comes with. It is a shared dish at a Thai table, so the bowl sits next to a plate of rice at 250, a fried omelette at 350 and a plate of pad kra pao. The soup is not the problem. The 3 plates around it are. If you make tom yum the main and eat it with one plate of rice, dinner is about 430 and you will be full.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order nam sai (clear). It saves about 100 calories a bowl over nam khon and tastes sharper.
  • Ask for extra prawns or add tofu. More protein in a 180 calorie bowl means you skip the second plate of rice.
  • Go easy on the nam prik pao on the table. Each extra spoon is 45 calories of oil.
  • Eat it as the main with one plate of rice, about 430 total, instead of as a side to 3 other dishes.
  • Tom yum with rice noodles (kuay tiao tom yum) is a full 400 calorie meal on its own if you want more bulk.

Common questions

Is tom yum goong good for weight loss?
The clear version at 180 calories per bowl is one of the best deals in Thai food. High protein, big volume, almost no fat. Order it clear and it is hard to overeat.
What is the difference between nam sai and nam khon?
Nam sai is clear broth, about 180 calories a bowl. Nam khon has evaporated milk and extra roasted chilli paste, about 280. Same prawns, 100 more calories.
How many calories in tom yum with rice?
A clear bowl plus a 250 calorie plate of jasmine rice is about 430. That is a full dinner.
Is tom yum keto?
The clear soup is about 10g carbs a bowl, so it fits. Skip the rice and watch the chilli paste, which has some sugar.
