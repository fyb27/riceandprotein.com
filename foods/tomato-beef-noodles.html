How Many Calories in Tomato Beef Macaroni (Ke Ngau Tung)?

Also known as 茄牛通, 鮮茄牛肉通粉, Tomato Beef Noodles, 茄牛麵, Cha Chaan Teng Tomato Beef Soup Noodles.

Ke ngau tung is macaroni in a tangy tomato soup with sliced beef, sometimes with an egg dropped in. It is the cha chaan teng lunch that dietitians keep recommending, HK$35 to HK$48 with a drink.

370kcal / 1 bowl
One bowl of tomato beef macaroni is around 370 calories, based on a Hong Kong registered dietitian's tally of cha chaan teng dishes (368 kcal). About 200 is the macaroni, 80 is the beef and the tomato broth is around 90. It carries about 20g of protein and only 10g of fat.

The real talk

Of the 12 cha chaan teng dishes in the HK01 dietitian ranking, tomato beef macaroni came in at 368 calories, sixth from the top and less than half of the luncheon meat instant noodles (785) that most people at the next table are eating. The macaroni is boiled, the beef is sliced lean flank, and the soup is tomato, ketchup and stock. There is nothing fried in the bowl unless you add a fried egg, which is another 100.

The same ingredients on a rice plate are a different story. 鮮茄牛肉飯 with a reduced portion of rice is 665 calories in a Weekend Weekly dietitian list, and a full rice portion pushes it over 750. Rice is denser than macaroni in soup, and the plate version uses more oil to stir fry the beef and tomato. So if you want the tomato beef flavour, the noodle soup gets you there for about 300 fewer calories.

Where it goes wrong is the sugar and salt in the soup. Cha chaan teng tomato broth leans on ketchup, and a bowl can hold 15 to 20g of sugar plus 1,500mg or more of sodium. Drinking every drop is what tips this from a light lunch into a sweet, salty one. Eat the macaroni and beef, take a few spoons of the soup, and the number stays near 320.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it with sliced beef, not minced beef patty. Minced beef in Hong Kong is fatty and adds about 80 calories.
  • Skip the fried egg on top. That is 100 calories; a poached egg dropped in the soup is 70.
  • Leave most of the soup. It holds nearly all the sugar and sodium.
  • Choose macaroni or rice vermicelli over instant noodles. The fried noodle cake alone adds about 150 calories.

Common questions

How many calories in a bowl of tomato beef macaroni?
About 370 calories per cha chaan teng bowl, according to a Hong Kong registered dietitian ranking (368 kcal). That is less than half of a luncheon meat and egg instant noodle bowl.
Is tomato beef macaroni healthy?
It is one of the better cha chaan teng choices: 20g of protein, 10g of fat, nothing fried. The soup is high in sugar and sodium, so do not drink all of it.
Tomato beef noodles or tomato beef rice, which is lower?
The noodle soup at about 370 calories. The rice plate is about 665 even with less rice, because of the rice portion and the oil used to stir fry.
