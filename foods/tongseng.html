How Many Calories in Tongseng?

Also known as Tongseng Kambing, Tongseng Sapi, Tongseng Solo, Goat Stew with Cabbage.

Tongseng is goat or beef stir fried with cabbage, tomato and kecap manis, then finished with a ladle of gulai broth. It is the sate seller's second dish, made in the same wok, and it is sweeter and thicker than gulai.

420kcal / 1 bowl (no rice)
A bowl of tongseng kambing is about 420 calories without rice. FatSecret Indonesia lists tongseng at 169 calories per 100g and 422 calories for a bowl, with 61% of the energy from fat. Add a plate of rice and the standard sate warung meal is around 680.

The real talk

Tongseng starts as gulai and then gets worse for you. The seller takes goat pieces, fries them in a wok with garlic and shallot, adds cabbage and tomato, pours in kecap manis, then tops it up with coconut milk gulai broth from the pot. So you get the coconut fat from the gulai and the sugar from the kecap in one bowl. A 250g bowl is 420 calories, 28g of it fat.

The meat is 80 to 100g of goat with the fat left on, about 220 calories and most of the 24g protein. The gulai broth, maybe 100ml, is 120. The kecap manis, two tablespoons stirred in, is 50 calories of sugar and it is why tongseng tastes the way it does. The cabbage and tomato are 20 and they are the only thing in the bowl you can add freely.

Tongseng sapi (beef) is a little leaner than kambing, about 380 a bowl, and tongseng ayam is around 320. The Solo style is thinner and sweeter; the Jakarta sate Madura cart version tends to be thicker with more broth and lands closer to 470. Prices are Rp 30,000 to 45,000 a bowl, more for kambing muda.

It is nearly always eaten with rice, and a lot of it, because the sauce is made for rice. 200g rice is 260, so the bowl becomes 680. Order sate kambing on the side, which is what everybody does, and dinner is 1,050.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for 'kuahnya sedikit'. Half the gulai broth is 60 calories saved and the kecap flavour is still there.
  • Ask for less kecap or 'jangan terlalu manis'. Each tablespoon of sweet soy is 25 calories of sugar.
  • Extra kol. The cabbage is 15 calories a handful and it soaks up the sauce so you use less rice.
  • Tongseng ayam or sapi instead of kambing. About 40 to 100 calories less per bowl.
  • Tongseng or sate, not both. The combo is the standard order and it is over 1,000 calories with rice.

Common questions

How many calories in tongseng?
About 420 calories for a bowl of tongseng kambing without rice, according to FatSecret Indonesia. With rice it is around 680.
Tongseng or gulai kambing, which is higher in calories?
Tongseng, by about 50 to 80 calories a bowl. It is gulai broth plus kecap manis plus a wok fry, so it has the same coconut fat with sugar on top.
Is tongseng healthy?
It has 24g protein, but 28g fat and about 12g of sugar from the kecap. Once in a while with less broth and rice it is fine. As a weekly sate warung habit with sate on the side it adds up fast.
How many calories in tongseng sapi?
About 380 calories per bowl. Beef used for tongseng is a bit leaner than goat with the fat left on.
Berapa kalori Tongseng?
Tongseng mengandung sekitar 420 kalori per 1 mangkuk (250g), dengan 24g protein, 14g karbohidrat dan 28g lemak. Angka ini perkiraan untuk porsi warung biasa, jadi bisa berbeda tergantung tempat dan ukuran porsi.
