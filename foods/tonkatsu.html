How Many Calories in Tonkatsu?

Also known as とんかつ, 豚カツ, ロースかつ, Rosu Katsu, Japanese Pork Cutlet.

A thick pork loin cutlet coated in panko breadcrumbs and deep-fried until the crust shatters, sliced and served with shredded cabbage, tonkatsu sauce and mustard. The teishoku adds rice, miso soup and pickles.

442kcal / 1 rosu katsu (cutlet only, no rice)
One rosu (loin) tonkatsu cutlet is about 442 calories on its own. That is Matsunoya's published figure for their single cutlet. As a teishoku with rice, cabbage and miso soup it is 830 calories, and as a katsudon on rice with egg it reaches 1,011.

The real talk

The cutlet alone is 442 calories, and 30g of that is fat, which means 270 of the 442 calories are fat calories. A 120g piece of raw pork loin has about 15g of fat to begin with. The panko crust then soaks up another 10 to 15g of oil in the fryer. That is why a hire (fillet) katsu, made from a leaner cut, comes in about 100 calories lower at the same weight. The crust is identical, the meat underneath is not.

The teishoku is where people lose track. Matsunoya's rosu katsu teishoku is 830 calories: 442 for the cutlet, about 250 for the rice, 28 for the miso soup, and the rest is cabbage, pickles and sauce. Many tonkatsu specialists offer free rice and cabbage refills, and a second scoop of rice is another 250. A 'refill twice' lunch is 1,300 calories, more than a full day of eating for some people, for about 1,000 yen.

Tonkatsu sauce is 25 calories per tablespoon, not terrible. The cabbage is 20 calories for the whole pile and worth eating first. The other thing to know is the size range. A regular cutlet is 120g raw. The 'thick cut' or 'rib loin' cutlets that specialist shops push are 180 to 200g and 650 to 700 calories on their own, so the same teishoku becomes 1,100.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order hire katsu instead of rosu. Same crust, leaner meat, about 100 calories less.
  • Skip the rice refill. One bowl is 250 calories, two is 500.
  • Eat the cabbage first with a squeeze of lemon and take the refill on that instead. Cabbage is 20 calories a pile.
  • Leave the fatty edge of the loin. On a rosu katsu that strip is 50 to 70 calories.
  • Katsu curry and katsudon add 250 to 600 calories over the plain cutlet. Have the cutlet as a teishoku.

Common questions

How many calories in a tonkatsu set meal?
About 830 calories for a rosu katsu teishoku with rice, cabbage and miso soup. That is Matsunoya's published number. Specialist shops with bigger cutlets are 900 to 1,100.
Hire or rosu katsu, which is lower in calories?
Hire (fillet) is about 100 calories lower per cutlet, around 340 versus 442, because fillet has about a third of the fat of loin.
Is tonkatsu high in protein?
Moderately. A single cutlet has about 19g of protein, but 30g of fat. Chicken karaage delivers more protein per calorie.
How many calories in tonkatsu sauce?
About 25 calories per tablespoon. Two tablespoons on a cutlet is 50 calories, less than the rice refill most people take without thinking.
