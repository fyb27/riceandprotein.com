How Many Calories in Tteokbokki?

Also known as 떡볶이, Spicy Rice Cakes, Ddeokbokki, Topokki.

Tteokbokki is cylinder rice cakes and fish cake simmered in a sweet gochujang sauce, sold by the cup at street carts and by the plate at bunsik shops for 3,000 to 5,000 won.

335kcal / 1 serving (1인분, street cart or bunsik shop)
A standard 1인분 of tteokbokki is about 335 calories for a 250g serving, per fatsecret.kr, and the Korean MFDS survey puts a 200g portion at 304. Almost all of it is carbs, about 66g, because rice cakes are compressed rice. The sauce adds sugar but very little fat.

The real talk

Rice cakes are rice with the air pressed out. 150g of tteok, roughly 12 to 15 pieces, is 215 calories, the same as a full bowl of cooked rice but in a much smaller pile. That is why a paper cup from a cart feels heavier than it looks, and why you feel full after one serving even though it is only 335 calories.

The sauce is gochujang, gochugaru, sugar and syrup, and a 60g ladle of it is about 50 calories with 10g of sugar. It is not the calorie problem. The fried add-ons are. A single tempura-battered item (튀김) is 100 to 150 calories, an egg is 75, and a slice of cheese melted on top is 65. Cheese tteokbokki with two twigim runs past 600.

Bunsik shop plates are usually bigger than the cart cup. A 3,500 won plate can be 300g to 350g, and the popular takeaway brands like Sinjeon list 137 kcal per 100g, so a 400g takeaway container is 550 calories before the sundae and twigim you ordered on the side.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order it with sundae rather than twigim. 100g of sundae is about 160 calories; 3 pieces of twigim are 350.
  • Skip the cheese on top. That is 65 to 130 calories of pure fat added to a dish that was almost fat-free.
  • Get the cup, not the plate. A 250g cart serving is 335; a 400g takeaway container is 550.
  • Eat the fish cake and cabbage first. They are protein and volume for a few calories, and you will leave rice cakes behind.
  • Add a boiled egg instead of twigim if you want it more filling. 75 calories and 6g of protein.

Common questions

How many calories in one serving of tteokbokki?
About 335 for a 250g street serving. The MFDS survey figure is 304 per 200g, so the number scales with the size of the cup.
Is tteokbokki fattening?
It is low in fat (5g) but dense in carbs (66g). One serving is fine; the problem is the twigim, cheese and sundae that go with it.
How many rice cakes are in a serving?
Usually 12 to 15 pieces, around 150g. That alone is 215 calories, the same as a bowl of rice.
Is rose tteokbokki higher in calories?
Yes, by a lot. The cream and cheese sauce pushes it to about 620 per serving, nearly double the standard version.
