How Many Calories in Tuaran Mee?

Sabah's double cooked egg noodles, wok fried with a rolled egg omelette and char siu. The double frying drives it.

650kcal / 1 plate
A plate of Tuaran mee is around 650 calories. The egg noodles are lightly deep fried then wok fried again, so they absorb oil at both stages, which is the main calorie driver. Note this is an estimate, there is no lab data for it.

The real talk

Tuaran mee is Sabah's pride, handmade egg noodles that are lightly deep fried then stir fried or braised, served with a rolled egg omelette, char siu and choy sum. Those gorgeous golden noodles are egg yolk rich to start with and absorb oil at both cooking stages, which is why a plate lands around 650 calories. The double frying is the story.

Straight with you: there is no official nutrition database entry for Tuaran mee, it is too niche. This number is built from the dish's known method and similar fried noodle plates, so treat it as a careful estimate, give or take 100 calories. The char siu and egg give you real protein at least.

How to eat it lighter

  • Ask for less oil if the stall will do it, the double frying is the calorie driver.
  • Lean on the char siu and egg omelette for protein over extra noodles.
  • Treat it as a richer plate, not an everyday light meal.

Common questions

How many calories in Tuaran mee?
Around 650 calories a plate, though this is an estimate since there is no official data for it. The egg noodles are double cooked, deep fried then wok fried, which drives the calories.
Why is Tuaran mee calorie dense?
The egg noodles are egg yolk rich and cooked twice, lightly deep fried then wok braised, so they absorb oil at both stages. Oil and noodles make up most of the plate.
Is Tuaran mee healthy?
It is a richer plate at around 650 calories, with decent protein from char siu and egg. Ask for less oil and treat it as a treat rather than an everyday meal.
Berapa kalori dalam Tuaran Mee?
Tuaran Mee ada kira-kira 650 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (420g), dengan 26g protein, 75g karbohidrat dan 26g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
