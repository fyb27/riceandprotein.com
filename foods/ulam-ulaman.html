How Many Calories in Ulam-Ulaman?

A traditional raw herb and vegetable platter served with sambal belacan, one of the lowest-calorie things you will find at any nasi campur stall.

60kcal / 1 plate with sambal belacan
One plate of ulam-ulaman with sambal belacan is about 60 calories for a 160g serving. That covers a mix of raw herbs and leaves, some long beans and cucumber, a few pods of petai, and a couple tablespoons of sambal belacan as the dip. Without the petai it drops to around 40 calories. With a richer tempoyak dip instead of sambal, you are looking at around 80. Either way, this is one of the lowest-calorie things you can put on your plate at a warung or nasi campur stall.

The real talk

Sixty calories for a whole plate of food, bro. That is basically free. The raw herbs and leaves, the cucumber, the long beans, even the sambal belacan dip, none of it adds up to much. The petai contributes about 19 calories for a few pods, and the sambal belacan another 19, so those two together are actually the biggest calorie contributors on the entire plate. Everything else is just leaves and water. If you are trying to eat big volume on a deficit, ulam-ulaman is exactly the kind of side dish you want to load up on. It fills the plate, it takes time to chew, and it costs you almost nothing calorie-wise. To be honest though, these numbers are a ballpark estimate since there is not much hard nutrition data for traditional Malaysian ulam spreads, but the range is going to be in this territory.

What actually changes the maths here is the dip. Sambal belacan at 2 tablespoons is already 19 calories, but if you start doubling up or swap it for tempoyak, the plate climbs from 60 to 80 or beyond. The herbs themselves are so low in calories that portion size barely matters. Skip the petai and you are at 40 calories. The ulam leaves and long beans are genuinely close to zero. Where people accidentally turn this into a higher-calorie dish is by pairing it with a rich gravy side or by getting a plate that has been sitting next to oily dishes at the nasi campur counter, where a little of that cooking oil transfers over. As a standalone side with plain sambal belacan, this is one of the smartest things to put on your plate.

How to eat it lighter

  • Keep the dip to 2 tablespoons of sambal belacan and skip requests for extra. That dip is doing almost a third of the calories here.
  • Skip the tempoyak option. Fermented durian tastes amazing but adds more calories than plain sambal for a much smaller dip portion.
  • Load up on the plain herb leaves and cucumber. They are so close to zero calories that eating twice as much changes almost nothing.
  • Skip the petai if you are really cutting hard. Saves you about 19 calories, which is small but it adds up if you are being precise.
  • Pair it with a lean protein like steamed fish or grilled chicken instead of a rich santan-based lauk, so the whole plate stays light.

Common questions

How many calories in Ulam-Ulaman?
A standard plate of ulam-ulaman with sambal belacan is around 60 calories for about 160g. Without petai it is closer to 40 calories, and with a tempoyak dip it goes up to about 80. These are estimates since detailed nutrition data for traditional ulam spreads is thin, but the numbers are in this range.
Is ulam-ulaman good for weight loss?
It is honestly one of the best side dishes you can pick if you are watching calories. Sixty calories for a full plate of food is almost nothing. The raw herbs and vegetables are very low in calorie density, so you get actual volume and something to chew on without spending much of your daily calorie budget. The main thing to watch is the dip, since richer dips like tempoyak add more calories than plain sambal belacan.
Why does ulam-ulaman smell after you eat it?
That is the petai lah. Stink beans contain sulphur compounds similar to asparagus, and your body breaks them down in a way that carries through to your urine and sweat. It passes within a day. Totally harmless, just a bit antisocial.
Berapa kalori dalam Ulam-Ulaman?
Ulam-Ulaman ada kira-kira 60 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (160g), dengan 4g protein, 8g karbohidrat dan 1g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
