How Many Calories in Vadai?

Also known as Vada, Medu Vadai.

A crispy deep-fried lentil fritter from the mamak, made with spiced dhal batter and available plain or with a prawn on top.

195kcal / 1 piece
One piece of vadai (about 60g) has 195 calories. That is not a lot for something this satisfying, but the catch is nobody eats just one. The calories come mostly from the dhal batter at 115 kcal and the oil absorbed during deep frying at 70 kcal. The spices and onion are basically free. So yes, 195 per piece is the honest number for a standard mamak medu vadai.

The real talk

Vadai gets a bit of a free pass because it sounds healthy, lentils lah, whole spices, no sugar. And honestly 195 kcal per piece is not outrageous. But 70 of those calories are pure absorbed frying oil. That is more than a third of the entire piece just from oil. The lentil batter does bring some protein and a bit of fibre, so it is not completely empty, but calling this a light snack would be wishful thinking. One piece is fine. The problem is the mamak charges like RM1.50 per piece and they come out hot and crispy and before you know it you have eaten four of them.

The real variable here is how many pieces you eat and what you dip it in. Four dhal vadai straight up is 780 kcal. Add two generous scoops of coconut chutney at roughly 60 kcal each and you are sitting at 900 kcal for what felt like a light snack. Prawn vadai bumps each piece to 215 kcal because of the prawn on top but also adds a bit more protein, so if you are eating two pieces anyway the prawn version is not a bad trade. The other thing that changes the maths is whether you are eating this as a starter before a full nasi kandar plate or as a standalone. Standalone, two pieces at 390 kcal is a decent snack. As a starter before a big meal, you probably did not need them.

How to eat it lighter

  • Stop at one or two pieces. The per-piece number is reasonable but vadai is dangerously easy to eat four or five without thinking.
  • Skip the coconut chutney if you are watching calories. A generous scoop can add 50 to 80 kcal on top of the vadai itself.
  • Choose dhal vadai over prawn vadai if you want to save about 20 kcal per piece. Small difference but it adds up.
  • Eat it as part of a meal rather than a standalone snack. Paired with a teh tarik kosong and some protein, it fills you up properly instead of just teasing your stomach.
  • At home, you can air fry lentil patties and cut the oil absorption significantly. Not quite the same crunch, but the calories drop by 40 to 50 kcal per piece.

Common questions

How many calories in Vadai?
One piece of vadai weighs about 60g and has 195 calories. That covers 5g protein, 14g carbs, and 13g fat. Prawn vadai runs slightly higher at around 215 kcal because of the prawn on top.
Is vadai healthy or just empty calories?
It is not empty calories exactly. You are getting lentil protein and some fibre from the dhal batter. The problem is the deep frying soaks up around 8g of oil per piece, which adds 70 kcal before you even count the batter. One or two pieces alongside a proper meal is fine. Eating five pieces as a snack is where the numbers get away from you.
How does vadai compare to roti canai for calories?
Roti canai plain is around 300 kcal for a full piece while vadai is 195 kcal per piece. But roti canai is bigger and more filling, so the comparison is not quite fair. Per gram, they are actually pretty similar because both are cooked in a lot of oil or fat. If you are just grabbing something quick at the mamak, one vadai is fewer calories than one roti canai.
Berapa kalori dalam Vadai?
Vadai ada kira-kira 195 kalori untuk 1 keping (60g), dengan 5g protein, 14g karbohidrat dan 13g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
