Vegetables & Sides Calories

Vegetables should be the safest thing on your plate, and steamed or lightly stir fried, they are. This is the category you pile on when you want a mountain of food for almost nothing. Volume eating starts here, whether that is a plate of kangkung, a bowl of sayur asem, or the banchan on a Korean table.

But be honest with yourself about the oil. Kangkung belacan glistening under the lamp at the tai chow is not a health food anymore, and neither is laing cooked in a litre of coconut milk. The ranking below shows which veg dishes are basically free and which ones are secretly a main.

Vegetables & Sides at a glance

There are 23 dishes ranked on this page. The lightest is Latok (Sea Grapes) at 30 kcal per 1 small plate, the heaviest is Gado-Gado at 390 kcal per 1 plate (vegetables, lontong, tofu, tempe, half egg, peanut sauce, emping), and the average lands around 174 calories a serving. 21 of them come in under 300 calories.

The spread here is narrower than most categories, about 360 calories between the lightest and the heaviest. That means portion size and what you order alongside it matter more than which dish you pick.

Common questions

Latok (Sea Grapes) is the lightest on this page at 30 calories per 1 small plate. There are 21 options here under 300 calories, so you have room to pick something you actually like.
What is the highest calorie vegetables & side here?
Gado-Gado tops the list at 390 calories per 1 plate (vegetables, lontong, tofu, tempe, half egg, peanut sauce, emping). Nothing else here comes close.
How many calories are in a typical vegetables & sides serving?
Across the 23 dishes on this page the average is about 174 calories per serving, with the middle of the pack around 180 (Paku Pakis). Figures are estimates for a normal portion.
