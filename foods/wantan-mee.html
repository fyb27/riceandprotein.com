How Many Calories in Wantan Mee?

Also known as Wanton Mee, Wonton Noodles.

Springy egg noodles with char siu and wantan. Lower fat than most fried noodles. One of the smarter picks.

510kcal / 1 plate (dry)
A plate of dry wantan mee is around 510 calories with a solid 25g of protein. Because it is tossed rather than deep fried in oil, it is one of the leaner noodle plates at a kopitiam.

The real talk

Wantan mee is quietly one of the better choices when you are trying to be good but still want noodles. The dry version is tossed in a bit of sauce and oil, not drowned and fried like char kuey teow. The char siu and wantan give you real protein, around 25g, for a moderate calorie count. That protein to calorie ratio beats most hawker plates.

Two things to watch. The char siu can be fatty and sweet, and the fried wantan (as opposed to soup wantan) adds oil. But compared to CKT or mee goreng, this is a smart order. If a stall does a soup version, that is lighter again.

How to eat it lighter

  • Choose the soup version over dry to cut some of the oil.
  • Ask for extra char siu or wantan for protein rather than extra noodles.
  • Go easy on the fried wantan, pick the boiled soup wantan if offered.

Common questions

Is wantan mee good for weight loss?
It is one of the better hawker noodles for it. At about 510 calories with 25g of protein, the protein to calorie ratio beats most fried noodle dishes. The soup version is lighter still.
How much protein in wantan mee?
About 25g, mostly from the char siu and wantan. That is a good amount for a noodle plate.
Is dry or soup wantan mee lower in calories?
Soup is a bit lower because the dry version is tossed in extra sauce and oil. Both are reasonable choices though.
Berapa kalori dalam Wantan Mee?
Wantan Mee ada kira-kira 510 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (320g), dengan 25g protein, 70g karbohidrat dan 14g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
