How Many Calories in Wat Tan Hor?

Flat rice noodles under a silky egg gravy. The gravy looks light but carries oil, starch and egg.

640kcal / 1 plate
A plate of wat tan hor is around 640 calories. The kuey teow is fried first, then smothered in an egg gravy thickened with starch and enriched with oil, which is where a lot of the calories come from.

The real talk

Wat tan hor looks gentler than char kuey teow because it is swimming in a smooth pale gravy instead of a dark oily fry. Do not be fooled. The flat noodles are charred in oil first, then the gravy on top is thickened with starch, bound with egg, and finished with a swirl of oil for that silky texture. Add the pork, prawns and fish cake and you are at around 640 calories with 28g of fat.

The upside is the protein is decent, around 24g, better than a lot of noodle plates. So it is not a terrible order, just a heavy one. Know that the innocent looking gravy is doing real calorie work.

How to eat it lighter

  • Do not drink all the leftover gravy. A lot of the oil settles in it.
  • Ask for less oil in the gravy if the stall will do it.
  • Load up on the prawns and pork for protein rather than asking for extra noodles.

Common questions

How many calories in wat tan hor?
About 640 calories a plate. The kuey teow is fried in oil first, then topped with an egg and starch gravy that also carries oil, which is where much of the calories come from.
Is wat tan hor healthier than char kuey teow?
They are close. Wat tan hor has a bit more protein from the egg gravy and toppings, but the gravy carries plenty of oil, so it is not a light dish either.
How much protein is in wat tan hor?
Around 24g, from the egg gravy, pork, prawns and fish cake. That is a decent amount for a noodle plate.
Berapa kalori dalam Wat Tan Hor?
Wat Tan Hor ada kira-kira 640 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (450g), dengan 24g protein, 68g karbohidrat dan 28g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
