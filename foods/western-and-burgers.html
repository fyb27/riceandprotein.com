Western & Burgers at a glance

There are 14 dishes ranked on this page. The lightest is Macaroni Chicken Soup at 277 kcal per 1 bowl, the heaviest is Baked Pork Chop Rice at 1300 kcal per 1 plate, and the average lands around 631 calories a serving. 1 of them come in under 300 calories, and 9 are 600 or more.

That spread is the whole point. Two dishes off the same menu can be 1023 calories apart, which is more than most people's entire daily deficit. Knowing which is which costs you nothing and changes the outcome.
