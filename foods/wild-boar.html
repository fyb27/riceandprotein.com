How Many Calories in Wild Boar (Babi Hutan)?

Wild boar, or babi hutan, is a non-halal game meat popular in Sabah and Sarawak, cooked as curry, stir-fry with bamboo shoot, or smoked, and it is leaner and gamier than the farmed pork you know.

460kcal / 1 serving wild boar dish (meat + gravy, no rice)
A standard serving of wild boar dish (about 290g of meat and gravy, no rice) comes to roughly 460 calories, with 54g protein, 23g fat, and only 5g carbs. That is for the curry version, which is the base estimate here. Smoked wild boar (sinalau bakas) drops to around 310 calories, while adding santan to the curry pushes it up to 540 calories.

The real talk

Babi hutan is a Borneo thing, mainly Sabah and Sarawak, and it is worth knowing about if you have never had it. This is wild game, not farmed pork, so the meat itself is leaner and a lot gamier in flavour. At 460 calories for a curry serving, the number is solid for what you get: 54g of protein is genuinely impressive, and the carbs are almost nothing since it is just meat and spices. The fat at 23g is moderate, not alarming. Honestly this is a pretty decent dish for a dieter if you are not Muslim and you happen to be eating in Sabah or Sarawak. Fair warning though: hard nutrition data for wild boar cooked the Malaysian way is basically nonexistent, so these numbers are a ballpark estimate based on the closest data available. Do not treat them as gospel.

What really shifts the maths here is the cooking method and the gravy. Sinalau bakas, the smoked version where the meat is just salted and dried over fire, skips most of the oil entirely and comes in at about 310 calories for a similar portion. The moment you add a curry paste and cooking oil, you are looking at 108 extra calories just from the oil absorbed into the dish. Throw in coconut milk and the whole thing jumps to 540 calories. So if you are watching numbers, ask for the stir-fry with bamboo shoot (tumis babi hutan) at around 400 calories, go easy on the gravy, and skip the santan version. Pair it with rice and a full plate sits around 720 calories, which is fine for a meal as long as you account for it.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order sinalau bakas where available. The smoked preparation skips the curry paste and most of the oil, cutting about 150 calories from the same amount of meat.
  • Skip the santan. Coconut milk adds roughly 80 calories to an already decent curry, and the dish honestly tastes great without it.
  • Go easy on the gravy. Most of the oil calories pool in the sauce, so scoop the meat and leave some of the liquid behind.
  • Ask for the bamboo shoot stir-fry version (tumis babi hutan) instead of curry. Less gravy, less oil, about 60 fewer calories.
  • Watch your rice portion. The meat itself is high protein and low carb, so do not cancel it out with a mountain of rice. Stick to 150g cooked rice instead of 200g and you save around 65 calories from the full plate total.

Common questions

How many calories in Wild Boar (Babi Hutan)?
A standard serving of wild boar curry (about 290g of meat and gravy, no rice) is around 460 calories. Smoked wild boar (sinalau bakas) is closer to 310 calories, while wild boar curry with coconut milk reaches about 540 calories. Add 200g of white rice and a full plate is around 720 calories.
What is babi hutan and is it halal?
Babi hutan literally means wild forest pig. It is game meat hunted in the jungles of Sabah and Sarawak, and it is a staple in the indigenous communities of Borneo. It is non-halal, so this one is only for non-Muslim diners. The meat is leaner and gamier than farmed pork, and it is commonly cooked as a curry, stir-fried with bamboo shoot, or smoked over fire as sinalau bakas.
Is wild boar good for weight loss?
Actually not bad, if you choose the right preparation. At 54g of protein per serving and only 5g of carbs, the meat itself is lean and filling. The calories mainly come from cooking oil and gravy. Stick to the smoked or stir-fried versions, go easy on the sauce, and it is a genuinely high-protein meal that fits into a calorie deficit without too much fuss.
Berapa kalori dalam Wild Boar?
Wild Boar ada kira-kira 460 kalori untuk satu hidangan biasa (290g), dengan 54g protein, 5g karbohidrat dan 23g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
