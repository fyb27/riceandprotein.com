How Many Calories in Yakiniku?

Also known as 焼肉, Japanese BBQ, Yakiniku Beef, Gyukaku, Kalbi and Harami.

Bite size cuts of beef, mostly kalbi (short rib) and harami (skirt), grilled over charcoal or gas at the table and dipped in tare, a sweet soy and garlic sauce. Ordered by the plate at Gyukaku, Anan, and every neighbourhood yakiniku-ya, usually with rice, kimchi and beer.

1000kcal / 1 person (200g meat with rice)
A typical one person yakiniku dinner, 100g of kalbi, 100g of harami, a 200g bowl of rice and tare, is about 1,000 calories. Kalbi is the heavy one at 350 to 400 per 100g because of the marbling; harami is around 250. The Japanese composition table's yakiniku teishoku, with only 100g of meat, comes in at 768. The number scales with plates, and plates are small.

The real talk

Yakiniku is priced by the plate and counted by the plate, and each plate is 80 to 100g. That is the trap. One plate of kalbi looks like 6 small pieces. It is 370 calories, more than a bowl of rice, and a normal order is 3 or 4 plates per person. The marbling that makes kalbi good is fat at 9 calories per gram, and a fatty short rib slice is 30 to 40% fat by weight. Harami is the lean choice at about 250 per 100g. Tongue (tan) is around 220. Tripe and offal are 150 to 200 and nobody orders enough of them to matter.

Then the rice and the tare. A 200g bowl of rice is 310 and at yakiniku it is a sauce sponge, so the tare that drips onto it adds 50 more. Tare itself is soy, sugar, garlic and sesame oil at about 15 calories per tablespoon, and over a meal you go through 3 or 4. The pickles and kimchi are free. The salad with sesame dressing is 120. Beer is 150 a glass, and yakiniku is the one meal in Japan where people order 3.

Tabehodai (all you can eat) at Gyukaku or Anan is where it becomes a 1,800 calorie sitting. Four hundred grams of meat is the average, 1,300 calories of it if half is kalbi, plus rice, plus the nomihodai beer. It is not a bad meal in terms of protein, 100g of it, but the fat is 90g and that is a full day's worth in 90 minutes. The version that works is 200g of meat with more harami than kalbi, one bowl of rice, one beer, and the free kimchi and lettuce wraps doing the volume.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order harami and tan over kalbi. About 250 and 220 per 100g against 370 for fatty short rib.
  • Two plates of meat per person, not four. Each plate is 80 to 100g and 250 to 370 calories.
  • Wrap the meat in the lettuce (sanchu) instead of piling it on rice. The lettuce is free, the rice is 310.
  • Salt and lemon (shio) on the meat instead of tare. Saves about 60 calories over a meal and the beef tastes of beef.
  • One beer. The second and third are 300 calories and they make you order the extra plate of kalbi.

Common questions

How many calories in yakiniku?
About 1,000 for a normal one person order of 200g meat with rice. A single 100g plate of kalbi is about 370, harami about 250.
How many calories in Gyukaku all you can eat?
Around 1,800 for a typical sitting of 400g meat, a bowl of rice and 2 beers. Heavy on kalbi and it passes 2,000.
What is the lowest calorie cut at yakiniku?
Tan (tongue) with salt at about 220 per 100g, then harami at 250. Kalbi is 370 and premium wagyu cuts pass 450.
Is yakiniku healthy?
It is 50g of protein per 200g of meat, which is good. It is also 45g of fat and 1,000 calories with rice. Choose lean cuts and wrap in lettuce and it is a fair dinner.
