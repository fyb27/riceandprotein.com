How Many Calories in Yakitori?

Also known as 焼き鳥, やきとり, 焼鳥, Grilled Chicken Skewers, Momo Tare.

Bite-sized chicken pieces on bamboo skewers grilled over charcoal, brushed with sweet soy tare or seasoned with salt. Sold by the stick at yakitori-ya and izakaya, with thigh, breast, skin, meatball and every organ on the menu.

231kcal / 2 skewers (thigh, tare sauce)
Two skewers of thigh yakitori with tare sauce are 231 calories, which is Torikizoku's published figure for its momo kizoku-yaki, so roughly 115 per skewer. The salt version is 208. Breast skewers are 165 for 2, tsukune meatballs 171, and chicken skin skewers around 380.

The real talk

Yakitori is one of the best deals in Japanese food if you order by cut. Two thigh skewers at Torikizoku are 231 calories with 19g of protein and cost 390 yen. Two breast skewers are 165 with 23g of protein. That is more protein than a 468 calorie plate of karaage for a third of the calories. The chicken is grilled, not fried, so the only fat is what was in the bird.

The cut is everything. Torikizoku's own table shows the range. Momo (thigh) tare 231, momo shio 208, mune (breast) tare 165, mune shio 146, tsukune tare 171, bonjiri (tail) 219, and kawa (skin) chips at 385. Skin is nearly all fat, 36g of it per serving. Tare adds 20 to 25 calories over shio because of the sugar in the sauce, small compared with choosing skin over breast.

The problem is never the skewers, it is the izakaya around them. A typical yakitori night is 8 to 10 skewers at about 100 each, so 900 calories, plus 2 or 3 beers at 150 each, plus a plate of fries or fried gyoza. That is 1,500 calories and 4,000 yen. The skewers were the best part of the order and the smallest part of the total.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order mune (breast), sasami (tenderloin) and negima. All under 90 calories a stick.
  • Choose shio over tare when you do not care. Saves about 12 calories per skewer.
  • Skip kawa (skin) and bonjiri (tail). A skin skewer is about 190, more than 2 breast skewers.
  • Add edamame, cabbage with miso or a tomato salad between rounds. All under 110 for a plate.
  • Swap beer for highball. A highball is about 100 calories versus 150 to 200 for a draught beer.

Common questions

How many calories in one yakitori skewer?
About 115 for a thigh skewer with tare, 80 for a breast skewer, and 190 for a skin skewer. Torikizoku's serving of 2 thigh skewers is 231.
Is yakitori healthy?
Grilled chicken on a stick is about as good as izakaya food gets. Two thigh skewers are 231 calories with 19g of protein. The danger is the beer and fried sides around it.
Tare or shio, which is lower in calories?
Shio, by about 10 to 12 calories per skewer. Torikizoku's momo is 231 with tare and 208 with shio for 2 skewers.
How many calories in a yakitori dinner?
Ten mixed skewers are about 1,000 calories. Add 2 beers and a fried side and the night is 1,500. Stick to breast and thigh with edamame and a highball and it is closer to 800.
