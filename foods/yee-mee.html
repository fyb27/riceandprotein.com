How Many Calories in Yee Mee?

Crispy deep fried noodle nest under a starchy egg gravy. The pre-fried nest carries oil before the gravy even lands.

620kcal / 1 plate (crispy)
A plate of crispy yee mee (kering) is around 620 calories. The noodle nest is deep fried before serving, so it carries a lot of oil before the starchy egg gravy is even poured over it.

The real talk

Yee mee, the crispy kering version, is a deep fried noodle nest with a thick, starchy egg gravy ladled over the top, plus pork, prawns, fish cake and veg. The catch is the noodle nest is deep fried, so it soaks up oil before the gravy even touches it. That makes the crispy version one of the heavier noodle plates at around 620 calories with 28g of fat.

The soup version, where the noodles soften in broth instead of being eaten crispy, is much lighter at around 450 because it skips most of that absorbed oil. The protein is decent either way thanks to the toppings, but if you want lighter, go soup not crispy.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order the soup version over the crispy kering one to cut the absorbed frying oil.
  • Do not drink all the leftover gravy, the oil settles in it.
  • Lean on the prawns, pork and fish cake for protein.

Common questions

How many calories in yee mee?
The crispy kering version is around 620 calories a plate. The deep fried noodle nest carries oil before the starchy gravy is even added. The soup version is lighter at around 450.
Why is crispy yee mee so high in calories?
The noodle nest is deep fried before serving, so it absorbs a lot of oil. Add the starchy egg gravy and toppings and a plate clears 600 calories.
Is yee mee soup lower in calories?
Yes, around 450 versus 620 for the crispy version, because the noodles soften in broth instead of being deep fried.
Berapa kalori dalam Yee Mee?
Yee Mee ada kira-kira 620 kalori untuk 1 pinggan (500g), dengan 28g protein, 62g karbohidrat dan 28g lemak. Angka ini anggaran untuk hidangan gerai biasa, jadi ia berbeza ikut kedai dan saiz hidangan.
