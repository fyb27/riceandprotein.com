How Many Calories in Yen Ta Fo?

Also known as เย็นตาโฟ, Yentafo, Yong Tau Foo Noodles (Thai), Pink Noodle Soup.

Thai noodle soup coloured pink with fermented red bean curd sauce, loaded with fish balls, fish tofu, squid, morning glory and a fried wonton or two. Sold at noodle shops for 50 to 70 baht, usually with sen yai (wide rice noodles).

290kcal / 1 bowl
A bowl of yen ta fo with sen yai is about 290 calories, the figure both the Thai Department of Health based tables and hdmall use for a standard bowl. The noodles are 130 of that, the fish balls and squid about 70, and the red fermented tofu sauce around 20. It is lower than most Thai noodle soups because there is no pork fat and no coconut.

The real talk

The pink colour scares people who assume it is sugar syrup. It is nam yen ta fo, a sauce made from fermented red bean curd, garlic, vinegar and a little sugar, and a bowl uses about 20g of it for around 20 calories. The broth itself is a light pork or fish stock at maybe 15 calories per bowl. So the two things that make yen ta fo look unusual add up to less than 40 calories.

Where the calories sit is the noodles and the toppings. 120g of sen yai is about 130 calories. A standard bowl carries 60g of fish balls, fish tofu and squid at about 70 calories, which is lean protein. The fried wonton skins and fried tofu on top are the one fatty part: 20g of fried wonton is around 50 calories, and a shop that piles them on can add 100 more.

The version to watch is yen ta fo with extras. Add pork cracklings (kaeb moo), extra fried wonton and a couple of pieces of pork blood jelly and a 290 calorie bowl becomes a 400 calorie one. The dry version (yen ta fo haeng) tossed with garlic oil is another 60 to 80 on top. Order it nam (soup), standard toppings, and it is about as light as a Thai noodle lunch gets.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order sen woon sen (glass noodles) or sen mee (thin rice vermicelli) instead of sen yai; either saves 30 to 40 calories a bowl.
  • Ask for mai ao kiao tod (no fried wonton); that is 50 to 100 calories of fried flour you do not need.
  • Take extra pak boong (morning glory); it is under 10 calories a handful and fills the bowl.
  • Order it nam (soup), not haeng (dry with garlic oil), which adds 60 to 80 calories.

Common questions

Is yen ta fo healthy?
For a noodle soup it is one of the lighter ones at about 290 calories a bowl with 16g of protein from fish balls and squid. The fried wonton is the only real fat source, and the sauce is fermented tofu, not syrup.
What is the pink sauce in yen ta fo made of?
Fermented red bean curd blended with garlic, vinegar and a little sugar. A bowl uses around 20g, which is about 20 calories.
Yen ta fo vs boat noodles, which is lower in calories?
They are close. A small boat noodle bowl is about 300 calories, but boat noodle bowls are half the size, so per gram yen ta fo is much lighter.
