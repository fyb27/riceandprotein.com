How Many Calories in Zaru Soba?

Also known as ざるそば, ざる蕎麦, Mori Soba, Cold Soba, Cold Buckwheat Noodles.

Cold buckwheat noodles on a bamboo tray with a soy and dashi dipping sauce, nori strips, wasabi and spring onion. The summer standard at every soba shop.

362kcal / 1 plate
A standard plate of zaru soba is about 362 calories, per calorie.slism.jp for a 411g serving including the dipping sauce. It has 16g of protein and only about 2g of fat. Almost the entire count is the noodles themselves, around 240g cooked.

The real talk

Zaru soba is about as light as a full noodle meal gets: 362 calories, 2g of fat, and 16g of protein because buckwheat has more protein than wheat. The dipping sauce (tsuyu) is about 40 calories for the small cup, and the nori and wasabi are nothing. A plate at a standing shop costs around 400 yen, and at a proper soba restaurant 900 to 1,200.

The catch is that it is not very filling for the calories. A 362 calorie plate is 71g of carbohydrate and almost nothing else, so you are hungry again in two hours. That is why soba shops sell the 'ten zaru' set with a plate of tempura, which is 532 calories per calorie.slism.jp, or the 'ni-mai mori' double portion at about 700. Most people do not stop at one plate.

The sobayu at the end is worth knowing about. It is the cloudy water the noodles were boiled in, poured into your leftover tsuyu and drunk like a soup. It is only about 15 to 20 calories and it turns a plate of noodles into something that feels like a finished meal. Drink that instead of ordering a second plate.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order zaru or mori, not ten zaru. The tempura plate adds about 170 calories.
  • One plate, then sobayu. The hot soba water is under 20 calories and closes the meal.
  • If you need protein, add a cold onsen egg for 80 calories rather than kakiage.
  • Watch the tsuyu. Dipping only the tip of the noodles is the traditional way, and it saves salt more than calories.

Common questions

Is soba good for weight loss?
It is one of the best restaurant noodle choices at 362 calories a plate with 2g of fat. The weakness is low fullness, so pair it with an egg or tofu side rather than a second plate.
How many calories are in soba noodles per 100g?
Cooked soba is about 130 calories per 100g per the Japanese food composition tables. A standard plate is 240g cooked, so around 312 calories before sauce.
Does zaru soba have gluten?
Usually yes. Most shop soba is 'ni-hachi', 80 percent buckwheat and 20 percent wheat flour. Only 'juwari' soba is 100 percent buckwheat.
