How Many Calories in Zha Leung (Rice Roll with Fried Dough Stick)?

Also known as 炸兩, Zha Leung, Cheung Fun with You Tiao, Fried Dough Rice Roll, 炸両.

Zha leung is a you tiao (fried dough stick) rolled inside a thin sheet of steamed rice noodle, cut in two, and dressed with sweet soy, sesame and sometimes hoisin. Congee shops sell it as the side to a bowl of congee for HK$16 to HK$24.

450kcal / 1 plate (2 pieces)
One plate of zha leung, about 200g, is around 450 calories, based on the Centre for Food Safety figure of 230 kcal per 100g. Roughly 230 of that is the fried dough stick, 140 is the rice roll, and the sauces and sesame oil add the rest. Fat is around 23g, nearly all from the you tiao.

The real talk

Zha leung looks like a light dim sum plate and is not. The rice roll on the outside is steamed and about 130 calories per 100g, but the dough stick inside is deep fried and roughly 380 per 100g. Wrapping one in the other does not dilute anything. CFS lists the finished dish at 230 calories per 100g with 12g of fat, which is about the same density as a slice of pizza, and a plate is a third of a day's fat.

Where it hurts is the pairing. Almost nobody eats zha leung alone; it is the side to congee. A bowl of century egg and lean pork congee is 260 calories. Add a plate of zha leung and breakfast is 710. Add a hot milk tea and it is 820. The congee was the smart part of that order. A plain steamed rice roll with soy (齋腸) instead of zha leung is about 130 calories and gets you most of the texture.

The sauces are not free either. Congee shops finish zha leung with sweet soy, sesame oil, sesame seeds and often a squeeze of hoisin and peanut sauce. That dressing is 60 to 80 calories on top of the fried dough. Sodium is around 480mg per 100g in the CFS figure, so a plate is close to 1,000mg before the soy in your congee counts. It is a once a week breakfast, not a daily one.

How to eat it lighter

  • Order plain rice roll (齋腸) with your congee instead. About 130 calories versus 450.
  • Share the plate. One piece of zha leung is 225 calories, still a snack rather than a meal.
  • Ask for less sauce (少醬). The dressing is 60 to 80 calories and a lot of sodium.
  • If you want the crunch, half a plain you tiao dipped in congee is about 135 calories, less than a full zha leung piece.

Common questions

How many calories in zha leung?
About 450 calories for a standard two piece plate, based on the Centre for Food Safety figure of 230 kcal per 100g. One piece is around 225.
Is zha leung or plain cheung fun healthier?
Plain rice roll by a wide margin: about 130 calories a plate versus 450 for zha leung. The fried dough stick inside is what adds the 300 difference.
What is zha leung made of?
A deep fried dough stick (you tiao, about 230 calories) wrapped in a sheet of steamed rice noodle (about 140), dressed with sweet soy and sesame.
