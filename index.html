03 Why most diets fail

Being from Malaysia, keto is hard. Asian food revolves around carbs. Mee goreng, nasi goreng, wan tan mee, chicken rice. Keto is also expensive to maintain. Paleo is similar, just messier. Intermittent fasting works but is hard to stick to long term. The real problem with all of them? One birthday party, one family dinner, and you feel like you've failed.

But carbs are not the culprit. It is the lack of awareness of what calories actually are. Think of your body like a car. Calories are fuel. Every day your body needs a certain amount to run. That number is your TDEE. Eat more than that number, you gain weight. Eat less, you lose weight. That is it.

No diet is magic. They are all just calorie control with extra rules. Volume eating has one rule: eat enough to hit your calorie target. Everything fits, as long as it fits the number.

04 The secret to making it easy

As someone who has lost 40kg, I have tried every diet out there. Keto, paleo, intermittent fasting. They all work on the same principle: calories in vs calories out. You cannot cheat biology. But there is one approach that made it feel like it was not a diet at all. Volume eating.

The idea is simple. Eat low calorie foods in large quantities. Say you are eating 2000 calories across two meals, 1000 calories each. Structure each meal like this: 250g of rice or potato. 300g of chicken breast. Then fill the rest of your plate with vegetables or fruit.

That is nearly 1kg of food in one sitting. You will have trouble finishing it.

Swap the vegetables to whatever you like. Broccoli, cauliflower, any greens. Hate vegetables? 1kg of watermelon is only 300 calories. Eat your rice and chicken and have 1kg of watermelon as dessert.

That is the secret. Eat foods that are filling and low in calories, and losing weight feels easy.

05 What to eat

Simple. Whole foods only. Nothing processed. Your plate has three parts.

Carbs: rice or potato. Fills you up, fuels your body, gives you energy to lift.

Protein: chicken breast or steak. Builds and keeps your muscle. Hit your protein target every day, non negotiable.

Volume: pile on vegetables, raw or stir fried. Bowl of soup on the side. Finish with fruit. Eat until you are stuffed.

06 Why you should lift

You are eating clean. But here is what takes it to the next level. Lifting heavy, at least twice or three times a week.

If all you do is eat less, you lose fat and muscle. Without muscle you will look scrawny, not lean. With muscle, you look completely different at the same weight.

Benefits of lifting: you build and retain muscle. More muscle means you burn more calories at rest, which means you can eat more. You look good, actually good. Your posture improves. You sleep better.

For the ladies, do not worry about getting bulky. It is extremely hard to get bulky. You will just end up looking amazing.

Lifting and eating clean go hand in hand. But if the gym feels like too much right now, start with the diet. Diet is 90% of the work anyway.
